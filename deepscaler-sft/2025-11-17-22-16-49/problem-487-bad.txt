First, we start by analyzing the problem. We have a circle with equation \(x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 2y + 1 = 0\). To put this equation in a more recognizable form, we can complete the square.

1. Rewrite the circle equation:
   \[
   x^2 - 2x + y^2 - 2y + 1 = 0 \implies (x - 1)^2 - 1 + (y - 1)^2 - 1 + 1 = 0 \implies (x - 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 1.
   \]
   Thus, the circle has center \((1, 1)\) and radius \(1\).

2. The point \(P\) is on the line \(3x + 4y + 3 = 0\). Let's denote \(P = (x_0, y_0)\) such that \(3x_0 + 4y_0 + 3 = 0\).

3. The tangents drawn from a point to a circle form right angles with the radii at the points of tangency. Therefore, \(PA\) and \(PB\) are the lengths of the tangents from point \(P\) to the circle, and \(CA = CB = 1\) (since they are the radii of the circle).

4. The quadrilateral \(PACB\) is a kite, and its area can be calculated as twice the area of triangle \(PAC\):
   \[
   \text{Area of } \triangle PAC = \frac{1}{2} \times PA \times CA \times \sin \angle PCA,
   \]
   but we can also use a more direct formula based on the lengths and properties of the tangents and circle.

5. To find the minimum area of quadrilateral \(PACB\), we need to consider the geometric properties and use calculus or optimization methods if necessary. We know that the area of the quadrilateral \(PACB\) can be written as twice the area of \(\triangle PAC\). The key is to minimize this area.

To proceed, let's derive the formula for the length of the tangents from point \(P\) to the circle. The length of the tangent from a point \((x_0, y_0)\) to the circle \((x - 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 1\) is given by:
\[
PA = PB = \sqrt{(x_0 - 1)^2 + (y_0 - 1)^2 - 1}.
\]

Since \(P\) is on the line \(3x + 4y + 3 = 0\), we substitute \(y_0 = -\frac{3x_0 + 3}{4}\) into the formula. The area of the quadrilateral \(PACB\) is then:
\[
\text{Area of } PACB = 2 \times \text{Area of } \triangle PAC = 2 \times \frac{1}{2} \times PA \times CA \times \sin \angle PCA = PA \times 1 \times \sin \angle PCA.
\]
Given that \(\sin \angle PCA = 1\) when \(\angle PCA = 90^\circ\) (which is the case here as the tangent and the radius are perpendicular), we get:
\[
\text{Area of } PACB = PA \times \sqrt{2} \quad (\text{because it's a right triangle and properties of tangents from a point to a circle}).
\]

Thus, the key is to minimize \(PA\). So, substitute \( y_0 = -\frac{3x_0 + 3}{4} \) into the formula for \( PA \):
\[
PA = \sqrt{\left( x_0 - 1 \right)^2 + \left( -\frac{3x_0 + 3}{4} - 1 \right)^2 - 1}.
\]

We want to minimize this expression. Let's implement this in Python to get the minimum value.

import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
x0 = sp.symbols('x0', real=True)
y0 = -(3 * x0 + 3) / 4

# Calculate the length of the tangent PA

# Find the minimum value of PA

# The minimum area of quadrilateral PACB will be 2 * (1/2 * PA * 1 * sin(90°)) = PA * sqrt(2)
