First, let's start by determining the volume relationship between the smaller beads and the larger sphere.

1. **Volume of a Sphere:**
   The formula for the volume of a sphere is given by:
   \[
   V = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3
   \]
   where \( r \) is the radius of the sphere.

2. **Volume of the Smaller Beads:**
   Each smaller bead has a radius of 3. So, the volume of one smaller bead is:
   \[
   V_{\text{small}} = \frac{4}{3} \pi (3)^3 = \frac{4}{3} \pi \cdot 27 = 36 \pi
   \]
   Since there are 27 smaller beads, the total volume of all the smaller beads is:
   \[
   V_{\text{total}} = 27 \cdot 36 \pi = 972 \pi
   \]

3. **Volume of the Larger Sphere:**
   Let the radius of the larger sphere be \( R \). The volume of the larger sphere is:
   \[
   V_{\text{large}} = \frac{4}{3} \pi R^3
   \]
   Since the volume of the larger sphere is equal to the combined volume of the 27 smaller beads, we can set the volumes equal to each other:
   \[
   \frac{4}{3} \pi R^3 = 972 \pi
   \]

4. **Solve for \( R \):**
   We can cancel \(\pi\) from both sides of the equation:
   \[
   \frac{4}{3} R^3 = 972
   \]
   Multiply both sides by \(\frac{3}{4}\):
   \[
   R^3 = 972 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 729
   \]
   Take the cube root of both sides:
   \[
   R = \sqrt[3]{729} = 9
   \]

So, the radius of the larger gold sphere is 9. Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python.

# Define the equation based on the volumes
R = sp.symbols('R')
volume_equation = (4/3) * sp.pi * R**3 - 972 * sp.pi

# Solve for R
solution = sp.solve(volume_equation, R)

# Get the positive solution (since radius cannot be negative)
radius_larger_sphere = [sol.evalf() for sol in solution if sol > 0][0]
