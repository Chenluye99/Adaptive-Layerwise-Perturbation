### Step 1: Set Up the Problem

1. **Define the Probabilities:**
   - Probability that a randomly selected person has influenza from region \(A\) is 0.06.
   - Probability that a randomly selected person has influenza from region \(B\) is 0.05.
   - Probability that a randomly selected person has influenza from region \(C\) is 0.04.

2. **Define the Population Ratios:**
   - Let the populations of regions \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\) be \(3k\), \(5k\), and \(2k\) respectively, where \(k\) is a scaling factor.

3. **Find the Total Probability That a Person Has Influenza:**

   To find the probability that a randomly selected person from all three regions has influenza, we use the law of total probability:
   \[
   P(\text{Influenza}) = P(\text{Influenza} \mid A) \cdot P(A) + P(\text{Influenza} \mid B) \cdot P(B) + P(\text{Influenza} \mid C) \cdot P(C)
   \]
   where \(P(A) = \frac{3k}{3k + 5k + 2k} = \frac{3}{10}\), \(P(B) = \frac{5k}{3k + 5k + 2k} = \frac{5}{10} = \frac{1}{2}\), and \(P(C) = \frac{2k}{3k + 5k + 2k} = \frac{2}{10} = \frac{1}{5}\).

   Substituting the probabilities, we get:
   \[
   P(\text{Influenza}) = 0.06 \cdot \frac{3}{10} + 0.05 \cdot \frac{5}{10} + 0.04 \cdot \frac{2}{10}
   \]

4. **Find the Probability That a Person is from Region \(A\) Given That They Have Influenza:**

   We use Bayes' theorem for this part:
   \[
   P(A \mid \text{Influenza}) = \frac{P(\text{Influenza} \mid A) \cdot P(A)}{P(\text{Influenza})}
   \]
   We already have \(P(\text{Influenza} \mid A) = 0.06\), \(P(A) = \frac{3}{10}\), and we calculated \(P(\text{Influenza})\) in the previous step.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to get the precise numerical results.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given probabilities
prob_influenza_given_A = 0.06
prob_influenza_given_B = 0.05
prob_influenza_given_C = 0.04

# Population ratios
prob_A = 3 / 10
prob_B = 5 / 10
prob_C = 2 / 10

# Calculate the total probability that a person has influenza
prob_influenza = (prob_influenza_given_A * prob_A) + (prob_influenza_given_B * prob_B) + (prob_influenza_given_C * prob_C)

# Calculate the probability that a person is from region A given that they have influenza
prob_A_given_influenza = (prob_influenza_given_A * prob_A) / prob_influenza

print(f"Probability that a randomly selected person has influenza: {prob_influenza}")
print(f"Probability that a person is from region A given that they have influenza: {prob_A_given_influenza}")
```
