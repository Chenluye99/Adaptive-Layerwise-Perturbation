To solve this problem, we will use the formula for the lateral surface area of a frustum of a right circular cone. The formula for the lateral surface area (LSA) of a frustum is given by:

\[ \text{LSA} = \pi (R + r) l \]

where:
- \( R \) is the radius of the lower base,
- \( r \) is the radius of the upper base,
- \( l \) is the slant height of the frustum.

First, we need to find the slant height \( l \) of the frustum. We can use the Pythagorean theorem to determine \( l \):

\[ l = \sqrt{ (R - r)^2 + h^2 } \]

where:
- \( R = 6 \) inches (radius of the lower base),
- \( r = 3 \) inches (radius of the upper base),
- \( h = 4 \) inches (height of the frustum).

Let's plug in these values and compute \( l \):

\[ l = \sqrt{ (6 - 3)^2 + 4^2 } = \sqrt{ 3^2 + 4^2 } = \sqrt{ 9 + 16 } = \sqrt{ 25 } = 5 \]

Now that we have the slant height, we can substitute \( R \), \( r \), and \( l \) into the formula for the lateral surface area:

\[ \text{LSA} = \pi (6 + 3) \cdot 5 = \pi \cdot 9 \cdot 5 = 45 \pi \]

Let's confirm this by coding the solution in Python to ensure the result is accurate.
```python
import math

# Given dimensions
R = 6  # radius of the lower base
r = 3  # radius of the upper base
h = 4  # height of the frustum

# Calculate the slant height, l
l = math.sqrt((R - r)**2 + h**2)

# Calculate the lateral surface area of the frustum
LSA = math.pi * (R + r) * l
```
