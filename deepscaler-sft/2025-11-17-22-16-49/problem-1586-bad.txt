1. **Understand the Angle:**
    - The angle \(240^\circ\) is in the third quadrant of the unit circle. 
    - The reference angle for \(240^\circ\) is \(240^\circ - 180^\circ = 60^\circ\).
    - In the third quadrant, the tangent function is positive, so \(\tan 240^\circ = \tan 60^\circ\).

2. **Find \(\tan 60^\circ\):**
    - From trigonometric values, we know that \(\tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}\).
    - Therefore, \(\tan 240^\circ = \sqrt{3}\).
