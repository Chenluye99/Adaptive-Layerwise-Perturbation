To solve this problem, let's start by understanding the given information. We are told that $h(x)$ is a polynomial of degree 5, and it is expressed as the product of $(x^2 - 7x + 10) \cdot g(x)$. 

First, let's factorize the quadratic polynomial \(x^2 - 7x + 10\):
\[ x^2 - 7x + 10 = (x - 2)(x - 5). \]
So, we have:
\[ h(x) = (x - 2)(x - 5) \cdot g(x). \]

We know that the degree of a product of polynomials is the sum of their degrees. Therefore, if \( (x - 2)(x - 5) \) is a polynomial of degree 2 and \( g(x) \) is a polynomial of degree \( b \), then the degree of \( h(x) \) will be:
\[ \text{Degree of } (x - 2)(x - 5) + \text{Degree of } g(x) = 2 + b. \]
We are given that the degree of $h(x)$ is 5. So, we can set up the following equation:
\[ 2 + b = 5. \]
Solving for \( b \):
\[ b = 5 - 2 = 3. \]

To ensure the solution is accurate, we will confirm this by coding a function to check our reasoning by creating polynomials with the described properties and verify their degrees.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define a polynomial g(x) of degree 3
x = sp.symbols('x')
b = 3
g = sp.poly(sp.expand(x**b + 2*x**(b-1) + 3*x**(b-2) + 4*x**(b-3) + 5), x)

# Define (x^2 - 7x + 10)
factor_poly = sp.poly((x - 2) * (x - 5), x)

# Compute h(x) = (x^2 - 7x + 10) * g(x)
```
