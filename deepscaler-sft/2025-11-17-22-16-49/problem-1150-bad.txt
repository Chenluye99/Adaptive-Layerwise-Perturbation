\[
\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 + t \\ 3t \\ 1 - t \end{pmatrix}.
\]

1. **Substitute the Parameterized Line into the Plane Equation:**
    - The line can be expressed in terms of the parameter \( t \) as:
      \[
      x = 1 + t, \quad y = 3t, \quad z = 1 - t.
      \]
    - Substitute these expressions into the plane equation \( x + y + cz = d \):
      \[
      (1 + t) + (3t) + c(1 - t) = d.
      \]
    - Simplify the left-hand side:
      \[
      1 + t + 3t + c - ct = d \implies 1 + c + (1 + 3 - c)t = d \implies 1 + c + (4 - c)t = d.
      \]

2. **Formulate the Equation:**
    - For the equation \( 1 + c + (4 - c)t = d \) to hold for all values of \( t \), the coefficient of \( t \) must be zero (otherwise, the left side would not be a constant for all \( t \)):
      \[
      4 - c = 0 \implies c = 4.
      \]
    - Substituting \( c = 4 \) back into the equation, we get:
      \[
      1 + 4 + (4 - 4)t = d \implies 5 = d.
      \]
    - Therefore, we find that \( c = 4 \) and \( d = 5 \).

    - The ordered pair \((c, d)\) is \((4, 5)\).
