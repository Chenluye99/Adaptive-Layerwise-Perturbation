First, let's begin by calculating the total number of ways to order the 3 chocolate chip cookies and 4 sugar cookies. We have 7 cookies in total, and any arrangement of these 7 cookies is a permutation of 7 items where 3 are identical of one kind, and 4 are identical of another kind. This is given by the multinomial coefficient:

\[
\frac{7!}{3!4!}
\]

Next, we need to consider the condition that either Amy eats a chocolate chip cookie first, she eats a chocolate chip cookie last, or both. Instead of directly computing this, it is more efficient to use complementary counting. We will calculate the number of orders where Amy does not eat a chocolate chip cookie either first or last, and then subtract this from the total number of orders.

### Step-by-step Solution:

1. **Calculate the Total Number of Orders:**
   The total number of ways to order 3 identical chocolate chip cookies (C) and 4 identical sugar cookies (S) is given by the multinomial coefficient:

   \[
   \text{Total orders} = \frac{7!}{3!4!} = \frac{5040}{6 \cdot 24} = \frac{5040}{144} = 35
   \]

2. **Calculate the Number of Orders Where Amy Does Not Eat a Chocolate Chip Cookie First or Last:**
   - If the first cookie is a sugar cookie, then we have 4 choices for the first cookie being a sugar cookie. Then we need to arrange 3 C and 3 S in the remaining 6 positions.
   - If the last cookie is a sugar cookie, then we have 4 choices for the last cookie being a sugar cookie. Then we need to arrange 3 C and 3 S in the first 6 positions.
   - We need to use inclusion-exclusion to determine the orders where neither the first nor the last cookie is a chocolate chip cookie.

   Let's label positions as 1 through 7 and consider these scenarios with Python.

### Implementation in Python:

We'll enumerate all the distinct permutations of the 7 cookies and count how many of those permutations satisfy the condition that either the first or last cookie is a chocolate chip cookie (or both).

Let's write this step-by-step in Python.
```python
import itertools

# Create the list of cookies [C, C, C, S, S, S, S]
cookies = ['C', 'C', 'C', 'S', 'S', 'S', 'S']

# Generate all distinct permutations of the list of cookies
distinct_permutations = set(tuple(perm) for perm in itertools.permutations(cookies))

# Count permutations that satisfy the condition that either the first or last cookie is a 'C' (or both)
count = 0

for perm in distinct_permutations:
    if perm[0] == 'C' or perm[-1] == 'C':
        count += 1

print(count)
```
