Let's solve for the area of \(\triangle ABE\) using coordinates and the formula for the area of a triangle given its vertices \((x_1, y_1)\), \((x_2, y_2)\), and \((x_3, y_3)\):

\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2) \right|
\]

Substitute \( A = (0, 0) \), \( B = (5, 0) \), and \( E = \left( 5, \frac{10}{3} \right) \):

\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| 0 \left( 0 - \frac{10}{3} \right) + 5 \left( \frac{10}{3} - 0 \right) + 5 \left( 0 - 0 \right) \right| = \frac{1}{2} \left| 0 + 5 \cdot \frac{10}{3} + 0 \right| = \frac{1}{2} \left| \frac{50}{3} \right| = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{50}{3} = \frac{25}{3}
\]

```python
import sympy as sp

# Coordinates of points
A = (0, 0)
B = (5, 0)
E = (5, 10 / 3)

# Function to calculate the area of a triangle given its vertices
def triangle_area(A, B, E):
    x1, y1 = A
    x2, y2 = B
    x3, y3 = E
    return sp.Rational(1, 2) * abs(x1*(y2 - y3) + x2*(y3 - y1) + x3*(y1 - y2))

# Calculate the area
area_ABE = triangle_area(A, B, E)
print(area_ABE)
```
