First, let's understand the problem. The outer perimeter of the frame measures 60 cm by 80 cm, and each side of the frame is 10 cm wide. We need to determine the dimensions of the actual mirror.

1. **Determine the dimensions of the mirror:**
    - The outer dimensions of the frame are 80 cm by 60 cm.
    - Since the frame has a width of 10 cm on each side, we need to subtract 10 cm from each side of both the length and the width to get the dimensions of the mirror.
    - Therefore, the dimensions of the mirror will be:
      \[
      \text{Length of mirror} = 80 \, \text{cm} - 2 \times 10 \, \text{cm} = 60 \, \text{cm}
      \]
      \[
      \text{Width of mirror} = 60 \, \text{cm} - 2 \times 10 \, \text{cm} = 40 \, \text{cm}
      \]

2. **Calculate the area of the mirror:**
    - The area of a rectangle is given by the product of its length and width.
    - So, the area of the mirror is:
      \[
      \text{Area of mirror} = 60 \, \text{cm} \times 40 \, \text{cm} = 2400 \, \text{cm}^2
      \]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python.
```python
# Given dimensions
outer_length = 80
outer_width = 60
frame_width = 10

# Calculate the dimensions of the mirror
mirror_length = outer_length - 2 * frame_width
mirror_width = outer_width - 2 * frame_width

# Calculate the area of the mirror
mirror_area = mirror_length * mirror_width
print(mirror_area)
```
Code execution result: 2400

Thus, the area of the mirror is \(\boxed{2400}\) square centimeters. This confirms our solution is accurate.
