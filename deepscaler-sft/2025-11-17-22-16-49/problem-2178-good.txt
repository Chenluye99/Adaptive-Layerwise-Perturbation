   - We know the formula for the area of a trapezoid is:
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times (\text{Base}_1 + \text{Base}_2) \times \text{Height}
     \]
   - Substituting the given values into the formula, we get:
     \[
     162 = \frac{1}{2} \times (16 + \text{Base}_2) \times 12
     \]
   - Simplifying the equation:
     \[
     162 = 6 \times (16 + \text{Base}_2)
     \]
     \[
     162 = 6 \times (16 + \text{Base}_2)

     \[
     27 = 16 + \text{Base}_2

     \[

2. **Calculate the Lengths of the Sides:**
   - We have the trapezoid with vertices at \((0, 0)\), \((0, 12)\), \((11, 12)\), and \((16, 0)\).
   - We need to compute the lengths of the non-parallel sides, i.e., the lengths of the segments connecting \((0, 12)\) to \((11, 12)\) and \((16, 0)\) to \((0, 0)\).

3. **Use the Distance Formula:**
   - The distance formula between two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is:
     \[
     \text{Distance} = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}
     \]
   - Let's calculate the lengths of the sides:
     - Length of the side between \((0, 12)\) and \((11, 12)\):
       \[
       \text{Length}_1 = \sqrt{(11 - 0)^2 + (12 - 12)^2} = \sqrt{11^2} = 11 \text{ cm}
       \]
     - Length of the side between \((16, 0)\) and \((0, 0)\):
       \[
       \text{Length}_2 = \sqrt{(0 - 16)^2 + (0 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{16^2} = 16 \text{ cm}
       \]
     - Length of the side between \((16, 0)\) and \((11, 12)\):
       \[
       \text{Length}_3 = \sqrt{(11 - 16)^2 + (12 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{(-5)^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13 \text{ cm}
       \]
     - Length of the side between \((0, 12)\) and \((0, 0)\):
       \[
       \text{Length}_4 = \sqrt{(0 - 0)^2 + (12 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{12^2} = 12 \text{ cm}
       \]

4. **Calculate the Perimeter:**
   - The perimeter of the trapezoid is the sum of all its sides:
     \[
     \text{Perimeter} = 16 + 11 + 13 + 12 = 52 \text{ cm}
