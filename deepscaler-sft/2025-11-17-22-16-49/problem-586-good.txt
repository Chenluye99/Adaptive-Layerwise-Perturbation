To solve this problem, we need to consider the worst-case scenario where we draw as many balls as possible without getting 10 balls of any particular color.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - There are 70 balls in total.
    - There are 20 red, 20 blue, 20 yellow balls.
    - The remaining balls are black and white. So, the number of black and white balls combined is \(70 - 20 - 20 - 20 = 10\).

2. **Formulate the Strategy:**
    - In the worst case, we could draw up to 9 balls of each of the red, blue, and yellow colors before drawing the 10th ball of any of these colors.
    - Additionally, we could draw all 10 black and white balls before drawing the 10th ball of any of the colors red, blue, or yellow.
    - So, the worst-case scenario involves drawing 9 balls each of the red, blue, and yellow colors (i.e., 9 + 9 + 9 = 27) plus all 10 black and white balls (i.e., 10).

    This means, in the worst case, we could draw \(9 + 9 + 9 + 10 = 27 + 10 = 37\) balls and still not have 10 balls of the same color. The next ball we draw will ensure that we have at least 10 balls of one color.

3. **Determine the Minimum Number of Balls to Draw:**
    - Therefore, the minimum number of balls that must be drawn to ensure that there are at least 10 balls of one color is 37 + 1 = 38.

```python
# We're solving for the minimum number of balls that need to be drawn to ensure there are at least 10 balls of the same color.
min_balls_to_draw = 0
```
