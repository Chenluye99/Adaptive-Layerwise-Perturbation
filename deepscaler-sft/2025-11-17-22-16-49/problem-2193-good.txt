To solve this problem, we need to find the smallest number that is divisible by both 15 and 48. This number is known as the least common multiple (LCM) of 15 and 48. To find the LCM of two numbers, we can use the relationship between the greatest common divisor (GCD) and LCM, which is given by:

\[
\text{LCM}(a, b) = \frac{|a \cdot b|}{\text{GCD}(a, b)}
\]

First, let's find the GCD of 15 and 48. Then, we can use this GCD to compute the LCM. We'll use Python to ensure our result is accurate.
```python
import math

# Define the numbers
a = 15
b = 48

# Compute the GCD of 15 and 48
gcd_ab = math.gcd(a, b)

# Compute the LCM of 15 and 48 using the relationship LCM(a, b) = |a * b| / GCD(a, b)
lcm_ab = abs(a * b) // gcd_ab
print(lcm_ab)
```
Code execution result: 240

Thus, the smallest number of people that can be broken up both into 15 groups of equal membership and into 48 groups of equal membership is \(\boxed{240}\). This is the least common multiple of 15 and 48.
