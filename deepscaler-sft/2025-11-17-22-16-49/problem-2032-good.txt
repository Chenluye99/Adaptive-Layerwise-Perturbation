Because it is tangent to the third circle with center \((s, 3s)\) and radius \(s\), the distance between the centers is also \(r + s\).

\sqrt{(r - 3s)^2 + (r - s)^2} = r + s

\[
\sqrt{(r - s)^2 + (r - 3s)^2} = r + s
