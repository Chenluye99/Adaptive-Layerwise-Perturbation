To solve this problem, let's start by understanding the problem thoroughly:

1. There are 10 players, and each day, the 10 players split into two groups of 5 players each.
2. Each combination of 5 players occurs exactly once over the course of the semester.
3. We need to determine how many times Justin and Tim are on the same team together.

First, let's work out the total number of ways to choose 5 players out of 10. This is given by the combination formula \( \binom{10}{5} \), which is equal to 252. Each of these 252 unique groups of 5 players will appear exactly once in the course of the semester.

For each group of 5 players chosen, there is only one other group of 5 players that will be formed from the remaining 5 players. So, for each way to choose the first group of 5 players, there is exactly one way to choose the second group of 5 players such that all 10 players are split into two groups of 5.

Next, we need to determine how many of these 252 different groups of 5 players include both Justin and Tim. If Justin and Tim are both in the same group of 5, we need to choose 3 more players from the remaining 8 players to complete that group of 5. The number of ways to choose 3 players from 8 is given by \( \binom{8}{3} \), which is equal to 56.

So, there are 56 different groups of 5 players that include both Justin and Tim. Each of these groups will form a team with the other 5 players, and since each match-up of 5 players occurs exactly once, each of these 56 groups will occur exactly once in the course of the semester.

Thus, Justin and Tim will be on the same team exactly 56 times over the course of the semester.

Let's confirm this by coding a solution in Python to ensure the accuracy of our answer.
```python
import itertools

# Get all combinations of 5 players out of 10
players = list(range(10))
combinations_of_5 = list(itertools.combinations(players, 5))

# Find the count where both Justin (0) and Tim (1) are in the same combination of 5 players
justin = 0
tim = 1
count_justin_tim_same_group = 0

for combo in combinations_of_5:
    if justin in combo and tim in combo:
        count_justin_tim_same_group += 1

print(count_justin_tim_same_group)
```
