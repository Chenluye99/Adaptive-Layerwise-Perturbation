To solve this problem, we need to find the height of a right circular cone that has a radius of 3 inches and a volume of 93 cubic inches.

The formula for the volume \( V \) of a right circular cone is given by:
\[ V = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h \]

where:
- \( r \) is the radius of the base of the cone,
- \( h \) is the height of the cone,
- \( \pi \) is a mathematical constant, approximately equal to 3.14159.

We are given:
- \( r = 3 \) inches,
- \( V = 93 \) cubic inches.

We need to find \( h \). Substituting the given values into the formula, we get:
\[ 93 = \frac{1}{3} \pi (3)^2 h \]
\[ 93 = \frac{1}{3} \pi \cdot 9 h \]
\[ 93 = 3 \pi h \]
\[ 93 = 3 \cdot 3.14159 \cdot h \]
\[ 93 = 9.42477 h \]
\[ h = \frac{93}{9.42477} \]

Let's compute this step by step using Python to ensure accuracy and get the final answer to the nearest whole number.
```python
import math

# Given values
V = 93  # volume in cubic inches
r = 3   # radius in inches

# Formula rearranged to solve for h: h = (3 * V) / (π * r^2)
h = (3 * V) / (math.pi * r**2)

# Rounding to the nearest whole number
h_nearest_whole_number = round(h)
print(h_nearest_whole_number)
```
