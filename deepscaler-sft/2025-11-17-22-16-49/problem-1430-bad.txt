First, let's define the problem and solve it step-by-step.

1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - The cook has 10 red peppers and 5 green peppers, making a total of 15 peppers.
    - We need to find the probability that, when the cook selects 6 peppers at random, at least 4 of them are green.
    - "At least 4 green peppers" means we need to consider the cases where the cook selects 4 green peppers, 5 green peppers, or 6 green peppers.

2. **Mathematical Formulation:**
    - To solve this, we can use combinations. The number of ways to choose 6 peppers out of 15 is given by \(\binom{15}{6}\).
    - We then need to consider the different ways in which the number of green peppers selected can be 4, 5, or 6.
    - The number of ways to choose \(k\) green peppers out of 5 is \(\binom{5}{k}\), and the number of ways to choose the remaining \(6 - k\) peppers from the 10 red peppers is \(\binom{10}{6 - k}\).
    - So, the number of ways to get exactly \(k\) green peppers in the selection of 6 peppers is \(\binom{5}{k} \times \binom{10}{6 - k}\).
    - We need to consider the valid ranges for \(k\) such that \(0 \leq k \leq 5\) and \(0 \leq 6 - k \leq 10\), but since there are only 5 green peppers, the relevant values of \(k\) are 4, 5, and 6 (as having 6 green peppers would mean selecting all 6 out of the available 5, which isn't possible directly but we can consider cases stepwise).

3. **Use Python to Calculate:**
    - We will loop through the possible cases and sum up the successful outcomes, then divide by the total number of outcomes to get the probability.

Let's implement this in Python.
```python
import math

# Total ways to choose 6 peppers out of 15
total_ways = math.comb(15, 6)

# Initialize counter for successful outcomes (at least 4 green peppers)
successful_outcomes = 0

# Check through all possible ways of choosing 6 peppers and count how many of those ways include at least 4 green peppers
for green_count in range(6):
    if 0 <= green_count <= 5:
        red_count = 6 - green_count
        
        if 0 <= red_count <= 10 and red_count + green_count == 6:
            successful_outcomes += math.comb(5, green_count) * math.comb(10, red_count)

# Calculate probability
probability = successful_outcomes / total_ways
print(probability)
```
