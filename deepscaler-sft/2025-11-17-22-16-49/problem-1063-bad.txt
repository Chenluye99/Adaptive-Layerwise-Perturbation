To solve this problem, let's start by formulating an equation for the number of widgets produced on Monday and on Tuesday.

1. **Number of Widgets Produced on Monday:**
   - Widgets per hour: \( w \)
   - Hours worked: \( t \)
   - Total widgets on Monday: \( \text{Widgets}_{\text{Monday}} = w \cdot t \)

2. **Number of Widgets Produced on Tuesday:**
   - Widgets per hour: \( w + 4 \)
   - Hours worked: \( t - 2 \)
   - Total widgets on Tuesday: \( \text{Widgets}_{\text{Tuesday}} = (w + 4) \cdot (t - 2) \)

3. **Find the Difference in Widgets Produced:**
   - Difference in widgets produced: \( \Delta \text{Widgets} = \text{Widgets}_{\text{Monday}} - \text{Widgets}_{\text{Tuesday}} \)

Given that \( w = 2t \), we can substitute \( w \) with \( 2t \) in the equations above.

- Substitute \( w \) with \( 2t \) in the expressions for Monday and Tuesday:

  \[
  \text{Widgets}_{\text{Monday}} = 2t \cdot t = 2t^2
  \]

  \[
  \text{Widgets}_{\text{Tuesday}} = (2t + 4) \cdot (t - 2)
  \]

  Now let's expand and simplify the expression for \(\text{Widgets}_{\text{Tuesday}}\):

  \[
  \text{Widgets}_{\text{Tuesday}} = (2t + 4) \cdot (t - 2) = 2t \cdot t + 4 \cdot t - 2 \cdot 2t - 4 \cdot 2 = 2t^2 + 4t - 4t - 8 = 2t^2 - 8
  \]

  Therefore, the difference in widgets produced is:

  \[
  \Delta \text{Widgets} = 2t^2 - (2t^2 - 8) = 2t^2 - 2t^2 + 8 = 8
  \]
