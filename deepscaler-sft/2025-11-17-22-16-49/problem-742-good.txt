First, let's start by noting that points \(P, Q, R, S\) form a trapezoid. For four points to form a trapezoid, one pair of opposite sides must be parallel. 

To determine the conditions, we need to check for which pair of sides are parallel. The slope of the line connecting two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is given by \(\frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\).

Let's calculate the slopes of the sides of the trapezoid formed by the points \(P, Q, R, S\):

1. **Slope of \(PQ\):**
   \[
   \text{slope of } PQ = \frac{\cos^2 x - \cot^2 x}{\cos x - \cot x}
   \]

2. **Slope of \(RS\):**
   \[
   \text{slope of } RS = \frac{\sin^2 x - \tan^2 x}{\sin x - \tan x}
   \]

3. **Slope of \(PR\):**
   \[
   \text{slope of } PR = \frac{\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x}{\cos x - \sin x}
   \]

4. **Slope of \(QS\):**
   \[
   \text{slope of } QS = \frac{\cos^2 x - \tan^2 x}{\cos x - \tan x}
   \]

For these points to form a trapezoid, either the slopes of \(PQ\) and \(RS\) are equal, or the slopes of \(PR\) and \(QS\) are equal. Let's set up these equations and check for the condition under which the trapezoid condition holds, i.e., two of these slopes must be equal.

    Q = (1 / np.tan(x), (1 / np.tan(x))**2)
