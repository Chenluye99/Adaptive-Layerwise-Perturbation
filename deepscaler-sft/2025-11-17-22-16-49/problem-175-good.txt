1. Let \( N \) be the total number of students in Ms. Hanson's class.

We want to find the maximum and minimum values of \( y \% \), where \( y \% \) is the percentage of students that changed their response from the beginning to the end of the school year.

\[ a + b = 0.60N \]
\[ c + d = 0.40N \]
\[ a + c = 0.80N \]
\[ b + d = 0.20N \]

\[ y = \frac{b + c}{N} \times 100 \]
