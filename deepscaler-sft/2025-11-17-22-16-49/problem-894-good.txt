To solve the problem, we need to find the mean (or average) of the whole numbers from 1 to 250. 

1. **Find the Sum of the Numbers from 1 to 250:**
   The sum of the first \( n \) natural numbers is given by the formula:
   \[
   \text{Sum} = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}
   \]
   For the numbers from 1 to 250, \( n = 250 \), so:
   \[
   \text{Sum} = \frac{250 \cdot 251}{2}
   \]

2. **Find the Mean:**
   The mean of the numbers from 1 to 250 is given by:
   \[
   \text{Mean} = \frac{\text{Sum}}{n} = \frac{\frac{250 \cdot 251}{2}}{250} = \frac{250 \cdot 251}{2 \cdot 250} = \frac{251}{2}
   \]

3. **Evaluate Numerically:**
   We can compute \(\frac{251}{2}\) to get the decimal value to the nearest tenth. Let's execute this step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Calculate the sum of the numbers from 1 to 250
n = 250
sum_1_to_250 = n * (n + 1) // 2

# Calculate the mean
mean = sum_1_to_250 / n

# Return the mean rounded to the nearest tenth
mean_rounded = round(mean, 1)
print(mean_rounded)
```
Code execution result: 125.5

Thus, the mean of the whole numbers from 1 to 250, expressed as a decimal to the nearest tenth, is \(\boxed{125.5}\).
