First, we need to identify the positive divisors of both 50 and 15. Then, we find the common divisors of these two sets, and finally, we compute the sum of those common divisors.

1. **Find the Divisors of 50:**
   The number 50 has the following divisors: 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50.
   
2. **Find the Divisors of 15:**
   The number 15 has the following divisors: 1, 3, 5, 15.
   
3. **Find the Common Divisors of 50 and 15:**
   The common divisors of 50 and 15 are the numbers that appear in both lists above. So, we compare the two lists and find the common elements: 1, 5.
   
4. **Sum the Common Divisors:**
   The sum of the common divisors 1 and 5 is \(1 + 5 = 6\).

Let's implement this step-by-step reasoning in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Step 1: Find all divisors of 50
divisors_of_50 = [i for i in range(1, 51) if 50 % i == 0]

# Step 2: Find all divisors of 15
divisors_of_15 = [i for i in range(1, 16) if 15 % i == 0]

# Step 3: Find common divisors of 50 and 15
common_divisors = [i for i in divisors_of_50 if i in divisors_of_15]

# Step 4: Calculate the sum of the common divisors
sum_of_common_divisors = sum(common_divisors)
print(sum_of_common_divisors)
```
Code execution result: 6

Thus, the sum of all positive divisors of 50 that are also divisors of 15 is \(\boxed{6}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
