### Part (Ⅰ)
1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - The box contains 3 black balls, 2 red balls, and 1 white ball, making a total of 6 balls.
    - If a customer can draw 2 balls at once, the total reward money will be at least 4 yuan.
    - The reward for drawing is as follows:
        - Each black ball gives 1 yuan.
        - Each red ball gives 2 yuan.
        - Each white ball gives 3 yuan.
    - We need to calculate the probability that the total reward is at least 4 yuan.

2. **Enumerate All Possible Outcomes:**
    - We can draw 2 balls out of 6 in different combinations.
    - We need to check each combination to see if the total reward is at least 4 yuan.

    - We will enumerate all ways to draw 2 balls from the 6 balls and compute the rewards for each combination.

```python
import itertools

# Define the balls in the box
balls = ['black', 'black', 'black', 'red', 'red', 'white']

# Get all possible ways to draw 2 balls out of 6
combinations = list(itertools.combinations(balls, 2))

# Calculate the rewards for each combination and count the number of combinations that give at least 4 yuan
count_at_least_4 = 0
total_combinations = len(combinations)

for combo in combinations:
    reward = sum(1 * (ball == 'black') + 2 * (ball == 'red') + 3 * (ball == 'white') for ball in combo)
    if reward >= 4:
        count_at_least_4 += 1

# Calculate the probability
prob_at_least_4 = count_at_least_4 / total_combinations

print(prob_at_least_4)
```
