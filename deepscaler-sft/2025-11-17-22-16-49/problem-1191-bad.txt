First, let's understand the problem step-by-step.

1. **Define the Function \( f(a) \):**
   The function is given as \( f(a) = \frac{1}{1 - a} \).

2. **Find the Inverse Function \( f^{-1}(a) \):**
   To find the inverse function, we start with:
   \[
   y = \frac{1}{1 - a}
   \]
   and solve for \( a \):
   \[
   y (1 - a) = 1 \implies y - ya = 1 \implies - ya = 1 - y \implies ya = y - 1 \implies a = \frac{y - 1}{y}
   \]
   So, substituting \( y \) back with \( a \), we get:
   \[
   f^{-1}(a) = \frac{a - 1}{a}
   \]

3. **Compute the Product \( f^{-1}(a) \times a \times f(a) \):**
   We already have:
   \[
   f(a) = \frac{1}{1 - a}
   \]
   and
   \[
   f^{-1}(a) = \frac{a - 1}{a}
   \]
   Now, let's compute the product:
   \[
   f^{-1}(a) \times a \times f(a) = \left(\frac{a - 1}{a}\right) \times a \times \frac{1}{1 - a}
   \]
   Notice that \( a \) in the numerator and denominator cancel out:
   \[
   f^{-1}(a) \times a \times f(a) = \frac{a - 1}{1 - a}
   \]
   Notice that \( a - 1 = -(1 - a) \), so:
   \[
   \frac{a - 1}{1 - a} = \frac{-(1 - a)}{1 - a} = -1 \quad \text{(as long as } 1 - a \neq 0 \text{, i.e., } a \neq 1)
   \]
   So, the product is:
   \[
   f^{-1}(a) \times a \times f(a) = -1
   \]
