To find the volume of the pyramid, let's follow these steps:

1. **Properties of a Regular Square Pyramid:**
    - A regular square pyramid has a square base and four triangular faces.
    - Each edge of the square base is of equal length (2 in this case).
    - Each of the four side edges is of equal length (√6 in this case).

2. **Find the Height of the Pyramid:**
    - Consider a cross-section of the pyramid, which is a square base with four equilateral triangles on top. 
    - To get the height of the pyramid, let's take the center of the square base and drop a perpendicular to one of the vertices of the base to get the height of one of these equilateral triangles. The center of a square with side length 2 is at a distance of 1 from any side, so the distance from the center of the square to any vertex is \(\sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}\).
    - Now, consider a right triangle formed by the height of the pyramid \( h \), the distance from the center of the base to the midpoint of one of the edges of the base (which is \(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}} = 1\)), and the side edge of the pyramid of length \(\sqrt{6}\). The side edge of the pyramid is the hypotenuse of this right triangle, and its legs are the height of the pyramid and 1 (half of the diagonal of the square base).
    - By the Pythagorean theorem, we get:
      \[
      (\sqrt{6})^2 = h^2 + 1^2 \implies 6 = h^2 + 1 \implies h^2 = 5 \implies h = \sqrt{5}
      \]

3. **Find the Volume of the Pyramid:**
    - The volume \( V \) of a pyramid is given by the formula:
      \[
      V = \frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}
      \]
    - The base of the pyramid is a square with side length 2, so the area of the base is:
      \[
      \text{Base Area} = 2 \times 2 = 4
      \]
    - The height of the pyramid, as found, is \( \sqrt{5} \).
    - Therefore, substituting these values into the volume formula:
      \[
      V = \frac{1}{3} \times 4 \times \sqrt{5} = \frac{4 \sqrt{5}}{3}
      \]

Let's confirm this by implementing the steps in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given values
base_edge_length = 2
side_edge_length = sp.sqrt(6)

# Calculate the height (h) of the pyramid
half_diagonal_base = sp.sqrt(2)  # Half of the diagonal of the square base
h = sp.sqrt(side_edge_length**2 - half_diagonal_base**2)

# Calculate the volume of the pyramid
base_area = base_edge_length ** 2
volume = (1/3) * base_area * h

print(volume)
```
