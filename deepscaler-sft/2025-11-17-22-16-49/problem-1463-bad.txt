To solve the problem, we need to find the product of the roots of the quadratic equation \(18t^2 + 45t - 500 = 0\).

For a general quadratic equation of the form \(at^2 + bt + c = 0\), the product of the roots is given by the formula \(\frac{c}{a}\).

In our equation, \(a = 18\), \(b = 45\), and \(c = -500\). So, the product of the roots is \(\frac{-500}{18} = -\frac{250}{9}\).

Let's confirm this using Python to ensure our result is accurate.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables and the equation
t = sp.symbols('t')
equation = 18*t**2 + 45*t - 500

# Find the roots of the equation
roots = sp.solve(equation, t)

# Calculate the product of the roots
product_of_roots = roots[0] * roots[1]
print(product_of_roots)
```
Code execution result: -250/9

Thus, the product of the roots of the equation \(18t^2 + 45t - 500 = 0\) is \(\boxed{-\frac{250}{9}}\). This confirms our earlier calculation.
