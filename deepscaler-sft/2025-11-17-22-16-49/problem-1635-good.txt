To solve this problem, we need to determine the probability that the Lakers will win the NBA finals in exactly 7 games. For this to happen:

1. The series must go to 7 games, meaning each team must win exactly 3 of the first 6 games.
2. The Lakers must win the 7th game.

First, let's outline the steps needed:

1. **Determine the Probability Distribution for the First 6 Games:**
    - Each game is an independent event with the probability of the Celtics winning a game being \(\frac{2}{3}\), and the probability of the Lakers winning a game being \(\frac{1}{3}\).
    - We need to find the probability that the Lakers and the Celtics each win exactly 3 out of the first 6 games. This is a binomial probability problem.

2. **Calculate the Probability of Each of the First 6 Games Resulting in 3 Wins for Each Team:**
    - The number of ways to choose 3 wins out of 6 games for the Lakers is \(\binom{6}{3}\).
    - The probability of any specific sequence of 3 wins and 3 losses for the Lakers in 6 games is \(\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^3\).
    - Therefore, the probability that the Lakers win exactly 3 out of the first 6 games, and the Celtics win the other 3, is \(\binom{6}{3} \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^3\).

3. **Determine the Probability that the Lakers Win the 7th Game:**
    - The probability that the Lakers win the 7th game is \(\frac{1}{3}\).

4. **Combine the Probabilities:**
    - The overall probability that the Lakers win exactly 3 out of the first 6 games and then win the 7th game is the product of the two probabilities calculated above.
