First, let’s define the probabilities for the spinner outcomes:

- Probability of landing on 1: \( P(1) = \frac{1}{2} \)
- Probability of landing on 2: \( P(2) = \frac{1}{4} \)
- Probability of landing on 3: \( P(3) = \frac{1}{6} \)
- Probability of landing on 4: \( P(4) = \frac{1}{12} \)

We need to find the probability that Max's outcome is greater than Zack's outcome. To do this, we can consider all possible pairs of outcomes for Max and Zack and determine how often Max's outcome is greater than Zack's outcome.

Let's define a double loop to calculate this probability. For each outcome of Max's spin, we'll compare it to every outcome of Zack's spin and compute the probability for each comparison. 

We'll use the following steps:

1. Initialize the total probability of Max landing on a larger number than Zack to 0.
2. Loop through all possible outcomes for Max's spin and for each outcome of Max, loop through all possible outcomes for Zack's spin.
3. For each pair of outcomes, check if Max's outcome is greater than Zack's outcome, and if so, add the product of the probabilities of obtaining those outcomes to the total probability.

Let's implement this using Python.
```python
# Define the probabilities for the outcomes of the spinner
probabilities = {
    1: 1/2,
    2: 1/4,
    3: 1/6,
    4: 1/12
}

# Initialize the total probability that Max's outcome is greater than Zack's outcome
prob_max_greater_than_zack = 0

# Loop through all possible outcomes for Max's and Zack's spins
for max_outcome in probabilities:
    for zack_outcome in probabilities:
        if max_outcome > zack_outcome:
            prob_max_greater_than_zack += probabilities[max_outcome] * probabilities[zack_outcome]

print(prob_max_greater_than_zack)
```
