### Step 1: Conditions for \( 3n \) to be a perfect square

- \( a \) must be even (so that the exponent of 2 in \( 3n \) is \( a \), which is even),
- \( b+1 \) must be even (so that the exponent of 3 in \( 3n \) is \( b+1 \), which is even, implying that \( b \) must be odd),
- All exponents in the prime factorization of \( k \) must be even.

### Step 2: Conditions for \( 2n \) to be a perfect cube

- \( a+1 \) must be a multiple of 3 (so that the exponent of 2 in \( 2n \) is \( a+1 \), which is a multiple of 3),
- \( b \) must be a multiple of 3 (so that the exponent of 3 in \( 2n \) is \( b \), which is a multiple of 3),
- All exponents in the prime factorization of \( k \) must be multiples of 3.
