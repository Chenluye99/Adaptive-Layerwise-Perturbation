To solve the problem, let's start by calculating the areas of the square and the circle individually, and then determine the area of their union.

1. **Area of the Square:**
   - The side length of the square is 10.
   - The area of the square is given by:
     \[
     \text{Area}_{\text{square}} = 10 \times 10 = 100
     \]

2. **Area of the Circle:**
   - The radius of the circle is 10.
   - The area of the circle is given by:
     \[
     \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = \pi \times 10^2 = 100\pi
     \]

3. **Area of Intersection:**
   - The circle is centered at one of the vertices of the square, and it has a radius of 10, which means it extends 10 units from that vertex.
   - Each side of the square is 10 units long, so the part of the circle that lies within the square is a quarter-circle (since a circle's full 360° would cover the entire circle, and only a quarter of that area is within one square's bounds when the circle is centered at one of its vertices).
   - The area of this quarter-circle is:
     \[
     \text{Area}_{\text{quarter-circle}} = \frac{1}{4} \times 100\pi = 25\pi
     \]

4. **Area of the Union:**
   - The area of the union of the square and the circle is the sum of the areas of the square and the circle, minus the area of their intersection (to avoid double-counting the overlapping portion):
     \[
     \text{Area}_{\text{union}} = \text{Area}_{\text{square}} + \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} - \text{Area}_{\text{quarter-circle}} = 100 + 100\pi - 25\pi = 100 + 75\pi
     \]

So, the area of the union of the regions enclosed by the square and the circle is \(\boxed{100 + 75\pi}\).

Let's confirm this by coding the solution in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the side length and radius
side_length = 10
radius = 10

# Calculate the area of the square
area_square = side_length ** 2

# Calculate the area of the circle
area_circle = sp.pi * radius ** 2

# Calculate the area of the quarter circle that lies within the square
area_quarter_circle = area_circle / 4

# Calculate the area of the union of the square and the circle
area_union = area_square + area_circle - area_quarter_circle

print(area_union)
```
