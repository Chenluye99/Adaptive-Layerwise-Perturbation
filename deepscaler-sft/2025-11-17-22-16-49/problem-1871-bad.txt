To solve this problem, we need to find the probability that the square root of a randomly selected two-digit whole number is less than 8.

1. **Identify the Range of Two-Digit Whole Numbers:**
    - Two-digit whole numbers range from 10 to 99, inclusive.
    - There are 90 two-digit numbers (99 - 10 + 1 = 90).

2. **Determine the Condition:**
    - We need the square root of a number to be less than 8. This means the number itself must be less than \(8^2 = 64\).
    - So, we are looking for all two-digit numbers that are less than 64, i.e., numbers from 10 to 63.

3. **Count the Numbers That Meet the Condition:**
    - The numbers that meet this condition are from 10 to 63, inclusive.
    - We need to count how many numbers are in this range.

4. **Calculate the Probability:**
    - The probability is the number of two-digit numbers that are less than 64, divided by the total number of two-digit numbers.
    - This can be written as: \(\frac{\text{Number of two-digit numbers from 10 to 63}}{\text{Total number of two-digit numbers}} = \frac{63 - 10 + 1}{90} = \frac{54}{90} = \frac{3}{5}\).

Let's confirm this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Define the range of two-digit numbers
two_digit_numbers = list(range(10, 100))

# Determine how many of these numbers have a square root that is less than 8
count_condition_met = sum(1 for num in two_digit_numbers if num < 64)

# Calculate the probability
total_two_digit_numbers = len(two_digit_numbers)
probability = count_condition_met / total_two_digit_numbers

# Return the probability as a fraction
from fractions import Fraction
probability_fraction = Fraction(count_condition_met, total_two_digit_numbers)

print(probability_fraction)
```
Code execution result: 3/5

Thus, the probability that the square root of a randomly selected two-digit whole number is less than 8 is \(\boxed{\frac{3}{5}}\). This confirms that our reasoning and solution are accurate.
