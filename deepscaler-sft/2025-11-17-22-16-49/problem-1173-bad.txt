First, let's start by computing the areas of the circles and the annular regions.

1. **Calculate the areas of the circles:**
    - The area of a circle is given by \( A = \pi r^2 \).
    - The inner circle with radius 1: \( A_1 = \pi \cdot 1^2 = \pi \).
    - The circle with radius 3: \( A_3 = \pi \cdot 3^2 = 9\pi \).
    - The circle with radius 5: \( A_5 = \pi \cdot 5^2 = 25\pi \).
    - The circle with radius 7: \( A_7 = \pi \cdot 7^2 = 49\pi \).

2. **Calculate the areas of the rings:**
    - The area of the first black ring (between radii 1 and 3): \( A_{\text{black1}} = A_3 - A_1 = 9\pi - \pi = 8\pi \).
    - The area of the white ring (between radii 3 and 5): \( A_{\text{white1}} = A_5 - A_3 = 25\pi - 9\pi = 16\pi \).
    - The area of the second black ring (between radii 5 and 7): \( A_{\text{black2}} = A_7 - A_5 = 49\pi - 25\pi = 24\pi \).

3. **Calculate the total black and white areas:**
    - Total black area: The inner circle is black and the second black ring is black, so:
      \[
      \text{Total Black Area} = A_1 + A_{\text{black2}} = \pi + 24\pi = 25\pi
      \]
    - Total white area: The first white ring is white, so:
      \[
      \text{Total White Area} = A_{\text{white1}} = 16\pi
      \]

4. **Find the ratio of the black area to the white area:**
    - The ratio of the black area to the white area is:
      \[
      \frac{\text{Total Black Area}}{\text{Total White Area}} = \frac{25\pi}{16\pi} = \frac{25}{16}
      \]

Thus, the ratio of the black area to the white area is \(\frac{25}{16}\). Let's confirm this with Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the radii of the circles
radii = [1, 3, 5, 7]

# Calculate the areas of the circles
areas = [sp.pi * r**2 for r in radii]

# Extract the areas needed to form the black and white regions
A1 = areas[0]
A3 = areas[1]
A5 = areas[2]
A7 = areas[3]

# Calculate the areas of the black and white regions
A_black1 = A3 - A1  # First black ring
A_white1 = A5 - A3  # First white ring
A_black2 = A7 - A5  # Second black ring

# Calculate the total black and white areas
total_black_area = A1 + A_black2
total_white_area = A_white1

# Calculate the ratio of black area to white area
ratio = total_black_area / total_white_area

print(ratio)
```
Code execution result: 25/16
