First, let's start by understanding the given problem and set up a plan before proceeding to code the solution.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - The area of the outer equilateral triangle is 16.
    - The area of the inner equilateral triangle is 1.
    - There are three congruent trapezoids formed by the inner and outer equilateral triangles.

2. **Formulate the Plan:**
    - The three trapezoids and the inner equilateral triangle together make up the outer equilateral triangle.
    - Therefore, the total area of the three trapezoids combined is the area of the outer equilateral triangle minus the area of the inner equilateral triangle.
    - Mathematically, this can be written as:
      \[
      \text{Total area of three trapezoids} = \text{Area of outer equilateral triangle} - \text{Area of inner equilateral triangle} = 16 - 1 = 15
      \]
    - Since there are three congruent trapezoids, the area of one of the trapezoids would be the total area of the three trapezoids divided by 3:
      \[
      \text{Area of one trapezoid} = \frac{15}{3} = 5
      \]

Let's confirm this step-by-step solution using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given areas of the triangles
area_outer_triangle = 16
area_inner_triangle = 1

# Calculate the combined area of the three trapezoids
area_three_trapezoids = area_outer_triangle - area_inner_triangle

# Calculate the area of one trapezoid
area_one_trapezoid = area_three_trapezoids / 3
print(area_one_trapezoid)
```
