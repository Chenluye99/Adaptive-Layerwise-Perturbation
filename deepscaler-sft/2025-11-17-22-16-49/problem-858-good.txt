First, let's break down the problem and solve it step-by-step.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have an 8-foot by 10-foot floor, so the total area of the floor is \(8 \times 10 = 80\) square feet.
    - The floor is tiled with 1-foot by 1-foot tiles, so there are \(8 \times 10 = 80\) tiles in total.
    - Each 1-foot by 1-foot tile has a pattern consisting of four white quarter circles, each with a radius of 0.5 feet.
    - Each quarter circle, when put together, forms a full circle with radius 0.5 feet. The area of a full circle with radius 0.5 feet is \(\pi \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^2 = \pi \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{\pi}{4}\) square feet.
    - Since there are four quarter circles on each tile, the total white area on each tile is \(\frac{\pi}{4} \times 4 = \pi\) square feet.
    - The area of each 1-foot by 1-foot tile is 1 square foot. So, the shaded area of each tile is \(1 - \pi\) square feet.
    - Since there are 80 tiles, the total shaded area of the floor is \(80 \times (1 - \pi)\) square feet.

2. **Formulate and Implement the Solution in Python:**
    - We'll compute this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Number of tiles
num_tiles = 8 * 10

# Area of one tile
tile_area = 1 * 1

# Area of the circles on one tile
radius = 0.5
circle_area_one_quarter = sp.pi * radius**2 / 4
circle_full = circle_area_one_quarter * 4
circle_area_one_tile = circle_full

# Shaded area of one tile
shaded_area_one_tile = tile_area - circle_area_one_tile

# Total shaded area of the floor
total_shaded_area = num_tiles * shaded_area_one_tile
print(total_shaded_area)
```
Code execution result: 80 - 20.0*pi

Thus, the total shaded area of the floor is \(\boxed{80 - 20\pi}\) square feet. This is the final answer.
