1. **Convert the Total Amount of Sugar Needed to an Improper Fraction or Decimal:**
   - Alice needs \( 2 \frac{1}{2} \) cups of sugar.
   - Converting the mixed number to an improper fraction, we get:
     \[
     2 \frac{1}{2} = 2 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{4}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}
     \]
   - Alternatively, converting it to a decimal, we get:
     \[
     2 \frac{1}{2} = 2.5 \text{ cups}
     \]

2. **Determine How Many \(\frac{1}{4}\) Cup Measurements are Needed:**
   - Alice's measuring cup holds \(\frac{1}{4}\) cup of sugar each time it is filled.
   - To find out how many \(\frac{1}{4}\) cups are needed to make \(\frac{5}{2}\) cups, we divide \(\frac{5}{2}\) by \(\frac{1}{4}\):
     \[
     \frac{5}{2} \div \frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{2} \times \frac{4}{1} = \frac{5 \times 4}{2 \times 1} = \frac{20}{2} = 10
     \]
   - This tells us that Alice needs to fill her \(\frac{1}{4}\) cup measure 10 times to get \( \frac{5}{2} \) cups of sugar.
