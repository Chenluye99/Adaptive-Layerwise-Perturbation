2. The hyperbola is vertically oriented because it opens upwards and downwards.

4. The hyperbola also has asymptotes which provide more information. The asymptotes of this hyperbola would be the lines that the hyperbola approaches but never touches, and they can be derived from the equation as well. For the standard form \(\frac{(y - k)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(x - h)^2}{b^2} = 1\), the equations of the asymptotes would be:
   \[
   y - k = \pm \frac{a}{b} (x - h)
   \]
   From the graph, it seems that the asymptotes pass through \((6, -5)\) and extend with slopes of \(\pm \frac{2}{4} = \pm \frac{1}{2}\). This implies \(\frac{a}{b} = \frac{1}{2}\), and since \( a = 2 \), we have:
   \[
   \frac{2}{b} = \frac{1}{2} \implies b = 4
   \]
5. The center of the hyperbola from the graph appears to be at \((6, -5)\), so \( h = 6 \) and \( k = -5 \).

Now, we have the values:
\[ h = 6, \quad k = -5, \quad a = 2, \quad b = 4. \]

Therefore, we can find \( h + k + a + b \):

\[

\]
