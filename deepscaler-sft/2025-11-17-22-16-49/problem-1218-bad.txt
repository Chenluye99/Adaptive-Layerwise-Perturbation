First, let's start by understanding the properties of the complex numbers \(a, b, c\) that form an equilateral triangle in the complex plane.

1. **Properties of Equilateral Triangle in Complex Plane:**
    - If \(a, b, c\) form an equilateral triangle, then one of the complex numbers can be obtained by rotating the other by \( \pm 60^\circ \) (i.e., multiplying by \( e^{ \pm i \cdot \frac{\pi}{3} } = \pm \frac{1}{2} + i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \)).
    - So, we can express \( b \) and \( c \) in terms of \( a \) as follows:
      \[
      b = a \cdot e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} \quad \text{and} \quad c = a \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}
      \]
      or some permutation thereof.

2. **Sum of the Complex Numbers:**
    - The sum \( a + b + c \) is:
      \[
      a + b + c = a + a \cdot e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} + a \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}
      \]
    - We know that \( e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} = \frac{1}{2} + i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \) and \( e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}} = \frac{1}{2} - i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \). Substituting these in, we get:
      \[
      a \cdot e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} = a \left( \frac{1}{2} + i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)
      \]
      \[
      a \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}} = a \left( \frac{1}{2} - i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)
      \]
    - Adding these together with \( a \):
      \[
      a + a \left( \frac{1}{2} + i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right) + a \left( \frac{1}{2} - i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right) = a \left( 1 + \frac{1}{2} + i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{1}{2} - i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right) = a \left( 1 + 1 \right) = 2a
      \]
      So, we have:
      \[
      a + b + c = 2a \quad \text{or} \quad a + b + c = 2a \cdot e^{i \frac{2\pi}{3}} \quad \text{or} \quad a + b + c = 2a \cdot e^{-i \frac{2\pi}{3}}
      \]
      but in all cases, the magnitude will be the same (as long as \(a, b, c\) form an equilateral triangle), meaning:
      \[
      |a + b + c| = |2a|
      \]
    - We are given that \( |a + b + c| = 36 \), so:
      \[
      |2a| = 36 \implies |a| = 18
      \]

3. **Expression for \( |ab + ac + bc| \):**
    - To find \( |ab + ac + bc| \), we can substitute \( b = a \cdot e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} \) and \( c = a \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}} \) into the expression:
      \[
      ab = a \cdot (a \cdot e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}}) = a^2 \cdot e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}}
      \]
      \[
      ac = a \cdot (a \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}) = a^2 \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}
      \]
      \[
      bc = (a \cdot e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}}) \cdot (a \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}) = a^2 \cdot (e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}}) = a^2 \cdot e^0 = a^2
      \]
    - So, we have:
      \[
      ab + ac + bc = a^2 \cdot e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} + a^2 \cdot e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}} + a^2 = a^2 \left( e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} + e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}} + 1 \right)
      \]
    - We know that:
      \[
      e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} + e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}} = \left( \frac{1}{2} + i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right) + \left( \frac{1}{2} - i \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right) = 1
      \]
      Therefore:
      \[
      e^{i \frac{\pi}{3}} + e^{-i \frac{\pi}{3}} + 1 = 1 + 1 = 2
      \]
    - Thus:
      \[
      ab + ac + bc = a^2 \cdot 2
      \]
    - Given that \( |a| = 18 \), it follows that \( |a^2| = 18^2 = 324 \), so:
      \[
      |ab + ac + bc| = |2 \cdot a^2| = 2 \cdot 324 = 648
      \]
