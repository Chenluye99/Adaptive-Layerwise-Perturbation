To solve this problem, let's start by formulating the problem mathematically:

1. **Define the Problem:**
    - Let the length of the thin candle be \( L_1 = 20 \) cm, and it burns completely in 4 hours, so its rate of burning is \(\frac{20}{4} = 5 \text{ cm/hour}\).
    - Let the length of the thick candle be \( L_2 = 20 \) cm, and it burns completely in 5 hours, so its rate of burning is \(\frac{20}{5} = 4 \text{ cm/hour}\).
    - After \( t \) hours, the length of the thin candle will be \( L_1(t) = 20 - 5t \) and the length of the thick candle will be \( L_2(t) = 20 - 4t \).
    - We need to find the time \( t \) when the length of the thin candle is twice the length of the thick candle, i.e., \( 20 - 5t = 2 (20 - 4t) \).

2. **Set Up the Equation:**
    - Substitute and solve for \( t \):
      \[
      20 - 5t = 2 (20 - 4t)
      \]
      \[
      20 - 5t = 40 - 8t
      \]
      \[
      8t - 5t = 40 - 20
      \]
      \[
      3t = 20
      \]
      \[
      t = \frac{20}{3}
      \]

3. **Implement in Python:**
    - We can confirm this solution by coding the equation and solving it using Python.
Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variable
t = sp.symbols('t')

# Define the equation based on the problem statement
eq = 20 - 5 * t - 2 * (20 - 4 * t)

# Solve the equation for t
solution = sp.solve(eq, t)[0]
print(solution)
```
Code execution result: 20/3

Thus, the solution obtained by solving the equation is \( t = \frac{20}{3} \).

So, after \(\boxed{\frac{20}{3}}\) hours, the thin candle will become twice as short as the thick candle. This is our final answer.
