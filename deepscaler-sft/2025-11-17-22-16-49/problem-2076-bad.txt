To solve this problem, we need to determine how long it will take for the lion to catch up to the elephant, given their respective speeds and the initial distance between them.

1. **Initial Setup:**
    - The initial distance between the elephant and the lion is 1 mile.
    - The elephant runs away from the lion at a speed of 19 miles per hour.
    - The lion runs towards the elephant at a speed of 24 miles per hour.

2. **Relative Speed:**
    - The relative speed at which the distance between the elephant and the lion is decreasing is the sum of their speeds because they are moving towards each other.
    - So, the relative speed is \(19 + 24 = 43\) miles per hour.

3. **Time Calculation:**
    - The time it takes for the lion to catch the elephant is the initial distance divided by their relative speed.
    - The initial distance is 1 mile, and the relative speed is 43 miles per hour.
    - Therefore, the time in hours is \(\frac{1}{43}\).

4. **Convert Time to Minutes:**
    - To convert the time from hours to minutes, we multiply the time by 60 (since there are 60 minutes in an hour).
    - So, the time in minutes is \(\frac{1}{43} \times 60\).

Let's implement this in Python to get the precise result.
```python
# Given values
initial_distance = 1  # in miles
speed_elephant = 19  # in miles per hour
speed_lion = 24  # in miles per hour

# Calculate the relative speed
relative_speed = speed_elephant + speed_lion  # in miles per hour

# Calculate the time in hours for the lion to catch the elephant
time_hours = initial_distance / relative_speed

# Convert the time to minutes
time_minutes = time_hours * 60
print(time_minutes)
```
