To find the area of a triangle given its vertices at \((x_1, y_1)\), \((x_2, y_2)\), and \((x_3, y_3)\), we can use the following formula:

\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2) \right|
\]

The vertices of our triangle are \((4, -1)\), \((10, 3)\), and \((4, 5)\). So, we can substitute these coordinates into the formula:

\[
(x_1, y_1) = (4, -1), \quad (x_2, y_2) = (10, 3), \quad (x_3, y_3) = (4, 5)
\]

Substitute these values into the formula:

\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| 4(3 - 5) + 10(5 - (-1)) + 4(-1 - 3) \right|
\]

First, let's compute the expression inside the absolute value:

\[
4(3 - 5) = 4(-2) = -8
\]

\[
10(5 - (-1)) = 10(5 + 1) = 10 \cdot 6 = 60
\]

\[
4(-1 - 3) = 4(-4) = -16
\]

Now, sum these results:

\[
-8 + 60 - 16 = 36
\]

So, we get:

\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| 36 \right| = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 36 = 18
\]

Let's confirm this by implementing the solution in Python.
```python
# Coordinates of the vertices
x1, y1 = 4, -1
x2, y2 = 10, 3
x3, y3 = 4, 5

# Calculate the area using the formula
area = 0.5 * abs(x1 * (y2 - y3) + x2 * (y3 - y1) + x3 * (y1 - y2))
print(area)
```
Code execution result: 18.0

Thus, the area of the triangle with vertices at \((4, -1)\), \((10, 3)\), and \((4, 5)\) is \(\boxed{18}\) square units. This confirms our solution is correct.
