To solve this problem, we will follow these steps:

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - The equation of the ellipse is \(\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1\) with \(a > b > 0\).
    - The foci of the ellipse are \(F_1 = (-c, 0)\) and \(F_2 = (c, 0)\) where \(c = \sqrt{a^2 - b^2}\).
    - Given that \(\overrightarrow{PF_1} \cdot \overrightarrow{PF_2} = 0\), it means that \(\overrightarrow{PF_1}\) and \(\overrightarrow{PF_2}\) are orthogonal, so \(\angle F_1PF_2 = 90^\circ\).
    - Also, given that \(\tan \angle PF_1F_2 = \frac{1}{2}\).

2. **Set Up the Problem Using Coordinates:**
    - Let's assume point \(P = (x, y)\) lies on the ellipse, so it satisfies \(\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1\).
    - The vectors \(\overrightarrow{PF_1}\) and \(\overrightarrow{PF_2}\) are \(\overrightarrow{PF_1} = (-c - x, -y)\) and \(\overrightarrow{PF_2} = (c - x, -y)\).
    - The dot product condition \(\overrightarrow{PF_1} \cdot \overrightarrow{PF_2} = 0\) gives:
      \[
      (-c - x)(c - x) + (-y)(-y) = 0 \implies (x + c)(x - c) + y^2 = 0 \implies x^2 - c^2 + y^2 = 0 \implies x^2 + y^2 = c^2.
      \]
    - We also know that \(\tan \angle PF_1F_2 = \frac{1}{2}\). Since \(\angle PF_1F_2\) is the angle between the vectors \(\overrightarrow{PF_1}\) and the x-axis, and \(\overrightarrow{F_1F_2} = (2c, 0)\), we have:
      \[
      \tan \angle PF_1F_2 = \left| \frac{-y}{-c - x} \right| = \frac{|y|}{c + x} = \frac{1}{2}.
      \]
      So, we get:
      \[
      |y| = \frac{1}{2} (c + x).
      \]

3. **Solve the Equations:**
    - Substitute \(y^2 = c^2 - x^2\) from \(x^2 + y^2 = c^2\) into the above equation:
      \[
      y = \pm \frac{1}{2} (c + x).
      \]
    - Substitute this into \(x^2 + y^2 = c^2\):
      \[
      x^2 + \left( \frac{1}{2} (c + x) \right)^2 = c^2 \implies x^2 + \frac{1}{4} (c + x)^2 = c^2 \implies x^2 + \frac{1}{4} (c^2 + 2cx + x^2) = c^2.
      \]
      \[
      x^2 + \frac{1}{4} c^2 + \frac{1}{2} cx + \frac{1}{4} x^2 = c^2 \implies \frac{5}{4} x^2 + \frac{1}{2} cx + \frac{1}{4} c^2 = c^2 \implies 5x^2 + 2cx + c^2 = 4c^2 \implies 5x^2 + 2cx - 3c^2 = 0.
      \]
    - This is a quadratic equation in \(x\):
      \[
      5x^2 + 2cx - 3c^2 = 0.
      \]
    - We solve this quadratic equation using the quadratic formula \(x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\):
      \[
      x = \frac{-2c \pm \sqrt{(2c)^2 - 4 \cdot 5 \cdot (-3c^2)}}{2 \cdot 5} = \frac{-2c \pm \sqrt{4c^2 + 60c^2}}{10} = \frac{-2c \pm \sqrt{64c^2}}{10} = \frac{-2c \pm 8c}{10}.
      \]
      So, we get two solutions:
      \[
      x = \frac{6c}{10} = \frac{3c}{5}, \quad x = \frac{-10c}{10} = -c.
      \]
    - Since \(x = -c\) would not give a valid point on the ellipse (as it would imply \(y = 0\) and thus \(\overrightarrow{PF_1} \cdot \overrightarrow{PF_2} \neq 0\)), we take \(x = \frac{3c}{5}\).
    - Substituting \(x = \frac{3c}{5}\) into \(x^2 + y^2 = c^2\):
      \[
      \left( \frac{3c}{5} \right)^2 + y^2 = c^2 \implies \frac{9c^2}{25} + y^2 = c^2 \implies y^2 = c^2 - \frac{9c^2}{25} = \frac{25c^2 - 9c^2}{25} = \frac{16c^2}{25} \implies y = \pm \frac{4c}{5}.
      \]
    - Now, substituting \(x = \frac{3c}{5}\) and \(y = \frac{4c}{5}\) into \(\tan \angle PF_1F_2 = \frac{1}{2}\):
      \[
      \frac{\frac{4c}{5}}{c + \frac{3c}{5}} = \frac{1}{2} \implies \frac{\frac{4c}{5}}{\frac{8c}{5}} = \frac{1}{2} \implies \frac{4c}{8c} = \frac{1}{2} \implies \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2},
      \]
      which is true, so our values for \(x\) and \(y\) are correct.

4. **Find the Eccentricity:**
    - The eccentricity \(e\) of the ellipse is given by \(e = \frac{c}{a}\).
    - We know that \(c = \sqrt{a^2 - b^2}\). To find the relationship between \(a\) and \(b\), we use the fact that \((x, y)\) lies on the ellipse:
      \[
      \frac{\left( \frac{3c}{5} \right)^2}{a^2} + \frac{\left( \frac{4c}{5} \right)^2}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{\frac{9c^2}{25}}{a^2} + \frac{\frac{16c^2}{25}}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{9c^2}{25a^2} + \frac{16c^2}{25b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{9c^2}{25a^2} + \frac{16c^2}{25b^2} = 1.
      \]
    - Multiplying through by 25, we get:
      \[
      \frac{9c^2}{a^2} + \frac{16c^2}{b^2} = 25.
      \]
    - Substituting \(c^2 = a^2 - b^2\) into the equation, we get:
      \[
      \frac{9(a^2 - b^2)}{a^2} + \frac{16(a^2 - b^2)}{b^2} = 25 \implies 9 \left( 1 - \frac{b^2}{a^2} \right) + 16 \left( \frac{a^2 - b^2}{b^2} \right) = 25.
      \]
    - Let's set \(\frac{b^2}{a^2} = k\), so \(\frac{a^2 - b^2}{b^2} = \frac{a^2}{b^2} - 1 = \frac{1}{k} - 1\):
      \[
      9 (1 - k) + 16 \left( \frac{1}{k} - 1 \right) = 25 \implies 9 - 9k + \frac{16}{k} - 16 = 25 \implies -9k + \frac{16}{k} - 7 = 25 \implies -9k + \frac{16}{k} = 32.
      \]
    - Multiplying through by \(k\), we get a quadratic equation:
      \[
      -9k^2 + 16 = 32k \implies 9k^2 + 32k - 16 = 0.
      \]
    - We solve this quadratic equation using the quadratic formula \(k = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\):
      \[
      k = \frac{-32 \pm \sqrt{32^2 - 4 \cdot 9 \cdot (-16)}}{2 \cdot 9} = \frac{-32 \pm \sqrt{1024 + 576}}{18} = \frac{-32 \pm \sqrt{1600}}{18} = \frac{-32 \pm 40}{18}.
      \]
      So, we get two solutions:
      \[
      k = \frac{8}{18} = \frac{4}{9}, \quad k = \frac{-72}{18} = -4.
      \]
    - Since \(k = \frac{b^2}{a^2}\) must be between 0 and 1, we take \(k = \frac{4}{9}\), so \(\frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{4}{9}\) or \(b^2 = \frac{4}{9} a^2\).
    - Substituting this into \(c^2 = a^2 - b^2\):
      \[
      c^2 = a^2 - \frac{4}{9} a^2 = \frac{5}{9} a^2 \implies c = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{3} a.
      \]
    - The eccentricity \(e = \frac{c}{a} = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3} a}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}\).
