To solve this problem, let's start by understanding what a dilation matrix is and how its determinant is computed.

A dilation transformation centered at the origin with a scale factor of 7 is represented by the matrix:

\[
\mathbf{D} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 & 0 \\ 0 & 7 \end{pmatrix}
\]

The determinant of a \(2 \times 2\) matrix \(\mathbf{D} = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}\) is given by:

\[
\det \mathbf{D} = ad - bc
\]

For our matrix \(\mathbf{D} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 & 0 \\ 0 & 7 \end{pmatrix}\), the elements are \(a = 7\), \(b = 0\), \(c = 0\), and \(d = 7\). Substituting these values into the determinant formula, we get:

\[
\det \mathbf{D} = (7)(7) - (0)(0) = 49
\]

Let's verify this with Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import numpy as np

# Define the dilation matrix D with scale factor 7
D = np.array([[7, 0], [0, 7]])

# Calculate the determinant of D
```
