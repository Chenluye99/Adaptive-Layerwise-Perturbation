- The probability of Larry Ponds earning a walk on any plate appearance is \( \frac{2}{5} \).
- The probability of Larry Ponds not earning a walk on any plate appearance is \( 1 - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3}{5} \).

There are two scenarios where Larry Ponds earns a walk exactly once in his next two plate appearances:

1. He earns a walk on his first plate appearance and does not earn a walk on his second plate appearance.
2. He does not earn a walk on his first plate appearance and earns a walk on his second plate appearance.

We can calculate the probability for each of these scenarios and then sum them up.

1. Probability of earning a walk on the first plate appearance and not on the second:
\[ P(\text{Walk on 1st and no walk on 2nd}) = \frac{2}{5} \times \frac{3}{5} \]

2. Probability of not earning a walk on the first plate appearance and earning a walk on the second:
\[ P(\text{No walk on 1st and walk on 2nd}) = \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{2}{5} \]

So, the total probability of earning a walk exactly once in two plate appearances is the sum of these two probabilities:
\[ P(\text{Exactly 1 walk in 2 plate appearances}) = \frac{2}{5} \times \frac{3}{5} + \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{2}{5} \]

Let's calculate this using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Define the probabilities
prob_walk = 2 / 5
prob_no_walk = 3 / 5
```
