To solve this problem, let's start by considering the properties of a geometric sequence.

1. **Geometric Sequence Definition**:
    - A geometric sequence can be written as \(a, ar, ar^2, ar^3, \ldots\), where \(a\) is the first term and \(r\) is the common ratio.
    - The \(n\)-th term of the geometric sequence is given by \(a_n = ar^{n-1}\).

2. **Sum of the First \(n\) Terms of a Geometric Sequence**:
    - The sum of the first \(n\) terms of a geometric sequence is given by:
      \[
      S_n = a \frac{1 - r^n}{1 - r} \quad \text{(if } r \neq 1\text{)}
      \]
    - We are given that \(S_4 = 1\) and \(S_8 = 3\).

3. **Set Up Equations**:

      \[
      S_4 = a \frac{1 - r^4}{1 - r} = 1
      \]
      \[

      \[
      \frac{1 - r^4}{1 - r} = x \quad \text{(1)}

      \[
