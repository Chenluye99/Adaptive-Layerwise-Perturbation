First, let's simplify the expression \((1 - \frac{a}{a+1}) \div \frac{a^2 - 1}{a^2 + 2a + 1}\).

1. **Simplify the Numerator \(1 - \frac{a}{a+1}\):**
   \[
   1 - \frac{a}{a+1} = \frac{(a+1) - a}{a+1} = \frac{1}{a+1}
   \]

2. **Simplify the Denominator \(\frac{a^2 - 1}{a^2 + 2a + 1}\):**
   - Notice that \(a^2 - 1 = (a - 1)(a + 1)\).
   - Notice that \(a^2 + 2a + 1 = (a + 1)^2\).
   - Substituting these factorizations, we get:
     \[
     \frac{a^2 - 1}{a^2 + 2a + 1} = \frac{(a - 1)(a + 1)}{(a + 1)^2} = \frac{a - 1}{a + 1}
     \]

3. **Combine the Results:**
   - Now, we substitute these simplified forms back into the original expression:
     \[
     \left(1 - \frac{a}{a+1}\right) \div \frac{a^2 - 1}{a^2 + 2a + 1} = \frac{1}{a+1} \div \frac{a - 1}{a + 1}
     \]
   - Division by a fraction is the same as multiplication by its reciprocal:
     \[
     \frac{1}{a+1} \div \frac{a - 1}{a + 1} = \frac{1}{a+1} \cdot \frac{a + 1}{a - 1} = \frac{1}{a - 1}
     \]

4. **Evaluate the Expression for \(a = \sqrt{2} + 1\):**
   - Substitute \(a = \sqrt{2} + 1\) into \(\frac{1}{a - 1}\):
     \[
     a - 1 = (\sqrt{2} + 1) - 1 = \sqrt{2}
     \]
     So, we get:
     \[
     \frac{1}{a - 1} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
     \]
