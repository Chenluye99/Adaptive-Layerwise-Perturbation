First, we need to rewrite the given equation of the circle in standard form by completing the square. The given equation is:

\[ x^2 + 6x + y^2 - 12y - 4 = 0 \]

1. **Complete the Square:**
    - For the \(x\) terms: \(x^2 + 6x\), we take the coefficient of \(x\) (which is 6), divide it by 2 (getting 3), and then square it (getting 9). So, we add and subtract 9:
      \[
      x^2 + 6x = (x + 3)^2 - 9
      \]
    - For the \(y\) terms: \(y^2 - 12y\), we take the coefficient of \(y\) (which is -12), divide it by 2 (getting -6), and then square it (getting 36). So, we add and subtract 36:
      \[
      y^2 - 12y = (y - 6)^2 - 36
      \]

2. **Rewrite the Equation:**
    Substitute these back into the original equation:
    \[
    (x + 3)^2 - 9 + (y - 6)^2 - 36 - 4 = 0
    \]
    Simplify this to get:
    \[
    (x + 3)^2 + (y - 6)^2 - 49 = 0 \implies (x + 3)^2 + (y - 6)^2 = 49
    \]

This is now in the standard form of a circle's equation \((x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2\), where \((h, k)\) is the center of the circle and \(r\) is the radius. From the equation \((x + 3)^2 + (y - 6)^2 = 49\), we can see that the center of the circle is \((-3, 6)\) and the radius \(r\) is \(\sqrt{49} = 7\).

3. **Find the Circumference:**
    The formula for the circumference \(C\) of a circle is given by:
    \[
    C = 2 \pi r
    \]
    Substituting the radius \(r = 7\), we get:
    \[
    C = 2 \pi \cdot 7 = 14 \pi
    \]

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure the result is accurate.
