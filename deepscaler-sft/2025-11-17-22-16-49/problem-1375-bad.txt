1. **Problem Understanding**:
    - We have a cylindrical can with a height of 6 inches.
    - The volume of the cylinder is given by the formula: \( V = \pi r^2 h \), where \( r \) is the radius and \( h \) is the height.
    - For our cylinder, the volume is: \( V = \pi r^2 \cdot 6 = 6 \pi r^2 \).
    - The lateral surface area of the cylinder (which is the area of the label that covers the lateral surface) is given by the formula: \( A = 2 \pi r h \), where \( r \) is the radius and \( h \) is the height.
    - For our cylinder, the lateral surface area is: \( A = 2 \pi r \cdot 6 = 12 \pi r \).
    - According to the problem, the volume of the cylinder is equal to the lateral surface area of the label: 
      \[
      6 \pi r^2 = 12 \pi r
      \]

2. **Formulate and Solve the Equation**:
    - We can simplify the equation by dividing both sides by \( 6 \pi \):
      \[
      r^2 = 2r
      \]
    - Rearrange the equation to:
      \[
      r^2 - 2r = 0
      \]
    - Factorize the equation:
      \[
      r(r - 2) = 0
      \]
    - This gives us two solutions:
      \[
      r = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad r = 2
      \]
    - Since \( r = 0 \) is not a valid solution for a physical cylinder (as the radius must be a positive value), we have:
      \[
      r = 2
      \]
