1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - Let's assume that the Tasty Candy Company puts \( x \) pieces of candy into each one-pound bag.
    - Mike bought 4 one-pound bags, so he had \( 4x \) pieces of candy initially. He gave 15 pieces of candy to each person in his class and had 23 pieces left over. So, the equation for Mike is:
      \[
      4x = 15 \cdot \text{(number of people in Mike's class)} + 23
      \]
    - Betsy bought 5 one-pound bags, so she had \( 5x \) pieces of candy initially. She gave 23 pieces of candy to each teacher in her school and had 15 pieces left over. So, the equation for Betsy is:
      \[
      5x = 23 \cdot \text{(number of teachers in Betsy's school)} + 15
      \]

2. **Formulating Equations:**
    - Let's denote the number of people in Mike's class as \( m \) and the number of teachers in Betsy's school as \( t \). Then, the equations become:
      \[
      4x = 15m + 23
      \]
      \[
      5x = 23t + 15
      \]
    - We need to find the smallest positive integer value of \( x \) that satisfies both equations for some integers \( m \) and \( t \).

3. **Iterative Solution:**
    - We will iterate over possible values of \( x \) starting from 1 and check for each value if there exist integers \( m \) and \( t \) that make both equations hold true.

Let's implement this in Python.
```python
# We will iterate over possible values of x and check for each value if there exist integers m and t that satisfy both equations.

def find_min_candies_per_bag():
    x = 1
    
    while True:
        found_m = False
        found_t = False
        
        # Check for Mike's equation: 4x = 15m + 23
        for m in range(1, 1000):
            if 4 * x == 15 * m + 23:
                found_m = True
                break
        
        # Check for Betsy's equation: 5x = 23t + 15
        for t in range(1, 1000):
            if 5 * x == 23 * t + 15:
                found_t = True
                break
        
        if found_m and found_t:
            return x
        
        x += 1

min_candies_per_bag = find_min_candies_per_bag()
print(min_candies_per_bag)
```
