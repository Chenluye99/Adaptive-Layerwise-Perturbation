1. **Given Equation:**
   \[(b \sin C)(b \cos C + c \cos B) = 42.\]

   - By the Law of Cosines, we have \(\cos C = \frac{a^2 + b^2 - c^2}{2ab}\) and \(\cos B = \frac{a^2 + c^2 - b^2}{2ac}\).

   \[ \sin C = \frac{c}{2R} \]
