First, let's understand the problem.

1. The alloy (brass) is made up of 80% copper and 20% zinc.
2. Henri's brass trumpet contains 48 ounces of copper.

We need to find out how many ounces of zinc are in the trumpet.

Let's denote the total weight of the brass trumpet by \( W \).

Since the brass is 80% copper, we have:
\[ 0.80 \times W = 48 \]

We can solve this equation to get the total weight \( W \) of the trumpet:

\[ W = \frac{48}{0.80} \]

Once we have \( W \), we can find the weight of the zinc in the trumpet. Since the brass is 20% zinc, the weight of the zinc is:
\[ 0.20 \times W \]

Let's start by solving it step-by-step using Python.
```python
# Given data
copper_percentage = 0.80
copper_ounces = 48

# Calculate the total weight of the brass trumpet (W)
W = copper_ounces / copper_percentage

# Calculate the weight of zinc in the trumpet (20% of W)
zinc_percentage = 0.20
zinc_ounces = zinc_percentage * W
print(zinc_ounces)
```
Code execution result: 12.0

Thus, the number of ounces of zinc in Henri's brass trumpet is \(\boxed{12}\)
