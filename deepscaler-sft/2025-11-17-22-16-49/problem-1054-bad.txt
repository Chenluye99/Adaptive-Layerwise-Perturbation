To solve this problem, we can use the formula for the area of a triangle:

\[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{altitude} \]

We are given:
- The area of the triangle = 600 square feet
- The length of the corresponding base = 30 feet

Let's denote the altitude by \( h \). Substituting the known values into the formula, we get:

\[ 600 = \frac{1}{2} \times 30 \times h \]

First, we can simplify the right side of the equation:

\[ 600 = 15h \]

To solve for \( h \), we can divide both sides of the equation by 15:

\[ h = \frac{600}{15} \]

Now, let's compute this value using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given values
area = 600
```
