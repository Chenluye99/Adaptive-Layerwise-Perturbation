First, let's outline the problem and solve it step-by-step.

1. **Determine the total number of marbles:**
    - There are 4 red marbles.
    - There are 5 white marbles.
    - There are 6 blue marbles.
    - Therefore, the total number of marbles is \(4 + 5 + 6 = 15\).

2. **Calculate the total number of ways to draw 3 marbles from 15:**
    - The number of ways to choose 3 marbles from 15 is given by the combination formula \( \binom{15}{3} \).
    - This is calculated as:
      \[
      \binom{15}{3} = \frac{15!}{3!(15-3)!} = \frac{15!}{3! \cdot 12!} = \frac{15 \cdot 14 \cdot 13}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 455
      \]

3. **Calculate the number of ways to draw 3 marbles of the same color:**
    - **Red Marbles:** The number of ways to choose 3 red marbles from 4 is \( \binom{4}{3} \).
      \[
      \binom{4}{3} = \frac{4!}{3!(4-3)!} = \frac{4!}{3! \cdot 1!} = \frac{4}{1} = 4
      \]
    - **White Marbles:** The number of ways to choose 3 white marbles from 5 is \( \binom{5}{3} \).
      \[
      \binom{5}{3} = \frac{5!}{3!(5-3)!} = \frac{5!}{3! \cdot 2!} = \frac{5 \cdot 4}{2 \cdot 1} = 10
      \]
    - **Blue Marbles:** The number of ways to choose 3 blue marbles from 6 is \( \binom{6}{3} \).
      \[
      \binom{6}{3} = \frac{6!}{3!(6-3)!} = \frac{6!}{3! \cdot 3!} = \frac{6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 20
      \]
    - The total number of ways to draw 3 marbles of the same color is:
      \[
      \binom{4}{3} + \binom{5}{3} + \binom{6}{3} = 4 + 10 + 20 = 34
      \]

4. **Calculate the probability:**
    - The probability that the three marbles drawn are all the same color is the ratio of the number of favorable outcomes to the total number of outcomes.
    - So, the probability is:
      \[
      P(\text{all same color}) = \frac{34}{455}
      \]

Let's use Python to verify these calculations.
```python

# Total number of marbles
total_marbles = 4 + 5 + 6

total_ways = math.comb(total_marbles, 3)
```
