Let's begin by understanding the relationship between the areas of the similar triangles and their corresponding heights.

1. **Properties of Similar Triangles:**
    - When two triangles are similar, the ratio of their corresponding sides is the same.
    - The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides (or heights, medians, etc.).

2. **Given Information:**
    - The ratio of the areas of the smaller triangle to the larger triangle is \( \frac{1}{4} \).
    - Let the height of the smaller triangle be \( h_1 = 3 \) cm.
    - Let the height of the larger triangle be \( h_2 \) cm.

3. **Relating the Areas:**
    - If the ratio of the areas of the smaller triangle to the larger triangle is \(\frac{1}{4}\), then the ratio of their corresponding heights must be \(\frac{1}{2}\) because \((\frac{h_1}{h_2})^2 = \frac{1}{4} \implies \frac{h_1}{h_2} = \frac{1}{2}\).

4. **Finding the Height of the Larger Triangle:**
    - From the above equation, we get \( \frac{h_1}{h_2} = \frac{1}{2} \), so \( h_2 = 2 \cdot h_1 \).
    - Substituting \( h_1 = 3 \) cm, we get \( h_2 = 2 \cdot 3 = 6 \) cm.

Let's confirm this by coding it to ensure the result is accurate.
```python
# Given values
height_smaller_triangle = 3  # height of the smaller triangle in centimeters
area_ratio = 1 / 4            # ratio of the area of the smaller triangle to the larger triangle

# The ratio of the corresponding heights of the triangles is the square root of the ratio of their areas
height_ratio = area_ratio ** 0.5

# Calculate the height of the larger triangle
height_larger_triangle = height_smaller_triangle / height_ratio
print(height_larger_triangle)
```
Code execution result: 6.0

Thus, the corresponding height of the larger triangle is \(\boxed{6}\) centimeters. This confirms our reasoning and calculation are accurate.
