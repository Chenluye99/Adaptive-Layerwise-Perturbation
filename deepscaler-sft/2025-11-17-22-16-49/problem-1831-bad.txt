To solve this problem, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total percentage of cars sold that are Audis, Toyotas, and Acuras.
2. Subtract this total percentage from 100% to find the percentage of cars that are Hondas.
3. Calculate the number of Hondas sold by using the percentage found in step 2 and the fact that the dealer sold 200 cars in total.

Let's proceed with these steps in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Step 1: Calculate the total percentage of cars sold that are Audis, Toyotas, and Acuras
percent_audi = 15
percent_toyota = 22
percent_acura = 28

total_percent_other_brands = percent_audi + percent_toyota + percent_acura

# Step 2: Calculate the percentage of cars that are Hondas
percent_honda = 100 - total_percent_other_brands

# Step 3: Calculate the number of Hondas sold
total_cars_sold = 200
num_hondas_sold = (percent_honda / 100) * total_cars_sold
print(num_hondas_sold)
```
Code execution result: 70.0

So, the number of Hondas that the dealer sold is \(\boxed{70}\). This is our final answer.
