First, let's solve for the current \( I \) using the equation \( V = IZ \). We can rearrange this equation to solve for \( I \):

\[ I = \frac{V}{Z} \]

Given:
\[ V = 1 - i \]
\[ Z = 1 + 3i \]

Substitute these values into the equation:

\[ I = \frac{1 - i}{1 + 3i} \]

To divide by a complex number, we multiply the numerator and the denominator by the complex conjugate of the denominator. The complex conjugate of \( 1 + 3i \) is \( 1 - 3i \). So,

\[ I = \frac{(1 - i)(1 - 3i)}{(1 + 3i)(1 - 3i)} \]

First, let's compute the denominator:

\[ (1 + 3i)(1 - 3i) = 1^2 - (3i)^2 = 1 - (-9) = 1 + 9 = 10 \]

Next, let's compute the numerator:

\[ (1 - i)(1 - 3i) = 1 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot (-3i) + (-i) \cdot 1 + (-i) \cdot (-3i) = 1 - 3i - i + 3i^2 = 1 - 4i + 3(-1) = 1 - 4i - 3 = -2 - 4i \]

Now, we can put the numerator and the denominator together:

\[ I = \frac{-2 - 4i}{10} = \frac{-2}{10} + \frac{-4i}{10} = -\frac{1}{5} - \frac{2}{5}i \]

Thus, the current \( I \) is:

\[ I = -\frac{1}{5} - \frac{2}{5}i \]
