First, let's solve the problem step-by-step.

1. **Find the Greatest Common Factor (GCD) of 3 and 6:**
    - The GCD of two numbers is the largest number that divides both of them without leaving a remainder.
    - For 3 and 6, the divisors of 3 are 1 and 3, and the divisors of 6 are 1, 2, 3, and 6. So, the greatest common factor is 3.

2. **Find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of 3 and 6:**
    - The LCM of two numbers is the smallest number that is a multiple of both of them.
    - For 3 and 6, the multiples of 3 are 3, 6, 9, 12, ... and the multiples of 6 are 6, 12, 18, ... So, the smallest number that is a multiple of both 3 and 6 is 6. Hence, the LCM of 3 and 6 is 6.

3. **Sum of GCD and LCM:**
    - Now, we need to find the sum of the GCD of 3 and 6 (which is 3) and the LCM of 3 and 6 (which is 6).
    - So, the sum is \(3 + 6 = 9\).

Let's confirm this with Python code to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Calculate GCD of 3 and 6
gcd_3_6 = math.gcd(3, 6)

# Calculate LCM of 3 and 6 using the formula: LCM(a, b) = abs(a * b) // GCD(a, b)
lcm_3_6 = abs(3 * 6) // gcd_3_6

# Calculate the sum of GCD and LCM
result = gcd_3_6 + lcm_3_6
print(result)
```
Code execution result: 9

Thus, the sum of the greatest common factor of 3 and 6 and the least common multiple of 3 and 6 is \(\boxed{9}\). The result has been confirmed as accurate through the Python code.
