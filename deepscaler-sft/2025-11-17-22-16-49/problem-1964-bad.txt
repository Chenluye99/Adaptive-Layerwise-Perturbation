To solve this problem, let's first understand the transformation involved.

1. **Understanding the Problem:**
    - The domain of \( h(x) \) is \([-8, 8]\).
    - We define \( g(x) = h\left(\frac{x}{2}\right) \).
    - For \( g(x) \) to be defined, the argument of \( h \) must be in the domain of \( h \), i.e., \(-8 \leq \frac{x}{2} \leq 8 \).

2. **Formulate the Constraints:**
    - To find the domain of \( g(x) \), we need to solve the inequality \(-8 \leq \frac{x}{2} \leq 8 \):
      \[
      -8 \leq \frac{x}{2} \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{x}{2} \leq 8
      \]
    - Multiplying all parts of the inequalities by 2, we get:
      \[
      -16 \leq x \quad \text{and} \quad x \leq 16
      \]
    - Combining these, the domain of \( g(x) \) is \([-16, 16]\).

3. **Find the Width of the Domain:**
    - The width (or length) of the interval \([-16, 16]\) is given by:
      \[
      16 - (-16) = 16 + 16 = 32
      \]
