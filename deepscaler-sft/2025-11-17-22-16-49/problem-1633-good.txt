First, let's solve the problem step-by-step.

1. **Identify the Properties of the Equilateral Triangle:**
    - An equilateral triangle has all sides of equal length, and in this case, each side is 9 units.
    - The formula for the circumradius \( R \) of an equilateral triangle with side length \( a \) is given by:
      \[
      R = \frac{a}{\sqrt{3}}
      \]
    - Substituting \( a = 9 \):
      \[
      R = \frac{9}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{9 \sqrt{3}}{3} = 3 \sqrt{3}
      \]

2. **Find the Area of the Circle:**
    - The area \( A \) of a circle is given by:
      \[
      A = \pi R^2
      \]
    - Substituting \( R = 3 \sqrt{3} \):
      \[
      A = \pi (3 \sqrt{3})^2 = \pi (3^2 \cdot (\sqrt{3})^2) = \pi (9 \cdot 3) = 27 \pi
      \]

Thus, the area of the circle is \( 27 \pi \).
