   Two vectors \(\overrightarrow{AB} = (3, 1)\) and \(\overrightarrow{OC} = (m, m+1)\) are parallel if and only if their components are proportional, i.e.,
   \[
   \frac{3}{m} = \frac{1}{m+1} \implies 3(m + 1) = m \implies 3m + 3 = m \implies 2m = -3 \implies m = -\frac{3}{2}
