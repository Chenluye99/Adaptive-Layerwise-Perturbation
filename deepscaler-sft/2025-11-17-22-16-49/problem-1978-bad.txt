To solve this problem, let's start by understanding what it means for each person to shake hands with exactly two other people in a group of nine people. This requirement implies that the handshaking pattern can be represented as a 2-regular graph (each vertex has a degree of 2). A 2-regular graph is simply a disjoint union of cycles, and since we have nine people, the only possible configuration that uses all nine people is a single 9-cycle (a cycle that includes all nine vertices).

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Formulate the Problem as a Cycle Graph:**
   - We need to count the number of ways to form a single 9-cycle with nine people. The number of different ways to arrange 9 people in a cycle is given by \((9-1)!\), which is \(8!\). This is because arranging 9 people in a line can be done in \(9!\) ways, but since a cycle does not have a starting point or direction, we divide by 9 (the number of ways to rotate the cycle) and by 2 (the number of ways to reflect the cycle), so it's just \(8!\).

2. **Count the Number of Ways to Form a 9-Cycle:**
   - The number of ways to form a 9-cycle is \(8!\):
     \[
     8! = 40320
     \]

3. **Find the Remainder When Divided by 1000:**
   - To get the remainder when 40320 is divided by 1000, we compute \(40320 \mod 1000\):
     \[
     40320 \mod 1000 = 320
     \]
