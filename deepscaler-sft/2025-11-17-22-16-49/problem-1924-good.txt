First, we need to convert the base 7 numbers \(24_7\) and \(30_7\) to their decimal (base 10) equivalents.

1. Convert \(24_7\) to decimal:
   - The number \(24_7\) means \(2 \cdot 7^1 + 4 \cdot 7^0 = 2 \cdot 7 + 4 \cdot 1 = 14 + 4 = 18_{10}\).

2. Convert \(30_7\) to decimal:
   - The number \(30_7\) means \(3 \cdot 7^1 + 0 \cdot 7^0 = 3 \cdot 7 + 0 \cdot 1 = 21_{10}\).

Next, we will find the product of these two decimal numbers:
\[ 18_{10} \times 21_{10} = 378_{10}. \]

Now, we need to convert this product (378 in decimal) back to base 7.

To convert a decimal number to base 7, we repeatedly divide the number by 7 and keep track of the remainders.

Let's perform this conversion step-by-step:

1. Divide 378 by 7:
   - \( 378 \div 7 = 54 \) with a remainder of 0.
2. Divide 54 by 7:
   - \( 54 \div 7 = 7 \) with a remainder of 5.
3. Divide 7 by 7:
   - \( 7 \div 7 = 1 \) with a remainder of 0.
4. Divide 1 by 7:
   - \( 1 \div 7 = 0 \) with a remainder of 1.

Reading the remainders from bottom to top gives us the base 7 representation of 378, which is \( 1050_7 \).

Next, we need to find the sum of the digits of \( 1050_7 \):

\[ 1 + 0 + 5 + 0 = 6. \]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Step 1: Convert the base 7 numbers 24_7 and 30_7 to decimal
num1_base_10 = int("24", 7)
num2_base_10 = int("30", 7)

# Step 2: Compute the product of these two numbers in decimal
product_base_10 = num1_base_10 * num2_base_10

# Step 3: Convert the product back to base 7
def to_base_7(n):
    if n == 0:
        return "0"
    digits = []
    while n:
        digits.append(int(n % 7))
        n //= 7
    return ''.join(str(x) for x in digits[::-1])

product_base_7 = to_base_7(product_base_10)

# Step 4: Compute the sum of the digits of the product in base 7
sum_of_digits = sum(int(digit) for digit in product_base_7)

print((product_base_7, sum_of_digits))
```
Code execution result: ('1050', 6)

Thus, the base 7 representation of the product of \(24_7\) and \(30_7\) is \(1050_7\), and the sum of its digits is \(\boxed{6}\). This confirms that our solution is accurate.
