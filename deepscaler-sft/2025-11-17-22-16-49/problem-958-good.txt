To solve this problem, let's start by understanding some properties of a rhombus.

1. **Properties of a Rhombus:**
    - All sides of a rhombus are of equal length.
    - The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles (90 degrees).

2. **Given:**
    - The diagonals of the rhombus are 10 inches and 24 inches.

3. **Find:**
    - The perimeter of the rhombus.

4. **Steps to Find the Solution:**
    - The diagonals bisect each other at right angles, so each diagonal is split into two equal segments.
      - The first diagonal (10 inches) is split into two segments of 5 inches each.
      - The second diagonal (24 inches) is split into two segments of 12 inches each.
    - These segments form the legs of a right-angled triangle, with the side of the rhombus as the hypotenuse.
    - We can apply the Pythagorean theorem to find the length of one side of the rhombus. If the legs of the right-angled triangle are 5 inches and 12 inches, then the length of the hypotenuse (one side of the rhombus) is given by:
      \[
      \text{side} = \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13 \text{ inches}
      \]
    - Since all four sides of a rhombus are of equal length, each side of the rhombus is 13 inches.
    - The perimeter of the rhombus is the sum of the lengths of all four sides:
      \[
      \text{Perimeter} = 4 \times \text{side} = 4 \times 13 = 52 \text{ inches}
      \]

Let's confirm this with Python code for accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given lengths of the diagonals
d1 = 10
d2 = 24

# Each diagonal is split into two equal segments
half_d1 = d1 / 2
half_d2 = d2 / 2

# Use the Pythagorean theorem to find the length of one side of the rhombus
side_length = sp.sqrt(half_d1**2 + half_d2**2)

# The perimeter of the rhombus is 4 times the length of one of its sides
perimeter = 4 * side_length
print(perimeter)
```

Thus, the perimeter of the rhombus is \(\boxed{52}\) inches. This confirms our solution is accurate.
