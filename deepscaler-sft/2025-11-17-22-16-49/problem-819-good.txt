We need to find the smallest possible perimeter of a right triangle where all the side lengths are integer multiples of 8. 

1. **Define the Problem Mathematically:**
    - A right triangle satisfies the Pythagorean theorem: \(a^2 + b^2 = c^2\), where \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\) are the side lengths of the triangle, and \(c\) is the hypotenuse.
    - Since each side length is a multiple of 8, we have \(a = 8m\), \(b = 8n\), and \(c = 8k\) for some integers \(m, n, k\).
    - Substituting into the Pythagorean theorem, we get \((8m)^2 + (8n)^2 = (8k)^2 \Rightarrow 64m^2 + 64n^2 = 64k^2 \Rightarrow m^2 + n^2 = k^2\).
    - So, \(m, n, k\) are the lengths of a right triangle, but without the factor of 8.

2. **Find the Smallest Primitive Pythagorean Triple:**
    - The smallest primitive Pythagorean triple is \((3, 4, 5)\), since \(3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2\).
    - Multiplying this by 8, we get the side lengths \((8 \cdot 3, 8 \cdot 4, 8 \cdot 5) = (24, 32, 40)\).

3. **Calculate the Perimeter:**
    - The perimeter of this triangle is \(24 + 32 + 40 = 96\).
