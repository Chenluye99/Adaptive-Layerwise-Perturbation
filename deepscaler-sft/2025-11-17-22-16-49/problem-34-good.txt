Heron's formula states that the area \(\Delta\) of a triangle with sides \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\) is given by:
\[
\Delta = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)}
\]
where \( s \) is the semi-perimeter of the triangle:
\[
s = \frac{a + b + c}{2}

import sympy as sp

z = sp.symbols('z', positive=True)

solution = sp.solve(lhs - rhs, z)
