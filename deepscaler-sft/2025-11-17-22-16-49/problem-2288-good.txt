    \]

    We need to express \(2^{25}\) as some integer \(n\) to the fifth power, i.e., \(n^5\). This means we need to find an integer \(n\) such that:
    \[
    n^5 = 2^{25}
    \]
    To find \(n\), we take the fifth root of both sides:
    \[
    n = (2^{25})^{1/5} = 2^{25/5} = 2^5 = 32
    \]
