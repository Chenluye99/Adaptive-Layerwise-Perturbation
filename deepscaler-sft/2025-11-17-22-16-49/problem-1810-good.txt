    - An arithmetic progression (AP) is defined by its first term \(a_1\) and common difference \(d\).
    - Given that the common difference \(d = 1\), we can write the general term of the AP as \(a_n = a_1 + (n-1) \cdot 1 = a_1 + n - 1\).

    - We are given that the sum of the first 98 terms is 137:
      \[
