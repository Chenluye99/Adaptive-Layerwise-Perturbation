To solve the problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Find the Radius of the Sphere:**
    - The formula for the surface area of a sphere is given by:
      \[
      \text{Surface Area} = 4 \pi r^2
      \]
    - We are given that the surface area of the sphere is \(324 \pi \, \text{cm}^2\). Therefore, we can set up the equation:
      \[
      4 \pi r^2 = 324 \pi
      \]
    - By canceling \(\pi\) from both sides, we get:
      \[
      4 r^2 = 324
      \]
    - Dividing both sides by 4, we obtain:
      \[
      r^2 = 81
      \]
    - Taking the square root of both sides, we find:
      \[
      r = 9
      \]

2. **Find the Volume of the Sphere:**
    - The formula for the volume of a sphere is given by:
      \[
      \text{Volume} = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3
      \]
    - Substituting \( r = 9 \) into the formula, we get:
      \[
      \text{Volume} = \frac{4}{3} \pi (9)^3 = \frac{4}{3} \pi (729) = \frac{4 \cdot 729}{3} \pi = \frac{2916}{3} \pi = 972 \pi
      \]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given surface area of the sphere
surface_area = 324 * sp.pi

# Define the equation for the surface area of a sphere
r = sp.symbols('r')
surface_area_eq = 4 * sp.pi * r**2 - surface_area

# Solve for the radius r
radius = sp.solve(surface_area_eq, r)[1]  # We take the positive solution

# Calculate the volume of the sphere using the radius
volume = (4/3) * sp.pi * radius**3
print(volume)
```
Code execution result: 972.0*pi

Thus, the volume of the sphere is \(\boxed{972 \pi}\) cubic centimeters. This confirms our solution is accurate.
