1. **Matrix Equation Setup**:

    - \(\mathbf{M}^2 = \mathbf{M} \cdot \mathbf{M} = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} a^2 + bc & ab + bd \\ ac + dc & bc + d^2 \end{pmatrix}\).
    - \(\mathbf{M}^3 = \mathbf{M} \cdot \mathbf{M}^2 = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} a^2 + bc & ab + bd \\ ac + dc & bc + d^2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} a(a^2 + bc) + b(ac + dc) & a(ab + bd) + b(bc + d^2) \\ c(a^2 + bc) + d(ac + dc) & c(ab + bd) + d(bc + d^2) \end{pmatrix}\).
    - We set \(\mathbf{M}^3 = \mathbf{I}\), which means:
      \[
      \mathbf{M}^3 = \begin{pmatrix} a(a^2 + bc) + b(ac + dc) & a(ab + bd) + b(bc + d^2) \\ c(a^2 + bc) + d(ac + dc) & c(ab + bd) + d(bc + d^2) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}.
      \]
2. **Formulate Equations**:
    - This gives us a system of four equations by comparing corresponding entries of the matrices:
      \[
      \begin{cases}
      a(a^2 + bc) + b(ac + dc) = 1, \\
      a(ab + bd) + b(bc + d^2) = 0, \\
      c(a^2 + bc) + d(ac + dc) = 0, \\
      c(ab + bd) + d(bc + d^2) = 1.
      \end{cases}
      \]
