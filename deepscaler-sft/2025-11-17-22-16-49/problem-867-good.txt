To solve this problem, we need to determine the value of \( g(4) \) given the functions \( f(x) = 2x - 3 \) and \( g(f(x)) = 5 - 4x \).

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have \( f(x) = 2x - 3 \).
    - We also have \( g(f(x)) = 5 - 4x \).

2. **Find the Value of \( x \) That Makes \( f(x) = 4 \):**
    - We need to find what value of \( x \) makes \( f(x) = 4 \).
    - Set up the equation: 
      \[
      2x - 3 = 4
      \]
    - Solve for \( x \):
      \[
      2x - 3 = 4 \implies 2x = 7 \implies x = \frac{7}{2}
      \]

3. **Substitute \( x = \frac{7}{2} \) into \( g(f(x)) = 5 - 4x \):**
    - Substitute \( x = \frac{7}{2} \) into the expression \( g(f(x)) = 5 - 4x \):
      \[
      g(f\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)) = 5 - 4 \left(\frac{7}{2}\right) = 5 - 4 \cdot \frac{7}{2} = 5 - 2 \cdot 7 = 5 - 14 = -9
      \]
    - Since \( f\left(\frac{7}{2}\right) = 4 \), it follows that:
      \[
      g(4) = -9
      \]
