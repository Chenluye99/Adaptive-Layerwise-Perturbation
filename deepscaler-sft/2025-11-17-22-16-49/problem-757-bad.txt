To solve this problem, we need to identify the common ratio of the given infinite geometric series: \(\frac{5}{6} - \frac{4}{9} + \frac{32}{135} - \dots\).

The common ratio \(r\) of a geometric series is found by dividing any term by its preceding term. So, we can calculate it as follows:

\[ r = \frac{-\frac{4}{9}}{\frac{5}{6}} = \frac{-\frac{4}{9}}{\frac{5}{6}} = -\frac{4}{9} \times \frac{6}{5} = -\frac{4 \cdot 6}{9 \cdot 5} = -\frac{24}{45} = -\frac{8}{15} \]

To ensure the accuracy, we can also check this by dividing the third term by the second term:

\[ r = \frac{\frac{32}{135}}{-\frac{4}{9}} = \frac{\frac{32}{135}}{-\frac{4}{9}} = \frac{32}{135} \times \frac{-9}{4} = \frac{32 \cdot -9}{135 \cdot 4} = \frac{-288}{540} = -\frac{8}{15} \]

Both calculations yield the same result, confirming that the common ratio is \(-\frac{8}{15}\).
