1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - We have a rectangular piece of paper with dimensions 4 units by 5 units.
    - Several lines are drawn parallel to the edges of the paper. These lines determine intersections that form smaller rectangles.
    - A rectangle is considered "basic" if:
      - All four sides of the rectangle are segments of the drawn line segments.
      - No segments of the drawn lines lie inside this rectangle.
    - The total length of all lines drawn is 2007 units.
    - We need to find the maximum possible number of basic rectangles that can be formed and denote this number as \( N \).

2. **Formulating a Plan:**
    - To solve this problem, we need to understand how to draw the lines and determine the conditions for a rectangle to be "basic".
    - Drawing lines parallel to the edges of the paper means that we will have sets of horizontal and vertical lines inside the 4x5 rectangle.
    - Let's assume we have \( n \) horizontal lines and \( m \) vertical lines. The total length of these lines will be the sum of the lengths of all the individual line segments.
    - The number of basic rectangles that can be formed by \( n \) horizontal lines and \( m \) vertical lines is \((n-1) \times (m-1)\) because each pair of horizontal lines forms a space for a height of the smaller rectangles, and each pair of vertical lines forms a space for a width of the smaller rectangles.

3. **Setting Up Constraints:**
    - Each horizontal line, taken from two fixed positions, spans 5 units (the width of the paper) and if there are \( n \) such lines, there will be \( n - 1 \) spaces in between them. So, the total length of all horizontal lines added together is \( 5 \cdot (n) \) (each line is 5 units long, and we have \( n \) such lines).
    - Similarly, each vertical line, taken from two fixed positions, spans 4 units (the height of the paper), and if there are \( m \) such lines, there will be \( m - 1 \) spaces in between them. So, the total length of all vertical lines added together is \( 4 \cdot (m) \) (each line is 4 units long, and we have \( m \) such lines).
    - The problem states that the total length of all lines drawn is 2007 units. Hence, we have the equation:
      \[
      5n + 4m = 2007
      \]
    - We need to maximize the number of basic rectangles, which is \((n-1) \cdot (m-1)\), under the above constraint.

4. **Implementing in Python:**
    - We need to iterate through possible values of \( n \) and \( m \) to find the values that maximize \((n-1) \cdot (m-1)\) while satisfying \( 5n + 4m = 2007 \).
    - We can use a loop to check all possible pairs of integers \((n, m)\) and count which pair gives the maximum number of basic rectangles.

Let's implement this plan in Python.
```python
max_basic_rectangles = 0
best_n, best_m = 0, 0

# We iterate over possible values of n and m
for n in range(1, 2008):
    for m in range(1, 2008):
        if 5 * n + 4 * m == 2007:
            num_basic_rectangles = (n - 1) * (m - 1)
            if num_basic_rectangles > max_basic_rectangles:
                max_basic_rectangles = num_basic_rectangles
                best_n, best_m = n, m

print(((max_basic_rectangles, best_n, best_m)))
```
