To solve this problem, we need to determine the probability that the expression \( ad - bc \) is even when \( a, b, c, d \) are chosen independently and uniformly from the set \{0, 1, 2, ..., 2007\}.

### Step-by-step Solution:

1. **Determine When \( ad - bc \) is Even:**
    - The expression \( ad - bc \) is even if and only if the parity (odd or even nature) of \( ad \) and \( bc \) are the same (both are even or both are odd).

      - \( ad \) is even if at least one of \( a \) or \( d \) is even.
      - \( bc \) is even if at least one of \( b \) or \( c \) is even.
    - Therefore, \( ad - bc \) is even if:
      1. Both \( ad \) and \( bc \) are even, or
      2. Both \( ad \) and \( bc \) are odd.

    - **Case 2: Both \( ad \) and \( bc \) are odd.**
      - \( a \) and \( d \) are both odd.
      - \( b \) and \( c \) are both odd.
