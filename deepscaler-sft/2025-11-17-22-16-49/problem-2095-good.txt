To solve the problem of finding the units digit of \( 7^{53} \), we only need to focus on the units digit of 7 and how it behaves when raised to different powers.

First, let's observe the pattern of the units digits of the powers of 7:

\[
\begin{align*}
7^1 & = 7 \quad (\text{units digit is } 7) \\
7^2 & = 49 \quad (\text{units digit is } 9) \\
7^3 & = 343 \quad (\text{units digit is } 3) \\
7^4 & = 2401 \quad (\text{units digit is } 1) \\
7^5 & = 16807 \quad (\text{units digit is } 7) \\
\end{align*}
\]

We can see that the units digits start to repeat every 4 numbers: 7, 9, 3, 1.

This implies that the units digit of \( 7^n \) depends on the remainder when \( n \) is divided by 4:

- If \( n \equiv 1 \pmod{4} \), the units digit is 7.
- If \( n \equiv 2 \pmod{4} \), the units digit is 9.
- If \( n \equiv 3 \pmod{4} \), the units digit is 3.
- If \( n \equiv 0 \pmod{4} \), the units digit is 1.

To find the units digit of \( 7^{53} \), we need to determine the remainder when 53 is divided by 4:

\[ 53 \div 4 = 13 \text{ remainder } 1 \]

So, \( 53 \equiv 1 \pmod{4} \). This means that the units digit of \( 7^{53} \) is the same as the units digit of \( 7^1 \), which is 7.

# Calculate 7^53 and get the units digit
