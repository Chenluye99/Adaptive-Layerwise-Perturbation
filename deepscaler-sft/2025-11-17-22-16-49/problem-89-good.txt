### Leverage Additional Constraints and Properties
1. **Using Ptolemy's Theorem:**
   For a cyclic quadrilateral \(ABCD\), Ptolemy's theorem states:
   \[
   AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + AD \cdot BC
   \]
   Substituting the known values \(AC = 3\), \(BD = 4\), and \(BC = 4\), we get:
   \[
   3 \cdot 4 = AB \cdot CD + AD \cdot 4 \implies 12 = AB \cdot CD + 4 \cdot AD
   \]
   We know from our previous relationship that \(AB \cdot CD = 4 \cdot AD\). So, we substitute \(AB \cdot CD\) in the equation:
   \[
   12 = 4 \cdot AD + 4 \cdot AD \implies 12 = 8 \cdot AD \implies AD = \frac{12}{8} = \frac{3}{2}
   \]
