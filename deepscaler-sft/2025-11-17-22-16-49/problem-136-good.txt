1. **Understand the Geometry:**
    - A regular square pyramid has a square base and four triangular faces that meet at a common vertex (the apex of the pyramid).
    - The base edge length is given as 2, so the side length of the square base is 2.
    - The side edge length is given as \(\sqrt{6}\), which means the length of the edge from any of the base vertices to the apex is \(\sqrt{6}\).

2. **Find the Height of the Pyramid:**
    - To determine the height of the pyramid, we can drop a perpendicular from the apex to the center of the square base. This perpendicular line is the height of the pyramid (let's call it \(h\)).
    - The distance from the center of the square base to any of its vertices is half the length of the diagonal of the square. The length of the diagonal of the square base is \(2 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot 2 = 2 \sqrt{2}\), so the distance from the center of the square base to any of its vertices is \(\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{2} = \sqrt{2}\).
    - Consider a right triangle formed by the height of the pyramid (\(h\)), the distance from the center of the base to one of its vertices (\(\sqrt{2}\)), and the side edge of the pyramid (\(\sqrt{6}\)). By the Pythagorean theorem, we have:
      \[
      (\sqrt{6})^2 = h^2 + (\sqrt{2})^2 \implies 6 = h^2 + 2 \implies h^2 = 4 \implies h = 2
      \]

3. **Find the Volume of the Pyramid:**
    - The volume \(V\) of a pyramid is given by the formula:
      \[
      V = \frac{1}{3} \text{Base Area} \cdot \text{Height}
      \]
    - The base area of the square is \(2 \times 2 = 4\).
    - We found that the height \(h\) of the pyramid is 2.
    - Substituting these values into the volume formula, we get:
      \[
      V = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = \frac{8}{3}
      \]
