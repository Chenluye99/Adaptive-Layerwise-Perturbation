To solve the problem, let's start with the given information:
- \( AC = 10 \)
- \( BK = 7 \)
- \( BC = 13 \)

We know that \( K \) is a point on \( BC \) such that \( AK \) is an altitude of \(\triangle ABC\). This means that \( AK \) is perpendicular to \( BC \). Therefore, \( K \) divides \( BC \) into two segments, \( BK = 7 \) and \( KC = BC - BK = 13 - 7 = 6 \).

To find the area of \(\triangle ABC\), we can use the formula for the area of a triangle when the base and height are known. Here, \( BC \) can be considered as the base, and \( AK \) as the height.

We can use the Pythagorean theorem in \(\triangle ACK\) and \(\triangle BAK\) to find \( AK \).

1. Consider \(\triangle ACK\):
   \[
   AC^2 = AK^2 + KC^2 \implies 10^2 = AK^2 + 6^2 \implies 100 = AK^2 + 36 \implies AK^2 = 64 \implies AK = 8
   \]
   We can check this by considering \(\triangle BAK\):
   \[
   AB^2 = AK^2 + BK^2 \implies AB^2 = 8^2 + 7^2 \implies AB^2 = 64 + 49 \implies AB^2 = 113 \implies AB = \sqrt{113}
   \]
   However, we don't actually need \( AB \) to find the area, and we only need \( AK \), which we have determined to be 8.

2. Now that we have the height \( AK = 8 \) and the base \( BC = 13 \), we can use the formula for the area of a triangle:
   \[
   \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 13 \times 8 = 52
   \]

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given lengths
AC = 10
BK = 7
BC = 13

# Calculate KC
KC = BC - BK

# Use the Pythagorean theorem to solve for AK in triangle ACK
AK = sp.sqrt(AC**2 - KC**2)

# Now we have AK, we can calculate the area of triangle ABC
area_ABC = 0.5 * BC * AK
print(area_ABC)
```
