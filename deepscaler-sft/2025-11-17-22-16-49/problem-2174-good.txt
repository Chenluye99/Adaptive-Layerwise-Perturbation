    - The circle is centered at the midpoint of the side it is based on, so if the side that forms the diameter is along the x-axis from (0, 0) to (12, 0), then the circle is centered at (6, 0).
    - The parts of the circle that are inside the equilateral triangle will be segments of the circle.

2. **Find the Area of the Equilateral Triangle:**
    - The formula for the area of an equilateral triangle with side length \(a\) is \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} a^2\).
    - For our triangle with side length 12, the area is \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 12^2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 144 = 36 \sqrt{3}\).

3. **Find the Area of the Circle:**
    - The area of the full circle with radius 6 is \(\pi \cdot 6^2 = 36\pi\).

4. **Find the Area of the Segments of the Circle That Make Up the Shaded Regions:**

    - The area of a 60° sector of the circle is \(\frac{60}{360} \cdot 36\pi = 6\pi\).
    - The side length of each smaller equilateral triangle that forms part of the segment (with each side of length 6) is 6. So, the area of each smaller equilateral triangle is \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 6^2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 36 = 9 \sqrt{3}\).

    - Since we have two such segments (one for each of the two small shaded regions), the sum of the areas of these two small shaded regions is \( 2 \cdot (6 \pi - 9 \sqrt{3}) = 12 \pi - 18 \sqrt{3} \).
