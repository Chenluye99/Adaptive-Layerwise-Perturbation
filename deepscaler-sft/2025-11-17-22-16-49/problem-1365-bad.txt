First, let's solve the problem step-by-step.

1. **Understanding the Problem:**
    - We need to find the remainder when \(5^{2010}\) is divided by 7.
    - This is equivalent to finding \(5^{2010} \mod 7\).

2. **Using Number Theory (Fermat's Little Theorem):**
    - Fermat's Little Theorem states that if \( p \) is a prime number and \( a \) is an integer not divisible by \( p \), then \( a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p} \).
    - Here, 7 is a prime number, so by Fermat's Little Theorem, \( 5^{6} \equiv 1 \pmod{7} \) because \( 7 - 1 = 6 \).

3. **Simplifying the Exponent:**
    - We can use this fact to simplify \( 5^{2010} \mod 7 \).
    - We note that \( 2010 = 6 \cdot 335 \), so \( 5^{2010} = (5^6)^{335} \).
    - Consequently, \( (5^6)^{335} \equiv 1^{335} \equiv 1 \pmod{7} \).

4. **Conclusion:**
    - Therefore, the remainder when \( 5^{2010} \) is divided by 7 is 1.
