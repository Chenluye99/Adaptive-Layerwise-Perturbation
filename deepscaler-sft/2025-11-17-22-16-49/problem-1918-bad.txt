To solve the problem, let's define some variables and set up equations based on the problem statement.

1. Let's say the total number of games played by the team be \( G \).
2. Let the number of games won by the team be \( W \). According to the problem, the team won \(\frac{2}{9}\) of its games, so we have:
   \[
   W = \frac{2}{9} G
   \]
3. The problem also states that the team lost 15 more games than it won. If the number of games lost is \( L \), then:
   \[
   L = W + 15
   \]
4. The total number of games played is the sum of the games won and the games lost:
   \[
   G = W + L
   \]
5. We can substitute the expression for \( L \) from the second equation into the third equation:
   \[
   G = W + (W + 15) = 2W + 15
   \]
6. Now, substitute the expression for \( W \) from the first equation into this equation:
   \[
   G = 2 \left( \frac{2}{9} G \right) + 15 = \frac{4}{9} G + 15
   \]
7. To solve for \( G \), we rearrange this equation:
   \[
   G - \frac{4}{9} G = 15 \implies \frac{9}{9} G - \frac{4}{9} G = 15 \implies \frac{5}{9} G = 15
   \]
   \[
   G = 15 \times \frac{9}{5} = 27
   \]

So, the total number of games played by the team this year is \( G = 27 \).
