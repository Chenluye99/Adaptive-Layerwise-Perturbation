Let's solve this step-by-step.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We know the midpoint \( M \) of the line segment \(\overline{AB}\) is given by \( M(2, 5) \).
    - The coordinates of point \( A \) are \( (3, 1) \).
    - We need to find the coordinates of point \( B \), say \( (x, y) \), such that the midpoint formula holds.

2. **Midpoint Formula:**
    - The midpoint \( M \) of a line segment with endpoints \( A(x_1, y_1) \) and \( B(x_2, y_2) \) is given by:
      \[
      M \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right)
      \]
    - Substituting the known values, we get:
      \[
      M \left( \frac{3 + x}{2}, \frac{1 + y}{2} \right) = (2, 5)
      \]

3. **Set Up Equations:**
    - This can be split into two separate equations:
      \[
      \frac{3 + x}{2} = 2 \quad \text{(1)}
      \]
      \[
      \frac{1 + y}{2} = 5 \quad \text{(2)}
      \]
    - We will solve these equations to find the coordinates of point \( B \).

4. **Solve for \( x \) and \( y \):**
    - From Equation (1):
      \[
      3 + x = 4 \implies x = 4 - 3 \implies x = 1
      \]
    - From Equation (2):
      \[
      1 + y = 10 \implies y = 10 - 1 \implies y = 9
      \]
    - So, the coordinates of point \( B \) are \( (1, 9) \).

5. **Find the Product of the Coordinates of Point \( B \):**
    - The product of the coordinates of point \( B \) is \( 1 \cdot 9 = 9 \).
