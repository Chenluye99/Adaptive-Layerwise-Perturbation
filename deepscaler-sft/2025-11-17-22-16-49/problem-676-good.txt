To solve this problem, let's start by understanding the geometric properties and constraints of the problem.

1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - Circle \( C \) has a radius of 10 cm, so its diameter is \( 2 \times 10 = 20 \) cm.
    - The largest possible inscribed triangle in Circle \( C \) that has one side as a diameter of the circle will have its base as the diameter of the circle. The height of this triangle will be the radius of the circle (since the height is measured from the center of the circle to the opposite side, and it will be perpendicular to the diameter).

2. **Geometric Properties of the Triangle:**
    - The base of the triangle will be the diameter of the circle, so the base of the triangle is 20 cm.
    - The height of the triangle will be equal to the radius of the circle, so the height of the triangle is 10 cm.
    - The area of a triangle is given by the formula \(\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}\).

3. **Formulating the Calculation:**
    - Substituting the base and height into the formula, we get:
      \[
      \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 20 \times 10 = \frac{1}{2} \times 200 = 100 \text{ square centimeters}
      \]

Let's confirm this by implementing the calculation in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python

radius = 10

# Calculate the diameter of the circle
diameter = 2 * radius

# The height of the triangle is the radius of the circle

# Calculate the area of the triangle using the formula 1/2 * base * height
```
