To solve this problem, we need to determine the rate at which George Adrian picked apples and then use that rate to find out how many apples he picked in 3 hours.

1. **Determine the Rate of Picking Apples:**
    - George Adrian picked 15,832 pounds of apples in 8 hours.
    - So, his rate of picking apples is \(\frac{15,832 \text{ pounds}}{8 \text{ hours}} = 1,979 \text{ pounds per hour}\).

2. **Find Out How Many Pounds of Apples He Picked in 3 Hours:**
    - With a rate of 1,979 pounds per hour, the number of pounds of apples picked in 3 hours would be \(1,979 \text{ pounds/hour} \times 3 \text{ hours} = 5,937 \text{ pounds}\).

Let's confirm this with Python code to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given data
total_apples = 15832
total_hours = 8

# Calculate the rate of picking apples (pounds per hour)
rate_per_hour = total_apples / total_hours

# Calculate the number of apples picked in 3 hours
hours_of_interest = 3
apples_picked_in_3_hours = rate_per_hour * hours_of_interest
print(apples_picked_in_3_hours)
```
