First, let's set up the problem and derive the relationships.

1. **Define the quadratic function and its properties:**
   The quadratic function is \( f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c \).

   - **Sum of the Zeros (Roots):** By Vieta's formulas, the sum of the zeros of the quadratic equation \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) is given by \(-\frac{b}{a}\).
   - **Product of the Zeros:** The product of the zeros of the quadratic equation \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) is given by \(\frac{c}{a}\).
   - **Sum of the Coefficients:** The sum of the coefficients of the function \(f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c\) is \(a + b + c\) (obtained by evaluating \(f(1)\)).

2. **Set up the Equation:**
   According to the problem, the sum of the zeros, the product of the zeros, and the sum of the coefficients are all equal. So, we set up the following equations:
   \[
   -\frac{b}{a} = \frac{c}{a} = a + b + c
   \]

3. **Formulate and Solve the Equations:**
   Let's call the common value \(k\). So, we have:
   \[
   -\frac{b}{a} = k, \quad \frac{c}{a} = k, \quad \text{and} \quad a + b + c = k
   \]
   From the first two equations, we get:
   \[
   -b = k \cdot a \quad \Rightarrow \quad b = -k \cdot a \tag{1}
   \]
   \[
   c = k \cdot a \tag{2}
   \]
   Substitute \(b\) and \(c\) into the third equation:
   \[
   a + (-k \cdot a) + (k \cdot a) = k \quad \Rightarrow \quad a - k \cdot a + k \cdot a = k \quad \Rightarrow \quad a = k
   \]
   So, we have \(k = a\). This means:
   \[
   b = -k \cdot a = -a^2, \quad c = k \cdot a = a^2
   \]
   Therefore, the quadratic function becomes:
   \[
   f(x) = ax^2 - a^2 x + a^2
   \]
