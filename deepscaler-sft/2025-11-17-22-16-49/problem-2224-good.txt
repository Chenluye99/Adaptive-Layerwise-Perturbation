Given the expression \((2 - 7i)(a + bi)\) and the condition that this product is pure imaginary, we want to find \(\frac{a}{b}\).

   \[
   (2 - 7i)(a + bi) = 2a + 2bi - 7ai - 7bi^2.
   \]

   \[
   2a + 2bi - 7ai + 7b = (2a + 7b) + (2b - 7a)i.
   \]
   For this expression to be pure imaginary, the real part must be zero:
   \[
   2a + 7b = 0.
   \]

2. **Set Up the Equation:**

   \[
   2a = -7b \implies a = -\frac{7}{2}b \implies \frac{a}{b} = -\frac{7}{2}.
