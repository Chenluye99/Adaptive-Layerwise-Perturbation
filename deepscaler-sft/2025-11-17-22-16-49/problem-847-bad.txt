1. **Solve for \( x \):**
   The equation \(\log_5 (x + 4) = 3\) can be rewritten in exponential form as:
   \[
   x + 4 = 5^3
   \]
   Since \(5^3 = 125\), we get:
   \[
   x + 4 = 125 \implies x = 125 - 4 \implies x = 121
   \]

   \[
   \log_{11} 121 = \log_{11} (11^2) = 2
   \]
