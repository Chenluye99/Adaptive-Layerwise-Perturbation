1. **Define the Rectangle:**
    - The rectangle has a width of 3 (from 0 to 3) and a height of 2 (from 0 to 2).
    - So, the area of the rectangle is \(3 \times 2 = 6\).

2. **Set Up the Condition \(x < y\):**
    - We need to determine the region within this rectangle where \(x < y\).
    - For a point \((x, y)\) to satisfy \(x < y\), \(y\) must be greater than \(x\).
    - We need to find the region of the rectangle where \(0 \leq x < y \leq 2\).

3. **Find the Area Where \(x < y\):**
    - The line \(x = y\) cuts through the rectangle from \((0, 0)\) to \((2, 2)\), but since the height of the rectangle is only 2, the relevant region of interest is bounded above by \(y = 2\) and below by \(y = x\).
    - The region where \(x < y\) will be a right triangle with vertices at \((0, 0)\), \((0, 2)\), and \((2, 2)\).

4. **Calculate the Area of the Triangle Formed by \(x < y\):**
    - The base of this triangle is 2 (from 0 to 2 on the \(x\)-axis), and the height of this triangle is 2 (from 0 to 2 on the \(y\)-axis).
    - The area of this triangle is \(\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 2 = 2\).

5. **Calculate the Probability:**
    - The probability that a randomly chosen point \((x, y)\) from the rectangle satisfies \(x < y\) is the ratio of the area where \(x < y\) to the total area of the rectangle.
    - So, the probability is \(\frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}\).
