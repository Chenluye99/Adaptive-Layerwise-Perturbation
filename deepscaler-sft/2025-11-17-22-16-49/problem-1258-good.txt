To solve this problem, we need to determine the area that Spot can reach with his 2-yard rope attached to one of the vertices of a regular hexagon with side length 1 yard.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - A regular hexagon has six equal sides and six equal angles.
    - Each internal angle of a regular hexagon is 120°.
    - When Spot is tethered to a vertex of the hexagon with a 2-yard rope, he can trace out portions of circles centered at that vertex with radii of 2 yards.

2. **Visualize the Area Spot Can Reach:**
    - The doghouse itself is a regular hexagon with a side length of 1 yard. The area of a single side of the hexagon is one-sixth of the area of a circle with a radius of 2 yards (because the rope can extend 2 yards in all directions from the vertex, tracing out a full 240° arc of a circle of radius 2 yards when wrapped around the hexagon).
    - Additionally, the dog can trace out a complete 360° circle (with a smaller overlap because it comes into contact with the hexagon's sides) for the part of the rope that extends beyond the adjacent sides of the hexagon.

3. **Calculate the Area:**
    - For the full 240° arc of a 2-yard radius, the area is \(\frac{240}{360} \cdot \pi \cdot 2^2 = \frac{2}{3} \cdot \pi \cdot 4 = \frac{8 \pi}{3}\).
    - For the additional areas of circles of radius 1 yard (as the rope extends past the adjacent sides), Spot can trace a 60° sector of a circle of 1-yard radius for each of the two adjacent sides he can move around (one of such sectors for each adjacent side he encounters, each being a 60° sector of a circle of radius 1 yard), contributing additional areas of \(2 \cdot \frac{60}{360} \cdot \pi \cdot 1^2 = 2 \cdot \frac{1}{6} \cdot \pi \cdot 1 = \frac{\pi}{3}\) for each 60° sector, and because these sectors overlap and are taken into account as part of Spot's mobile range.

Summarizing, we get the area Spot can reach consists of the mentioned 240° arc of a 2-yard circle plus the additional sectors where constraints from the hexagon shape of the doghouse doesn't further deny further reachable areas. To sum up everything in Python ensuring correctness:

Let's proceed and confirm it by programming.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Calculate the area of the 240° arc of a circle with radius 2
arc_240 = (240 / 360) * sp.pi * 2**2

# For each of the 60° sectors of circle of radius 1 (joining adjacent boundaries or the doghouse impact)
arc_60_small_hexagon = 2 * (60 / 360) * sp.pi * 1**2

# Therefore, the total possible free area Spot can reach, outside of hexagon and as it follows the curves formed by rope reaching all external width vs the confinements by dog house
total_area_spot_reaches = arc_240 + arc_60_small_hexagon

print(total_area_spot_reaches)
```
