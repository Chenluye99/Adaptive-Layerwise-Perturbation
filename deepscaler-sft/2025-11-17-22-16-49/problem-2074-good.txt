To solve this problem, we need to utilize properties of similar triangles and the given dimensions of the frustum.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - The altitude (height) of the frustum is 24 cm.
    - The area of the lower base of the frustum is \( 225 \pi \) square cm, so the radius of the lower base is \( \sqrt{\frac{225 \pi}{\pi}} = 15 \) cm.
    - The area of the upper base of the frustum is \( 25 \pi \) square cm, so the radius of the upper base is \( \sqrt{\frac{25 \pi}{\pi}} = 5 \) cm.
    - We need to find the height of the small cone that was cut off to form this frustum.

2. **Set Up the Problem Using Similar Triangles:**
    - Let the height of the original larger cone be \( H \) and the height of the smaller cone that was cut off be \( h \).
    - When a smaller cone is cut off the top of the larger cone to form the frustum, the smaller cone and the larger cone are similar.
    - The smaller cone that was cut off will have a height of \( h \) and the larger cone will have a height of \( H \). The top base of the frustum corresponds to the base of the smaller cone, and the bottom base of the frustum corresponds to the base of the larger cone.
    - The ratio of the radii of the smaller cone to the larger cone is the same as the ratio of their heights, so:
      \[
      \frac{5}{15} = \frac{h}{H}
      \]
      Simplifying, we get:
      \[
      \frac{1}{3} = \frac{h}{H} \implies h = \frac{H}{3}
      \]
    - The height of the frustum is the difference in height between the original larger cone and the smaller cone that was cut off, so:
      \[
      H - h = 24
      \]
      Substituting \( h = \frac{H}{3} \) into the equation above, we get:
      \[
      H - \frac{H}{3} = 24 \implies \frac{2H}{3} = 24 \implies 2H = 72 \implies H = 36
      \]
    - Now, substituting \( H = 36 \) back into the equation for \( h \):
      \[
      h = \frac{36}{3} = 12
      \]

So, the height of the small cone that was cut off is 12 cm. Let's confirm this solution by implementing it in Python to ensure our steps and calculations are accurate.
```python
from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variables
H, h = symbols('H h')

# Set up the equations based on our setup
eq1 = Eq(H - h, 24)
eq2 = Eq(h, H / 3)

# Solve the system of equations
solution = solve((eq1, eq2), (H, h))
print(solution)
```
