First, let's understand the problem clearly.

1. **Initial Situation:**
    - Let's assume the initial volume of the molten iron is \( V \).
    - When the molten iron solidifies into a block of iron, its volume reduces by \(\frac{1}{34}\). This means the new volume after solidification is \( V - \frac{1}{34} V = \frac{33}{34} V \).

2. **Situation After Melting:**
    - Now, if this block of iron melts back into molten iron, it should return to its original volume \( V \), but we need to find out by how much its volume increases compared to its volume after it had solidified, which is \(\frac{33}{34} V\).

3. **Volume Increase Calculation:**
    - The increase in volume when the block of iron melts back into molten iron is \( V - \frac{33}{34} V = \frac{1}{34} V \).
    - The percentage increase in volume compared to its volume after solidification is \(\frac{\frac{1}{34} V}{\frac{33}{34} V} = \frac{1}{33}\).

So, if the volume increase is \(\frac{1}{34} V\), the fractional increase in volume compared to its volume after solidification is \(\frac{\frac{1}{34}}{\frac{33}{34}} = \frac{1}{33}\).

To get the final answer in a more interpretable format, let's use Python to confirm this and get the increase in volume as a numeric value.

Let's implement this in Python.
```python
# Let's assume an initial volume V for the molten iron, and we can choose any value for V because it will cancel out in the final percentage calculation. We'll choose V = 34 for simplicity, because it will make the calculations straightforward.

V = 34

# Volume after solidification
V_after_solidification = (33 / 34) * V

# Volume increase when the block melts back into molten iron
volume_increase = V - V_after_solidification

print(volume_increase)
```
