Given:
- \(|\vec{a}| = 1\)
- \(\vec{b} = (1, \sqrt{3})\)
- \((\vec{b} - \vec{a}) \perp \vec{a}\)

First, substitute the given values into the equation \((\vec{b} - \vec{a}) \perp \vec{a}\). By definition, if \((\vec{b} - \vec{a}) \perp \vec{a}\), then their dot product is zero:
\[
(\vec{b} - \vec{a}) \cdot \vec{a} = 0.
\]

Expanding this, we get:
\[
\vec{b} \cdot \vec{a} - \vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} = 0.
\]
Since \(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} = |\vec{a}|^2 = 1^2 = 1\), the equation simplifies to:
\[
\vec{b} \cdot \vec{a} - 1 = 0 \implies \vec{b} \cdot \vec{a} = 1.
\]

Next, calculate \(\vec{b} \cdot \vec{a}\):
\[
\vec{b} \cdot \vec{a} = 1 \cdot a_1 + \sqrt{3} \cdot a_2 = a_1 + \sqrt{3} \cdot a_2 = 1.
\]
So, we have the equation:
\[
a_1 + \sqrt{3} \cdot a_2 = 1 \quad \text{(1)}
\]
where \(\vec{a} = (a_1, a_2)\).

Now, we want to determine the angle \(\theta\) between \(\vec{a}\) and \(\vec{b}\). The dot product of \(\vec{a}\) and \(\vec{b}\) can also be written in terms of the magnitudes and cosine of the angle between them:
\[
\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}| |\vec{b}| \cos \theta.
\]
We already know that \(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 1\), \(|\vec{a}| = 1\), and we need to determine \(|\vec{b}|\):
\[
|\vec{b}| = \sqrt{1^2 + (\sqrt{3})^2} = \sqrt{1 + 3} = \sqrt{4} = 2.
\]
So,
\[
1 = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot \cos \theta \implies 1 = 2 \cos \theta \implies \cos \theta = \frac{1}{2}.
\]
The angle \(\theta\) for which \(\cos \theta = \frac{1}{2}\) is:
\[
\theta = \frac{\pi}{3}.
\]

Let's confirm this result with Python by solving the equation \(a_1 + \sqrt{3} \cdot a_2 = 1\) and then verifying the angle calculation.

