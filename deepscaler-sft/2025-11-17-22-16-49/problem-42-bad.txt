To solve this problem, we need to determine the probability that the top card of the deck is red and the second card is black.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - A standard deck of 52 cards has 26 red cards (13 hearts and 13 diamonds) and 26 black cards (13 spades and 13 clubs).
    - We need to find the probability that the first card drawn is red, and the second card drawn is black.

2. **Formulate the Solution:**
    - The probability that the first card is red is \(\frac{26}{52} = \frac{1}{2}\).
    - After drawing the first card, there are 51 cards left in the deck. If the first card was red, there are still 26 black cards left in the deck.
    - Therefore, the probability that the second card is black given that the first card was red is \(\frac{26}{51}\).

3. **Combine the Probabilities:**
    - The combined probability that the first card is red and the second card is black is the product of the two probabilities:
      \[
      P(\text{First card is red and second card is black}) = \frac{26}{52} \times \frac{26}{51} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{26}{51} = \frac{26}{102} = \frac{13}{51}
      \]
