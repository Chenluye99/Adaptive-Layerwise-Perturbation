1. **Define the Variables:**
    - Let \( s \) be the side length of the square bases.

2. **Set Up the Surface Area Equation:**
    - The surface area (SA) of the rectangular prism with square bases consists of:
      - 2 square bases, each with area \( s^2 \)
      - 4 rectangular sides, each with area \( s \cdot h \)
    - So, the total surface area is given by:
      \[
      \text{SA} = 2s^2 + 4sh
      \]
    - Substitute \( h = s + 3 \) into the surface area formula:
      \[
      \text{SA} = 2s^2 + 4s(s + 3) = 2s^2 + 4s^2 + 12s = 6s^2 + 12s
      \]
    - We are given that the surface area should be at least 90 square units, so we have the inequality:
      \[
      6s^2 + 12s \geq 90
      \]
    - Simplify this inequality:
      \[
      6s^2 + 12s - 90 \geq 0 \implies s^2 + 2s - 15 \geq 0
      \]
