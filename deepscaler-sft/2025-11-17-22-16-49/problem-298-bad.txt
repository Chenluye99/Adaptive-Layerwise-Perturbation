- Probability of the phone call being answered at the third ring: \(P(\text{3rd}) = 0.4\)
- Probability of the phone call being answered at the fourth ring: \(P(\text{4th}) = 0.1\)

The probability of the phone call being answered within the first four rings is the sum of the probabilities of it being answered at the first, second, third, or fourth ring. Mathematically, this can be expressed as:
\[ P(\text{within 4 rings}) = P(\text{1st}) + P(\text{2nd}) + P(\text{3rd}) + P(\text{4th}) \]

Now, let's compute this using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Define the given probabilities
P_1st = 0.1

P_4th = 0.1

print(P_within_4_rings)
```
