First, let's understand the bounds and properties of the trapezoid defined by the lines \( y = x \), \( y = 10 \), \( y = 5 \), and the \( y \)-axis.

1. **Boundaries of the Trapezoid:**
    - The line \( y = 10 \) is a horizontal line at \( y = 10 \).
    - The line \( y = 5 \) is a horizontal line at \( y = 5 \).
    - The line \( y = x \) is a diagonal line passing through the origin, so for any point \((x, y)\) on this line, \( y = x \).
    - The \( y \)-axis is the line \( x = 0 \).

2. **Intersection Points:**
    - The intersection of \( y = 10 \) and \( y = x \) is at the point \((10, 10)\), because setting \( y = 10 \) in \( y = x \) gives \( x = 10 \).
    - The intersection of \( y = 5 \) and \( y = x \) is at the point \((5, 5)\), because setting \( y = 5 \) in \( y = x \) gives \( x = 5 \).
    - The intersection of \( y = 10 \) with the \( y \)-axis is at \((0, 10)\).
    - The intersection of \( y = 5 \) with the \( y \)-axis is at \((0, 5)\).

3. **Vertices of the Trapezoid:**
    - The vertices of the trapezoid are \((0, 10)\), \((10, 10)\), \((0, 5)\), and \((5, 5)\).

4. **Calculate the Area of the Trapezoid:**
    - The formula for the area of a trapezoid is given by:
      \[
      \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times (\text{Base}_1 + \text{Base}_2) \times \text{Height}
      \]
    - Here, the two parallel sides (bases) of the trapezoid are along the lines \( y = 10 \) and \( y = 5 \), so:
      - \(\text{Base}_1 = 10\) (distance from \((0, 10)\) to \((10, 10)\) along the x-axis),
      - \(\text{Base}_2 = 5\) (distance from \((0, 5)\) to \((5, 5)\) along the x-axis),
      - The height of the trapezoid is the vertical distance between the lines \( y = 10 \) and \( y = 5 \), which is \( 10 - 5 = 5 \).

Substitute these values into the formula:
\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times (10 + 5) \times 5 = \frac{1}{2} \times 15 \times 5 = \frac{1}{2} \times 75 = 37.5
\]

# Function to compute the area of the trapezoid based on its vertices
def trapezoid_area():
    # vertices of the trapezoid
    x1, y1 = 0, 10
    x2, y2 = 10, 10
    x3, y3 = 0, 5
    x4, y4 = 5, 5
    
    # Calculate the length of the parallel sides (bases)
    base1 = x2 - x1  # distance between (0, 10) and (10, 10)
    base2 = x4 - x3  # distance between (0, 5) and (5, 5)
    
    # Calculate the height of the trapezoid (vertical distance between y = 10 and y = 5)
    height = 10 - 5
    
    # Calculate the area using the trapezoid area formula
    area = 0.5 * (base1 + base2) * height
    return area

# Get the area
area = trapezoid_area()
print(area)
