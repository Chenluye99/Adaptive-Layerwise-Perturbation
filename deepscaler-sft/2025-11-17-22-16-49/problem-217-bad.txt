We need to solve for \( p \) in the equation \( fp - w = 10000 \) given that \( f = 5 \) and \( w = 5 + 125i \).

1. Substitute the given values into the equation:
   \[
   5p - (5 + 125i) = 10000
   \]
2. Simplify and solve for \( p \):
   \[
   5p - 5 - 125i = 10000
   \]
   \[
   5p = 10000 + 5 + 125i
   \]
   \[
   5p = 10005 + 125i
   \]
   \[
   p = \frac{10005 + 125i}{5}
   \]
3. Now, divide each term by 5:
   \[
   p = \frac{10005}{5} + \frac{125i}{5}
   \]
   \[
   p = 2001 + 25i
   \]

Let's confirm this by implementing the calculations in Python.
```python
# Given values

right_hand_side = 10000
```
