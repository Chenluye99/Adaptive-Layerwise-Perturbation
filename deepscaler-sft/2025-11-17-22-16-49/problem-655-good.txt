\[ (x - a)(x - b) = x^2 - (a + b)x + ab. \]

\[ - (a + b) = 1, \]
\[ ab = -n. \]

From the first equation, we have:
\[ a + b = -1. \]

From the second equation, we have:
\[ n = -ab. \]

# Initialize a set to store unique values of n that meet the criteria
