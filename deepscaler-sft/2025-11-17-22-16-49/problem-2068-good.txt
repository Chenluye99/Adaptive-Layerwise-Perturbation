To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We are given an infinite geometric series with a common ratio \( r = \frac{-1}{3} \).
    - The sum of the infinite geometric series is 25.
    - We need to find the second term of this series.

2. **Formulate the Problem Mathematically:**
    - The sum \( S \) of an infinite geometric series with the first term \( a \) and common ratio \( r \) (where \( |r| < 1 \)) is given by:
      \[
      S = \frac{a}{1 - r}
      \]
    - Substituting the given values, we have:
      \[
      25 = \frac{a}{1 - \left( \frac{-1}{3} \right)} = \frac{a}{1 + \frac{1}{3}} = \frac{a}{\frac{4}{3}} = \frac{a \cdot 3}{4}
      \]
    - This simplifies to:
      \[
      25 = \frac{3a}{4}
      \]
    - Solving for \( a \):
      \[
      25 \cdot 4 = 3a \implies 100 = 3a \implies a = \frac{100}{3}
      \]

3. **Find the Second Term of the Series:**
    - The second term \( t_2 \) of a geometric series is given by \( a \cdot r \). Substituting \( a = \frac{100}{3} \) and \( r = \frac{-1}{3} \):
      \[
      t_2 = a \cdot r = \frac{100}{3} \cdot \frac{-1}{3} = \frac{100 \cdot -1}{3 \cdot 3} = \frac{-100}{9}
      \]
