The formula for the projection of a vector \(\mathbf{v}\) onto \(\mathbf{n}\) is:
\[
\text{proj}_{\mathbf{n}} \mathbf{v} = \frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{n}}{\mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{n}} \mathbf{n}
\]

\[
\mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{n} = 2^2 + (-2)^2 + 4^2 = 4 + 4 + 16 = 24

\[

\[
\text{proj}_{\mathbf{n}} \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 0 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{36}{24} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{3}{2} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -3 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}
