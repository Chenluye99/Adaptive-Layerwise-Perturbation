To solve this problem, we need to compute the probability that we get one fork, one spoon, and one knife when we randomly remove three pieces of silverware from the drawer containing 6 forks, 6 spoons, and 6 knives.

Here are the steps to solve the problem:

1. **Determine the Total Number of Ways to Choose 3 Pieces of Silverware:**
   The total number of ways to choose 3 pieces of silverware from 18 pieces is given by the combination formula \(\binom{18}{3}\):
   \[
   \binom{18}{3} = \frac{18!}{3!(18-3)!} = \frac{18!}{3! \cdot 15!} = \frac{18 \cdot 17 \cdot 16}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 816
   \]

2. **Determine the Number of Ways to Get One Fork, One Spoon, and One Knife:**
   The number of ways to choose 1 fork from 6 forks, 1 spoon from 6 spoons, and 1 knife from 6 knives is given by:
   \[
   \binom{6}{1} \cdot \binom{6}{1} \cdot \binom{6}{1} = 6 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 216
   \]

3. **Calculate the Probability:**
   The probability of getting one fork, one spoon, and one knife is the ratio of the number of favorable outcomes to the total number of outcomes:
   \[
   P(\text{one fork, one spoon, and one knife}) = \frac{216}{816} = \frac{9}{34}
   \]

Let's confirm this with Python code.
```python
import math

# Calculate the total number of ways to choose 3 pieces of silverware from 18
total_ways = math.comb(18, 3)

# Calculate the number of ways to choose 1 fork, 1 spoon, and 1 knife from 6 forks, 6 spoons, and 6 knives respectively
ways_to_get_one_fork = math.comb(6, 1)
ways_to_get_one_spoon = math.comb(6, 1)
ways_to_get_one_knife = math.comb(6, 1)

number_of_favorable_outcomes = ways_to_get_one_fork * ways_to_get_one_spoon * ways_to_get_one_knife

# Calculate the probability
probability = number_of_favorable_outcomes / total_ways
print(probability)
```

To express this as a fraction, we recognize that \(0.2647058823529412 = \frac{9}{34}\).

Thus, the probability that we get one fork, one spoon, and one knife when we randomly remove three pieces of silverware is \(\boxed{\frac{9}{34}}\). This confirms our previous calculation.
