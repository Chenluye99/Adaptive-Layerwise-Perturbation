We are given that there are 70 balls in total, and 20 of them are red, 20 are blue, and 20 are yellow. Therefore, the number of black and white balls combined is \(70 - (20 + 20 + 20) = 70 - 60 = 10\). This means there are 10 balls that are either black or white.

To ensure that we have at least 10 balls of one color, we should consider the worst-case scenario. In the worst-case scenario, we pick as many balls as possible without getting 10 balls of one color. We can pick up to 9 balls each from the red, blue, yellow, black, and white groups before we are forced to pick a 10th ball of one of these colors to ensure we have 10 balls of one color.

1. We can pick up to 9 red balls.
2. We can pick up to 9 blue balls.
3. We can pick up to 9 yellow balls.
4. We can pick up to 9 black balls.
5. We can pick up to 9 white balls.

After picking these 45 balls (9 from each of the four groups that have 20 balls each and up to 9 from the black and white group), if we pick one more ball, it will be necessary that the ball we pick is the 10th ball of one of the colors (since we already have 9 balls each from red, blue, and yellow, and we could have picked all 9 black and 9 white without having 10 of any particular color yet, and the next ball will push us to have 10 balls of one color).

So, the total number of balls we can pick without having 10 of any one color is 9 (red) + 9 (blue) + 9 (yellow) + 9 (black) + 9 (white) = 45. The next ball drawn (the 46th ball) will ensure that we have 10 balls of one color because all previous balls drawn didn't give us 10 of any one color.

Hence, we need to pick 46 balls to be sure that we have at least 10 balls of one color.

```python
# We need to simulate this process and confirm that 46 is the correct answer.

# After drawing 45 balls, the counts of each color can be 9 at most.
```
