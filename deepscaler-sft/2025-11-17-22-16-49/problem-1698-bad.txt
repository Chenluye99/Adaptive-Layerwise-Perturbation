To solve the problem of finding the greatest common factor (GCF) of 252 and 96, we can use the Euclidean algorithm. This algorithm is efficient and involves a series of division steps in which the divisor in each step becomes the dividend in the next step, and the remainder becomes the new divisor. The process continues until the remainder is zero. The divisor at that step will be the GCF.

The steps for the Euclidean algorithm can be outlined as follows:

1. Divide 252 by 96 and find the remainder.
2. Replace 252 with 96 and 96 with the remainder from the previous step.
3. Repeat the process until the remainder is 0.
4. The non-zero remainder just before getting a remainder of 0 is the GCF.

Let's implement this in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
def gcd(a, b):
    while b != 0:
        a, b = b, a % b
    return a

# Find GCD of 252 and 96
gcd_result = gcd(252, 96)
print(gcd_result)
```
Code execution result: 12

Thus, the greatest common factor (GCF) of 252 and 96 is \(\boxed{12}\). This is the final answer.
