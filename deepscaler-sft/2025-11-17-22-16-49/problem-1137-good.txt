1. **Define the Problem:**
    - We are given that the radius of the circle is \( x + 6 \).
    - We are also given that the diameter of the circle is \( 3x + 5 \).

2. **Relationship Between Diameter and Radius:**
    - By definition, the diameter of a circle is twice its radius. That is,
      \[
      \text{Diameter} = 2 \times \text{Radius}
      \]
    - Substituting the expressions given in the problem, we get:
      \[
      3x + 5 = 2(x + 6)
      \]
    - We can solve this equation to find \( x \):
      \[
      3x + 5 = 2x + 12 \implies 3x - 2x = 12 - 5 \implies x = 7
      \]

3. **Find the Radius with \( x = 7 \):**
    - Substitute \( x = 7 \) back into the expression for the radius:
      \[
      \text{Radius} = x + 6 = 7 + 6 = 13
      \]

4. **Find the Circumference of the Circle:**
    - The formula for the circumference \( C \) of a circle is given by:
      \[
      C = 2 \pi \times \text{Radius}
      \]
    - Substituting the radius we found:
      \[
      C = 2 \pi \times 13 = 26 \pi
      \]

Let's verify this process with Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variable
x = sp.symbols('x')

# Define the equations based on the problem statement
radius = x + 6
diameter = 3 * x + 5

# Set up the equation that the diameter is twice the radius
eq = sp.Eq(diameter, 2 * radius)

# Solve for x
solution = sp.solve(eq, x)[0]

# Find the radius by substituting the solution back into the radius expression
radius_value = radius.subs(x, solution)

# Calculate the circumference of the circle
circumference = 2 * sp.pi * radius_value
print(circumference)
```
Code execution result: 26*pi

Thus, the circumference of the circle, in terms of \(\pi\), is \(\boxed{26 \pi}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
