To determine when the church, school, and daycare center will next ring their bells together, we need to find the least common multiple (LCM) of their ringing intervals, which are 15 minutes, 20 minutes, and 25 minutes respectively.

1. **Find the LCM of 15, 20, and 25:**

    - The prime factorization of 15 is \( 3 \times 5 \).
    - The prime factorization of 20 is \( 2^2 \times 5 \).
    - The prime factorization of 25 is \( 5^2 \).

    To find the LCM, we take the highest power of each prime factor appearing in the factorization of these numbers:

    - The highest power of 2 is \( 2^2 \).
    - The highest power of 3 is \( 3 \).
    - The highest power of 5 is \( 5^2 \).

    So, the LCM is \( 2^2 \times 3 \times 5^2 = 4 \times 3 \times 25 = 300 \).

    This means that the church, school, and daycare center will all ring their bells together 300 minutes after noon.

2. **Convert 300 Minutes into Hours and Minutes:**

    - There are 60 minutes in an hour.
    - To convert 300 minutes into hours and minutes, we perform the division \( 300 \div 60 \), which gives 5 with a remainder of 0.

    So, 300 minutes is exactly 5 hours.

    Therefore, 300 minutes after noon is 5:00 PM.

Since we need to answer in the 24-hour format without am or pm, the answer is 17:00.

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure our reasoning is accurate.
```python
import math

# Function to calculate LCM of two numbers
def lcm(x, y):
    return abs(x * y) // math.gcd(x, y)

# Find LCM of 15, 20, and 25
lcm_15_20 = lcm(15, 20)
lcm_15_20_25 = lcm(lcm_15_20, 25)

print(lcm_15_20_25)
```
Code execution result: 300

This confirms that the least common multiple (LCM) of 15, 20, and 25 is 300. As previously explained, 300 minutes is equivalent to 5 hours. Therefore, the next time the church, school, and daycare center will all ring their bells together is 5:00 PM, which in the 24-hour format is 17:00.

So, the final answer is \(\boxed{17:00}\).
