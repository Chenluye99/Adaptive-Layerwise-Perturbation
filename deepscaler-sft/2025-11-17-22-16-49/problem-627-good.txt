We need to find half of the absolute value of the difference of the squares of 21 and 15 added to the absolute value of the difference of their cubes.

1. Calculate the squares of 21 and 15, then find the difference of these squares.
2. Take the absolute value of this difference.
3. Calculate the cubes of 21 and 15, then find the difference of these cubes.
4. Take the absolute value of this difference.
5. Add the results from steps 2 and 4.
6. Finally, find half of the result from step 5.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python.
```python
# Step 1: Calculate the squares of 21 and 15, then find the difference
square_21 = 21 ** 2
square_15 = 15 ** 2
difference_of_squares = square_21 - square_15

# Step 2: Take the absolute value of this difference
abs_difference_of_squares = abs(difference_of_squares)

# Step 3: Calculate the cubes of 21 and 15, then find the difference
cube_21 = 21 ** 3
cube_15 = 15 ** 3
difference_of_cubes = cube_21 - cube_15

# Step 4: Take the absolute value of this difference
abs_difference_of_cubes = abs(difference_of_cubes)

# Step 5: Add the results from steps 2 and 4
sum_of_abs_differences = abs_difference_of_squares + abs_difference_of_cubes

# Step 6: Find half of the result from step 5
final_result = sum_of_abs_differences / 2

print(final_result)
```
