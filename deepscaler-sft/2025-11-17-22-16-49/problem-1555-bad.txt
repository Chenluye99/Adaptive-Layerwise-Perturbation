First, let's start by understanding and solving the problem step-by-step.

1. **Identify the Problem:**
    - The word "SIXTEEN" has 6 letters, including 2 'E's, S, I, X, T, N.
    - We need to find the probability that the two 'E's are not next to each other when the letters are randomly arranged.

2. **Find the Total Number of Arrangements:**
    - The total number of ways to arrange the word "SIXTEEN" is given by the formula for permutations of a multiset: \(\frac{6!}{2!}\), where 6 is the total number of letters, and 2! accounts for the fact that there are 2 identical 'E's.

3. **Find the Number of Arrangements Where the Two 'E's Are Next to Each Other:**
    - To solve this, we can treat the two 'E's as a single entity or "block". This "block" of 'EE' along with the other 4 distinct letters (S, I, X, T, N) can be arranged in 5 positions. The number of ways to arrange these 5 "items" is \(5!\).
    - So, the number of ways to arrange the "SIXTEEN" word such that the two 'E's are next to each other is \(5!\).

4. **Find the Number of Arrangements Where the Two 'E's Are Not Next to Each Other:**
    - The number of ways to arrange the word "SIXTEEN" such that the two 'E's are not next to each other is the total number of arrangements minus the number of arrangements where the 'E's are next to each other.
    - That is, \(\frac{6!}{2!} - 5!\).

5. **Calculate the Probability:**
    - Probability is calculated as the ratio of the number of favorable outcomes to the total number of outcomes. So, the probability that the two 'E's are not next to each other is:
      \[
      P(\text{E's not next to each other}) = \frac{\text{Number of ways to arrange such that 'E's are not next to each other}}{\text{Total number of ways to arrange 'SIXTEEN'}}
      \]

Let's perform the calculations step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Total number of ways to arrange "SIXTEEN"
total_ways = math.factorial(6) // math.factorial(2)

# Number of ways to arrange such that the two 'E's are next to each other
ways_E_together = math.factorial(5)

# Number of ways to arrange such that the two 'E's are not next to each other
ways_E_not_together = total_ways - ways_E_together

# Probability that the two 'E's are not next to each other
prob_E_not_together = ways_E_not_together / total_ways
print(prob_E_not_together)
```
