Let's define the initial amount of money Monsieur Dupont had as \( x \) francs and \( y \) centimes. So, the total initial amount of money in centimes is \( 100x + y \).

After spending some money at the restaurant, Monsieur Dupont noticed that the money he had left is one fifth of the money he initially had. Therefore, the money he has left is \(\frac{100x + y}{5} \).

Let's say he has \( a \) francs and \( b \) centimes left. Therefore, the amount of money left is \( 100a + b \).

From the problem, we know:

1. The centimes he had left, \( b \), is equal to the francs he initially had, \( x \):
   \[
   b = x
   \]

2. The francs he had left, \( a \), is five times less than the initial centimes he had, \( y \):
   \[
   a = \frac{y}{5}
   \]

Hence, the amount of money left is \( 100a + b = 100 \cdot \frac{y}{5} + x = 20y + x \).

Since the money left is one fifth of the initial amount:
\[
20y + x = \frac{100x + y}{5}
\]

Let's solve this equation step-by-step.

First, multiply both sides of the equation by 5 to clear the fraction:
\[
5 \cdot (20y + x) = 100x + y
\]

This simplifies to:
\[
100y + 5x = 100x + y
\]

Rearrange the equation to isolate the variables on one side:
\[
100y - y = 100x - 5x
\]

This simplifies to:
\[
99y = 95x
\]

Divide through by 1 (the equation is already in its simplest form that can be solved through integer solutions or code iteration):

\[ 99y = 95x \]

Next, we look for values of \(x\) and \(y\) that satisfy this equation and make sure that \(a\) and \(b\) remain integers. We can iterate through possible values and check constraints because both \(a\) and \(b\) must be non-negative integers. We also know that when translating back from \( a \) and \( b \) to find the initial amount and then subtract the remaining amount to check correctness. 

We can write code to solve this equation iteratively to get accurate results.
```python
# We will iterate through possible values for x and y to find valid integers that satisfy the equation 99y = 95x
    
solution = None

# We will check a reasonable range of values for x and y, given that franc and centime values should be reasonable (typically within a reasonable range for such currency problems, they stay around a few hundred Francs or Centimes).
for x in range(1, 1000):
    for y in range(1, 1000):
        if 99 * y == 95 * x:
            a = y // 5
            b = x
            
            # Checking if 'a' and 'b' are non-negative integers
            if 0 <= a and 0 <= b:
                initial_amount = 100 * x + y
                remaining_amount = 100 * a + b
                
                # Check if the remaining amount is 1/5 of the initial amount
                if remaining_amount == initial_amount / 5:
                    solution = (x, y, initial_amount, remaining_amount)
                    break

print(solution)
```
