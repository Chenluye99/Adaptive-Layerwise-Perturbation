\[ f(x) = a(x + 1)(x - 1)(x - 2) \]

\[ f(x) = a(x^2 - 1)(x - 2) = a(x^3 - 2x^2 - x + 2) = ax^3 - 2ax^2 - ax + 2a \]

\[ f(0) = 2a = 2 \implies a = 1 \]

\[ b = -2a = -2 \cdot 1 = -2 \]
