To solve this problem, we need to consider various steps in terms of permutations and constraints.

1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - We need to arrange 5 volunteers and 2 elderly people in a row, making a total of 7 positions.
    - The 2 elderly people must be adjacent to each other.
    - The 2 elderly people cannot be at the ends of the row.

2. **Steps to Solve the Problem:**
    - First, treat the 2 elderly people as a single "block" or "super person" that can move around, so we have 6 positions to place this "block" along with the 5 volunteers, giving us 6 places to choose from for the "block" (it can be placed starting from position 2 to position 6 because it can't be placed at the first or last position of the row if it is to remain adjacent).
    - For each placement of the "block" of the 2 elderly people, there are 2 ways to arrange the 2 elderly people within the block (i.e., Elderly 1 Elderly 2 or Elderly 2 Elderly 1).
    - After placing the "block" of 2 elderly people, we need to arrange the 5 volunteers and the 1 "block" in the remaining 6 positions. There are 6! (6 factorial) ways to arrange these 6 entities (the "block" and the 5 volunteers).
    - So, the total arrangements considering the constraints can be formulated as follows: 
        1. Choose a position for the "block" of 2 elderly people from positions 2 to 6 (there are 5 possible positions).
        2. For each of these positions, there are 2 ways to arrange the 2 elderly people within the block.
        3. Then, arrange the remaining 5 volunteers and the 1 "block" in 6 positions, which can be done in 6! ways.

3. **Formulating and Implementing in Python:**
We can use Python's `sympy` library to ensure the result is accurate, but this can be solved using Python's itertools library for permutations as well. Let's implement this step-by-step in Python.
```python
import itertools

# List all individuals: 5 volunteers and 2 elderly people, label volunteers as V1, V2, V3, V4, V5 and elderly people as E1, E2.
people = ['V1', 'V2', 'V3', 'V4', 'V5', 'E1', 'E2']

# To store all valid arrangements
valid_arrangements = []

# Generate all permutations of the list of 7 people
all_permutations = list(set(itertools.permutations(people)))

# Check each permutation and add it to valid_arrangements if it meets the conditions:
for perm in all_permutations:
    arrangement = list(perm)
    
    # Check if the 2 elderly people are adjacent
    idx_elderly_1 = arrangement.index('E1')
    idx_elderly_2 = arrangement.index('E2')
    
    if abs(idx_elderly_1 - idx_elderly_2) == 1:
        # Check if the 2 elderly people are not at the ends of the row
        if idx_elderly_1 not in [0, 6] and idx_elderly_2 not in [0, 6]:
            valid_arrangements.append(perm)

# Get the number of valid arrangements
num_valid_arrangements = len(valid_arrangements)
print(num_valid_arrangements)
```
