To solve the problem, let's denote the original cost price of each Super VCD as \( C \).

1. **Initial Price Increase:**
    - The store initially increases the price of the Super VCD by 40%, so the new price after this increase is \( C \times 1.40 \).

2. **Discount and Free Fare:**
    - The store then advertises a "10% discount and a free 50 yuan taxi fare" promotion. So, the price after the 10% discount is \( C \times 1.40 \times 0.90 \).
    - After the discount, a free 50 yuan taxi fare doesn't affect the price but affects the profit. The final selling price after the promotion would be \( C \times 1.40 \times 0.90 \).

3. **Profit Calculation:**
    - The problem states that after all this, the store makes a profit of 340 yuan. So, the final selling price is equal to the cost price plus 340 yuan:
      \[
      C \times 1.40 \times 0.90 = C + 340
      \]
    - Let's denote the cost price of each Super VCD as \( C \). We can set up the equation as follows:
      \[
      1.40 \times 0.90 \cdot C = C + 340
      \]
      Simplifying the left side, we get:
      \[
      1.26 \cdot C = C + 340
      \]
    - Rearrange the equation to isolate \( C \):
      \[
      1.26C - C = 340 \implies 0.26C = 340
      \]
      \[
      0.26C = 340 \implies C = \frac{340}{0.26}
      \]
