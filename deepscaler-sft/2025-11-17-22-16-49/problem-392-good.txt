To solve this problem, we need to calculate the perimeter of the shape formed by placing the two rectangles as described. Here's the step-by-step process:

1. **Understand the Configuration:**
    - We have a \(2 \, \text{inch} \times 6 \, \text{inch}\) rectangle placed vertically. 
    - We then place a \(3 \, \text{inch} \times 2 \, \text{inch}\) rectangle horizontally on top of the middle of the vertical rectangle.

2. **Visualize the Shape:**
    - The vertical rectangle has a height of 6 inches and a width of 2 inches.
    - The horizontal rectangle that is placed on top has a height of 2 inches and a width of 3 inches.
    - When we place the horizontal rectangle on top of the middle of the vertical rectangle, the top edge of the vertical rectangle is 3 inches high, so placing a 2-inch tall horizontal rectangle on it means that there will be a 1-inch gap below the horizontal rectangle and 3 inches extending above from the bottom of the vertical rectangle.

3. **Determine the Dimensions of the Combined Shape:**
    - The overall height of the "T" shape is the height of the vertical rectangle plus the height of the horizontal rectangle, i.e., \(6 + 2 = 8\) inches.
    - The overall width of the "T" shape is determined by the longer dimension of the two rectangles when placed in this manner. Since the vertical part has a width of 2 inches, and the horizontal part has a width of 3 inches, the overall width is 3 inches (because it’s extended horizontally).

    So, the dimensions of the shape formed are effectively 8 inches in height and 3 inches in width.

4. **Calculate the Perimeter:**
    - The perimeter of a shape is given by the formula: \( \text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (\text{Height} + \text{Width}) \).
    - Substituting the dimensions we have: \(\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (8 + 3) = 2 \times 11 = 22 \) inches.

Let's confirm this with a little more detail in the code to ensure accuracy. We'll create a function to calculate the perimeter based on the geometric considerations explained above.

```python
# We will define the function that computes the perimeter of the "T" shape described.

def calculate_perimeter():
    # Heights and widths of the rectangles
    height_vertical = 6
    width_vertical = 2
    
    height_horizontal = 2
    width_horizontal = 3
    
    # When placing the horizontal rectangle on top of the vertical one, the overall height is the sum of both heights
    overall_height = height_vertical + height_horizontal
    
    # The overall width will be determined by the width of the larger part when viewed as a whole shape, which is 3 inches in this case
    overall_width = max(width_vertical, width_horizontal)
    
    # Now, compute the perimeter of the combined shape
    perimeter = 2 * (overall_height + overall_width)
    
    return perimeter

perimeter = calculate_perimeter()
print(perimeter)
```
