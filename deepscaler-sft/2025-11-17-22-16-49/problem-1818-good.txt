First, let's understand the problem. The number \(131_a\) in base \(a\) is equivalent to the number 55 in base 10. To convert \(131_a\) to a base 10 number, we use the following formula for a number in base \(a\):

\[ 1 \cdot a^2 + 3 \cdot a^1 + 1 \cdot a^0 = 55 \]

This simplifies to:

\[ a^2 + 3a + 1 = 55 \]

Rearrange the equation to form a standard quadratic equation:

\[ a^2 + 3a + 1 - 55 = 0 \]

\[ a^2 + 3a - 54 = 0 \]

We can solve this quadratic equation using the quadratic formula:

\[ a = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \]

Here, \(a = 1\), \(b = 3\), and \(c = -54\). Substituting these values into the quadratic formula:

\[ a = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{3^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot (-54)}}{2 \cdot 1} \]

\[ a = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{9 + 216}}{2} \]

\[ a = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{225}}{2} \]

\[ a = \frac{-3 \pm 15}{2} \]

This gives us two potential solutions:

\[ a = \frac{12}{2} = 6 \]

\[ a = \frac{-18}{2} = -9 \]

Since the base of a numeral system must be a positive integer, we discard the negative solution. Therefore, the only viable solution is:

\[ a = 6 \]

To ensure the accuracy, let's check if substituting \( a = 6 \) back into the original equation holds true. We substitute \( a = 6 \) into the equation \( a^2 + 3a - 54 = 0 \):

\[ 6^2 + 3 \cdot 6 - 54 = 36 + 18 - 54 = 0 \]

Since this is true, our solution is verified as correct. Now, let's confirm it by coding the solution in Python as well.
