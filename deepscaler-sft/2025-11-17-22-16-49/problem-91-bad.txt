\[ \text{Volume} = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h \]

Let's define the given measurements for the cones:

- Cone \( C \) has a radius \( r_C = 16.4 \) and height \( h_C = 30.5 \).
- Cone \( D \) has a radius \( r_D = 30.5 \) and height \( h_D = 16.4 \).

We can now compute the volumes of the two cones using the volume formula.

\[ \text{Volume}_C = \frac{1}{3} \pi (16.4)^2 (30.5) \]

2. Calculate the volume of cone \( D \):

\[ \text{Volume}_D = \frac{1}{3} \pi (30.5)^2 (16.4) \]
