1. **Define the quadratic function**: Since \( p(x) \) is a quadratic function with rational coefficients, it can be expressed as \( p(x) = ax^2 + bx + c \) where \( a, b, \) and \( c \) are rational numbers.

2. **Set up the equations based on given conditions**:
    - We know that \( p(\sqrt{7}) = 22 \), so substituting into the function:
      \[
      p(\sqrt{7}) = a (\sqrt{7})^2 + b (\sqrt{7}) + c = 7a + b \sqrt{7} + c = 22
      \]
    - Similarly, for \( p(\sqrt{11}) = 30 \):
      \[
      p(\sqrt{11}) = a (\sqrt{11})^2 + b (\sqrt{11}) + c = 11a + b \sqrt{11} + c = 30
      \]

3. **Formulate a system of equations**:
    - The above expressions yield two equations which involve both rational and irrational parts:
      \[
      7a + c + b \sqrt{7} = 22
      \]
      \[
      11a + c + b \sqrt{11} = 30
      \]
    - For the above equations to hold, the coefficients of the rational and irrational parts must separately be equal on both sides. Therefore, we get two separate systems to solve for \(a, b, c\):
      - Rational part: \( 7a + c = 22 \)
      - Irrational part: \( b \sqrt{7} = 0 \) and \( b \sqrt{11} = 0 \) independently cannot hold directly, but it implies \( b = 0 \) (as \(\sqrt{7}\) and \(\sqrt{11}\) are linearly independent over the rationals).
      - Hence, we get:
        \[
        7a + c = 22 \quad \text{(1)}
        \]
        \[
        11a + c = 30 \quad \text{(2)}
        \]

4. **Solve for \(a\) and \(c\)**:
    - Subtract equation (1) from equation (2):
      \[
      (11a + c) - (7a + c) = 30 - 22 \implies 4a = 8 \implies a = 2
      \]
    - Substitute \( a = 2 \) back into equation (1):
      \[
      7(2) + c = 22 \implies 14 + c = 22 \implies c = 8
      \]
    - We do not have another independent equation to solve for \( b \) directly from the steps above, but because of the form and context of the problem, it is reasonable to assume \( b = 0 \) (as any non-zero \( b \) would make the function's values at \(\sqrt{7}\) and \(\sqrt{11}\) not fitting the form without additional variables or constraints).

5. **Form the quadratic function**:
    - The function is:
      \[
      p(x) = 2x^2 + 0 \cdot x + 8 = 2x^2 + 8
      \]

6. **Find \( p(\sqrt{17}) \)**:
    - Substitute \( \sqrt{17} \) into the function:
      \[
      p(\sqrt{17}) = 2 (\sqrt{17})^2 + 8 = 2 \cdot 17 + 8 = 34 + 8 = 42
      \]
