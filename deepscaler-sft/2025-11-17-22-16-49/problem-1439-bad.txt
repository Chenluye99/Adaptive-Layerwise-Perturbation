First, let's outline the steps we need to solve the problem:

1. **Convert days to hours**: Since there are 24 hours in a day, the total number of hours of music in the library is \( 12 \text{ days} \times 24 \text{ hours/day} = 288 \text{ hours} \).
2. **Find the average space per hour**: Given that the total disk space used is 16,000 megabytes, we can find the average space used per hour by dividing the total disk space by the total number of hours of music. That is, \(\frac{16,000 \text{ MB}}{288 \text{ hours}}\).
3. **Compute and round the result**: Calculate the above expression and round the result to the nearest whole number.

Let's implement this step-by-step solution using Python.
```python
# Given values
total_disk_space = 16000  # in megabytes
total_days_of_music = 12
hours_per_day = 24

# Calculate total hours of music
total_hours_of_music = total_days_of_music * hours_per_day

# Calculate average megabytes per hour
average_megabytes_per_hour = total_disk_space / total_hours_of_music

# Round the result to the nearest whole number
average_megabytes_per_hour_rounded = round(average_megabytes_per_hour)
print(average_megabytes_per_hour_rounded)
```
Code execution result: 56

Thus, on average, one hour of music in this library takes up \(\boxed{56}\) megabytes of disk space. This is the final answer after confirming it through computation.
