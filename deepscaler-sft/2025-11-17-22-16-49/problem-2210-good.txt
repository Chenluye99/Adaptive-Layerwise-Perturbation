First, let's determine the vertices of the triangular region. The triangular region is formed by the coordinate axes and the line given by the equation \(2x + y = 6\).

1. **Find the intercepts of the line with the coordinate axes:**
    - **x-intercept**: Set \( y = 0 \) in the equation \(2x + y = 6\):
      \[
      2x + 0 = 6 \implies x = 3
      \]
      So, the x-intercept is \((3, 0)\).
    - **y-intercept**: Set \( x = 0 \) in the equation \(2x + y = 6\):
      \[
      2 \cdot 0 + y = 6 \implies y = 6
      \]
      So, the y-intercept is \((0, 6)\).

2. **Identify the vertices of the triangle:**
   - The line intersects the x-axis at \((3, 0)\).
   - The line intersects the y-axis at \((0, 6)\).
   - The third vertex of the triangle is the origin \((0, 0)\).

So, the vertices of the triangle are \((0, 0)\), \((3, 0)\), and \((0, 6)\).

3. **Calculate the area of the triangle:**
   - The formula for the area of a triangle with vertices \((x_1, y_1)\), \((x_2, y_2)\), and \((x_3, y_3)\) is:
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2) \right|
     \]
   - Substituting the vertices \((0, 0)\), \((3, 0)\), and \((0, 6)\) into the formula:
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| 0(0 - 6) + 3(6 - 0) + 0(0 - 0) \right| = \frac{1}{2} \left| 0 + 3 \cdot 6 + 0 \right| = \frac{1}{2} \left| 18 \right| = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 18 = 9
     \]

Let's confirm this by coding the solution in Python.
```python
# Define the vertices of the triangle
x1, y1 = 0, 0
x2, y2 = 3, 0
x3, y3 = 0, 6

# Calculate the area using the formula for the area of a triangle given its vertices
area = 0.5 * abs(x1*(y2 - y3) + x2*(y3 - y1) + x3*(y1 - y2))
print(area)
```
