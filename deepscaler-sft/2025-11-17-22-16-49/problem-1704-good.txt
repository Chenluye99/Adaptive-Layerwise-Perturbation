To solve this problem, let's start by understanding the properties of a regular hexagon.

1. **Properties of a Regular Hexagon:**
    - A regular hexagon has six equal sides.
    - Each internal angle of a regular hexagon is 120 degrees.
    - A regular hexagon can be divided into 6 equilateral triangles, with each side of the hexagon being the same as the side of these equilateral triangles.

2. **Longest Diagonal of a Regular Hexagon:**
    - The longest diagonal of a regular hexagon spans from one vertex to the opposite vertex, essentially forming two sides of the equilateral triangles that make up the hexagon. Therefore, the length of the longest diagonal is twice the side length of the hexagon.
    - If we denote the side length of the hexagon as \( s \), then the length of the longest diagonal is \( 2s \).

3. **Perimeter of a Regular Hexagon:**
    - The perimeter of the hexagon is the sum of the lengths of all its sides. Since a hexagon has 6 sides, each of length \( s \), the perimeter is \( 6s \).

4. **Ratio of the Measure of the Longest Diagonal to the Measure of the Perimeter:**
    - We need to find the ratio of the longest diagonal to the perimeter. This can be written as:
      \[
      \text{Ratio} = \frac{\text{Length of the longest diagonal}}{\text{Perimeter}} = \frac{2s}{6s} = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}
      \]

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# We will define a function that takes the side length of the hexagon and returns the length of the longest diagonal and the perimeter.
def calculate_ratio():
    # Let's assume the side length of the hexagon is s (it can be any positive value, as it will cancel out in the ratio)
    s = 1  # we can assume s = 1 for simplicity, as it is a ratio and any value for s will give the same ratio
    
    # Length of the longest diagonal of the hexagon is 2 * s
    longest_diagonal = 2 * s
    
    # Perimeter of the hexagon is 6 * s
    perimeter = 6 * s
    
    # Calculate the ratio of the longest diagonal to the perimeter
    ratio = longest_diagonal / perimeter
    return ratio

# Get the ratio
ratio = calculate_ratio()
print(ratio)
```
