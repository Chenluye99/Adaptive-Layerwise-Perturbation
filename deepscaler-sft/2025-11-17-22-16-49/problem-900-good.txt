Let's start by analyzing the equation \((x - 5x + 12)^2 + 1 = -|x|\):

    - The right side of the equation, \(-|x|\), is always non-positive because \(|x| \geq 0\) and taking its negative makes \(-|x| \leq 0\).

3. **Conclusion:**
