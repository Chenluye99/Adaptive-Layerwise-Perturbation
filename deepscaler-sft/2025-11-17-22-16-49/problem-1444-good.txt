1. **Identify the Problem:**
    - We have a class of 28 students.
    - Three of these students are the top three geography students.
    - We need to choose 4 students out of the 28, and we want the group of 4 to include at least 2 of the top 3 geography students.
    - We need to find the probability of this event.

2. **Formulate the Probability Calculation:**
    - First, calculate the total number of ways to choose 4 students out of 28.
    - Then, calculate the number of ways to choose 4 students such that the group includes at least 2 of the top 3 geography students.
    - The probability is the ratio of the number of favorable outcomes to the total number of outcomes.

3. **Calculate Total Number of Ways to Choose 4 Students Out of 28:**
    - This is given by the combination formula \( \binom{28}{4} \), which is calculated as \( \frac{28!}{4!(28-4)!} = \frac{28!}{4!24!} \).

4. **Calculate Number of Favorable Outcomes:**
    - We need to consider different scenarios where the group of 4 includes at least 2 of the top 3 geography students:
        - Include exactly 2 of the top 3 students and 2 of the other 25 students.
        - Include all 3 of the top 3 students and 1 of the other 25 students.
    - We can use combination formulas for these scenarios as well.

    - The number of ways to choose exactly 2 of the top 3 students and 2 of the other 25 students is \( \binom{3}{2} \times \binom{25}{2} \).
    - The number of ways to choose all 3 of the top 3 students and 1 of the other 25 students is \( \binom{3}{3} \times \binom{25}{1} \).

5. **Compute and Get the Final Probability:**
    - Sum the number of favorable outcomes and divide by the total number of ways to get the probability.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to ensure the calculations are accurate.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Calculate the total number of ways to choose 4 students out of 28
total_ways = sp.binomial(28, 4)

# Calculate the number of ways to choose 4 students such that the group includes at least 2 of the top 3 geography students

# Number of ways to choose exactly 2 of the top 3 students and 2 of the other 25 students
ways_2_top_3_and_2_others = sp.binomial(3, 2) * sp.binomial(25, 2)

# Number of ways to choose all 3 of the top 3 students and 1 of the other 25 students
ways_3_top_3_and_1_other = sp.binomial(3, 3) * sp.binomial(25, 1)

# Total number of ways to choose 4 students such that the group includes at least 2 of the top 3 geography students
favorable_ways = ways_2_top_3_and_2_others + ways_3_top_3_and_1_other

# Calculate the probability
```
