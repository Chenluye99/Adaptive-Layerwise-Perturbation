First, let's break down the problem of complex number dilation step-by-step.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have a complex number \( z_0 = 2i \).
    - The center of dilation is \( c = -1 + 4i \).
    - The scale factor of the dilation is \( k = -2 \).

2. **Formula for Dilation of a Complex Number:**
    - The formula for dilating a complex number \( z_0 \) centered at \( c \) with a scale factor \( k \) is given by:
      \[
      z' = k (z_0 - c) + c
      \]
    - Here, \( z_0 = 2i \), \( c = -1 + 4i \), and \( k = -2 \).

3. **Substitute the Values into the Formula:**
    - First, compute \( z_0 - c \):
      \[
      z_0 - c = 2i - (-1 + 4i) = 2i + 1 - 4i = 1 - 2i
      \]
    - Then, multiply by the scale factor \( k = -2 \):
      \[
      -2 \cdot (1 - 2i) = -2 + 4i
      \]
    - Finally, add the center of dilation \( c = -1 + 4i \):
      \[
      z' = -2 + 4i + (-1 + 4i) = -2 + 4i - 1 + 4i = -3 + 8i
      \]

So, the dilation of \( 2i \) centered at \( -1 + 4i \) with a scale factor of \( -2 \) takes it to \( -3 + 8i \).

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python.
```python
# Define the complex numbers
z0 = 2j        # 2i

# Perform the dilation
```
