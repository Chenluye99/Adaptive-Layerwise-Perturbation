To evaluate this infinite geometric series, we need to identify its first term (\(a\)) and its common ratio (\(r\)).

The given series is:
\[
\frac{4}{3} - \frac{5}{12} + \frac{25}{144} - \frac{125}{1728} + \dots
\]

1. **Identify the First Term (\(a\)):**
   The first term of the series is:
   \[
   a = \frac{4}{3}
   \]

2. **Identify the Common Ratio (\(r\)):**
   The common ratio \(r\) of a geometric series is given by the ratio of the second term to the first term:
   \[
   r = \frac{-\frac{5}{12}}{\frac{4}{3}} = -\frac{5}{12} \cdot \frac{3}{4} = -\frac{5 \cdot 3}{12 \cdot 4} = -\frac{15}{48} = -\frac{5}{16}
   \]

   We can confirm this by checking the ratio of the third term to the second term:
   \[
   r = \frac{\frac{25}{144}}{-\frac{5}{12}} = \frac{25}{144} \cdot \left(-\frac{12}{5}\right) = \frac{25 \cdot (-12)}{144 \cdot 5} = \frac{-300}{720} = -\frac{5}{12}
   \]

   And the ratio of the fourth term to the third term:
   \[
   r = \frac{-\frac{125}{1728}}{\frac{25}{144}} = -\frac{125}{1728} \cdot \frac{144}{25} = -\frac{125 \cdot 144}{1728 \cdot 25} = -\frac{18000}{43200} = -\frac{5}{12}
   \]

   We see that the common ratio \(r = -\frac{5}{16}\) holds consistently. 

3. **Sum of the Infinite Geometric Series:**
   The sum \(S\) of an infinite geometric series with first term \(a\) and common ratio \(r\) (where \(|r| < 1\)) is given by:
   \[
   S = \frac{a}{1 - r}
   \]
   Substituting \(a = \frac{4}{3}\) and \(r = -\frac{5}{16}\):
   \[
   S = \frac{\frac{4}{3}}{1 - \left(-\frac{5}{16}\right)} = \frac{\frac{4}{3}}{1 + \frac{5}{16}} = \frac{\frac{4}{3}}{\frac{16}{16} + \frac{5}{16}} = \frac{\frac{4}{3}}{\frac{21}{16}} = \frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{16}{21} = \frac{4 \cdot 16}{3 \cdot 21} = \frac{64}{63}
   \]
