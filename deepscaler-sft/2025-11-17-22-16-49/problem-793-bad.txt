To solve this problem, we need to determine the rotation matrix that transforms \(\begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix}\) to \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix}\). The general form of a 2D rotation matrix is:

\[ R(\theta) = \begin{pmatrix} \cos \theta & -\sin \theta \\ \sin \theta & \cos \theta \end{pmatrix} \]

We need to find the angle \(\theta\) such that:

\[ \begin{pmatrix} \cos \theta & -\sin \theta \\ \sin \theta & \cos \theta \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix} \]

This will give us two equations:

\[ \cos \theta \cdot (-4) - \sin \theta \cdot 7 = 1 \]
\[ \sin \theta \cdot (-4) + \cos \theta \cdot 7 = 8 \]

We can solve these equations to get the values of \(\cos \theta\) and \(\sin \theta\).

Let's denote:
\[ a = \cos \theta \]
\[ b = \sin \theta \]

So, we have the system of equations:
\[ -4a - 7b = 1 \]
\[ -4b + 7a = 8 \]

We can solve this system of equations using Python.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
a, b = sp.symbols('a b')

# Define the equations
eq1 = -4 * a - 7 * b - 1
eq2 = -4 * b + 7 * a - 8

# Solve the system of equations
solution = sp.solve((eq1, eq2), (a, b))
print(solution)
```
