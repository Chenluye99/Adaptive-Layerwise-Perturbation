First, let's solve the problem step-by-step.

1. **Square Each Integer and Find the Remainders:**
    - We need to square each integer \( n \) in the range \( 1 \le n \le 10 \).
    - Then, we find the remainder when each square is divided by 11.

2. **Find the Distinct Remainders:**
    - Collect all the distinct remainders obtained in the previous step.

3. **Sum the Distinct Remainders:**
    - Add up all the distinct remainders to get \( m \).

4. **Find the Quotient When \( m \) is Divided by 11:**
    - Perform the division of \( m \) by 11 and get the quotient.

Let's implement this step-by-step process in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Step 1: Square each integer in the range 1 to 10 and find the remainders when divided by 11
squares = [(n ** 2) % 11 for n in range(1, 11)]

# Step 2: Get the distinct remainders
distinct_remainders = set(squares)

# Step 3: Sum the distinct remainders to get m
m = sum(distinct_remainders)

# Step 4: Find the quotient when m is divided by 11
quotient = m // 11

print((squares, distinct_remainders, m, quotient))
```
Code execution result: ([1, 4, 9, 5, 3, 3, 5, 9, 4, 1], {1, 3, 4, 5, 9}, 22, 2)

So, we have the following results after completing the steps:

- The remainders when the squares of the integers from 1 to 10 are divided by 11 are: \([1, 4, 9, 5, 3, 3, 5, 9, 4, 1]\).
- The distinct remainders are: \{1, 3, 4, 5, 9\}.
- The sum of the distinct remainders, which we call \( m \), is: 22.
- The quotient when \( m \) is divided by 11 is: 2.

Thus, the final answer is \(\boxed{2}\). This is our solution.
