To solve this problem, we need to find the points of intersection of the graphs of \( y = x^4 \) and \( y = 7x^2 - 10 \). To find the points of intersection, we set the equations equal to each other:
\[ x^4 = 7x^2 - 10. \]
Rearrange the equation to:
\[ x^4 - 7x^2 + 10 = 0. \]
This is a quadratic equation in terms of \( x^2 \). Let's make a substitution \( z = x^2 \). Then, the equation becomes:
\[ z^2 - 7z + 10 = 0. \]
We solve this quadratic equation for \( z \) using the quadratic formula \( z = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \), where \( a = 1 \), \( b = -7 \), and \( c = 10 \):
\[ z = \frac{-(-7) \pm \sqrt{(-7)^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot 10}}{2 \cdot 1} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{49 - 40}}{2} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{9}}{2} = \frac{7 \pm 3}{2}. \]
This gives two solutions:
\[ z = \frac{7 + 3}{2} = 5, \]
\[ z = \frac{7 - 3}{2} = 2. \]
So, we have:
\[ x^2 = 5 \quad \text{or} \quad x^2 = 2. \]
This means the \( x \)-coordinates of the points of intersection are \( \pm \sqrt{5} \) and \( \pm \sqrt{2} \). Thus, we identify that \( m = 5 \) and \( n = 2 \). We need to find the value of \( m - n \):
\[ m - n = 5 - 2 = 3. \]
Let's confirm this by coding the solution to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables and equations
x = sp.symbols('x')
eq1 = x**4
eq2 = 7 * x**2 - 10

# Find the intersection points
intersection_points = sp.solve(eq1 - eq2, x)

# Get the distinct positive x-coordinates of the intersection points
positive_x_coords = [pt.evalf() for pt in intersection_points if pt > 0]

# Extract m and n based on the form ±√m and ±√n
m = max(positive_x_coords) ** 2
n = min(positive_x_coords) ** 2

# Calculate m - n
result = m - n
print(result)
```
