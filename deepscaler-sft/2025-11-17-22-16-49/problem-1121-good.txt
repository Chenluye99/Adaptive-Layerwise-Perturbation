To solve the problem, we need to find the integers between 1000 and 2000 that are divisible by 15, 20, and 25. An integer that has 15, 20, and 25 as factors must be divisible by the least common multiple (LCM) of these three numbers.

First, let's determine the LCM of 15, 20, and 25.

1. **Prime Factorization:**
    - \(15 = 3 \times 5\)
    - \(20 = 2^2 \times 5\)
    - \(25 = 5^2\)

2. **Find the LCM:**
    - The LCM is obtained by taking the highest power of each prime number appearing in the factorization of these numbers.
    - So, LCM \( = 2^2 \times 3 \times 5^2 = 4 \times 3 \times 25 = 300 \).

Thus, any number that is divisible by 15, 20, and 25 must be divisible by 300. Now, we need to find which multiples of 300 lie between 1000 and 2000.

3. **Find the Multiples of 300 Between 1000 and 2000:**
    - The smallest multiple of 300 that is greater than or equal to 1000 is 1200 (since \(300 \times 4 = 1200\)).
    - The largest multiple of 300 that is less than or equal to 2000 is 1800 (since \(300 \times 6 = 1800\) and \(300 \times 7 = 2100\) which is greater than 2000).

So, the multiples of 300 between 1000 and 2000 are 1200, 1500, and 1800. We can confirm this by listing them and then counting them.

Let's write the Python code to ensure the accuracy of this result.
