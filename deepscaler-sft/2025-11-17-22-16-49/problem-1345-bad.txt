First, let's start by setting up the problem. A domino is made up of two congruent squares placed side by side. Each square has a side length of \( s \).

### Step-by-Step Breakdown:

1. **Determine the Perimeter of the Domino:**
    - When two squares are placed side by side, the resulting shape of the domino will have 4 unit lengths on top and bottom and 3 unit lengths on the sides (from two squares combined).
    - So, the total perimeter of the domino will be \( 2s + 2s + s + s + s + s = 8s \) (since there are 8 edges making up the perimeter, 4 from the top and bottom and 4 from the sides).
    - We are given that the perimeter of the domino is 60 units, so we can set up the equation:
      \[
      8s = 60
      \]

2. **Solve for Side Length \( s \):**
    - To find \( s \), we solve the equation above:
      \[
      s = \frac{60}{8} = \frac{15}{2} = 7.5
      \]

3. **Find the Area of the Domino:**
    - Each square in the domino has a side length of \( s \), so the area of one square is \( s^2 \).
    - Since the domino is made up of two of these squares placed side by side, the total area of the domino will be:
      \[
      \text{Area} = 2 \cdot s^2
      \]
    - Substituting \( s = 7.5 \) into the equation, we get:
      \[
      \text{Area} = 2 \cdot (7.5)^2 = 2 \cdot 56.25 = 112.5
      \]

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure the accuracy of the result.
```python
# Define the given perimeter of the domino
perimeter_domino = 60

# Calculate the side length of the squares that form the domino
s = perimeter_domino / 8

# Calculate the area of the domino, which consists of two squares with side length s
area_of_domino = 2 * (s ** 2)
print(area_of_domino)
```

Thus, the area of the domino is \(\boxed{112.5}\) square units. This confirms our reasoning and solution are correct.
