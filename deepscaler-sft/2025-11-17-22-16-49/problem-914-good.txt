First, let's outline the steps needed to solve this problem:

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - There are six congruent circles, each of which is tangent to its two neighbors and internally tangent to a larger circle \(\cal C\) with radius 30.
    - Let the radius of each of the six smaller circles be \( r \).
    - Since each of the smaller circles is tangent to its two neighbors and all six smaller circles fit within the larger circle \(\cal C\), the centers of the six smaller circles form a regular hexagon with side length \( 2r \) (because the distance between the centers of two adjacent smaller circles is \( 2r \)).
    - The distance from the center of the larger circle \(\cal C\) to the center of any of the smaller circles is \( 30 - r \) (because the smaller circle is internally tangent to \(\cal C\)).
    - But, the distance from the center of the larger circle to the center of any of the smaller circles is also the radius of the circumscribed circle of the regular hexagon formed by the centers of the smaller circles, which is \( 2r \).

    Therefore, we get:
    \[
    30 - r = 2r \implies 30 = 3r \implies r = 10
    \]

2. **Calculate the Areas:**
    - The area of the larger circle \(\cal C\) with radius 30 is given by:
      \[
      \text{Area of } \cal C = \pi \cdot 30^2 = 900\pi
      \]
    - The area of one of the smaller circles with radius 10 is:
      \[
      \text{Area of one smaller circle} = \pi \cdot 10^2 = 100\pi
      \]
    - Since there are six smaller circles, the total area of the six smaller circles is:
      \[
      \text{Total area of six smaller circles} = 6 \cdot 100\pi = 600\pi
      \]
    - The area \(K\) of the region inside \(\cal C\) and outside all six smaller circles is:
      \[
      K = 900\pi - 600\pi = 300\pi
      \]

3. **Find \(\lfloor K \rfloor\):**
    - To get the final answer, we need to compute \( \lfloor 300\pi \rfloor \). We know that \(\pi \approx 3.14159\), so:
      \[
      300\pi \approx 300 \cdot 3.14159 = 942.477
      \]
    - Therefore, \(\lfloor 300\pi \rfloor = 942\).

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure accuracy.

```python
import math

# Given values
radius_of_larger_circle = 30
num_smaller_circles = 6

# Calculate the radius of the smaller circles
radius_of_smaller_circle = radius_of_larger_circle / 3

# Calculate the area of the larger circle
area_of_larger_circle = math.pi * (radius_of_larger_circle ** 2)

# Calculate the area of one smaller circle
area_of_one_smaller_circle = math.pi * (radius_of_smaller_circle ** 2)

# Calculate the total area of the six smaller circles
total_area_of_smaller_circles = num_smaller_circles * area_of_one_smaller_circle

# Calculate the area K of the region inside the larger circle and outside all six smaller circles
K = area_of_larger_circle - total_area_of_smaller_circles

# Get the greatest integer less than or equal to K
result = math.floor(K)
print(result)
```
