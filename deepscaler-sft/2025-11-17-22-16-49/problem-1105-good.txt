1. **Geometric Series Formula:**
   The \( n^{\text{th}} \) term of a geometric series is given by \( a \cdot r^{n-1} \), where \( a \) is the first term and \( r \) is the common ratio. We are told that Bill picks out \( k \) different numbers from this series, and all of these numbers have the same number of digits.

2. **Constraint on Numbers Having Same Number of Digits:**
   For a number to have the same number of digits, the value of the numbers must lie within a certain interval. A number \( x \) has \( d \) digits if and only if \( 10^{d-1} \leq x < 10^d \).

3. **Find the Range for Each Number of Digits:**
   For the \( n^{\text{th}} \) term of the geometric series, \( a \cdot r^{n-1} \) should have the same number of digits as \( a \cdot r^{m-1} \) for some earlier \( m \). So, for \( a \cdot r^{n-1} \) and \( a \cdot r^{m-1} \) to have the same number of digits, we require:
   \[
   10^{d-1} \leq a \cdot r^{n-1} < 10^d
   \]
   and
   \[
   10^{d-1} \leq a \cdot r^{m-1} < 10^d
   \]
   where \( d \) is the number of digits.

4. **Determine Maximum \( k \):**
