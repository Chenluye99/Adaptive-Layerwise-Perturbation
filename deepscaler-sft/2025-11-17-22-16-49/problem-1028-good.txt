To solve the problem, we need to determine the probability that the sum of the numbers on two six-sided dice is less than 11. 

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - A six-sided die has faces numbered from 1 to 6.
    - When two dice are rolled, there are a total of \(6 \times 6 = 36\) possible outcomes.
    - We need to count how many of these outcomes result in the sum of the numbers on the two dice being less than 11.

2. **Enumerate Outcomes and Count Favorable Outcomes:**
    - We will iterate through all possible outcomes of rolling two dice, which range from (1, 1) to (6, 6).
    - For each outcome, we will compute the sum of the numbers and check if the sum is less than 11.
    - We will count the number of outcomes that satisfy this condition.

3. **Calculate Probability:**
    - The probability is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of outcomes, i.e., \(\frac{\text{Number of favorable outcomes}}{36}\).

Let's implement this step-by-step process in Python to get the accurate result.
```python
import itertools

# Generate all possible outcomes when two six-sided dice are rolled
outcomes = list(itertools.product(range(1, 7), repeat=2))

# Count the number of outcomes where the sum of the numbers on the two dice is less than 11
favorable_outcomes = [outcome for outcome in outcomes if sum(outcome) < 11]

# Calculate the probability
num_favorable_outcomes = len(favorable_outcomes)
total_outcomes = len(outcomes)
probability = num_favorable_outcomes / total_outcomes

print(probability)
```
