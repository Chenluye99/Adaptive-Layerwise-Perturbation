1. **Define the Variables:**
    - Let \( z = x + yi \) where \( x \) and \( y \) are real numbers, and \( i \) is the imaginary unit.
    - The modulus condition \( |z| = 1 \) means that \( \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = 1 \), or equivalently, \( x^2 + y^2 = 1 \).
    - The complex conjugate of \( z \) is \( \overline{z} = x - yi \).

2. **Express the Given Condition:**
    - We need to evaluate the expression \(\left| \frac{z}{\overline{z}} + \frac{\overline{z}}{z} \right| = 1 \).
    - Substitute \( z = x + yi \) and \( \overline{z} = x - yi \):
      \[
      \frac{z}{\overline{z}} = \frac{x + yi}{x - yi}
      \]
      and
      \[
      \frac{\overline{z}}{z} = \frac{x - yi}{x + yi}.
      \]
    - To simplify \(\frac{x + yi}{x - yi}\) and \(\frac{x - yi}{x + yi}\), we multiply the numerator and the denominator by the conjugate of the denominator:
      \[
      \frac{x + yi}{x - yi} \cdot \frac{x + yi}{x + yi} = \frac{(x + yi)^2}{(x - yi)(x + yi)} = \frac{x^2 + 2xyi - y^2}{x^2 + y^2},
      \]
      and
      \[
      \frac{x - yi}{x + yi} \cdot \frac{x - yi}{x - yi} = \frac{(x - yi)^2}{(x + yi)(x - yi)} = \frac{x^2 - 2xyi - y^2}{x^2 + y^2}.
      \]
    - Since \( x^2 + y^2 = 1 \) (from the condition \( |z| = 1 \)), we get:
      \[
      \frac{z}{\overline{z}} = x^2 + 2xyi - y^2,
      \]
      and
      \[
      \frac{\overline{z}}{z} = x^2 - 2xyi - y^2.
      \]
    - Adding these gives:
      \[
      \frac{z}{\overline{z}} + \frac{\overline{z}}{z} = (x^2 - y^2 + 2xyi) + (x^2 - y^2 - 2xyi) = 2(x^2 - y^2).
      \]
    - So, we need:
      \[
      \left| 2(x^2 - y^2) \right| = 1 \implies |x^2 - y^2| = \frac{1}{2}.
      \]
3. **Formulate the Equation System:**
    - We now have two equations:
      \[
      x^2 + y^2 = 1,
      \]
      and
      \[
      |x^2 - y^2| = \frac{1}{2}.
      \]
    - This gives us two cases to consider:
      1. \( x^2 - y^2 = \frac{1}{2} \)
      2. \( x^2 - y^2 = -\frac{1}{2} \)

4. **Solve the System of Equations:**
    - We can solve these equations simultaneously to find the values of \( x \) and \( y \).

import sympy as sp
