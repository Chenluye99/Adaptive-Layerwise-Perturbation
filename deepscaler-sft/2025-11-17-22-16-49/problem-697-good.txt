\[ 22^2 \times 55^2 = 10^2 \times N^2, \]

we can start by simplifying and solving the equation step-by-step.

1. First, compute the left-hand side (LHS) of the equation:
\[ 22^2 \times 55^2. \]

2. Next, compute the right-hand side (RHS) of the equation and express it in a form that isolates \( N^2 \):
\[ 10^2 \times N^2. \]

3. Equate the LHS and RHS, so we get:
\[ 22^2 \times 55^2 = 10^2 \times N^2. \]

4. Rearrange the equation to solve for \( N^2 \):
\[ N^2 = \frac{22^2 \times 55^2}{10^2}. \]

5. Take the square root of both sides to solve for \( N \):
\[ N = \sqrt{\frac{22^2 \times 55^2}{10^2}}. \]
