1. **Probability of Getting a Question Incorrectly:**

2. **Probability of Getting All Four Questions Incorrectly:**
   - The probability that William gets a specific question wrong is \(\frac{4}{5}\). Therefore, the probability that he gets all four questions wrong is \(\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^4\).

3. **Probability of Getting At Least One Question Correct:**
   - The probability that William gets at least one of the final four questions right is the complement of the probability that he gets all four questions wrong. So, we can calculate it as:
     \[
     P(\text{at least one correct}) = 1 - P(\text{all four wrong})
     \]
   - Substituting in the probability calculated above, we get:
     \[
     P(\text{all four wrong}) = \left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^4
     \]
     \[
     P(\text{at least one correct}) = 1 - \left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^4
     \]

Let's now calculate this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Calculate the probability that William gets all four questions wrong
prob_all_wrong = (4 / 5) ** 4

prob_at_least_one_correct = 1 - prob_all_wrong
```
