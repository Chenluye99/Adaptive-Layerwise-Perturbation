### Step 2: Perform Chi-Square Test

To determine if we can be 99% confident that there is a difference in the quality of the products produced by Machine A and Machine B, we perform a chi-square test of independence. The null hypothesis is that the quality of product production is independent of the machine tool, and the alternative hypothesis is that there is a difference.

We will use the formula for the chi-square statistic:
\[ K^2 = \frac{n(ad - bc)^2}{(a + b)(c + d)(a + c)(b + d)} \]

Where:
- \(a = 150 \) (First-class from Machine A)
- \(b = 50 \) (Second-class from Machine A)
- \(c = 120 \) (First-class from Machine B)
- \(d = 80 \) (Second-class from Machine B)
- \(n = 400 \) (Total number of products)
