To solve the problem of finding the projection of one vector onto another, we can use the formula for the projection of vector $\mathbf{a}$ onto vector $\mathbf{b}$, which is given by:

\[
\text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}} \mathbf{a} = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{b}} \mathbf{b}
\]

However, if we are only interested in the scalar projection (i.e., the magnitude of the projection of $\mathbf{a}$ onto $\mathbf{b}$), we can use:

\[
\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}
\]

For this specific problem, we need to find the scalar projection of $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$ onto $\begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$. 

Let's denote:
\[
\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}
\]

First, we compute the dot product $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}$:
\[
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 1 \cdot 8 + (-2) \cdot 1 = 8 - 2 = 6
\]

Next, we compute the magnitude of $\mathbf{b}$:
\[
\|\mathbf{b}\| = \sqrt{8^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{64 + 1} = \sqrt{65}
\]

The scalar projection of $\mathbf{a}$ onto $\mathbf{b}$ is then given by:
\[
\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{65}}
\]

To get the final answer in a simplified form, we can rationalize the denominator if needed, but for most practical purposes, we can leave it in this form. Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the vectors
a = sp.Matrix([1, -2])
b = sp.Matrix([8, 1])

# Calculate the dot product a . b
dot_product = a.dot(b)

# Calculate the magnitude of b
magnitude_b = b.norm()

# Calculate the scalar projection
scalar_projection = dot_product / magnitude_b
print(scalar_projection)
```
Code execution result: 6*sqrt(65)/65

Thus, the scalar projection of the vector $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$ onto the vector $\begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is:

\(\boxed{\frac{6 \sqrt{65}}{65}}\)
