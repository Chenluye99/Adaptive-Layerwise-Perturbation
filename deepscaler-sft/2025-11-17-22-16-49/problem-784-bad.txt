2. **Define Function \( s(x) \):**

   - Domain of \( s(x) \) is \(\{1, 2, 3, 4\}\).

   - To get possible values of \( s(r(x)) \), we first need to determine what values \( r(x) \) can take for \( x \) in \(\{-1, 0, 1, 2\}\). Since the range of \( r(x) \) is \(\{0, 2, 4, 6\}\), these are the inputs that will be passed to \( s(x) \).

     - \( s(2) = 2 + 1 = 3 \)

4. **Find the Sum of All Possible Values of \( s(r(x)) \):**
