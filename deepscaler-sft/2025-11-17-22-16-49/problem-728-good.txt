\[ f(m + n) + f(mn - 1) = f(m) f(n) + 2 \]

for all integers \( m \) and \( n \). We will then determine the possible values of \( f(2) \), find the number of distinct possible values (i.e., \( n \)), and compute their sum (i.e., \( s \)). Finally, we will find the product \( n \times s \).

     \[

     \]
     So, we have:
     \[
     f(1) + f(-1) = f(1) f(0) + 2 \quad \text{(Equation 2)}
