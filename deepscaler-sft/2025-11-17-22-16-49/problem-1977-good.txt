To solve the problem, let's start by analyzing the given equation and transforming it into the standard form of a circle's equation.

The given equation is:
\[x^2 + 10x + y^2 + 6y - k = 0.\]

We need to convert this equation into the standard form of a circle's equation, which is \((x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2\), where \((h, k)\) is the center of the circle and \(r\) is the radius.

To do this, we will complete the square for both \(x\) and \(y\) terms.

1. **Complete the Square for \(x\):**
   - The \(x\) terms are \(x^2 + 10x\).
   - To complete the square, we take the coefficient of \(x\), which is 10, divide it by 2 to get 5, and then square it to get 25.
   - So, we rewrite \(x^2 + 10x\) as \((x + 5)^2 - 25\).

2. **Complete the Square for \(y\):**
   - The \(y\) terms are \(y^2 + 6y\).
   - To complete the square, we take the coefficient of \(y\), which is 6, divide it by 2 to get 3, and then square it to get 9.
   - So, we rewrite \(y^2 + 6y\) as \((y + 3)^2 - 9\).

Substituting these back into the original equation, we get:
\[
(x + 5)^2 - 25 + (y + 3)^2 - 9 - k = 0.
\]
This simplifies to:
\[
(x + 5)^2 + (y + 3)^2 - 34 - k = 0,
\]
or
\[
(x + 5)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 34 + k.
\]

We know that the right-hand side of the equation \((x + 5)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = r^2\) must be equal to the square of the radius of the circle. Here, we are given that the radius is 6, so \(r = 6\) and \(r^2 = 36\). Therefore, we set:
\[
34 + k = 36.
\]
Solving for \(k\):
\[
k = 36 - 34 = 2.
\]

Thus, the value of \(k\) that makes the equation represent a circle of radius 6 is \(k = 2\). Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure the accuracy.
```python
# We will set up the equation and solve for k as described above to ensure our result is accurate.

from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variables
k = symbols('k')

# The equation we derived was 34 + k = 36
equation = Eq(34 + k, 36)

# Solve for k
solution = solve(equation, k)[0]
print(solution)
```
Code execution result: 2
