\[
\cot 10^\circ = \frac{\cos 10^\circ}{\sin 10^\circ}
\]
\[
\tan 5^\circ = \frac{\sin 5^\circ}{\cos 5^\circ}
\]

So, we have:

\[
\cot 10^\circ + \tan 5^\circ = \frac{\cos 10^\circ}{\sin 10^\circ} + \frac{\sin 5^\circ}{\cos 5^\circ}
\]

\[
\cot 10^\circ + \tan 5^\circ = \frac{\cos 10^\circ \cdot \cos 5^\circ + \sin 5^\circ \cdot \sin 10^\circ}{\sin 10^\circ \cdot \cos 5^\circ}
\]

\[
\cos (a - b) = \cos a \cdot \cos b + \sin a \cdot \sin b
\]

Here, let \(a = 10^\circ\) and \(b = 5^\circ\), then:

\[
\cos 10^\circ \cdot \cos 5^\circ + \sin 5^\circ \cdot \sin 10^\circ = \cos (10^\circ - 5^\circ) = \cos 5^\circ
\]

So, the expression becomes:

\[
\cot 10^\circ + \tan 5^\circ = \frac{\cos 5^\circ}{\sin 10^\circ \cdot \cos 5^\circ} = \frac{1}{\sin 10^\circ}
\]

Thus:

\[
\cot 10^\circ + \tan 5^\circ = \csc 10^\circ
