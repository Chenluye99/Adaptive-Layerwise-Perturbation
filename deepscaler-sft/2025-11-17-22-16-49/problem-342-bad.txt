To solve this problem, let's start by setting up a mathematical model for the situation described.

1. **Initial Setup:**
    - Let the initial volume of the molten iron be \( V \).
    - When the molten iron solidifies, its volume reduces by \(\frac{1}{34}\) of its initial volume.
    - So, the volume of the solid iron is \( V - \frac{1}{34}V = \frac{33}{34}V \).

2. **Melting Back Process:**
    - When the solid iron is melted back into molten iron, its volume will again be \( V \).
    - We need to determine how much its volume increases when it melts back compared to its volume as solid iron, which is \(\frac{33}{34}V\).

3. **Finding the Increase in Volume:**
    - The increase in volume when the solid iron melts back into molten iron is \( V - \frac{33}{34}V = \frac{1}{34}V \).
    - To find the percentage increase in volume, we divide this increase by the solid iron volume and multiply by 100:
      \[
      \text{Percentage Increase} = \frac{\frac{1}{34}V}{\frac{33}{34}V} \times 100 = \frac{\frac{1}{34}}{\frac{33}{34}} \times 100 = \frac{1}{33} \times 100 = \frac{100}{33}
      \]
    - To get the numerical value, we can compute \(\frac{100}{33}\) directly.

Let's implement this in Python to get the accurate result.
```python
# Calculate the percentage increase in volume
percentage_increase = (1 / 33) * 100
print(percentage_increase)
```
