To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Choose 11 Players out of 15 to form the starting lineup:**
   The number of ways to choose 11 players out of 15 is given by the combination formula \( \binom{15}{11} \), which is the same as \( \binom{15}{4} \) because \( \binom{n}{k} = \binom{n}{n-k} \). So, we calculate \( \binom{15}{4} \).

2. **Choose 2 Captains out of the 11 Selected Players:**
   After choosing the 11 players, we need to choose 2 captains from these 11 players. The number of ways to choose 2 captains out of 11 is given by the combination formula \( \binom{11}{2} \).

3. **Combine Both Steps:**
   For each way of choosing the 11 players, there are \( \binom{11}{2} \) ways to choose the 2 captains. Therefore, the total number of ways to choose the starting lineup and then choose the 2 captains is the product of these two values.

Let's proceed with the calculations in Python.
```python
import math

# Calculate the number of ways to choose 11 players out of 15
num_ways_to_choose_11_players = math.comb(15, 11)

# Calculate the number of ways to choose 2 captains out of 11 selected players
num_ways_to_choose_2_captains = math.comb(11, 2)

# The total number of ways to choose the starting lineup and designate the 2 captains
total_ways = num_ways_to_choose_11_players * num_ways_to_choose_2_captains
print(total_ways)
```

Thus, the number of ways to choose the starting lineup of 11 players from 15 members and then designate 2 captains from those 11 players is \(\boxed{75075}\). This is our final answer.
