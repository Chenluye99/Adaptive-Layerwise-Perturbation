First, let's establish the relationship between the arcs of circle \( A \) and circle \( B \).

1. **Arc Length Formula:**
   The length of an arc of a circle is given by:
   \[
   \text{Arc Length} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2 \pi r
   \]
   where \(\theta\) is the central angle of the arc and \( r \) is the radius of the circle.

2. **Setting Up the Equation:**
   - For circle \( A \), let the radius be \( r_A \) and the angle be \( 45^\circ \). So, the length of the arc is:
     \[
     \text{Arc Length}_A = \frac{45^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 2 \pi r_A = \frac{1}{8} \times 2 \pi r_A = \frac{\pi r_A}{4}
     \]
   - For circle \( B \), let the radius be \( r_B \) and the angle be \( 30^\circ \). So, the length of the arc is:
     \[
     \text{Arc Length}_B = \frac{30^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 2 \pi r_B = \frac{1}{12} \times 2 \pi r_B = \frac{\pi r_B}{6}
     \]

3. **Equating the Arc Lengths:**
   According to the problem, the lengths of these arcs are equal:
   \[
   \frac{\pi r_A}{4} = \frac{\pi r_B}{6}
   \]
   We can cancel out \(\pi\) from both sides of the equation:
   \[
   \frac{r_A}{4} = \frac{r_B}{6}
   \]
   Cross-multiply to solve for the ratio of the radii:
   \[
   6 r_A = 4 r_B \implies 3 r_A = 2 r_B \implies \frac{r_A}{r_B} = \frac{2}{3}
   \]

4. **Finding the Ratio of the Areas:**
   The area of a circle is given by \( \pi r^2 \). So, the areas of circle \( A \) and circle \( B \) are:
   \[
   \text{Area}_A = \pi r_A^2
   \]
   \[
   \text{Area}_B = \pi r_B^2
   \]
   We need to find the ratio of the area of circle \( A \) to the area of circle \( B \):
   \[
   \frac{\text{Area}_A}{\text{Area}_B} = \frac{\pi r_A^2}{\pi r_B^2} = \frac{r_A^2}{r_B^2}
   \]
   Substitute the ratio \(\frac{r_A}{r_B} = \frac{2}{3}\):
   \[
   \frac{r_A^2}{r_B^2} = \left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^2 = \frac{4}{9}
   \]

Thus, the ratio of the area of circle \( A \) to the area of circle \( B \) is \(\frac{4}{9}\). Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure our result is accurate.
```python
# Given that (pi * r_A / 4) = (pi * r_B / 6), we solve for the ratio of the radii r_A / r_B as derived.

from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variables
r_A, r_B = symbols('r_A r_B')

# Set up the equation based on the arc lengths being equal
eq = Eq((r_A / 4), (r_B / 6))

# Solve for the ratio r_A / r_B
ratio = solve(eq, r_A)[0] / r_B

# Calculate the ratio of the areas, Area_A / Area_B = (r_A^2) / (r_B^2)
ratio_of_areas = (ratio) ** 2

print(ratio_of_areas)
```
