1. The sum of the roots \( p + q = a \).
2. The product of the roots \( pq = 2a \).

By substituting \( a = p + q \) from the first equation into the second equation, we get:
\[ pq = 2(p + q). \]

\[ pq - 2p - 2q = 0. \]

\[ pq - 2p - 2q + 4 = 4, \]
