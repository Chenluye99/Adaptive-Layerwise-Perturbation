      \[
      f(g(4)) = 4^2 = 16
      \]
      So, we get:
      \[
      f(g(4)) = 16
      \]
    - This means that there exists some value of \( y \) such that \( f(y) = 16 \) and \( y = g(4) \). So, if we let \( y = g(4) \), then:
      \[
      f(g(4)) = 16 \implies f(y) = 16
      \]

      \[
      g(16) = y^3
      \]

      \[
      16 = y^3
      \]
      Therefore:
      \[
      y = \sqrt[3]{16}
      \]
    - But since we know that \( y = g(4) \), we have:
      \[
      g(4) = \sqrt[3]{16}
      \]
    - Now, we need to compute \([g(4)]^3\):
      \[
      [g(4)]^3 = (\sqrt[3]{16})^3 = 16
