First, let's analyze the problem and understand the geometric arrangement of the pipes in Crate B.

1. **Problem Understanding**:
    - Each pipe has a diameter of 10 cm, so the radius of each pipe is 5 cm.
    - In Crate B, the pipes are arranged such that each layer of pipes is offset by half the diameter (5 cm) from the layer below it, forming a staggered pattern. The height of Crate B is to be determined, and we need to find how high three such pipes can be stacked in this arrangement such that the top of the third pipe is at height \( h \).

2. **Geometric Arrangement Analysis**:
    - The bottom pipe (let's call it the 1st pipe) is at height 0.
    - The 2nd pipe is placed such that its center is 5 cm horizontally from the center of the 1st pipe, but offset vertically by the vertical rise from the previous layer. To determine this vertical distance, we consider that the centers of the pipes form an equilateral triangle, and the height of such a triangle is \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \times \text{side length}\). Here, the side length of the equilateral triangle of pipe centers is 10 cm (diameter of the pipe), so the vertical distance between the centers of the 1st and 2nd pipes is:
      \[
      5 \sqrt{3} \, \text{cm}
      \]
    - The 3rd pipe is placed similarly, offset horizontally by 10 cm (diameter of the pipe) and vertically by another \(5 \sqrt{3} \, \text{cm}\) from the 2nd pipe's center.

3. **Calculating the Height \( h \)**:
    - The height from the base to the center of the 2nd pipe is \( 5 \sqrt{3} \, \text{cm} \).
    - The height from the base to the center of the 3rd pipe is the sum of the heights to the centers of the 2nd and 3rd pipes:
      \[
      5 \sqrt{3} + 5 \sqrt{3} = 10 \sqrt{3} \, \text{cm}
      \]
    - The height from the base to the top of the 3rd pipe is the height to the center of the 3rd pipe plus the radius of the 3rd pipe:
      \[
      h = 10 \sqrt{3} + 5
      \]

Let's implement this in Python to get the precise value.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
radius = 5  # radius of the pipes in cm

# Calculate the height from the base to the center of the third pipe
height_to_center_of_3rd_pipe = 10 * sp.sqrt(3)

# Add the radius of the third pipe to get the height from the base to the top of the third pipe

print(height)
```

Thus, the height \( h \) of the pile of 3 pipes in Crate B is \(\boxed{5 + 10 \sqrt{3}}\) cm. This is the final answer.
