To solve this problem, let's start by recalling some properties of a right triangle and the definition of a median in this context.

1. **Properties of a Right Triangle:**
    - A right triangle with leg lengths of 8 and 15 units will have a hypotenuse that can be calculated using the Pythagorean theorem:
      \[
      \text{Hypotenuse} = \sqrt{8^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17
      \]
    - The median to the hypotenuse of a right triangle is half the length of the hypotenuse. This is a well-known property of right triangles: the median to the hypotenuse is equal to half the hypotenuse.

2. **Median to the Hypotenuse:**
    - In this case, the hypotenuse is 17 units long, so the length of the median to the hypotenuse is:
      \[
      \text{Median to the Hypotenuse} = \frac{17}{2}
      \]
    - To express this as a common fraction, it is already in its simplest fractional form, but we should ensure it doesn't need to be simplified further, and that's the case here.

Let's confirm this with Python for verification.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Calculate the hypotenuse
leg1 = 8
leg2 = 15
hypotenuse = sp.sqrt(leg1**2 + leg2**2)

# The median to the hypotenuse of a right triangle is half the length of the hypotenuse
median_to_hypotenuse = hypotenuse / 2
print(median_to_hypotenuse)
```
Code execution result: 17/2

Thus, the length of the median to the hypotenuse is \(\boxed{\frac{17}{2}}\). This confirms that our solution is correct. The median to the hypotenuse of the right triangle with leg lengths 8 and 15 is \(\frac{17}{2}\).
