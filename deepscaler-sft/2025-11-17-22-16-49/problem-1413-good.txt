1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - The lateral surface area of a cylinder is given by the formula: \[ A = 2 \pi r h \]
    - We are given that the height \( h = 6 \) cm, and the lateral surface area is \( 48 \pi \) square centimeters. We can substitute these values into the formula to find the radius \( r \):
      \[
      2 \pi r \cdot 6 = 48 \pi
      \]
    - Simplifying this equation, we get:
      \[
      12 \pi r = 48 \pi \implies 12 r = 48 \implies r = \frac{48}{12} = 4
      \]
      So, the radius \( r = 4 \) cm.
    - The volume \( V \) of a cylinder is given by the formula: \[ V = \pi r^2 h \]
    - Substituting the values of \( r \) and \( h \):
      \[
      V = \pi \cdot 4^2 \cdot 6 = \pi \cdot 16 \cdot 6 = 96 \pi
      \]

2. **Implement the Solution in Python:**
Let's confirm our result with Python code.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
r = sp.symbols('r')

# Given values
h = 6
lateral_surface_area = 48 * sp.pi

# Equation for lateral surface area
eq = 2 * sp.pi * r * h - lateral_surface_area

# Solve for r
radius_solution = sp.solve(eq, r)[0]

# Calculate the volume of the cylinder
volume = sp.pi * radius_solution**2 * h
print(volume)
```
Code execution result: 96*pi

Thus, the volume of the cylindrical tube is \(\boxed{96 \pi}\) cubic centimeters. The calculation is confirmed to be accurate.
