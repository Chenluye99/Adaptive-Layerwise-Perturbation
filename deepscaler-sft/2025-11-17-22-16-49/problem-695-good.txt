First, let's begin by setting up the problem and understanding the geometric configuration.

1. **Identify Key Information:**
    - \(ABCD\) is a square.
    - \(AE \perp DE\).
    - \(AE = 8\) units.
    - \(DE = 6\) units.

2. **Find the Side Length of the Square:**
    - Since \(AE \perp DE\), triangle \(AED\) is a right triangle with legs of lengths 8 and 6.
    - We can use the Pythagorean theorem to get the length of \(AD\), which is the same as the side length of the square.
    - \[
        AD = \sqrt{AE^2 + DE^2} = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{64 + 36} = \sqrt{100} = 10
    \]
    - Hence, the side length of the square \(ABCD\) is 10 units.

3. **Find the Area of Square \(ABCD\):**
    - The area of the square \(ABCD\) is given by:
    \[
        \text{Area}_{\text{square}} = \text{side length}^2 = 10^2 = 100
    \]

4. **Find the Area of \(\triangle AED\):**
    - The area of \(\triangle AED\) is given by:
    \[
        \text{Area}_{\triangle AED} = \frac{1}{2} \times AE \times DE = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 6 = \frac{1}{2} \times 48 = 24
    \]

5. **Find the Area of Pentagon \(AEDCB\):**
    - The area of pentagon \(AEDCB\) is the area of square \(ABCD\) minus the area of \(\triangle AED\):
    \[
        \text{Area}_{\text{pentagon } AEDCB} = \text{Area}_{\text{square}} - \text{Area}_{\triangle AED} = 100 - 24 = 76
    \]

Let's confirm this step-by-step process by implementing it in Python for accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given lengths
AE = 8
DE = 6

# Calculate the side length of the square using Pythagorean theorem
side_length = sp.sqrt(AE**2 + DE**2)

# Calculate the area of the square
area_square = side_length ** 2

# Calculate the area of triangle AED
area_triangle_AED = (1/2) * AE * DE

# Calculate the area of pentagon AEDCB
area_pentagon_AEDCB = area_square - area_triangle_AED

print(area_pentagon_AEDCB)
```
