To solve the problem, let's start by analyzing the shape of the T formed by the two rectangles.

1. **Understanding the Formation of the T Shape:**
    - We have two \(2 \text{ inch} \times 4 \text{ inch}\) rectangles.
    - We place these two rectangles such that one rectangle is positioned horizontally and the other is positioned vertically on top of the vertical side of the first rectangle, forming the shape of a 'T'.

2. **Visualizing the Dimensions:**
    - The horizontal rectangle has dimensions \(2 \text{ inch} \times 4 \text{ inch}\).
    - The vertical rectangle, when placed on top of the vertical side of the horizontal rectangle, will have its bottom edge aligned with the top edge of the horizontal rectangle. So, the vertical rectangle will have a height of 4 inches (same as the height of the horizontal rectangle) and a width of 2 inches, but it extends the width of the whole shape by another 2 inches, making the total width of the 'T' shape 4 inches (since the original horizontal rectangle was 2 inches wide and the vertical part adds another 2 inches of width at its base).

    Therefore, the overall dimensions of the T shape will be:
    - Width: 4 inches (because the horizontal rectangle is 2 inches wide and the vertical part, when placed on top, adds to the width, making it 4 inches wide overall)
    - Height: 6 inches (because the vertical rectangle is 4 inches tall and it extends the height by another 2 inches at the top, making it 6 inches tall overall)

3. **Calculating the Perimeter:**
    - The perimeter of a shape is the sum of the lengths of all its sides.
    - For our T shape, it has two horizontal edges of length 4 inches each (one from the horizontal rectangle and one extended from the vertical rectangle), two vertical edges of length 2 inches each (one from the horizontal rectangle and one from the vertical rectangle), and two vertical edges of length 4 inches each (one extending from each side of the horizontal rectangle and forming part of the sides of the T shape).

    By tracing the outline, we can see the T shape has the following sides:
    - 4 inches (top of the T)
    - 4 inches (bottom of the T, coming from the horizontal rectangle)
    - 2 inches (left vertical part of the T, coming from the vertical rectangle)
    - 2 inches (right vertical part of the T, coming from the vertical rectangle)
    - 4 inches (left side of the T, extending from the horizontal and vertical rectangles)
    - 4 inches (right side of the T, extending from the horizontal and vertical rectangles)

    Therefore, the perimeter can be calculated by summing these lengths.

Let's implement this reasoning in Python to make sure we get an accurate result.
```python
# The dimensions of the T shape based on the description
width = 4
height = 6

# Calculate the perimeter of the T shape
# T shape has 4 sides: two at the top and bottom, two vertical sides
# Corresponding to each of the parts contributing to the outline of the shape
perimeter_T = 2 * (width + height)

print(perimeter_T)
```
