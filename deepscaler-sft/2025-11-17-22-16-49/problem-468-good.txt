Next, we need to find the variance of this data set. The formula for the variance \( s^2 \) of a data set is:

\[
s^2 = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N (x_i - \mu)^2
\]

where \( N \) is the number of data points, \( x_i \) are the data points, and \( \mu \) is the mean of the data set. We already know that the mean \(\mu = 6\).

Substitute the data points into the formula:

\[
s^2 = \frac{1}{5} \left[ (4 - 6)^2 + (5 - 6)^2 + (7 - 6)^2 + (9 - 6)^2 + (5 - 6)^2 \right]
\]

import numpy as np
