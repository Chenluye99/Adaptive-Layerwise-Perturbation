To solve this problem, let's set up some equations based on the given information.

1. Let's denote the number of ants of Species A on Day 0 as \( A_0 \) and the number of ants of Species B on Day 0 as \( B_0 \).
2. According to the problem, on Day 0, the total number of ants is 30:
   \[
   A_0 + B_0 = 30
   \]
3. On Day 5, the number of ants of Species A will be \( A_0 \cdot 2^5 = A_0 \cdot 32 \), and the number of ants of Species B will be \( B_0 \cdot 3^5 = B_0 \cdot 243 \).
4. On Day 5, the total number of ants is 3281:
   \[
   A_0 \cdot 32 + B_0 \cdot 243 = 3281
   \]

Now, we have a system of two equations:
\[
\begin{cases}
A_0 + B_0 = 30 \\
32 A_0 + 243 B_0 = 3281
\end{cases}
\]

We will solve this system of equations step by step.

First, express \( B_0 \) from the first equation:
\[
B_0 = 30 - A_0
\]

Next, substitute this expression into the second equation:
\[
32 A_0 + 243 (30 - A_0) = 3281
\]

Now, solve for \( A_0 \):
\[
32 A_0 + 7290 - 243 A_0 = 3281 \\
-211 A_0 + 7290 = 3281 \\
-211 A_0 = 3281 - 7290 \\
-211 A_0 = -4009 \\
A_0 = \frac{4009}{211} = 19
\]

Now, substitute \( A_0 = 19 \) back into the first equation to find \( B_0 \):
\[
19 + B_0 = 30 \\
B_0 = 30 - 19 = 11
\]

So, on Day 0, there are 19 ants of Species A and 11 ants of Species B. To find out how many ants of Species A there are on Day 5, we calculate:
\[
A_5 = A_0 \cdot 32 = 19 \cdot 32 = 608
\]
