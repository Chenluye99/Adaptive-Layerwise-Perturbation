To rationalize the denominator of \(\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}\), we can follow these steps:

1. First, express \(\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}\) as \(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}}\).
2. To rationalize the denominator, we multiply both the numerator and the denominator by \(\sqrt{3}\):
   \[
   \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{3}}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}
   \]

So, the expression \(\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}\) with the rationalized denominator is \(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\).

