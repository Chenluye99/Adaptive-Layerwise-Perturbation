First, let's start by understanding the problem and working through it step-by-step.

1. **Find the side length of the square:**
    - The perimeter of the square is 32 inches.
    - Since a square has four equal sides, the length of each side of the square is \( \frac{32}{4} = 8 \) inches.

2. **Find the radius of the inscribed circle:**
    - A circle is inscribed in the square, which means that the circle is touching all four sides of the square.
    - The diameter of the circle is equal to the side length of the square. So, the diameter of the circle is 8 inches.
    - Therefore, the radius of the circle is half of the diameter, which is \( \frac{8}{2} = 4 \) inches.

3. **Find the circumference of the circle:**
    - The formula for the circumference \( C \) of a circle is given by \( C = 2 \pi r \), where \( r \) is the radius of the circle.
    - Substituting the radius \( r = 4 \) into the formula, we get \( C = 2 \pi \cdot 4 = 8 \pi \).

So, the circumference of the circle is \( 8 \pi \) inches.

Let's confirm this step-by-step solution by implementing it in Python.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given perimeter of the square
perimeter_square = 32

# Calculate the side length of the square
side_length_square = perimeter_square / 4

# The diameter of the inscribed circle is equal to the side length of the square
diameter_circle = side_length_square

# The radius of the circle is half of its diameter
radius_circle = diameter_circle / 2

# Calculate the circumference of the circle
circumference_circle = 2 * sp.pi * radius_circle

print(circumference_circle)
```
Code execution result: 8.0*pi

Thus, the circumference of the circle is \(\boxed{8 \pi}\) inches. This confirms our earlier solution.
