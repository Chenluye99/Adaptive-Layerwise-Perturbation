To solve this problem, let's start by calculating the areas of the black and white regions using the radii of the concentric circles.

1. **Calculate Areas of Circles**:
   - The area of a circle is given by the formula \( A = \pi r^2 \).
   - The radii of the circles are 1, 3, 5, and 7.

2. **Determine Black and White Areas**:
   - **Inner Circle ( radius = 1 )**: This is the black region.
     - Black area (radius = 1): \( \pi \cdot 1^2 = \pi \).
   - **Ring 1 (between radii = 1 and 3 )**: This is the white region.
     - White area (ring 1): \( \pi \cdot 3^2 - \pi \cdot 1^2 = \pi (9 - 1) = 8 \pi \).
   - **Ring 2 (between radii = 3 and 5 )**: This is the black region.
     - Black area (ring 2): \( \pi \cdot 5^2 - \pi \cdot 3^2 = \pi (25 - 9) = 16 \pi \).
   - **Outer Ring (between radii = 5 and 7 )**: This is the white region.
     - White area (ring 3): \( \pi \cdot 7^2 - \pi \cdot 5^2 = \pi (49 - 25) = 24 \pi \).

3. **Calculate Total Black and White Areas**:
   - **Total Black Area**: \( \pi + 16 \pi = 17 \pi \).
   - **Total White Area**: \( 8 \pi + 24 \pi = 32 \pi \).

4. **Find the Ratio of Black Area to White Area**:
   - The ratio of the black area to the white area is \(\frac{17 \pi}{32 \pi} = \frac{17}{32}\).

Let's implement this step-by-step calculation in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Radius of the circles
radii = [1, 3, 5, 7]

# Calculate areas
areas = [sp.pi * r**2 for r in radii]

# Calculate black and white areas
black_area = areas[0] + (areas[2] - areas[1])
white_area = (areas[1] - areas[0]) + (areas[3] - areas[2])

# Calculate the ratio of black area to white area
ratio = black_area / white_area

print(ratio)
```
Code execution result: 17/32

Thus, the ratio of the black area to the white area is \(\boxed{\frac{17}{32}}\). This confirms our calculations were accurate.
