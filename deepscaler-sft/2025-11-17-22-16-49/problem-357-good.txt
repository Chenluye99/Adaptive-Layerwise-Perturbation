First, let's start by defining the random variables and their properties.

1. **Random Variable ξ:**
   - ξ follows a binomial distribution \( B(5, 0.5) \).
   - For a binomial distribution \( B(n, p) \), the expected value (mean) \( E(\xi) \) and the variance \( D(\xi) \) are given by:
     \[
     E(\xi) = n \cdot p
     \]
     \[
     D(\xi) = n \cdot p \cdot (1 - p)
     \]
   - For our specific case where \( n = 5 \) and \( p = 0.5 \):
     \[
     E(\xi) = 5 \cdot 0.5 = 2.5
     \]
     \[
     D(\xi) = 5 \cdot 0.5 \cdot (1 - 0.5) = 5 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.5 = 1.25
     \]

2. **Random Variable η:**
   - η is defined as \( \eta = 5 \cdot \xi \).
   - The expected value (mean) of η, \( E(\eta) \), can be computed using the property of expected values:
     \[
     E(\eta) = E(5 \cdot \xi) = 5 \cdot E(\xi) = 5 \cdot 2.5 = 12.5
     \]
   - The variance of η, \( D(\eta) \), can be computed using the property of variances:
     \[
     D(\eta) = D(5 \cdot \xi) = 5^2 \cdot D(\xi) = 25 \cdot 1.25 = 31.25
     \]

Let's confirm these results by implementing this in Python using `sympy`.
