1. **Setup:**
    - Consider a circle with radius \( r \).
    - The lengths of the chords are 2, 3, and 4, and they subtend central angles of \(\alpha\), \(\beta\), and \(\alpha + \beta\) respectively.

2. **Formulating Equations:**
    - For the first chord of length 2, the equation is: \[
        2 = 2r \sin \left( \frac{\alpha}{2} \right) \implies 1 = r \sin \left( \frac{\alpha}{2} \right)
    \]
    - For the second chord of length 3, the equation is: \[

    \]
    - For the third chord of length 4, the equation is: \[
        4 = 2r \sin \left( \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} \right) \implies 2 = r \sin \left( \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} \right)
    \]

3. **Expressing Sines:**
    - From the first equation: \[
        \sin \left( \frac{\alpha}{2} \right) = \frac{1}{r}
    \]
    - From the second equation: \[
        \sin \left( \frac{\beta}{2} \right) = \frac{3}{2r}
    \]
    - From the third equation: \[
        \sin \left( \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} \right) = \frac{2}{r}

4. **Using Sum-to-Product Identities:**
