To solve this problem, let's follow these steps:

1. **Determine the Total Time in Seconds:**
    - Casey has to paint 300 dimples.
    - It takes him 2 seconds to paint one dimple.
    - Therefore, the total time in seconds needed to paint all 300 dimples is \(300 \times 2\) seconds.

2. **Convert the Total Time to Minutes:**

    - To get the total time in minutes, we need to divide the total time in seconds by 60.

Let's perform this calculation using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given data
num_dimples = 300
time_per_dimple_seconds = 2

# Calculate total time in seconds
total_time_seconds = num_dimples * time_per_dimple_seconds

# Convert total time to minutes
total_time_minutes = total_time_seconds / 60
print(total_time_minutes)
```
