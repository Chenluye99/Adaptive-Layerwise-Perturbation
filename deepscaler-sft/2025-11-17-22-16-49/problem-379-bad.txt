To solve this problem, we need to set up equations based on how the lengths of the candles change over time as they burn.

1. **Initial Lengths:**
    - Both candles start at 20 cm.
    - The thin candle takes 4 hours to burn completely, so it burns at a rate of \( \frac{20 \text{ cm}}{4 \text{ hours}} = 5 \text{ cm/hour} \).
    - The thick candle takes 5 hours to burn completely, so it burns at a rate of \( \frac{20 \text{ cm}}{5 \text{ hours}} = 4 \text{ cm/hour} \).

2. **Lengths After \( t \) Hours:**
    - After \( t \) hours, the length of the thin candle will be \( 20 - 5t \).
    - After \( t \) hours, the length of the thick candle will be \( 20 - 4t \).

3. **Setting Up the Equation:**
    - We want to find the time \( t \) when the thin candle is twice as short as the thick candle. Mathematically, this means we want to find \( t \) such that the length of the thin candle is half the length of the thick candle.
    - So, we set up the equation: 
      \[
      20 - 5t = \frac{1}{2} (20 - 4t)
      \]

4. **Solve the Equation:**
    - Let's solve this equation step-by-step:
      \[
      20 - 5t = \frac{1}{2} (20 - 4t)
      \]
      \[
      20 - 5t = 10 - 2t
      \]
      \[
      20 - 10 = 5t - 2t
      \]
      \[
      10 = 3t
      \]
      \[
      t = \frac{10}{3}
      \]
      \[
      t = \frac{10}{3} \approx 3.3333 \text{ hours}
      \]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure the accuracy of the result.
```python
from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variable
t = symbols('t', positive=True)

# Define the equation based on the problem statement
thin_candle_length = 20 - 5 * t
thick_candle_length = 20 - 4 * t

# Set up the equation where the thin candle is twice as short as the thick candle
equation = Eq(thin_candle_length, 0.5 * thick_candle_length)

# Solve for t
solution = solve(equation, t)[0]
print(solution)
```

So, the solution we obtained is \( t = \frac{10}{3} \) hours, which confirms our earlier solution.
