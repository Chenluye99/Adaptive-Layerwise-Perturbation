To solve this problem, we need to find all integer solutions to the equation \(x^2 - y^2 = 2000^2\). We can start by rewriting the equation using the difference of squares:

\[x^2 - y^2 = (x - y)(x + y) = 2000^2.\]

Let's denote \(a = x - y\) and \(b = x + y\). Then, we have:

\[a \cdot b = 2000^2.\]

Here, \(a\) and \(b\) must be such that both \(x\) and \(y\) are integers. From the definitions of \(a\) and \(b\), we get:

\[x = \frac{a + b}{2}, \quad y = \frac{b - a}{2}.\]

For \(x\) and \(y\) to be integers, both \(\frac{a + b}{2}\) and \(\frac{b - a}{2}\) must be integers. This means that \(a + b\) and \(b - a\) must both be even. \[(2m)(2n) = 2000^2 \implies 4mn = 2000^2 \implies mn = \frac{2000^2}{4} = 1000^2.\]
