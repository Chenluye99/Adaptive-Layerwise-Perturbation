First, let's analyze the problem. We have a right triangle \(XYZ\) where:
- \(\angle YXZ = 90^\circ\),
- \(\angle XYZ = 60^\circ\),
- The length of the side opposite to \(\angle XZ = 60^\circ\) (which is side \(XZ\)) is 12.

Since \(\angle YXZ = 90^\circ\) and \(\angle XYZ = 60^\circ\), \(\angle XZY = 30^\circ\). This means that triangle \(XYZ\) is a 30-60-90 triangle. In a 30-60-90 triangle, the sides have a specific ratio:
- The side opposite the 30° angle is \( \frac{1}{2} \) of the hypotenuse.
- The side opposite the 60° angle is \( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \) of the hypotenuse.

Here, side \(XZ\) is the side opposite the 30° angle in this triangle and it is given to be 12. In a 30-60-90 triangle, the side opposite the 30° angle is half the hypotenuse. So, if we label the hypotenuse as \(XY\), then:
\[ XZ = \frac{1}{2} \cdot XY \]
Since \(XZ = 12\):
\[ 12 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot XY \implies XY = 12 \cdot 2 = 24 \]

To get the length of side \(YZ\) (which is the side opposite the 60° angle), we know that the side opposite the 60° angle is \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\) of the hypotenuse:
\[ YZ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot XY = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot 24 = 12 \sqrt{3} \]
