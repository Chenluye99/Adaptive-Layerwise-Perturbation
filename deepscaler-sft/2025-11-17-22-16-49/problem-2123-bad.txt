1. **Initial Setup:**
    - We have a cube with an edge length of 9 inches.
    - A sphere is inscribed in this cube, so the diameter of the sphere is equal to the edge length of the cube, which is 9 inches. Therefore, the radius of the sphere is \( \frac{9}{2} \) inches.

2. **Inscribing a Smaller Cube in the Sphere:**
    - Now, we inscribe a smaller cube in this sphere. For a cube to be inscribed in a sphere, the sphere's diameter must be equal to the space diagonal of the cube.
    - Let the edge length of the smaller cube be \( a \). The space diagonal of a cube with edge length \( a \) is given by \( a \sqrt{3} \).
    - Since the space diagonal of the smaller cube is equal to the diameter of the sphere, we set up the equation:
      \[
      a \sqrt{3} = 9
      \]
    - Solving for \( a \):
      \[
      a = \frac{9}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{9 \sqrt{3}}{3} = 3 \sqrt{3}
      \]

3. **Volume of the Smaller Cube:**
    - The volume \( V \) of a cube with edge length \( a \) is given by \( V = a^3 \).
    - Substituting \( a = 3 \sqrt{3} \):
      \[
      V = (3 \sqrt{3})^3 = 3^3 \cdot (\sqrt{3})^3 = 27 \cdot 3 \sqrt{3} = 81 \sqrt{3}
      \]
