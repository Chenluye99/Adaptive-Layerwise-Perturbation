First, let’s understand the series given: \(0.1_2 - 0.01_2 + 0.001_2 - 0.0001_2 + 0.00001_2 - \ldots\)

This is a geometric series in base 2. To write it out in base 10, we need to convert each term from base 2 to base 10.

1. \(0.1_2 = \frac{1}{2}\)
2. \(0.01_2 = \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2^2}\)
3. \(0.001_2 = \frac{1}{8} = \frac{1}{2^3}\)
4. \(0.0001_2 = \frac{1}{16} = \frac{1}{2^4}\)
5. \(0.00001_2 = \frac{1}{32} = \frac{1}{2^5}\)

We can see that the series in base 10 is:

\[
\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{8} - \frac{1}{16} + \frac{1}{32} - \ldots
\]

This is a geometric series with the first term \(a = \frac{1}{2}\) and the common ratio \(r = -\frac{1}{2}\).

The sum \(S\) of an infinite geometric series is given by the formula:

\[
S = \frac{a}{1 - r}
\]

Substituting \(a = \frac{1}{2}\) and \(r = -\frac{1}{2}\):

\[
S = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{1 - \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{1 + \frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{3}{2}} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3} = \frac{1}{3}
\]

So, the sum of the series is \(\frac{1}{3}\).
