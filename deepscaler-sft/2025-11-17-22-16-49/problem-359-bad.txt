If a polynomial with rational coefficients has an irrational root, its conjugate must also be a root. Therefore, the roots of the polynomial must also include the conjugates of \(1 + \sqrt{2}\) and \(1 + \sqrt{3}\), i.e., \(1 - \sqrt{2}\) and \(1 - \sqrt{3}\).

   \]

The polynomial we are looking for is the product of these two quadratic polynomials:
\[
(x^2 - 2x - 1)(x^2 - 2x - 2)
\]
