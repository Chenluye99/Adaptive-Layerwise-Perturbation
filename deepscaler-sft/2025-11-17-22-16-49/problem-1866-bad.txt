To solve the problem, we need to use some geometric properties and formulas involving the area of the faces and the dihedral angle between them.

First, recall the formula for the volume \( V \) of a tetrahedron when the areas of the faces and the angle between them are known. If the areas of faces \(ABC\) and \(BCD\) are \(A_{ABC}\) and \(A_{BCD}\) respectively, and the angle between them is \(\theta\), the volume \( V \) of the tetrahedron can be given by:

\[ V = \frac{1}{6} \cdot BC \cdot h_{ABC} \cdot h_{BCD} \cdot \sin \theta \]

where \( BC \) is the common edge between the faces, and \( h_{ABC} \) and \( h_{BCD} \) are the altitudes of faces \(ABC\) and \(BCD\) respectively, from the vertex opposite to edge \( BC \).

However, we can use another formula that directly makes use of the given areas and the angle between the faces:

\[ V = \frac{1}{6} \cdot BC \cdot A_{ABC} \cdot A_{BCD} \cdot \frac{\sin \theta}{BC^2} \]

Given that \( A_{ABC} = 120 \), \( A_{BCD} = 80 \), \( BC = 10 \), and \(\theta = 30^\circ\), we can substitute these values into the formula. Note that \(\sin 30^\circ = \frac{1}{2}\).

So, the formula simplifies to:

\[ V = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 10 \cdot 120 \cdot 80 \cdot \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{10^2} = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 10 \cdot 120 \cdot 80 \cdot \frac{1}{20} = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 10 \cdot 120 \cdot 80 \cdot \frac{1}{20} = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 10 \cdot 120 \cdot 4 = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 4800 = 800 \cdot \frac{1}{6} = \frac{4800}{6} = 800 \cdot \frac{1}{6} = 800 \cdot \frac{1}{6} = 800 \cdot \frac{1}{6} = 80 \]

Let's confirm this by coding it to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given values
A_ABC = 120
A_BCD = 80
BC = 10
theta = 30  # angle in degrees

# Calculate sin(30 degrees)
sin_theta = sp.sin(sp.rad(theta))

# Formula for the volume of the tetrahedron
V = (1/6) * BC * A_ABC * A_BCD * sin_theta / (BC ** 2)

print(V)
```
