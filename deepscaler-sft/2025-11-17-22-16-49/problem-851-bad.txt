First, let's start by calculating the volume of milk in the hemispherical cup.

The formula for the volume of a hemisphere is:
\[ V_{\text{hemisphere}} = \frac{2}{3} \pi r^3 \]
where \( r \) is the radius of the hemisphere. Here, the radius \( r \) is 3 inches. So, we can substitute this value in to get:
\[ V_{\text{hemisphere}} = \frac{2}{3} \pi (3)^3 = \frac{2}{3} \pi \cdot 27 = 18 \pi \]

Next, we need to determine the minimum height of a right cylindrical container that has a radius of 2 inches and can hold this volume of milk. The formula for the volume of a cylinder is:
\[ V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi r^2 h \]
where \( r \) is the radius of the cylinder and \( h \) is the height of the cylinder. We know the volume of milk from the hemisphere is \( 18 \pi \), and the radius of the cylinder is 2 inches, so we substitute these values in:
\[ 18 \pi = \pi (2)^2 h = \pi \cdot 4 \cdot h = 4 \pi h \]

We can solve for \( h \) by dividing both sides of the equation by \( 4 \pi \):
\[ 18 \pi = 4 \pi h \implies 18 = 4 h \implies h = \frac{18}{4} = \frac{9}{2} = 4.5 \]

So, the minimum height of the cylindrical container to hold all of the milk is \( 4.5 \) inches. Let's confirm this by coding it to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given values
radius_hemisphere = 3
radius_cylinder = 2
volume_hemisphere = (2/3) * sp.pi * radius_hemisphere**3

# Volume of the cylinder, set up the equation to solve for height 'h'
h = sp.symbols('h', positive=True)
volume_cylinder = sp.pi * radius_cylinder**2 * h

# Set the volume of the hemisphere equal to the volume of the cylinder and solve for h
solution = sp.solve(volume_hemisphere - volume_cylinder, h)[0]
print(solution.evalf())
```
