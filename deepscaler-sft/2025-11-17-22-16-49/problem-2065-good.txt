To solve the given problem, we need to determine the values of \(A\) and \(B\) that satisfy the equation:

\[
\frac{A}{x-5} + B(x+1) = \frac{-3x^2 + 12x + 22}{x-5}
\]

Let's start by isolating \(\frac{A}{x-5}\) on the left side:

\[
\frac{A}{x-5} + B(x+1) = \frac{-3x^2 + 12x + 22}{x-5}
\]

First, let's get a common denominator on the left side of the equation:

\[
\frac{A}{x-5} + B(x+1) = \frac{A + B(x+1)(x-5)}{x-5}
\]

So, we can equate the numerators:

\[
A + B(x+1)(x-5) = -3x^2 + 12x + 22
\]

Now, let's expand \(B(x+1)(x-5)\):

\[
B(x+1)(x-5) = B(x^2 - 5x + x - 5) = B(x^2 - 4x - 5)
\]

So, we get:

\[
A + B(x^2 - 4x - 5) = -3x^2 + 12x + 22
\]

This is a polynomial equation, and for it to hold for all values of \(x\), the coefficients of corresponding powers of \(x\) on both sides must be equal. Therefore, we can compare the coefficients of \(x^2\), \(x\), and the constant terms:

1. The coefficient of \(x^2\) on the left side is 0 (from \(A\)), and on the right side, it is \(-3\):
   \[
   B = -3
   \]

2. The coefficient of \(x\) on the left side is \(-4B\), and on the right side, it is \(12\):
   \[
   -4B = 12
   \]
   Substituting \(B = -3\) into this equation, we get:
   \[
   -4(-3) = 12 \implies 12 = 12
   \]
   This is true, so our value for \(B\) is consistent with this coefficient.

3. The constant term on the left side is \(A - 5B\), and on the right side, it is \(22\):
   \[
   A - 5B = 22
   \]
   Substituting \(B = -3\) into this equation, we get:
   \[
   A - 5(-3) = 22 \implies A + 15 = 22 \implies A = 7
   \]

So, we have \(A = 7\) and \(B = -3\). Therefore, \(A + B = 7 + (-3) = 4\).

Let's verify this solution by substituting back into the original equation to ensure it holds. We'll use Python to make sure our result is accurate.
