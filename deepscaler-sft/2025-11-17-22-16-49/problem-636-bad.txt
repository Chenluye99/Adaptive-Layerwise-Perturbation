When we rotate point \(A\) (which has coordinates \((0, 10)\) initially, but its exact coordinates will be derived based on the distance \(DA = 17\) and the angle) around \(D\) by \(60^\circ\) counterclockwise, we get the coordinates of \(A\).

\[
A = (17 \cos(60^\circ), 17 \sin(60^\circ))
