To solve this problem, we need to find the distance between two parallel lines in the 2D plane. The given lines are:

1. \(\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix} + t \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\)
2. \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix} + s \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\)

Both lines have the same direction vector \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\), so they are parallel. To find the distance between two parallel lines, we can use the formula for the distance between a point on one line and the other line:

\[ \text{Distance} = \frac{| \mathbf{n} \cdot (\mathbf{p}_2 - \mathbf{p}_1) |}{\| \mathbf{n} \|} \]

where \(\mathbf{n}\) is a normal vector to the direction vector of the lines, \(\mathbf{p}_1\) and \(\mathbf{p}_2\) are points on the first and second lines, respectively.

First, we need to find a normal vector to the direction vector \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\). A normal vector to \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\) is \(\begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\) (since the dot product of \(\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(\begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\) is \(1 \cdot 7 + (-7) \cdot 1 = 7 - 7 = 0\)).

Next, take points \(\mathbf{p}_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}\) on the first line and \(\mathbf{p}_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix}\) on the second line. The vector between these points is:

\[
\mathbf{p}_2 - \mathbf{p}_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 - 2 \\ -5 - (-3) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}
\]

Now, we use the normal vector \(\mathbf{n} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\) and the vector \(\mathbf{p}_2 - \mathbf{p}_1 = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\):

1. Compute the dot product \(\mathbf{n} \cdot (\mathbf{p}_2 - \mathbf{p}_1)\):

\[
\mathbf{n} \cdot (\mathbf{p}_2 - \mathbf{p}_1) = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} = 7 \cdot (-1) + 1 \cdot (-2) = -7 - 2 = -9
\]

2. Compute the magnitude of \(\mathbf{n}\):

\[
\| \mathbf{n} \| = \sqrt{7^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{49 + 1} = \sqrt{50} = 5 \sqrt{2}
\]

3. Finally, compute the distance:

\[
\text{Distance} = \frac{| -9 |}{5 \sqrt{2}} = \frac{9}{5 \sqrt{2}} = \frac{9 \sqrt{2}}{10}
\]
