To determine Dave's average speed for the entire ride, we need to use the formula for average speed, which is the total distance traveled divided by the total time taken.

1. **Calculate the total distance traveled:**
    - Dave rode 30 miles at 10 miles per hour.
    - Dave also rode 10 miles at 30 miles per hour.
    - So, the total distance \(D_{\text{total}}\) is:
      \[
      D_{\text{total}} = 30 + 10 = 40 \text{ miles}
      \]

2. **Calculate the time taken for each part of the trip:**
    - The time taken to ride 30 miles at 10 miles per hour is:
      \[
      T_1 = \frac{30 \text{ miles}}{10 \text{ miles/hour}} = 3 \text{ hours}
      \]
    - The time taken to ride 10 miles at 30 miles per hour is:
      \[
      T_2 = \frac{10 \text{ miles}}{30 \text{ miles/hour}} = \frac{1}{3} \text{ hours}
      \]

3. **Calculate the total time taken for the entire ride:**
    - The total time \(T_{\text{total}}\) is:
      \[
      T_{\text{total}} = T_1 + T_2 = 3 + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{9}{3} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3} \text{ hours}
      \]

4. **Calculate the average speed:**
    - The average speed \(V_{\text{avg}}\) is given by:
      \[
      V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{D_{\text{total}}}{T_{\text{total}}} = \frac{40 \text{ miles}}{\frac{10}{3} \text{ hours}} = 40 \times \frac{3}{10} = 12 \text{ miles/hour}
      \]

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure our calculations are accurate.
```python
# Given values
distance1 = 30  # miles
speed1 = 10     # miles per hour

distance2 = 10  # miles
speed2 = 30     # miles per hour

# Calculate the total distance
total_distance = distance1 + distance2

# Calculate the time taken for each part of the trip
time1 = distance1 / speed1
time2 = distance2 / speed2

# Calculate the total time taken
total_time = time1 + time2

# Calculate the average speed
average_speed = total_distance / total_time
print(average_speed)
```

Thus, the average speed for Dave's entire ride is \(\boxed{12}\) miles per hour. This confirms our calculations are correct.
