2. **Properties of a Tree:**
    - In any tree with \( n \) vertices (cities), the number of edges (roads) is \( n - 1 \). So, our tree has 2010 cities, meaning it has \( 2009 \) roads.
    - In a tree, the sum of the degrees of all vertices is equal to twice the number of edges. Therefore, if we let \( d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_{2010} \) be the degrees of the vertices (cities), we have:
      \[
      d_1 + d_2 + \cdots + d_{2010} = 2 \times 2009 = 4018
      \]

- \( d_{k+1}, d_{k+2}, \ldots, d_{2010} \) as the degrees of the remaining \( 2010 - k \) cities, each with \( 1 \leq d_i \leq 2 \).
