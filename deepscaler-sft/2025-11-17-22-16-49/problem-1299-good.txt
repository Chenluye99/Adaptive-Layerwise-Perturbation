To solve the problem, we need to find the equation of the directrix of the parabola given by the equation \( y = x^2 \).

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Understand the Equation:**
    - The equation \( y = x^2 \) is in the standard form of a parabola that opens upwards, which is \( y = \frac{1}{4p} x^2 \). By comparing these, we can see that \( \frac{1}{4p} = 1 \), so \( 4p = 1 \) and thus \( p = \frac{1}{4} \).

2. **Directrix of a Parabola:**
    - For a parabola in the form \( y = \frac{1}{4p} x^2 \), the directrix is the line given by \( y = -p \).
    - Since we have determined that \( p = \frac{1}{4} \), the equation of the directrix is \( y = -\frac{1}{4} \).

Let's confirm this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python

p = 1 / 4

# The directrix of the parabola y = (1 / (4 * p)) * x^2 is y = -p
directrix = -p
print(directrix)
```
