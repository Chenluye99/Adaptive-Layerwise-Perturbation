    - A triangular prism has:

        - 9 edges.

2. **Adding a Pyramid to a Face of the Prism:**
    - A pyramid in general has a base and several triangular faces connecting the base to a common vertex (apex).
    - If we use one of the triangular faces of the prism as the base of the pyramid, the pyramid will have:
        - 1 base which is shared with the prism (so it doesn't add any new faces to the fusion if it’s counted as part of the prism's face),
        - 3 new triangular faces because a triangular base will connect to 3 vertices of the new apex, 
        - 1 new vertex (apex of the pyramid).

    - Therefore, the pyramid contributes:
        - 3 new faces,
        - 1 new vertex,
        - 3 new edges.

3. **Resulting Shape After Fusion:**

        - Edges: The original prism has 9 edges, and adding the pyramid will add 3 new edges, so it will have \(9 + 3 = 12\) edges in total.

4. **Computing the Sum:**
