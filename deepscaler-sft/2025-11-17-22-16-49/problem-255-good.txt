To solve the problem, we will follow these steps:

1. Generate the sequence of numbers from 2 to 420 with a step of 2.
2. Iterate through all pairs of the generated sequence.
3. Check if the sum of each pair of numbers is divisible by 7.
4. Count how many such pairs exist.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python.
```python
# Step 1: Generate the sequence of numbers from 2 to 420 with a step of 2
sequence = list(range(2, 421, 2))

# Step 2 and 3: Iterate through all pairs and check the condition
count = 0

for i in range(len(sequence)):
    for j in range(i + 1, len(sequence)):
        if (sequence[i] + sequence[j]) % 7 == 0:
            count += 1

print(count)
```
