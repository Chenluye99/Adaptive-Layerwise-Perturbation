To solve this problem, let's consider the possible outcomes and their associated probabilities and winnings.

1. **Deck Composition and Probabilities:**
    - A standard 52-card deck consists of 4 suits (hearts, diamonds, clubs, spades).
    - Each suit has 13 cards: Ace, 2, 3, ..., 10, Jack, Queen, King.
    - So, there are 4 Aces, 4 each of the 2 through 10, 4 Jacks, 4 Queens, and 4 Kings.

2. **Outcomes and Winnings:**
    - **Ace:**
        - You get 1 dollar.
        - There are 4 Aces in the deck.
        - Probability of drawing an Ace: \( \frac{4}{52} = \frac{1}{13} \).
    - **Cards 2 through 10:**
        - You win a number of dollars equal to the value of the card.
        - There are 9 cards each for 2 through 10, so there are 4 cards each of 2 through 10.
        - Probability of drawing a card from 2 through 10: \( \frac{4 \times 9}{52} = \frac{36}{52} = \frac{9}{13} \).
    - **Face Cards (Jack, Queen, King):**
        - You win 20 dollars.
        - There are 3 face cards per suit, so there are 4 Jacks, 4 Queens, and 4 Kings.
        - Probability of drawing a face card: \( \frac{4 \times 3}{52} = \frac{12}{52} = \frac{3}{13} \).

3. **Additional Effects Based on Suit:**
    - If you draw a club (\clubsuit), your winnings are doubled.
    - If you draw a spade (\spadesuit), your winnings are tripled.

Let's enumerate and calculate all possible scenarios considering these conditions. We can use Python to precisely calculate the expected value of the game.
