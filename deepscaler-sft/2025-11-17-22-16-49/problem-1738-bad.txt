From the triangle given, we know the following:

1. Angle \( \angle X PY = 45^\circ \), so triangle \( \triangle XYZ \) is a right isosceles triangle because any right triangle with a 45° angle is isosceles (the two non-right angles must be 45° each, because the sum of angles in a triangle is 180°).
2. In a right isosceles triangle, the two legs are of equal length and the hypotenuse is \( \sqrt{2} \) times the length of a leg.

Given that \( XZ = 12 \sqrt{2} \), we can set up the relationship for the legs of the isosceles right triangle. Let the length of each leg (i.e., \( XY \) and \( YZ \)) be \( a \). Then, the hypotenuse of this triangle \( XZ \) is given by:

\[ XZ = a \sqrt{2} \]

We know that:

\[ a \sqrt{2} = 12 \sqrt{2} \]

We can solve for \( a \) by dividing both sides of the equation by \( \sqrt{2} \):

\[ a = 12 \]
