To solve the problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Find the area of the equilateral triangle**:
   - The formula for the area \(A\) of an equilateral triangle with side length \(s\) is given by:
     \[
     A = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} s^2
     \]
   - For our equilateral triangle with side length \(s = 8\):
     \[
     A = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 8^2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 64 = 16 \sqrt{3}
     \]

2. **Find the perimeter of the equilateral triangle**:
   - The formula for the perimeter \(P\) of an equilateral triangle with side length \(s\) is given by:
     \[
     P = 3s
     \]
   - For our equilateral triangle with side length \(s = 8\):
     \[
     P = 3 \cdot 8 = 24
     \]

3. **Find the ratio of the area to the perimeter**:
   - We need to find the ratio of the numerical value of the area to the numerical value of the perimeter:
     \[
     \text{Ratio} = \frac{A}{P} = \frac{16 \sqrt{3}}{24} = \frac{16 \sqrt{3}}{24} = \frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{3}
     \]

