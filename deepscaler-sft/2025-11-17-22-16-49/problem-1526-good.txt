To solve the problem of finding one third of 5.4 and expressing it as a simplified fraction, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate One Third of 5.4:**
   - First, we need to find one third of 5.4. Mathematically, this is given by \(\frac{5.4}{3}\).
   - Performing the division, we get \(\frac{5.4}{3} = 1.8\).

2. **Express 1.8 as a Fraction:**
   - The decimal 1.8 can be expressed as a fraction. We know that \(1.8 = \frac{18}{10}\).
   - To simplify \(\frac{18}{10}\), we find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 18 and 10, which is 2. Dividing both the numerator and the denominator by 2, we get \(\frac{18 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = \frac{9}{5}\).

3. **Conclusion:**
   - The simplified fraction form of one third of 5.4 is \(\frac{9}{5}\).

Let's confirm this by using Python to ensure our steps and result are accurate.
```python
import math

# Step 1: Calculate one third of 5.4
one_third_of_5_4 = 5.4 / 3

# Step 2: Express 1.8 as a fraction and simplify it
numerator = 18
denominator = 10

# Get the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 18 and 10
gcd = math.gcd(numerator, denominator)

# Simplify the fraction by dividing by the GCD
simplified_numerator = numerator // gcd
simplified_denominator = denominator // gcd

print(((one_third_of_5_4, (simplified_numerator, simplified_denominator))))
```
Code execution result: (1.8, (9, 5))

This confirms that one third of 5.4 is \(1.8\), and as a simplified fraction, it is \(\frac{9}{5}\).

Thus, the final answer is \(\boxed{\frac{9}{5}}\). This is the accurate and simplified fraction form of the answer.
