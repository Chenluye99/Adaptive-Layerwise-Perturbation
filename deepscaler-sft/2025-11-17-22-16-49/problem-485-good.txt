First, let's start by understanding the problem. We have a cylinder with a height of 2, and the circumferences of its two bases lie on the surface of a sphere with a diameter of \(2\sqrt{6}\), which means the radius of the sphere is \(\sqrt{6}\).

When the circumferences of the cylinder's bases lie on the surface of the sphere, we can form a right circular cylinder inscribed in a sphere. The distance from the center of the sphere to the center of the top or bottom base of the cylinder is \(\sqrt{6^2 - r^2}\), where \(r\) is the radius of the cylinder's base. The height of the cylinder is 2, so half of the height is 1, and it connects the centers of the top and bottom bases of the cylinder.

We can use the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle formed by the following:
1. The radius of the sphere (\(\sqrt{6}\)).
2. The radius of the base of the cylinder (\(r\)).
3. Half the height of the cylinder (1).

So, the relationship can be written as:
\[
(\sqrt{6})^2 = r^2 + 1^2
\]
This simplifies to:
\[
6 = r^2 + 1
\]
Subtracting 1 from both sides, we get:
\[
5 = r^2
\]
Thus:
\[
r = \sqrt{5}
\]

Next, we need to calculate the surface area of the cylinder. The surface area \(A\) of a cylinder is given by the formula:
\[
A = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h
\]
where:
- \(r\) is the radius of the base of the cylinder,
- \(h\) is the height of the cylinder.

In our case, \(r = \sqrt{5}\) and \(h = 2\). Substituting these values into the formula, we get:
\[
A = 2\pi (\sqrt{5})^2 + 2\pi (\sqrt{5}) (2)
\]
\[
A = 2\pi \cdot 5 + 2\pi \cdot 2 \cdot \sqrt{5}
\]
\[
A = 10\pi + 4\pi \sqrt{5}
\]

We can now confirm this by coding it in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the radius and height
r = sp.sqrt(5)
h = 2

# Calculate the surface area of the cylinder
surface_area = 2 * sp.pi * r**2 + 2 * sp.pi * r * h
print(surface_area)
```
Code execution result: 4*sqrt(5)*pi + 10*pi

This is the same expression we derived, confirming that our solution is correct. So, the surface area of the cylinder is:

\(\boxed{4\sqrt{5}\pi + 10\pi}\)
