    - For \( p(q(x)) \) to be defined, the expression inside the square root must be non-negative, i.e., \( -(8x^2 + 10x - 3) \geq 0 \).
    - This is equivalent to \( 8x^2 + 10x - 3 \leq 0 \).

    - We can use the quadratic formula \( x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \) where \( a = 8 \), \( b = 10 \), and \( c = -3 \).
