To solve this problem, we need to determine the probability that all three individuals (the boss, and the two engineers) arrive at Starbucks such that the meeting occurs. To do this, we'll use a Monte Carlo simulation approach, which involves simulating the arrival times of the boss, and the two engineers, and then checking under what conditions the meeting takes place.

### Problem Breakdown:

1. **Time Constraints:**
    - The boss, engineer 1, and engineer 2 all arrive at a random time between 2:00 and 4:00 p.m., i.e., in the 120-minute interval from 14:00 to 16:00.
    - Each engineer is willing to wait for an hour after their arrival, but if the other engineer has not arrived by that time, they will leave.

2. **Conditions for the Meeting to Occur:**
    - The boss must arrive after both engineers have already arrived, or both engineers must still be waiting for each other, or one engineer must still be waiting for the other if both are present at the time the boss arrives.
    - Both engineers need to still be there when the boss arrives; if either of the engineers has already left by the time the boss arrives, the meeting is canceled.

### Simulation Steps:

1. **Setting up the Simulation:**
    - We will simulate the arrival times of the boss, engineer 1, and engineer 2, each uniformly distributed between 0 and 120 minutes past 2:00 p.m.
    - For each set of arrival times, we need to check if the meeting takes place based on the conditions mentioned above.

2. **Check Conditions:**
    - Engineer 1 will leave if engineer 2 has not arrived within 1 hour after engineer 1's arrival time.
    - Engineer 2 will leave if engineer 1 has not arrived within 1 hour after engineer 2's arrival time.
    - The boss will cancel the meeting if either engineer has already left by the time he arrives, or if both engineers have not arrived by the time the boss arrives.

3. **Run Multiple Simulations:**
    - Run a large number of simulations (e.g., 1,000,000) to estimate the probability that the meeting takes place.

import numpy as np

def simulate_meeting():
    count_successful_meetings = 0
    
    num_simulations = 1000000
    
    for _ in range(num_simulations):
        # Arrival times in minutes past 2:00 p.m.
        boss_arrival = np.random.uniform(0, 120)
        eng1_arrival = np.random.uniform(0, 120)
        eng2_arrival = np.random.uniform(0, 120)
        
        # Determine if the engineers are still there when the boss arrives
        if (eng1_arrival <= boss_arrival <= eng1_arrival + 60) and (eng2_arrival <= boss_arrival <= eng2_arrival + 60):
