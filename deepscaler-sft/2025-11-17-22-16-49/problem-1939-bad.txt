\[ y = \frac{4x - 16}{3x - 4} \]

Then, the given equation

\[ \left( \frac{4x - 16}{3x - 4} \right)^2 + \left( \frac{4x - 16}{3x - 4} \right) = 12 \]

becomes

\[ y^2 + y = 12. \]

This can be rewritten as

\[ y^2 + y - 12 = 0. \]

We can solve this quadratic equation using the quadratic formula \( y = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \), where \( a = 1 \), \( b = 1 \), and \( c = -12 \):

\[ y = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot (-12)}}{2 \cdot 1} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 48}}{2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{49}}{2} = \frac{-1 \pm 7}{2}. \]

This gives us two solutions:

\[ y = \frac{6}{2} = 3, \]
\[ y = \frac{-8}{2} = -4. \]

So, we have:

\[ \frac{4x - 16}{3x - 4} = 3, \]
\[ \frac{4x - 16}{3x - 4} = -4. \]

We will solve each of these equations separately for \( x \).

\[ 4x - 16 = 3 (3x - 4), \]
\[ 4x - 16 = 9x - 12, \]
\[ 4x - 9x = -12 + 16, \]
\[ -5x = 4, \]
\[ x = -\frac{4}{5}. \]

\[ 4x - 16 = -4 (3x - 4), \]
\[ 4x - 16 = -12x + 16, \]
