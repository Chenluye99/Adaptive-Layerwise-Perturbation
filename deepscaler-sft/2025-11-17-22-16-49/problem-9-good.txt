1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - The function is given by \( f(x) = a^2 \sin 2x + (a - 2) \cos 2x \).
    - The function is symmetric with respect to the line \( x = -\frac{\pi}{8} \). For a function to be symmetric with respect to a vertical line, the value of the function at points equidistant from the line of symmetry must be the same. Mathematically, this means that \( f\left( -\frac{\pi}{8} + t \right) = f\left( -\frac{\pi}{8} - t \right) \) for all \( t \).

2. **Find the Condition for Symmetry:**
    - To check this, substitute \( x = -\frac{\pi}{8} + t \) and \( x = -\frac{\pi}{8} - t \) into \( f(x) \):
      \[
      f\left( -\frac{\pi}{8} + t \right) = a^2 \sin \left( 2 \left( -\frac{\pi}{8} + t \right) \right) + (a - 2) \cos \left( 2 \left( -\frac{\pi}{8} + t \right) \right)
      \]
      \[
      f\left( -\frac{\pi}{8} - t \right) = a^2 \sin \left( 2 \left( -\frac{\pi}{8} - t \right) \right) + (a - 2) \cos \left( 2 \left( -\frac{\pi}{8} - t \right) \right)
      \]
    - For the function to be symmetric with respect to \( x = -\frac{\pi}{8} \), the above two expressions must be equal for all \( t \). This condition will give us constraints on \( a \).

3. **Simplify and Solve for \( a \):**

    - For these two expressions to be equal for all \( t \), the coefficients of \(\sin(2t)\) and \(\cos(2t)\) must independently be equal. This means we can equate the coefficients of \(\sin(2t)\) and \(\cos(2t)\) from both expressions and solve for \( a \).
