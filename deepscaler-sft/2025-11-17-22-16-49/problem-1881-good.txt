1. **Angle Between Two Vectors:**
   The angle \(\theta\) between two vectors \(\mathbf{u}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\) can be found using the dot product formula:
   \[
   \cos \theta = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\| \|\mathbf{v}\|}
   \]
   where \(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}\) is the dot product of \(\mathbf{u}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\), and \(\|\mathbf{u}\|\) and \(\|\mathbf{v}\|\) are the magnitudes (or lengths) of \(\mathbf{u}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\), respectively.

2. **Dot Product:**
   The dot product \(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}\) is given by:
   \[
   \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = a \cdot b + b \cdot c + c \cdot a = ab + bc + ca
   \]

3. **Magnitudes of the Vectors:**
   The magnitudes of \(\mathbf{u}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\) are:
   \[
   \|\mathbf{u}\| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}
   \]
   \[
   \|\mathbf{v}\| = \sqrt{b^2 + c^2 + a^2} = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}
   \]
   (The magnitudes of \(\mathbf{u}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\) are the same because they have the same components, just in different orders.)

4. **Substitute into Cosine Formula:**
   Substituting the dot product and magnitudes into the formula, we get:
   \[
   \cos \theta = \frac{ab + bc + ca}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2} \sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}} = \frac{ab + bc + ca}{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}
   \]
   Hence,
   \[
   \cos \theta = \frac{ab + bc + ca}{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}
   \]
   To find the maximum angle \(\theta\), we need to minimize \(\cos \theta\), because the minimum value of \(\cos \theta\) will correspond to the maximum value of \(\theta\).
