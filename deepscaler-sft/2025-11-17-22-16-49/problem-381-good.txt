First, let's start by understanding the problem. To find the area of the square, we need to determine the side length of the square. The side length of the square is the distance between the given adjacent vertices.

The distance \(d\) between two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is given by the formula:

\[ d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} \]

For the points \((-1, 4)\) and \((2, -3)\), we can substitute these values into the formula:

\[ d = \sqrt{(2 - (-1))^2 + (-3 - 4)^2} = \sqrt{(2 + 1)^2 + (-3 - 4)^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + (-7)^2} = \sqrt{9 + 49} = \sqrt{58} \]

So, the distance between the points \((-1, 4)\) and \((2, -3)\) is \(\sqrt{58}\). This distance is the length of one of the sides of the square.

The area \(A\) of the square is given by the side length squared:

\[ A = (\sqrt{58})^2 = 58 \]

Let's verify this with Python to ensure the accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Coordinates of the points
x1, y1 = -1, 4
x2, y2 = 2, -3

# Calculate the distance between the points
distance = sp.sqrt((x2 - x1)**2 + (y2 - y1)**2)

# Calculate the area of the square
area = distance ** 2
print(area)
```
