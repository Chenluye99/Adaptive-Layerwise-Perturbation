Let's start by determining the relationship between the number of workers and the time it takes to complete the task. The key idea here is that the total amount of work required to build a house remains constant regardless of the number of workers.

1. **Determine the Total Work Required:**
    - Six construction workers can build a house in 1.5 days.
    - The total amount of work required to build a house can be thought of as "worker-days". Hence, six workers working for 1.5 days will contribute a total of \(6 \times 1.5 = 9\) worker-days of work to complete the house.

2. **Find the Work Rate per Worker:**
    - This means that it takes 9 worker-days to build a house.

3. **Determine the Time for 15 Workers:**
    - Let's say it takes \( T \) days for 15 workers to build the house.
    - The total amount of work done by 15 workers in \( T \) days would be \( 15 \times T \) worker-days.
    - Since the total amount of work required to build the house is 9 worker-days, we can set up the equation:
      \[
      15 \times T = 9
      \]
    - Solving for \( T \):
      \[
      T = \frac{9}{15} = \frac{3}{5}
      \]

Let's confirm this step-by-step process with Python to ensure our solution is accurate.
```python
# Given: 6 workers can build a house in 1.5 days
workers_6 = 6
days_6_workers = 1.5

# Calculate total work in worker-days required to build a house
total_worker_days = workers_6 * days_6_workers

# Now we want to find out how long it will take for 15 workers to complete the same amount of work
workers_15 = 15

# Let T be the number of days it takes for 15 workers to complete the work
# We know that 15 * T = total_worker_days
T = total_worker_days / workers_15

print(T)
```
Code execution result: 0.6

We know that \(0.6 = \frac{3}{5}\). Thus, it takes \(\frac{3}{5}\) days for 15 construction workers to finish building a house.

So, the final answer is:

\(\boxed{\frac{3}{5}}\)
