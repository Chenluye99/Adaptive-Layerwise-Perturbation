- The radius and height of cylinder \( D \) as \( r_D \) and \( h_D \) respectively.

   \[
   h_C = 2 r_D

2. The diameter of cylinder \( C \) is equal to the height \( h \) of cylinder \( D \), so:
   \[
   2 r_C = h_D = h
   \]
   This implies:
   \[
   r_C = \frac{h}{2}
   \]
   and
   \[
   h_D = h
   \]

The volume of a cylinder is given by the formula:
\[
V = \pi r^2 h
\]

\[
V_C = \pi r_C^2 h_C = \pi \left( \frac{h}{2} \right)^2 (2 r_D) = \pi \cdot \frac{h^2}{4} \cdot 2 r_D = \frac{\pi h^2}{2} r_D
\]

And the volume of cylinder \( D \) is:
\[
V_D = \pi r_D^2 h_D = \pi r_D^2 \cdot h
\]

We are given that the volume of cylinder \( D \) is three times the volume of cylinder \( C \):
