To solve the problem, we first need to determine the directrix of the parabola given by the equation \( y = -2x^2 + 4x - 8 \).

1. **Convert the Equation to Vertex Form:**
   The given equation is in the standard form \( y = ax^2 + bx + c \). To find the directrix, it is often easier to convert this equation to vertex form \( y = a(x - h)^2 + k \), where \((h, k)\) is the vertex of the parabola.

2. **Find the Vertex:**
   The x-coordinate of the vertex of a parabola given by \( y = ax^2 + bx + c \) can be found using the formula:
   \[
   h = -\frac{b}{2a}
   \]
   For the equation \( y = -2x^2 + 4x - 8 \), we have \( a = -2 \), \( b = 4 \), and \( c = -8 \). Substituting these values into the formula, we get:
   \[
   h = -\frac{4}{2 \cdot (-2)} = -\frac{4}{-4} = 1
   \]
   To find the y-coordinate of the vertex, we substitute \( x = 1 \) back into the original equation:
   \[
   y = -2(1)^2 + 4(1) - 8 = -2 + 4 - 8 = -6
   \]
   So, the vertex of the parabola is \((1, -6)\).

3. **Convert to Vertex Form:**
   We can now rewrite the equation in vertex form by completing the square. Starting with \( y = -2x^2 + 4x - 8 \):
   \[
   y = -2(x^2 - 2x) - 8
   \]
   To complete the square inside the parentheses, we take the coefficient of \( x \), which is \(-2\), halve it to get \(-1\), and then square it to get \(1\). We add and subtract this square inside the parentheses:
   \[
   y = -2(x^2 - 2x + 1 - 1) - 8 = -2((x - 1)^2 - 1) - 8 = -2(x - 1)^2 + 2 - 8 = -2(x - 1)^2 - 6
   \]
   So, the equation in vertex form is:
   \[
   y = -2(x - 1)^2 - 6
   \]
   This is now in the form \( y = a(x - h)^2 + k \), where \( a = -2 \), \( h = 1 \), and \( k = -6 \).

4. **Find the Directrix:**
   For a parabola given by \( y = a(x - h)^2 + k \), the directrix is a horizontal line given by the equation:
   \[
   y = k - \frac{1}{4a}
   \]
   Substituting \( a = -2 \) and \( k = -6 \) into this formula, we get:
   \[
   y = -6 - \frac{1}{4(-2)} = -6 - \frac{1}{-8} = -6 + \frac{1}{8} = -6 + \frac{1}{8} = -\frac{48}{8} + \frac{1}{8} = -\frac{47}{8}
   \]
   So, the equation of the directrix is:
   \[
   y = -\frac{47}{8}
   \]
