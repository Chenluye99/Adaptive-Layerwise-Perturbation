      \[

    - To find the radius of the sphere, we use the formula for the radius \(r_s\) of a sphere inscribed in a cone with base radius \(r\) and height \(h\):
      \[
      r_s = \frac{r \cdot h}{\sqrt{r^2 + h^2} + r}
      \]

      \[
      r_s = \frac{3 \cdot 4}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} + 3} = \frac{12}{\sqrt{9 + 16} + 3} = \frac{12}{\sqrt{25} + 3} = \frac{12}{5 + 3} = \frac{12}{8} = \frac{3}{2}

    - The volume \(V_{\text{sphere}}\) of the sphere is given by the formula:
      \[
      V_{\text{sphere}} = \frac{4}{3} \pi r_s^3 = \frac{4}{3} \pi \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{4}{3} \pi \frac{27}{8} = \frac{4 \cdot 27}{3 \cdot 8} \pi = \frac{108}{24} \pi = \frac{9}{2} \pi
