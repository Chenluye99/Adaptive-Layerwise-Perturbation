\[ x^5 - 1 = 0 \]

The solutions to this equation are:

\[ x = 1, \, r, \, r^2, \, r^3, \, r^4 \]

where \( r, \, r^2, \, r^3, \, r^4 \) are the non-trivial 5th roots of unity and they satisfy the relation:

\[ r^5 = 1 \]

\[ x^5 - 1 = (x - 1)(x - r)(x - r^2)(x - r^3)(x - r^4) \]

\[ (r - 1)(r^2 - 1)(r^3 - 1)(r^4 - 1) \]

import cmath
