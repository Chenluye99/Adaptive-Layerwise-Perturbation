1. **Start with the function:**
   \[
   y = 4 - 5x
   \]

2. **Swap \( x \) and \( y \):**
   To find the inverse, we interchange \( x \) and \( y \):
   \[
   x = 4 - 5y
   \]

3. **Solve for \( y \):**
   We need to isolate \( y \) on one side of the equation.
   \[
   x = 4 - 5y
   \]
   Subtract 4 from both sides:
   \[
   x - 4 = -5y
   \]
   Divide both sides by -5:
   \[
   y = \frac{4 - x}{5}
   \]

4. **Write the Inverse Function:**
   The inverse function is:
   \[
   f^{-1}(x) = \frac{4 - x}{5}
   \]
