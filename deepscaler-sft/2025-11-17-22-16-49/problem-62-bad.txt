To solve this problem, let's start by understanding the constraints and requirements for a point to be called charming and for a line to be called strange.

1. **Strange Line Definition:**
    - A line is considered strange if it passes through the points \((a, 0)\) and \((0, 10 - a)\) for some \(a \in [0, 10]\).
    - The equation of a line passing through two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is given by:
      \[
      \frac{y - y_1}{x - x_1} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}
      \]
    - Substituting \((x_1, y_1) = (a, 0)\) and \((x_2, y_2) = (0, 10 - a)\), we get:
      \[
      \frac{y - 0}{x - a} = \frac{(10 - a) - 0}{0 - a} = \frac{10 - a}{-a} = \frac{a - 10}{a}
      \]
    - This simplifies to:
      \[
      y = \frac{a - 10}{a} (x - a)
      \]
    - Simplify further:
      \[
      y = \frac{a - 10}{a} x - \frac{a - 10}{a} \cdot a = \frac{a - 10}{a} x - (a - 10)
      \]
      \[
      y = \frac{a - 10}{a} x - a + 10
      \]
    - So, the equation of the line is:
      \[
      y = \frac{a - 10}{a} x - a + 10
      \]
