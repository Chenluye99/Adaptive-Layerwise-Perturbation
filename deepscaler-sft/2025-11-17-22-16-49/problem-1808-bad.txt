1. **Volume of the Sphere:**

    - The height of the cone is 4, and the radius of the base of the cone is 3. To find the radius of the inscribed sphere, we can use the formula for the radius \( r \) of a sphere inscribed in a cone with height \( h \) and base radius \( a \):
      \[
      r = \frac{a \cdot h}{\sqrt{a^2 + h^2} + h}
      \]
    - Substituting \( a = 3 \) and \( h = 4 \):
      \[
      r = \frac{3 \cdot 4}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} + 4} = \frac{12}{\sqrt{9 + 16} + 4} = \frac{12}{\sqrt{25} + 4} = \frac{12}{5 + 4} = \frac{12}{9} = \frac{4}{3}
      \]
    - The volume \( V_{\text{sphere}} \) of the sphere is given by:
      \[
      V_{\text{sphere}} = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3} \pi \left( \frac{4}{3} \right)^3 = \frac{4}{3} \pi \frac{64}{27} = \frac{256 \pi}{81}
      \]

2. **Volume of the Cone:**

      V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3} \pi a^2 h = \frac{1}{3} \pi (3)^2 (4) = \frac{1}{3} \pi \cdot 9 \cdot 4 = 12 \pi
      \]

3. **Find the Ratio:**
    - The ratio of the volume of the sphere to the volume of the cone is:
      \[
      \text{Ratio} = \frac{V_{\text{sphere}}}{V_{\text{cone}}} = \frac{\frac{256 \pi}{81}}{12 \pi} = \frac{256}{81 \cdot 12} = \frac{256}{972} = \frac{64}{243}
