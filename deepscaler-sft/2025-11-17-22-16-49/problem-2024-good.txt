1. **Properties of the Quadratic Equation:**
   The roots of the quadratic equation \(x^2 + Cx + D = 0\) are \(\tan A\) and \(\tan B\). Using Vieta's formulas, we have:
   \[
   \tan A + \tan B = -C \quad \text{(1)}
   \]
   \[
   \tan A \cdot \tan B = D \quad \text{(2)}
   \]

2. **Trigonometric Identity for \(\tan (A + B)\):**
   We know that:
   \[
   \tan (A + B) = \frac{\tan A + \tan B}{1 - \tan A \tan B}
   \]
   Substituting Vieta's formulas (1) and (2) into this identity, we get:
   \[
   \tan (A + B) = \frac{-C}{1 - D} = \frac{C}{D - 1}
   \]
