First, let's start by solving the quadratic equation \(2x^2 - 5x - 4 = 0\) using the quadratic formula:

The quadratic formula for a quadratic equation of the form \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) is given by:

\[
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
\]

For the equation \(2x^2 - 5x - 4 = 0\), we have \(a = 2\), \(b = -5\), and \(c = -4\). Plugging these values into the quadratic formula, we get:

\[
x = \frac{-(-5) \pm \sqrt{(-5)^2 - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot (-4)}}{2 \cdot 2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 + 32}}{4} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{57}}{4}
\]

This gives us the roots in the form:

\[
x = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{57}}{4}
\]

From this, we see that \(m = 5\), \(n = 57\), and \(p = 4\). The integers 5, 57, and 4 have a greatest common divisor (GCD) of 1, so the form \(x = \frac{m \pm \sqrt{n}}{p}\) is satisfied with these values.

Thus, the value of \(n\) is \(57\). Let's confirm this by coding the solution in Python to ensure the result is accurate.
