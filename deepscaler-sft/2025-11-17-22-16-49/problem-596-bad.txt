### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Form a 2020-gon and generate its vertices:**
   A 2020-gon has 2020 distinct vertices. These vertices can be labeled as \(0, 1, 2, \ldots, 2019\).

2. **Form all possible triangles:**
   The number of ways to choose 3 vertices from 2020 is given by the combination formula \(\binom{2020}{3}\):
   \[
   \binom{2020}{3} = \frac{2020 \cdot 2019 \cdot 2018}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1}
   \]

3. **Check when a triangle formed by three vertices is isosceles:**
   Three vertices \(i, j, k\) (with \(0 \leq i < j < k \leq 2019\)) form an isosceles triangle if at least two sides of the triangle formed by connecting these vertices are of the same length. In a 2020-gon, the length of a side is given by the minimum of the distance between the vertices and \(2020 - \text{distance}\). So, the lengths of the sides of the triangle formed by \(i, j, k\) will be \(|j - i| \mod 2020\), \(|k - j| \mod 2020\), and \(|k - i| \mod 2020\).

4. **Count isosceles triangles:**
   We need to check for each combination of three vertices if at least two of the side lengths are equal. We then count how many such combinations exist.

5. **Calculate Probability:**
   The probability that a randomly chosen triangle is isosceles is the ratio of the number of isosceles triangles to the total number of triangles:
   \[
   P(\text{Isosceles}) = \frac{\text{Number of Isosceles Triangles}}{\binom{2020}{3}}
   \]

6. **Express Probability in Fraction Form \(\frac{a}{b}\):**
   Once we have the probability in its simplest form \(\frac{a}{b}\), we need to find \(100a + b\).

Let's implement this step-by-step process in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math
from fractions import Fraction

# Total number of ways to choose 3 vertices out of 2020
total_triangles = math.comb(2020, 3)

# Count the number of isosceles triangles
isosceles_count = 0

for i in range(2020):
    for j in range(i+1, 2020):
        for k in range(j+1, 2020):
            side1 = min(abs(j - i), 2020 - abs(j - i))
            side2 = min(abs(k - j), 2020 - abs(k - j))
            side3 = min(abs(k - i), 2020 - abs(k - i))

                isosceles_count += 1
```
