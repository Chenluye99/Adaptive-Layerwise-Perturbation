1. **Understand the Asymptotes:**
    - The equations of the asymptotes of the hyperbola are given as \( y = 2x + 5 \) and \( y = -2x + 1 \).
    - For a hyperbola of the form \(\frac{(y - k)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(x - h)^2}{b^2} = 1\), the equations of the asymptotes are given by \( y - k = \pm \frac{a}{b} (x - h) \).
    - By comparing these, we can determine that \(\frac{a}{b} = 2\) and \(-\frac{a}{b} = -2\), which is consistent with the slopes of the given asymptotes. Hence, we have \( \frac{a}{b} = 2 \implies a = 2b \).

2. **Find the Center of the Hyperbola:**
    - The center of the hyperbola, \((h, k)\), is the intersection of the asymptotes. To find this, solve the system of equations formed by the asymptotes:
      \[
      y = 2x + 5,
      \]
      \[
      y = -2x + 1.
      \]
    - Set the right-hand sides equal to each other to solve for \( x \):
      \[
      2x + 5 = -2x + 1 \implies 4x = -4 \implies x = -1.
      \]
    - Substitute \( x = -1 \) back into either equation to solve for \( y \):
      \[
      y = 2(-1) + 5 = 3.
      \]
    - Thus, the center of the hyperbola is \((-1, 3)\), so \( h = -1 \) and \( k = 3 \).

3. **Form the Equation of the Hyperbola:**
    - Substitute \( h = -1 \) and \( k = 3 \) into the standard form of the hyperbola equation:
      \[
      \frac{(y - 3)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(x + 1)^2}{b^2} = 1.
      \]
    - We know that \( a = 2b \), so we substitute \( a^2 = (2b)^2 = 4b^2 \):
      \[
      \frac{(y - 3)^2}{4b^2} - \frac{(x + 1)^2}{b^2} = 1.
      \]

4. **Use the Point \((0, 7)\) to Find \( b \):**
    - The hyperbola passes through the point \((0, 7)\). Substitute \( x = 0 \) and \( y = 7 \) into the equation:
      \[
      \frac{(7 - 3)^2}{4b^2} - \frac{(0 + 1)^2}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{4^2}{4b^2} - \frac{1^2}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{16}{4b^2} - \frac{1}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{4}{b^2} - \frac{1}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{3}{b^2} = 1 \implies b^2 = 3.
      \]
    - Therefore, \( b = \sqrt{3} \) and \( a = 2b = 2\sqrt{3} \).

5. **Find \( a + h \):**
    - We have \( a = 2\sqrt{3} \) and \( h = -1 \), so:
      \[
      a + h = 2\sqrt{3} - 1.
