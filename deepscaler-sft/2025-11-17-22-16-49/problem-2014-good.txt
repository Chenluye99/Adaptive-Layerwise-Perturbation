To solve this problem, we need to determine the day of the week for the 284th day of the year 2003, given that the 25th day of the year 2003 falls on a Saturday.

1. **Determine the Date of the 25th Day of 2003:**
    - The 25th day of the year 2003 is January 25, 2003.
    - We know that January 25, 2003, is a Saturday.

2. **Find out the Date Corresponding to the 284th Day of 2003:**
    - The 284th day of the year 2003 would be the date in 2003 when we count 284 days from January 1, 2003.

3. **Calculate the Day of the Week for the 284th Day:**
    - To get to the 284th day, we need to count 284 - 25 = 259 days after January 25, 2003.
    - We can use Python to determine what day of the week the 284th day of 2003 falls on by using the `datetime` module.

Let's write some Python code to solve this step-by-step.
```python
import datetime

# Define the date for January 25, 2003
start_date = datetime.date(2003, 1, 25)

# Calculate the date for the 284th day of the year 2003
day_284 = datetime.date(2003, 1, 1) + datetime.timedelta(days=283)

# Get the name of the day for the 284th day of 2003
day_of_week = day_284.strftime("%A")
print(day_of_week)
```
Code execution result: Saturday

Thus, the 284th day of the year 2003 falls on a \(\boxed{\text{Saturday}}\). This completes the solution.
