To solve this problem, we need to determine the probability that Alex will arrive at the train station during the 10-minute window that the train is waiting.

1. **Problem Breakdown:**
    - The train arrives randomly any time between 1:00 and 2:00 (i.e., within a 60-minute window) and waits for 10 minutes after its arrival.
    - Alex also arrives randomly any time between 1:00 and 2:00 (i.e., within the same 60-minute window).
    - We need to find the probability that Alex will arrive during the 10-minute window when the train is present.

2. **Formulating a Solution:**
    - Let's define \( T \) as the time (in minutes past 1:00) that the train arrives.
    - The train will be at the station from \( T \) to \( T + 10 \).
    - Alex will arrive at some time \( A \) (also in minutes past 1:00) that is uniformly distributed between 0 and 60.

    We need to determine when Alex's arrival time \( A \) overlaps with the interval \([T, T + 10]\).

3. **Mathematical Formulation:**
    - For Alex and the train to meet, \( A \) must be in the interval \([T, T + 10]\).
    - This means \( T \leq A < T + 10 \).
    - We need to integrate over all possible values of \( T \) (from 0 to 60) and determine the fraction of time that there is an overlap for some \( A \).

4. **Setting Up the Integral:**
    - For a given \( T \), \( A \) can range from \( T \) to \( \min(T + 10, 60) \).
    - We need to consider two cases: 
        1. When \( T + 10 \leq 60 \) (i.e., \( T \leq 50 \)), \( A \) ranges from \( T \) to \( T + 10 \).
        2. When \( T + 10 > 60 \) (i.e., \( T > 50 \)), \( A \) ranges from \( T \) to 60.

    We will set up the integral over these ranges to get the total favorable outcomes and then divide by the total possible outcomes (which is a 60-minute window for both \( T \) and \( A \), so it is 60 * 60 = 3600 when considering each minute).

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
T, A = sp.symbols('T A')

# We will integrate over the range of possible values for T (from 0 to 60)
prob = 0

for t in range(60):
    start = t
    end = min(t + 10, 60)
    
    # For each T, integrate over the range where A can overlap with the train's presence time
    prob += (end - start) / 60 # We integrate over time intervals of size `end - start` and take the average probability over all T, considering a uniform distribution for A

prob = sp.Rational(prob, 60)

print(prob)
```
