There are \(12\) ranks left to choose from for each of the 2 cards, and we need to choose 2 cards from the 48 cards such that they come from different ranks. And for each of these 2 ranks, there are 4 choices for each card, so there are \(4 \times 4 = 16\) ways to choose 2 cards from these 2 ranks. Therefore, the number of ways to choose the 2 additional cards is \(66 \times 16 = 1056\).
