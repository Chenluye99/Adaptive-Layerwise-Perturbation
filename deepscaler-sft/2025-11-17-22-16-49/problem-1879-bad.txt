3. **Subtract \(\sqrt[4]{624}\) from \(\sqrt[4]{626}\):**

4. **Find the reciprocal of the result from the previous step:**
   - Compute \(\frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{626} - \sqrt[4]{624}}\)

5. **Find the closest integer to the result from the previous step.**

```python
import sympy as sp

# Step 1: Calculate 5^4 + 1 and 5^4 - 1
val1 = 5**4 + 1

fourth_root_val2 = val2 ** (1/4)

# Step 3: Subtract the fourth roots
difference = fourth_root_val1 - fourth_root_val2

# Step 4: Find the reciprocal of the difference
```
