To solve this problem, we need to determine what part of a wall Heidi can paint in 9 minutes if she can paint the entire wall in 45 minutes.

1. First, we need to find out Heidi's painting rate. Since she can paint the whole wall in 45 minutes, her painting rate is \(\frac{1 \text{ wall}}{45 \text{ minutes}} = \frac{1}{45} \text{ walls per minute}\).
2. Next, to find out how much of the wall she can paint in 9 minutes, we multiply her painting rate by 9:
   \[
   \text{Fraction of the wall painted in 9 minutes} = \frac{1}{45} \times 9 = \frac{9}{45} = \frac{1}{5}
   \]

Thus, Heidi can paint \(\frac{1}{5}\) of the wall in 9 minutes. Let's confirm this with Python code to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given values
time_to_paint_full_wall = 45  # in minutes
time_available = 9  # in minutes

# Calculate the fraction of the wall painted in the given time
fraction_painted = (time_available / time_to_paint_full_wall)

print(fraction_painted)
```
Code execution result: 0.2

This result, 0.2, is equivalent to the fraction \(\frac{1}{5}\). Therefore, the fractional part of the wall that Heidi can paint in 9 minutes is \(\boxed{\frac{1}{5}}\). The result has been confirmed by the code execution.
