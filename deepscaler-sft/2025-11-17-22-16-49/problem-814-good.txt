To solve this problem, let's follow these steps:

1. **Determine the Total Number of Arrangements:**
   The total number of ways to arrange 10 pieces of art is given by \(10!\) (10 factorial).

2. **Determine the Number of Favorable Arrangements:**
   To ensure that all three Escher prints are placed consecutively, we can treat the three Escher prints as a single "super piece." Therefore, instead of arranging 10 pieces, we are arranging 8 "pieces" (7 individual pieces plus 1 "super piece" that contains the 3 Escher prints).
   - There are 8! ways to arrange these 8 "pieces."
   - Within the "super piece," the 3 Escher prints can be arranged among themselves in 3! ways.

   So, the number of ways to arrange the 10 pieces of art such that the 3 Escher prints are consecutive is \(8! \times 3!\).

3. **Calculate the Probability:**
   The probability that all three Escher prints will be placed consecutively is given by the ratio of the number of favorable arrangements to the total number of arrangements:
   \[
   P(\text{all three Escher prints are consecutive}) = \frac{8! \times 3!}{10!}
   \]

Let's implement this using Python:
```python
import math

# Calculate factorial values
factorial_10 = math.factorial(10)
factorial_8 = math.factorial(8)
factorial_3 = math.factorial(3)

# Calculate the probability
numerator = factorial_8 * factorial_3
denominator = factorial_10
probability = numerator / denominator

print(probability)
```
