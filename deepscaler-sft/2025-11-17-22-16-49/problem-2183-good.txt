First, let's begin by computing the volume of one block of modeling clay and the volume of the cylindrical sculpture.

1. **Volume of One Block of Modeling Clay:**
   The dimensions of one block of modeling clay are 6 inches by 2 inches by 1 inch. The volume \( V_{\text{block}} \) of a rectangular prism is given by:
   \[
   V_{\text{block}} = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 6 \times 2 \times 1 = 12 \text{ cubic inches}
   \]

2. **Volume of the Cylindrical Sculpture:**
   The cylindrical sculpture has a height of 7 inches and a diameter of 4 inches, so its radius is 2 inches. The volume \( V_{\text{cylinder}} \) of a cylinder is given by:
   \[
   V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi \times \text{radius}^2 \times \text{height} = \pi \times 2^2 \times 7 = \pi \times 4 \times 7 = 28\pi \text{ cubic inches}
   \]

3. **Determine the Number of Blocks Needed:**
   To find out how many whole blocks of modeling clay are needed to make the cylindrical sculpture, we need to divide the volume of the cylindrical sculpture by the volume of one block of modeling clay and then round up to the nearest whole number because we can only use whole blocks of clay.

   \[
   \text{Number of blocks needed} = \left\lceil \frac{V_{\text{cylinder}}}{V_{\text{block}}} \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{28\pi}{12} \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{28 \times 3.14159}{12} \right\rceil \approx \left\lceil \frac{87.9646}{12} \right\rceil \approx \left\lceil 7.3304 \right\rceil = 8
   \]

Let's implement this in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Volume of one block of modeling clay
volume_block = 6 * 2 * 1

# Volume of the cylindrical sculpture
radius = 4 / 2
height = 7
volume_cylinder = math.pi * (radius ** 2) * height

# Calculate the number of blocks needed
num_blocks_needed = math.ceil(volume_cylinder / volume_block)
print(num_blocks_needed)
```
Code execution result: 8

Thus, the number of whole blocks of modeling clay needed to mold the cylindrical sculpture is \(\boxed{8}\). This is our final answer.
