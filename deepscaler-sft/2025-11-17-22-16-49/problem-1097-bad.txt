To solve the problem, we need to find the largest four-digit number in base-4 and then convert it to base-10.

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Identify the Largest Four-Digit Number in Base-4:**
    - A four-digit number in base-4 ranges from `1000_4` to `3333_4`.
    - The largest four-digit number in base-4 is `3333_4`.

2. **Convert the Base-4 Number to Base-10:**
    - To convert a number from base-4 to base-10, we use the formula:
      \[
      \text{digit}_3 \cdot 4^3 + \text{digit}_2 \cdot 4^2 + \text{digit}_1 \cdot 4^1 + \text{digit}_0 \cdot 4^0
      \]
    - For the number `3333_4`, the conversion to base-10 is:
      \[
      3 \cdot 4^3 + 3 \cdot 4^2 + 3 \cdot 4^1 + 3 \cdot 4^0
      \]
    - Calculate each term:
      \[
      3 \cdot 4^3 = 3 \cdot 64 = 192
      \]
      \[
      3 \cdot 4^2 = 3 \cdot 16 = 48
      \]
      \[
      3 \cdot 4^1 = 3 \cdot 4 = 12
      \]
      \[
      3 \cdot 4^0 = 3 \cdot 1 = 3
      \]
    - Sum these values to get the base-10 equivalent:
      \[
      192 + 48 + 12 + 3 = 255
      \]
