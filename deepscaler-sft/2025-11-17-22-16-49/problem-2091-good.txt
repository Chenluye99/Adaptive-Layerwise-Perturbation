To solve this problem, we need to count the number of ways to set the dials of the suitcase lock such that all four digits are different.

1. **Understanding the Problem:**
    - Each dial can have a digit from 0 to 9, so there are 10 choices for each dial.
    - We need to ensure that all four digits on the dials are different.
    - This is a permutation problem where we need to choose 4 different digits out of 10 and arrange them.

2. **Mathematical Formulation:**
    - The number of ways to choose 4 different digits from 10 and then arrange them is given by the permutation formula \( P(n, k) = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!} \), where \( n = 10 \) and \( k = 4 \).
    - This translates to calculating \( 10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \) because for the first digit, we have 10 choices, for the second digit, we have 9 choices (since it must be different from the first), for the third digit, we have 8 choices, and for the fourth digit, we have 7 choices.

3. **Implement the Calculation in Python:**
We will write a Python code to compute this product to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Calculate the number of permutations of choosing 4 different digits from 10
num_digits = 10
num_dials = 4

# This is equivalent to 10 * 9 * 8 * 7
num_settings = math.perm(num_digits, num_dials)
print(num_settings)
```
Code execution result: 5040

Thus, the number of different settings for the suitcase lock where all four digits are different is \(\boxed{5040}\). This ensures that all the steps are accurate and the result is reliable.
