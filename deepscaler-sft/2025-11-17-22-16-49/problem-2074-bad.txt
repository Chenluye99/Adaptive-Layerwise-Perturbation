1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have a frustum of a cone with an altitude of 24 cm.
    - The area of the lower base is \(225 \pi \) square centimeters. Therefore, the radius of the lower base (\(R\)) is given by:
      \[
      \pi R^2 = 225 \pi \implies R^2 = 225 \implies R = 15
      \]
    - The area of the upper base is \(25 \pi \) square centimeters. Therefore, the radius of the upper base (\(r\)) is given by:
      \[
      \pi r^2 = 25 \pi \implies r^2 = 25 \implies r = 5
      \]
    - When a smaller cone is cut off from the top to form the frustum, the smaller cone and the original larger cone are similar.

2. **Properties of Similar Cones:**
    - Let the altitude of the original larger cone be \(H\) and the altitude of the smaller cone that was cut off be \(x\).
    - Since the cones are similar, the ratios of the corresponding dimensions (height and radii) are the same. This means:
      \[
      \frac{r}{R} = \frac{x}{H - x}
      \]
    - Substituting the values of \(r\) and \(R\):
      \[
      \frac{5}{15} = \frac{x}{H - x} \implies \frac{1}{3} = \frac{x}{H - x}
      \]
    - Cross-multiply to get:
      \[
      1 \cdot (H - x) = 3 \cdot x \implies H - x = 3x \implies H = 4x
      \]
    - The altitude of the frustum is the difference in altitude between the larger cone and the smaller cone:
      \[
      H - x = 24
      \]
    - Substitute \( H = 4x \) into the above equation:
      \[
      4x - x = 24 \implies 3x = 24 \implies x = 8
      \]

3. **Conclusion:**
    - The altitude of the small cone that was cut off is \( x = 8 \).
