First, let's start by analyzing the equation:

\[ |{-2y}| \cdot \left|{-\frac{y}{2}}\right| = 36. \]

This can be simplified as follows:

1. \( |{-2y}| = |2y| = 2|y| \).
2. \( \left| -\frac{y}{2} \right| = \left| \frac{y}{-2} \right| = \frac{|y|}{2} \).

Substituting these into the equation, we get:

\[ 2|y| \cdot \frac{|y|}{2} = 36. \]

This simplifies to:

\[ |y| \cdot |y| = 36, \]

or

\[ |y|^2 = 36. \]

Taking the square root of both sides, we obtain:

\[ |y| = 6. \]

This means that:

\[ y = 6 \quad \text{or} \quad y = -6. \]

The possible values of \( y \) are 6 and -6. To find the product of all such real values of \( y \), we compute:

\[ 6 \cdot (-6) = -36. \]

Let's confirm this by coding the solution to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variable
y = sp.symbols('y', real=True)

# Define the equation
equation = sp.Eq(sp.Abs(-2 * y) * sp.Abs(-y / 2), 36)

# Solve the equation for y
solutions = sp.solve(equation, y)

# Calculate the product of all real values of y that satisfy the equation
product_of_solutions = sp.prod(solutions)
print((solutions, product_of_solutions))
```
Code execution result: ([-6, 6], -36)

This confirms that the solutions to the equation are \( y = 6 \) and \( y = -6 \), and the product of these solutions is \(-36\).

Thus, the final answer is:

\(\boxed{-36}\)
