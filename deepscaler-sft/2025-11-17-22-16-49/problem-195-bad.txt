To solve this problem, we need to calculate the area of both the equilateral triangle and the square and then determine what percent of the total area of the pentagon is the area of the equilateral triangle.

1. **Define the Side Length and Calculate Areas:**
    - Let the side length of both the square and the equilateral triangle be \( s \).
    - The area of the equilateral triangle with side length \( s \) is given by:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\triangle} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} s^2
      \]
    - The area of the square with side length \( s \) is:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\square} = s^2
      \]
    - The total area of the pentagon formed by placing the equilateral triangle on top of the square is the sum of the areas of the equilateral triangle and the square:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\text{pentagon}} = \text{Area}_{\triangle} + \text{Area}_{\square} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} s^2 + s^2 = \left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + 1 \right) s^2
      \]

2. **Calculate the Percentage:**
    - To find what percent of the area of the pentagon is the area of the equilateral triangle, we use the formula:
      \[
      \text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Area}_{\triangle}}{\text{Area}_{\text{pentagon}}} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} s^2}{\left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + 1 \right) s^2} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + 1} \right) \times 100
      \]
    - Let's simplify \(\frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + 1}\):
      \[
      \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + 1} = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}}{\frac{\sqrt{3} + 4}{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3} + 4}
      \]
    - Therefore, the percentage is:
      \[
      \text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3} + 4} \right) \times 100
      \]

Let's now implement this in Python to get the precise answer.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the expression for the percentage
numerator = sp.sqrt(3)
denominator = sp.sqrt(3) + 4
percentage = (numerator / denominator) * 100

print(percentage.evalf())
```
