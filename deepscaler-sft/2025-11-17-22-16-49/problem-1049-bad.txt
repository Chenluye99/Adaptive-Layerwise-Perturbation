To solve for \( n \) in the equation \( 5^{2n + 1} = \frac{1}{25} \), we can follow these steps:

1. First, we express \(\frac{1}{25}\) as a power of 5:
   \[
   \frac{1}{25} = 5^{-2}
   \]
   because \( 25 = 5^2 \), so \( \frac{1}{25} = 5^{-2} \).

2. Now, substitute this into the original equation:
   \[
   5^{2n + 1} = 5^{-2}
   \]

3. Since the bases are the same, we can set the exponents equal to each other:
   \[
   2n + 1 = -2
   \]

4. Solve the equation \( 2n + 1 = -2 \) for \( n \):
   \[
   2n + 1 = -2 \\
   2n = -2 - 1 \\
   2n = -3 \\
   n = -\frac{3}{2}
   \]
