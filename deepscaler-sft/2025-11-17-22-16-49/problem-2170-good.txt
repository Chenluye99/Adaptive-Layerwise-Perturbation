1. **Find the sum of the original 10 numbers:**
    - We know that the average of 10 numbers is 85.
    - The formula for the average of a set of numbers is given by:
      \[
      \text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum of the Numbers}}{\text{Number of Elements}}
      \]
    - Rearrange this formula to find the sum of the 10 numbers:
      \[
      \text{Sum of the 10 numbers} = \text{Average} \times \text{Number of Elements} = 85 \times 10 = 850
      \]

2. **Remove the numbers 70 and 76 from the set:**
    - First, calculate the sum of the numbers that are being removed:
      \[
      70 + 76 = 146
      \]
    - Subtract this sum from the original sum of the 10 numbers to get the sum of the remaining 8 numbers:
      \[
      \text{Sum of the remaining 8 numbers} = 850 - 146 = 704
      \]

3. **Find the average of the remaining 8 numbers:**
    - The average of the remaining 8 numbers is given by:
      \[
      \text{Average of the remaining 8 numbers} = \frac{\text{Sum of the remaining 8 numbers}}{\text{Number of Remaining Elements}} = \frac{704}{8}
      \]
    - Perform the division to get the final answer.

Let's implement this step-by-step solution in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Step 1: Calculate the sum of the original 10 numbers
original_average = 85
num_elements = 10
sum_of_original_numbers = original_average * num_elements

# Step 2: Remove the numbers 70 and 76 from the set
numbers_to_remove = [70, 76]
sum_of_numbers_to_remove = sum(numbers_to_remove)
sum_of_remaining_numbers = sum_of_original_numbers - sum_of_numbers_to_remove

# Step 3: Calculate the average of the remaining 8 numbers
num_remaining_elements = num_elements - len(numbers_to_remove)
average_of_remaining_numbers = sum_of_remaining_numbers / num_remaining_elements

print(average_of_remaining_numbers)
```
