2. From the above ratio equation, we can express \( y \) in terms of \( x \):
   \[
   x = \frac{1}{2} y \implies y = 2x
   \]

\[
2x = 4x - 36
\]

\[
2x = 4x - 36 \\
2x - 4x = -36 \\
-2x = -36 \\
x = 18
\]
