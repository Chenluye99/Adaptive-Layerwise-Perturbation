First, let's start by formulating the volume of the rectangular prism. The volume \( V \) of a rectangular prism is given by the product of its length, width, and height. In this case, the dimensions of the box are \( x+5 \), \( x-5 \), and \( x^2 + 25 \).

So, the volume \( V \) of the box can be expressed as:
\[ V = (x + 5)(x - 5)(x^2 + 25) \]

We know that the difference of squares simplifies \((x + 5)(x - 5)\) to \( x^2 - 25 \). Thus, the volume expression becomes:
\[ V = (x^2 - 25)(x^2 + 25) \]

To find the product, we expand it:
\[ V = (x^2 - 25)(x^2 + 25) = x^4 - 625 \]

We want to find the positive integer values of \( x \) such that the volume of the box is less than 700 units:
\[ x^4 - 625 < 700 \]
\[ x^4 < 1325 \]

Now, we need to find the integer solutions for \( x \) that satisfy this inequality. We will iterate through positive integers and check if \( x^4 < 1325 \) holds true. We can use Python to perform this iteration and check.

# Initialize a list to store the positive integer values of x that satisfy the condition
