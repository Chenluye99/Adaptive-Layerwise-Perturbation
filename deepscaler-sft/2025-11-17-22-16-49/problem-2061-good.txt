    - Given that \(\gcd(k, \ell) = 3\), we can substitute this into the equation to get \(\mathop{\text{lcm}}[k, \ell] = \frac{k \cdot \ell}{3}\).

    - Both \( k \) and \( \ell \) must be multiples of 3 (since their GCD is 3).

4. **Find the Solution by Iteration:**

    - We will keep track of the smallest \(\mathop{\text{lcm}}[k, \ell]\) encountered during the iteration.

```python
import math

min_lcm = float("inf")
best_pair = (0, 0)
```
