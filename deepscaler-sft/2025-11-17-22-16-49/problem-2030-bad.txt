To solve the problem, we need to use the properties of an arithmetic sequence.

1. **Properties of an Arithmetic Sequence:**
    - The \(n\)-th term of an arithmetic sequence can be given by: 
      \[
      a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d
      \]
      where \(a_n\) is the \(n\)-th term, \(a_1\) is the first term, and \(d\) is the common difference.

2. **Given Information:**
    - The first term \(a_1 = \frac{2}{3}\).
    - The ninth term \(a_9 = \frac{4}{5}\).

3. **Formulating Equations:**
    - Using the formula for the \(n\)-th term, the ninth term can be written as:
      \[
      a_9 = a_1 + 8d = \frac{4}{5}
      \]
    - Substituting the value of \(a_1\):
      \[
      \frac{2}{3} + 8d = \frac{4}{5}
      \]

4. **Solving for the Common Difference \(d\):**
    - Rearrange the equation to isolate \(d\):
      \[
      8d = \frac{4}{5} - \frac{2}{3}
      \]
    - Find a common denominator to perform the subtraction:
      \[
      \frac{4}{5} = \frac{4 \cdot 3}{5 \cdot 3} = \frac{12}{15}, \quad \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2 \cdot 5}{3 \cdot 5} = \frac{10}{15}
      \]
      \[
      \frac{4}{5} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{12}{15} - \frac{10}{15} = \frac{2}{15}
      \]
    - Thus, we have:
      \[
      8d = \frac{2}{15}
      \]
    - Solving for \(d\):
      \[
      d = \frac{2}{15 \cdot 8} = \frac{2}{120} = \frac{1}{60}
      \]

5. **Finding the Fifth Term:**
    - The fifth term \(a_5\) of the arithmetic sequence can be found using:
      \[
      a_5 = a_1 + 4d
      \]
    - Substitute \(a_1 = \frac{2}{3}\) and \(d = \frac{1}{60}\):
      \[
      a_5 = \frac{2}{3} + 4 \cdot \frac{1}{60} = \frac{2}{3} + \frac{4}{60} = \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{15}
      \]
    - Find a common denominator for \(\frac{2}{3}\) and \(\frac{1}{15}\):
      \[
      \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2 \cdot 5}{3 \cdot 5} = \frac{10}{15}
      \]
      \[
      \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{15} = \frac{10}{15} + \frac{1}{15} = \frac{11}{15}
      \]
