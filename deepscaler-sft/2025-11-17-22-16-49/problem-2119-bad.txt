To solve this problem, we will use the relationship between the greatest common divisor (GCD) and the least common multiple (LCM) of two numbers. For any two numbers \(a\) and \(b\), the following formula holds:

\[
\text{GCD}(a, b) \times \text{LCM}(a, b) = a \times b
\]

Given in the problem:
- The least common multiple (LCM) of the two numbers is \(7!\).
- The greatest common divisor (GCD) of the two numbers is 9.
- One of the integers is 315.

Let's denote the two numbers as \(a\) and \(b\), with \(a = 315\). We need to determine the value of \(b\).

1. First, compute \(7!\):

\[
7! = 7 \cdot 6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 5040
\]

2. Substitute the known values into the formula:

\[
\text{GCD}(315, b) \times \text{LCM}(315, b) = 315 \times b
\]

We know that \(\text{GCD}(315, b) = 9\) and \(\text{LCM}(315, b) = 5040\), so we can substitute these values in:

\[
9 \times 5040 = 315 \times b
\]

3. Now, solve for \(b\):

\[
45360 = 315 \times b \implies b = \frac{45360}{315}
\]

Let's compute this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Given values
lcm = math.factorial(7)  # 7!
gcd = 9
a = 315

# We know that GCD * LCM = a * b, so we can solve for b as follows:
b = (gcd * lcm) / a
print(b)
```
