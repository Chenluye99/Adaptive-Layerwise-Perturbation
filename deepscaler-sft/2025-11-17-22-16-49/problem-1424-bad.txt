### Step-by-Step Solution:

    - The fan rotates at 80 revolutions per minute.
    - The point halfway between the center and the outer edge of the fan will have a radius that is half of the outer edge radius.
    - If the outer edge of the fan has a radius \( R \), then the halfway point has a radius of \( \frac{R}{2} \).
    - The speed of this point is proportional to its distance from the center of rotation. The point on the outer edge has a speed given by \( 80 \times 2 \pi R \) radians per minute (since one full revolution corresponds to \( 2 \pi \) radians), and the point halfway between the center and the outer edge has a speed given by \( 80 \times 2 \pi \times \frac{R}{2} = 80 \times \pi R \) radians per minute.
    - In 15 minutes, this point travels 97,968 inches. So, in one minute, the distance covered by this point is \( \frac{97968}{15} = 6531.2 \) inches.

2. **Set Up the Equation to Find \( R \):**
    - The distance this point travels in one minute is the arc length it covers in one minute, which is given by its angular speed multiplied by its radius.
    - In 1 minute, the point completes \( 80 \) revolutions, so it covers \( 80 \cdot 2 \pi \cdot \frac{R}{2} = 80 \cdot \pi R \) radians, and the corresponding arc length at radius \( \frac{R}{2} \) is \( 80 \cdot \pi R \cdot \frac{R}{2 \pi} = 80 \cdot \frac{R}{2} = 40 R \) units of arc length \( 2 \pi \text{ radius units} \).
    - So, the distance it travels in one minute is \( 80 \cdot \pi \cdot \frac{R}{2} = 40 \pi R \) inches.
    - Given that this point travels 6,531.2 inches in one minute, we have:
      \[
      40 \pi R = 6531.2
      \]
    - Solving for \( R \):
      \[
      R = \frac{6531.2}{40 \pi}
      \]

3. **Find the Distance Traveled by a Point on the Outer Edge of the Fan in 30 Minutes:**
    - A point on the outer edge of the fan (with radius \( R \)) will travel a distance corresponding to \( 80 \cdot 2 \pi R \) radians per minute, or \( 80 \cdot 2 \pi R \cdot 30 \) inches in 30 minutes.
    - First, we compute \( R \) using Python, then use it to compute the distance traveled by a point on the outer edge in 30 minutes.

Let's execute this step-by-step in Python.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
R = sp.symbols('R')

# Set up the equation based on the given information
eq = 40 * sp.pi * R - 6531.2

# Solve for R
radius_solution = sp.solve(eq, R)[0]

# Now, calculate the distance traveled by a point on the outer edge of the fan in 30 minutes
revolutions_per_minute = 80
minutes = 30
radius_outer = radius_solution
distance_traveled_outer_edge = revolutions_per_minute * 2 * sp.pi * radius_outer * minutes

print(distance_traveled_outer_edge.evalf())
```
