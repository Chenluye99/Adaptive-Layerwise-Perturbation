The equations of the lines given are:

First, we will find the points of intersection of these lines, which will give us the vertices of the triangle.

### Step 1: Find the Points of Intersection

1. **Intersection of \( y = 0 \) and \( y = x + 4 \):**
   - Substitute \( y = 0 \) into \( y = x + 4 \):
     \[
     0 = x + 4 \implies x = -4
     \]
   - So, the intersection point is \((-4, 0)\).

2. **Intersection of \( y = 0 \) and \( x + 3y = 12 \):**
   - Substitute \( y = 0 \) into \( x + 3y = 12 \):
     \[
     x + 3 \cdot 0 = 12 \implies x = 12
     \]
   - So, the intersection point is \((12, 0)\).

3. **Intersection of \( y = x + 4 \) and \( x + 3y = 12 \):**
   - Substitute \( y = x + 4 \) into \( x + 3y = 12 \):
     \[
     x + 3(x + 4) = 12 \implies x + 3x + 12 = 12 \implies 4x + 12 = 12 \implies 4x = 0 \implies x = 0
     \]
   - Substitute \( x = 0 \) back into \( y = x + 4 \):
     \[
     y = 0 + 4 = 4
     \]
   - So, the intersection point is \((0, 4)\).

Thus, the vertices of the triangle are \((-4, 0)\), \((12, 0)\), and \((0, 4)\).

\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2) \right|
