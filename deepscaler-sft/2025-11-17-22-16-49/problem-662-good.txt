To solve this problem, we need to calculate the probability that the draws of the balls alternate in color (i.e., white, black, white, black, ... or black, white, black, white, ...).

### Step-by-Step Reasoning:

1. **Set Up the Problem:**
    - We have 4 white balls and 4 black balls.
    - When drawing the balls one at a time, we want the sequence of draws to alternate colors.
    - This means if the first ball drawn is white, the sequence should be white, black, white, black, ..., white, black.
    - Or if the first ball drawn is black, the sequence should be black, white, black, white, ..., black, white.

2. **Determine All Possible Sequences:**
    - The total number of ways to draw 8 balls out of the 8 positions is given by permutations of 4 white and 4 black balls, which is \(\frac{8!}{4!4!}\).

3. **Count the Favorable Outcomes:**
    - We need to count the number of permutations where the colors alternate.
    - There are only 2 ways for the draws to alternate: starting with a white ball or starting with a black ball.

4. **Calculate the Number of Alternating Sequences:**
    - Starting with a white ball: The sequence must be "W, B, W, B, W, B, W, B".
    - Starting with a black ball: The sequence must be "B, W, B, W, B, W, B, W".
    - For each of these two sequences, there is only 1 way to arrange the 4 white and 4 black balls respectively while maintaining the alternating pattern.

5. **Formulate the Probability:**
    - There are 2 favorable outcomes (one starting with a white ball and the other starting with a black ball).
    - The total number of ways to arrange 4 white and 4 black balls in 8 positions is \(\frac{8!}{4!4!}\).

# Define the balls
balls = ['W', 'W', 'W', 'W', 'B', 'B', 'B', 'B']

    # Check if the positions of white and black balls alternate properly

    for i in range(1, len(perm)):
