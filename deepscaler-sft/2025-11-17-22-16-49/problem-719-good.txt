First, let's start by using the properties of polynomials and the information given in the problem.

1. **Polynomial and Its Properties:**
    - The polynomial is $P(x) = x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c$.
    - The product of the zeros of the polynomial $P(x) = x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c$ is given by $-c$ (from Vieta's formulas).
    - The mean of the zeros of the polynomial is given by $-\frac{a}{3}$ (since the sum of the zeros is $-a$ and there are 3 zeros, so the mean is $\frac{-a}{3}$).
    - The sum of the coefficients of the polynomial is $1 + a + b + c$ (substitute $x = 1$ into $P(x)$).

2. **Given Conditions:**
    - The problem states that the mean of its zeros, the product of its zeros, and the sum of its coefficients are all equal.
    - The y-intercept of the graph of $y = P(x)$ is 2, which means $P(0) = c = 2$.
    - So, we have $c = 2$.
    - Substituting $c = 2$ into the conditions, we get:
      - Product of the zeros: $-c = -2$
      - Mean of the zeros: $-\frac{a}{3}$
      - Sum of the coefficients: $1 + a + b + c = 1 + a + b + 2 = a + b + 3$
    - According to the problem, these three quantities are equal:
      \[
      -\frac{a}{3} = -2 = a + b + 3
      \]

3. **Formulate the Equations:**
    - From $-\frac{a}{3} = -2$, we get:
      \[
      -\frac{a}{3} = -2 \implies \frac{a}{3} = 2 \implies a = 6
      \]
    - Substitute $a = 6$ and $c = 2$ into the equation $-\frac{a}{3} = a + b + 3$:
      \[
      -2 = 6 + b + 3 \implies -2 = 9 + b \implies b = -2 - 9 \implies b = -11
      \]
