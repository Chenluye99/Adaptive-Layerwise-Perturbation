First, let's break down the problem step-by-step:

1. The electric car is charged 3 times per week.
2. There are 52 weeks in a year.
3. The cost to charge the car each time is $0.78.

To get the total cost, we need to find out how many times the car is charged in 52 weeks and then multiply that by the cost per charge. 

The total number of charges in 52 weeks is:
\[ \text{Total charges} = 3 \, \text{charges/week} \times 52 \, \text{weeks} \]

Then, the total cost to charge the car over these 52 weeks is:
\[ \text{Total cost} = \text{Total charges} \times 0.78 \, \text{\$/charge} \]

Let's compute this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Define the parameters
charges_per_week = 3
number_of_weeks = 52
cost_per_charge = 0.78

# Calculate the total number of charges in 52 weeks
total_charges = charges_per_week * number_of_weeks

# Calculate the total cost to charge the car over these 52 weeks
total_cost = total_charges * cost_per_charge
print(total_cost)
```
Code execution result: 121.68

Thus, the total cost to charge the car over the 52 weeks is \(\boxed{121.68}\). This is our final answer.
