To solve this problem, let's start by expressing the matrices \(\mathbf{D}\) and \(\mathbf{R}\) in their standard forms.

1. **Definition of Matrices:**
    - **Dilation Matrix \(\mathbf{D}\):** A dilation with scale factor \(k\) is given by:
      \[
      \mathbf{D} = \begin{pmatrix} k & 0 \\ 0 & k \end{pmatrix}.
      \]
    - **Rotation Matrix \(\mathbf{R}\):** A rotation about the origin by an angle \(\theta\) counter-clockwise is given by:
      \[
      \mathbf{R} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos \theta & -\sin \theta \\ \sin \theta & \cos \theta \end{pmatrix}.
      \]

2. **Matrix Multiplication:**
    - The product \(\mathbf{R} \mathbf{D}\) is:
      \[
      \mathbf{R} \mathbf{D} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos \theta & -\sin \theta \\ \sin \theta & \cos \theta \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} k & 0 \\ 0 & k \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} k \cos \theta & -k \sin \theta \\ k \sin \theta & k \cos \theta \end{pmatrix}.
      \]
    - We are given that:
      \[
      \mathbf{R} \mathbf{D} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 & -4 \\ 4 & 8 \end{pmatrix}.
      \]
    - By comparing the elements of the matrices, we get the following system of equations:
      \[
      \begin{cases}
      k \cos \theta = 8, \\
      -k \sin \theta = -4, \\
      k \sin \theta = 4, \\
      k \cos \theta = 8.
      \end{cases}
      \]
    - From the equations \(k \cos \theta = 8\) and \(k \sin \theta = 4\), we can solve for \(\cos \theta\) and \(\sin \theta\):
      \[
      \cos \theta = \frac{8}{k}, \quad \sin \theta = \frac{4}{k}.
      \]

3. **Find \(\tan \theta\):**
    - We know that \(\tan \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta}\). Substituting the expressions for \(\cos \theta\) and \(\sin \theta\), we get:
      \[
      \tan \theta = \frac{\frac{4}{k}}{\frac{8}{k}} = \frac{4}{8} = \frac{1}{2}.
      \]

```python
import sympy as sp

# Define variables
k, theta = sp.symbols('k theta', positive=True)

# Define the equations based on matrix equality
eq1 = sp.Eq(k * sp.cos(theta), 8)
eq2 = sp.Eq(-k * sp.sin(theta), -4)

# Solve the system of equations
```
