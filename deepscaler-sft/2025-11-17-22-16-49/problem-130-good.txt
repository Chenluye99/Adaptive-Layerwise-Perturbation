First, let's understand the problem:

1. **Track Geometry:**
   - The ball rolls along a series of four semicircular arcs with radii \(R_1 = 120\) inches, \(R_2 = 50\) inches, \(R_3 = 90\) inches, and \(R_4 = 70\) inches.
   - For each semicircular arc, the length of the arc is half the circumference of a circle with that radius. The formula for the length of a semicircle is \(\pi R\).

2. **Distance Traveled by the Center of the Ball:**
   - The ball rolls without slipping, so the distance traveled by the center of the ball along the track is the same as the length of the path it follows. Each semicircular arc on the track will contribute to this path length.
   - Therefore, the total distance traveled by the center of the ball is the sum of the lengths of the four semicircular arcs.

   Mathematically,
   \[
   \text{Total Distance} = \pi R_1 + \pi R_2 + \pi R_3 + \pi R_4
   \]
   Substituting the given radii:
   \[
   \text{Total Distance} = \pi (120 + 50 + 90 + 70)
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total Distance} = \pi (120 + 50 + 90 + 70) = \pi (330) = 330\pi
   \]

import sympy as sp
