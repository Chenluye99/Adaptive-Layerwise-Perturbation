To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have two numbers, \(52_7\) and \(62_7\), which are numbers written in base 7.
    - We need to add these two numbers and then find the units digit of the result.

2. **Convert Base 7 Numbers to Base 10:**
    - First, convert \(52_7\) to its base 10 equivalent. The number \(52_7\) can be expressed in base 10 as:
      \[
      5 \cdot 7^1 + 2 \cdot 7^0 = 5 \cdot 7 + 2 \cdot 1 = 35 + 2 = 37_{10}
      \]
    - Next, convert \(62_7\) to its base 10 equivalent. The number \(62_7\) can be expressed in base 10 as:
      \[
      6 \cdot 7^1 + 2 \cdot 7^0 = 6 \cdot 7 + 2 \cdot 1 = 42 + 2 = 44_{10}
      \]

3. **Add the Two Base 10 Numbers:**
    - Add the two base 10 numbers obtained from the above conversions: \(37 + 44 = 81\).

4. **Find the Units Digit of the Result:**
    - The units digit of a number is the remainder when the number is divided by 10. So, we need to find the remainder when 81 is divided by 10:
      \[
      81 \mod 10 = 1
      \]
    - Therefore, the units digit of \(52_7 + 62_7\) is 1.

```python
# Step 1: Convert the base 7 numbers to base 10
```
