The sum of the cubes of the first \(n\) positive integers is given by the formula:
\[
\left( \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \right)^2
\]

\[
\left( \frac{100 \cdot 101}{2} \right)^2 = (50 \cdot 101)^2 = 5050^2
\]
