To find the total surface area of a hemisphere with radius 6 cm, we need to consider two parts:

1. The surface area of the hemisphere, which is half the surface area of a full sphere.
2. The area of the circular base of the hemisphere.

1. **Surface Area of the Hemisphere:**
   - The surface area of a full sphere of radius \( r \) is \( 4 \pi r^2 \).
   - Therefore, the surface area of a hemisphere (excluding its base) is half of this, i.e., \( \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \pi r^2 = 2 \pi r^2 \).

2. **Area of the Circular Base:**
   - The base of the hemisphere is a circle with radius \( r \).
   - The area of a circle is given by \( \pi r^2 \).

So, the total surface area of the hemisphere is the sum of the surface area of the hemisphere (including its base) and the area of the circular base:
\[ \text{Total Surface Area} = 2 \pi r^2 + \pi r^2 = 3 \pi r^2. \]

Given that the radius \( r = 6 \) cm, we substitute this value into the formula:
\[ \text{Total Surface Area} = 3 \pi (6)^2 = 3 \pi \cdot 36 = 108 \pi. \]

Let's confirm this by coding it in Python.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Radius of the hemisphere
r = 6

# Calculate the total surface area of the hemisphere
surface_area_hemisphere_excluding_base = 2 * sp.pi * r**2
area_of_circular_base = sp.pi * r**2
total_surface_area_hemisphere = surface_area_hemisphere_excluding_base + area_of_circular_base

print(total_surface_area_hemisphere)
```
Code execution result: 108*pi

Thus, the total surface area of the hemisphere with a radius of 6 cm is \(\boxed{108\pi}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
