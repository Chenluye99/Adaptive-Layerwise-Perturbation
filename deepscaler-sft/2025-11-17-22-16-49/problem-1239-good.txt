To solve this problem, we need to compute the expression \(2^3 \cdot 4^5 \cdot 6^7 \cdot 8^9\) and then find the remainder when this product is divided by 13. We can use Python to ensure the accuracy of our result by performing the calculations step-by-step.

1. First, compute each of the terms \(2^3\), \(4^5\), \(6^7\), and \(8^9\).
2. Then, multiply these results together.
3. Finally, find the remainder when the product is divided by 13.

Let's start by calculating each component and then find the final remainder using Python.
```python
# Step 1: Compute each term
term1 = 2 ** 3
term2 = 4 ** 5
term3 = 6 ** 7
term4 = 8 ** 9

# Step 2: Compute the product of the terms
product = term1 * term2 * term3 * term4

# Step 3: Find the remainder when the product is divided by 13
remainder = product % 13
print(remainder)
```
