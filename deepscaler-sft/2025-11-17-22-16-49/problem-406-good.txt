To solve this problem, we need to find the number of ways to get from point \(A\) to point \(B\) and then from point \(B\) to point \(C\).

1. **Define the Points:**
    - Point \(A\) is at the top-left corner, so we can consider it to be at \((0, 0)\).
    - Point \(B\) is 5 steps to the right and 2 steps down from \(A\), so \(B\) is at \((5, 2)\).
    - Point \(C\) is 7 steps to the right and 4 steps down from \(A\), so \(C\) is at \((7, 4)\).

2. **Find the Number of Paths from \(A\) to \(B\):**
    - To get from \(A\) at \((0, 0)\) to \(B\) at \((5, 2)\), we need to take 5 steps to the right (R) and 2 steps down (D).
    - The number of different ways to arrange 5 R's and 2 D's in a sequence of 7 steps is given by the binomial coefficient \(\binom{7}{2} = \frac{7!}{2!(7-2)!} = \frac{7!}{2!5!} = \frac{7 \times 6}{2 \times 1} = 21\).

3. **Find the Number of Paths from \(B\) to \(C\):**
    - To get from \(B\) at \((5, 2)\) to \(C\) at \((7, 4)\), we need to take 2 more steps to the right (R) and 2 more steps down (D) from the current position.
    - So, we need to take 2 R's and 2 D's in any order, and the number of ways to arrange 2 R's and 2 D's in a sequence of 4 steps is given by the binomial coefficient \(\binom{4}{2} = \frac{4!}{2!(4-2)!} = \frac{4!}{2!2!} = \frac{4 \times 3}{2 \times 1} = 6\).

4. **Find the Number of Paths from \(A\) to \(C\) that Pass Through \(B\):**
    - The total number of paths from \(A\) to \(C\) that pass through \(B\) is the product of the number of paths from \(A\) to \(B\) and the number of paths from \(B\) to \(C\).
    - So, the total number of paths from \(A\) to \(C\) that pass through \(B\) is \(21 \times 6 = 126\).

Let's implement this step-by-step solution in Python to confirm our result.
```python
import math

# Calculate number of paths from A to B
num_steps_A_to_B = 7  # 5 right and 2 down
num_right_A_to_B = 5
num_down_A_to_B = 2
paths_A_to_B = math.comb(num_steps_A_to_B, num_down_A_to_B)

# Calculate number of paths from B to C
num_steps_B_to_C = 4  # 2 right and 2 down
num_right_B_to_C = 2
num_down_B_to_C = 2
paths_B_to_C = math.comb(num_steps_B_to_C, num_down_B_to_C)

# Calculate number of paths from A to C that pass through B
paths_A_to_C_through_B = paths_A_to_B * paths_B_to_C
print(paths_A_to_C_through_B)
```
