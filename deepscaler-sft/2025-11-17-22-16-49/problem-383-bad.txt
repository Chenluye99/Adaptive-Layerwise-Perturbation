1. \(2.8 \times 1.5\)
2. \(0.28 \times 1.5\)

   \[

   \]

   \]

   - \(0.028 = \frac{28}{1000}\)

   \[
   0.028 \times 0.15 = \frac{28}{1000} \times \frac{15}{100} = \frac{28 \times 15}{1000 \times 100} = \frac{420}{100000} = 0.00420 = 0.0042
   \]
