First, let's start by converting the given time from hours to seconds.

We know:
- 1 hour is equal to 3600 seconds (since 1 hour = 60 minutes and 1 minute = 60 seconds, thus 1 hour = 60 * 60 = 3600 seconds).

Michael takes 0.30 hours to walk to school. To get the duration in seconds, we can multiply 0.30 by 3600.

Let's calculate this using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given data
hours_to_walk = 0.30

# Conversion factor from hours to seconds
```
