To find the volume of the truncated right circular cone (also known as a frustum of a cone), we can use the formula for the volume of a frustum:

\[ V = \frac{1}{3} \pi h (R^2 + r^2 + Rr) \]

where:
- \( R \) is the radius of the larger base,
- \( r \) is the radius of the smaller base,
- \( h \) is the height of the frustum.

Given the problem, we have:
- The radius of the larger base, \( R = 8 \) cm,
- The radius of the smaller base, \( r = 4 \) cm,
- The height of the frustum, \( h = 6 \) cm.

We substitute these values into the formula:

\[ V = \frac{1}{3} \pi \cdot 6 \cdot (8^2 + 4^2 + 8 \cdot 4) \]

First, let's compute the expression inside the parentheses:

\[ 8^2 = 64 \]
\[ 4^2 = 16 \]
\[ 8 \cdot 4 = 32 \]
\[ 8^2 + 4^2 + 8 \cdot 4 = 64 + 16 + 32 = 112 \]

Now substitute this back into the formula:

\[ V = \frac{1}{3} \pi \cdot 6 \cdot 112 = 2 \pi \cdot 112 = 224 \pi \]

So, the volume of the truncated cone is \( 224 \pi \) cubic centimeters. To get the numerical value, we can use Python to compute it.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given values
R = 8
r = 4
h = 6

# Volume formula for the frustum of a cone
volume = (1/3) * sp.pi * h * (R**2 + r**2 + R * r)
print(volume.evalf())
```
