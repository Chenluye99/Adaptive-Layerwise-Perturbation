   - Since \(\angle ABC = 60^\circ\), the coordinates of \(A\) will be influenced by this angle and the length of \(AB\). Let's take \(A\) such that \(AB = AD = 2\).

- Point \(D\) will then be at \((1, -\sqrt{3})\) such that \(AD = 2\) and \(AD \parallel BC\).

3. Determine the coordinates of point \(E\):

     \[
     (x - 4, y - 0) = (-1, -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}) \implies x = 3 \text{ and } y = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}
     \]
   - Thus, the coordinates of \(E\) are \((3, -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3})\).

4. Calculate the vectors \(\overrightarrow{CA}\) and \(\overrightarrow{BE}\):
