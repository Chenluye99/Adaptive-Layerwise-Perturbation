First, let's start by expanding the product of the two complex numbers \((5 - 3i)(-4 + 3i)\). To expand this product, we use the distributive property (also known as the FOIL method for binomials):

\[
(5 - 3i)(-4 + 3i) = 5 \cdot (-4) + 5 \cdot (3i) + (-3i) \cdot (-4) + (-3i) \cdot (3i)
\]

This simplifies to:

\[
= -20 + 15i + 12i - 9i^2
\]

Recall that \(i^2 = -1\), so \(-9i^2 = -9(-1) = 9\). Therefore, we have:

\[
= -20 + 15i + 12i + 9
\]

Combine the real parts and the imaginary parts:

\[
= (-20 + 9) + (15i + 12i) = -11 + 27i
\]

Thus, the simplified form of \((5 - 3i)(-4 + 3i)\) is \(-11 + 27i\). We can confirm this by implementing it in Python.
```python
# Defining the complex numbers
```
