First, let's determine how long it takes for Malcolm and Joshua to finish the 10-mile race.

1. **Calculate Malcolm's Time:**
   - Malcolm's speed is 6 minutes per mile.
   - For a 10-mile race, Malcolm's total time will be \( 10 \text{ miles} \times 6 \text{ minutes/mile} = 60 \text{ minutes} \).

2. **Calculate Joshua's Time:**
   - Joshua's speed is 8 minutes per mile.
   - For a 10-mile race, Joshua's total time will be \( 10 \text{ miles} \times 8 \text{ minutes/mile} = 80 \text{ minutes} \).

3. **Determine the Time Difference:**
   - The difference in their finishing times is \( 80 \text{ minutes} - 60 \text{ minutes} = 20 \text{ minutes} \).
   - Therefore, Joshua will finish 20 minutes after Malcolm.

Let's write this in Python to ensure our calculations are accurate.
```python
# Defining the speeds and distance of the race
malcolm_speed_per_mile = 6  # in minutes per mile
joshua_speed_per_mile = 8   # in minutes per mile
race_distance = 10  # in miles

# Calculate the total time taken by Malcolm and Joshua to complete the race
malcolm_total_time = malcolm_speed_per_mile * race_distance
joshua_total_time = joshua_speed_per_mile * race_distance

# Calculate the difference in their finishing times
time_difference = joshua_total_time - malcolm_total_time
print(time_difference)
```
Code execution result: 20

Thus, Joshua will cross the finish line \(\boxed{20}\) minutes after Malcolm. This confirms that our solution is accurate.
