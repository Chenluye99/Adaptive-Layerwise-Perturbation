To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - A cylinder is circumscribed around a sphere, meaning the sphere is inscribed in the cylinder.
    - The volume of the cylinder is given as 60 cubic centimeters.
    - We need to find the volume of the sphere that is inscribed in this cylinder.

2. **Mathematical Formulations:**
    - Let the radius of the sphere be \( r \).
    - The height of the cylinder will be equal to the diameter of the sphere, so the height of the cylinder, \( h \), is \( 2r \).
    - The volume of the cylinder is given by the formula: \[
        V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi r_{\text{cylinder}}^2 h
    \]
      where \( r_{\text{cylinder}} \) is the radius of the base of the cylinder, and \( h \) is the height of the cylinder. Since the sphere is inscribed in the cylinder, the radius of the base of the cylinder is also \( r \). So, the formula becomes: \[
        V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi r^2 (2r) = 2 \pi r^3
    \]
    - We know that the volume of the cylinder is 60 cubic centimeters, so we can set up the equation: \[
        2 \pi r^3 = 60
    \]
    - Solve for \( r^3 \): \[
        r^3 = \frac{60}{2 \pi} = \frac{30}{\pi}
    \]

3. **Find the Volume of the Sphere:**
    - The volume of a sphere is given by the formula: \[
        V_{\text{sphere}} = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3
    \]
    - Substitute \( r^3 = \frac{30}{\pi} \) into the formula for the volume of the sphere: \[
        V_{\text{sphere}} = \frac{4}{3} \pi \left( \frac{30}{\pi} \right) = \frac{4}{3} \cdot 30 = \frac{120}{3} = 40
    \]

Hence, the volume of the sphere is 40 cubic centimeters. Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given volume of the cylinder
V_cylinder = 60

# Set up the equation 2 * pi * r^3 = 60 and solve for r^3
r_cubed = sp.solve(2 * sp.pi * sp.symbols('r')**3 - V_cylinder, sp.symbols('r')**3)[0]

# Calculate the volume of the sphere using the formula (4/3) * pi * r^3
V_sphere = (4/3) * sp.pi * r_cubed
print(V_sphere)
```
Code execution result: 40.0000000000000

Thus, the volume of the sphere that is inscribed in the cylinder is \(\boxed{40}\) cubic centimeters. This confirms that our solution is accurate.
