1. **Understand the Parabolas:**
    - A parabola is defined by its focus and directrix. The distance from any point on the parabola to the focus is equal to its distance to the directrix.

2. **Formulate the Equations of the Parabolas:**
    - For the first parabola, the directrix is the $x$-axis. A point $(x, y)$ on this parabola will be equidistant from the focus $(3, -28)$ and the directrix $y = 0$. The distance from $(x, y)$ to the focus $(3, -28)$ is $\sqrt{(x - 3)^2 + (y + 28)^2}$. The distance from $(x, y)$ to the directrix $y = 0$ is $|y|$. So, we get the equation:

      \sqrt{(x - 3)^2 + (y + 28)^2} = |y|
