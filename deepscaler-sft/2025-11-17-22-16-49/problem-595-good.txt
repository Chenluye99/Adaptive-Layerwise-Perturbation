First, recall that for any two complex numbers \( w \) and \( z \), we have the identity for the sum of their cubes:

\[ w^3 + z^3 = (w + z)(w^2 - wz + z^2). \]

We are given:
1. \( |w + z| = 2 \)
2. \( |w^2 + z^2| = 18 \)

We want to find the smallest possible value of \( |w^3 + z^3| \).

First, let's express \( w^2 - wz + z^2 \) in terms of \( w^2 + z^2 \) and \( wz \):

\[ w^2 - wz + z^2 = (w^2 + z^2) - wz. \]

So, we have:

\[ w^3 + z^3 = (w + z)((w^2 + z^2) - wz). \]

Let's denote:
\[ w + z = a \]
\[ w^2 + z^2 = b \]

From the problem, we know:
\[ |a| = 2 \]
\[ |b| = 18 \]

We also know the identity for the product of the sum and difference of squares of \( w \) and \( z \):

\[ (w + z)^2 = w^2 + z^2 + 2wz \implies a^2 = b + 2wz \implies wz = \frac{a^2 - b}{2}. \]

Substitute \( wz \) into the expression for \( w^2 - wz + z^2 \):

\[ w^2 - wz + z^2 = b - wz = b - \frac{a^2 - b}{2} = \frac{2b - (a^2 - b)}{2} = \frac{3b - a^2}{2}. \]

Now, substitute this back into the expression for \( w^3 + z^3 \):

\[ w^3 + z^3 = a \cdot \frac{3b - a^2}{2} = \frac{a (3b - a^2)}{2}. \]

We want to find the smallest possible value of \( |w^3 + z^3| \), so we need to consider all possible values of \( a \) and \( b \) that satisfy the constraints \( |a| = 2 \) and \( |b| = 18 \):

\[ a = 2 \text{ or } a = -2, \]
\[ b = 18 \text{ or } b = -18. \]

We will evaluate \( |w^3 + z^3| \) for all combinations of these values and find the minimum. We'll use Python to iterate through these values and compute the result accurately.
