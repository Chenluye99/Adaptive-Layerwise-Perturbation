\[P(x) = x^4 + ax^3 - x^2 + ax + 1 = 0\]

has at least one real root. To proceed, we can follow these steps:

1. **Substitute Real Roots into the Polynomial:**
   Suppose \(r\) is a real root of the polynomial. Then, substituting \(r\) into the polynomial gives:

   \[r^4 + ar^3 - r^2 + ar + 1 = 0.\]

2. **Manipulate and Solve for \(a\):**
   Rearrange the equation to isolate \(a\):

   \[ar^3 + ar = -r^4 + r^2 - 1,\]
   \[a(r^3 + r) = -r^4 + r^2 - 1,\]
   \[a = \frac{-r^4 + r^2 - 1}{r^3 + r}.\]

3. **Determine When \(a\) is Real:**
   For \(a\) to be real, the right-hand side of the equation above must be real. We need to consider when the denominator \(r^3 + r \neq 0\) and evaluate the expression over all possible real values of \(r\).

4. **Find the Range of Possible Values for \(a\):**

# Get the critical points by differentiating 'a_expr' with respect to 'r' and solving for 'r'

critical_points = sp.solve(da_dr, r)
