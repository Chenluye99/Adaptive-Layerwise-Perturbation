To solve this problem, let's start with some definitions and properties of vector projections.

1. **Projection of \(\mathbf{v}\) onto \(\mathbf{w}\):**
   The projection of \(\mathbf{v}\) onto \(\mathbf{w}\) is given by:
   \[
   \mathbf{p} = \text{proj}_{\mathbf{w}} \mathbf{v} = \frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w}}{\mathbf{w} \cdot \mathbf{w}} \mathbf{w}
   \]
   The magnitude of this projection is:
   \[
   \|\mathbf{p}\| = \left| \frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w}}{\|\mathbf{w}\|^2} \right| \|\mathbf{w}\| = \frac{|\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w}|}{\|\mathbf{w}\|}
   \]

2. **Given Information:**
   We are given that:
   \[
   \frac{\|\mathbf{p}\|}{\|\mathbf{v}\|} = \frac{5}{7}
   \]
   This implies:
   \[
   \|\mathbf{p}\| = \frac{5}{7} \|\mathbf{v}\|
   \]

3. **Projection of \(\mathbf{p}\) onto \(\mathbf{v}\):**
   The projection of \(\mathbf{p}\) onto \(\mathbf{v}\) is given by:
   \[
   \mathbf{q} = \text{proj}_{\mathbf{v}} \mathbf{p} = \frac{\mathbf{p} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v}} \mathbf{v}
   \]
   The magnitude of this projection is:
   \[
   \|\mathbf{q}\| = \left| \frac{\mathbf{p} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{v}\|^2} \right| \|\mathbf{v}\| = \frac{|\mathbf{p} \cdot \mathbf{v}|}{\|\mathbf{v}\|}
   \]

4. **Relationship between \(\mathbf{p}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\):**
   Note that \(\mathbf{p}\) is a scalar multiple of \(\mathbf{w}\), i.e., \(\mathbf{p} = k \mathbf{w}\) for some scalar \(k\). Also, \(\mathbf{p}\) lies along the direction of \(\mathbf{w}\), and since \(\mathbf{p}\) is the projection of \(\mathbf{v}\) onto \(\mathbf{w}\), \(\mathbf{p}\) is also along the direction of the projection of \(\mathbf{w}\) onto \(\mathbf{v}\). 

   This means \(\mathbf{p}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\) are aligned in such a way that:
   \[
   \mathbf{p} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \|\mathbf{p}\| \|\mathbf{v}\| \cos \theta
   \]
   where \(\theta\) is the angle between \(\mathbf{p}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\). Since \(\mathbf{p}\) is the projection of \(\mathbf{v}\) onto \(\mathbf{w}\), and assuming \(\mathbf{w}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\) are such that the projections maintain alignment, we have:
   \[
   \mathbf{p} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \|\mathbf{p}\| \|\mathbf{v}\|
   \]
   because \(\cos \theta = 1\) (as they are in the same direction).

   Hence:
   \[
   \|\mathbf{q}\| = \frac{|\mathbf{p} \cdot \mathbf{v}|}{\|\mathbf{v}\|} = \frac{|\|\mathbf{p}\| \|\mathbf{v}\}|}{\|\mathbf{v}\|} = \|\mathbf{p}\| = \frac{5}{7} \|\mathbf{v}\|
   \]

   Therefore:
   \[
   \frac{\|\mathbf{q}\|}{\|\mathbf{v}\|} = \frac{5}{7}
   \]
