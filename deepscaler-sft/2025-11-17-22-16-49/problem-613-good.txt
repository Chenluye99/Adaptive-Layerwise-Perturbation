1. **Find the magnitudes of the vectors \(\overrightarrow{a}\) and \(\overrightarrow{b}\):**

   - The magnitude of \(\overrightarrow{a} = (-3, 4)\) is given by:
     \[
     |\overrightarrow{a}| = \sqrt{(-3)^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5
     \]
   - The magnitude of \(\overrightarrow{b} = (5, 2)\) is given by:
     \[
     |\overrightarrow{b}| = \sqrt{5^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{25 + 4} = \sqrt{29}
     \]

2. **Find the dot product \(\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}\):**

   - The dot product of \(\overrightarrow{a} = (-3, 4)\) and \(\overrightarrow{b} = (5, 2)\) is given by:
     \[
     \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = (-3) \cdot 5 + 4 \cdot 2 = -15 + 8 = -7
     \]
