1. **Initial Volumes:**
   - Both cylinders initially have radius 8 inches and height 3 inches.
   - The volume \( V \) of a cylinder is given by the formula:
     \[
     V = \pi r^2 h
     \]
   - So, the initial volume of each cylinder is:
     \[
     V_{\text{initial}} = \pi (8)^2 (3) = \pi \cdot 64 \cdot 3 = 192 \pi
     \]

2. **Modified Dimensions and Volumes:**
   - For the first cylinder, the radius is increased by \( x \) inches, so its new radius is \( 8 + x \) inches, and the height remains 3 inches.
     - Its new volume is:
       \[
       V_1 = \pi (8 + x)^2 \cdot 3
       \]
   - For the second cylinder, the height is increased by \( x \) inches, so its new height is \( 3 + x \) inches, and the radius remains 8 inches.
     - Its new volume is:
       \[
       V_2 = \pi \cdot 8^2 \cdot (3 + x) = \pi \cdot 64 \cdot (3 + x) = 64 \pi (3 + x)
       \]

3. **Setting the Volumes Equal:**
   - According to the problem, the resulting volumes are equal, so we set \( V_1 \) and \( V_2 \) equal to each other:
     \[
     \pi (8 + x)^2 \cdot 3 = 64 \pi (3 + x)
     \]
   - We can cancel \(\pi\) from both sides of the equation:
     \[
     3 (8 + x)^2 = 64 (3 + x)
     \]

     \[
     3 (64 + 16x + x^2) = 64 (3 + x)

     \[
     192 + 48x + 3x^2 = 192 + 64x

   - Rearrange all terms to one side of the equation:
     \[
     3x^2 + 48x + 192 - 64x - 192 = 0

     \[
     3x^2 - 16x = 0

   - Factor out \( x \):
     \[
     x (3x - 16) = 0

   - This gives us two solutions:

     x = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad 3x - 16 = 0
