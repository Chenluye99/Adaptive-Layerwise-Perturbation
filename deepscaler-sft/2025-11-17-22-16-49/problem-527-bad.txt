First, let's start by understanding the given problem and breaking it down.

1. **Initial Set Up:**
    - There are 8 people in the room with an average age of 35 years.
    - To find the total age of these 8 people, we use the formula for average:
      \[
      \text{Total age of 8 people} = \text{Average age} \times \text{Number of people} = 35 \times 8
      \]
    - So, the total age of the 8 people is \( 35 \times 8 = 280 \) years.

2. **After One Person Leaves:**
    - A 22-year-old person leaves the room. We need to subtract the age of this person from the total age calculated above:
      \[
      \text{Total age of the remaining 7 people} = 280 - 22 = 258 \text{ years}
      \]
    - Now, we need to find the average age of the remaining 7 people:
      \[
      \text{Average age of 7 people} = \frac{\text{Total age of the remaining 7 people}}{\text{Number of remaining people}} = \frac{258}{7}
      \]

3. **Compute the Result:**
    - We will compute \(\frac{258}{7}\) using Python to get the precise answer.

Let's execute this step-by-step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given data
average_age_8_people = 35
num_people_initial = 8
age_person_who_leaves = 22

# Calculate the total age of the 8 people
total_age_8_people = average_age_8_people * num_people_initial

# Calculate the total age of the remaining 7 people after the 22-year-old leaves
total_age_7_people = total_age_8_people - age_person_who_leaves

# Calculate the average age of the remaining 7 people
num_remaining_people = 7
average_age_7_people = total_age_7_people / num_remaining_people
print(average_age_7_people)
```
