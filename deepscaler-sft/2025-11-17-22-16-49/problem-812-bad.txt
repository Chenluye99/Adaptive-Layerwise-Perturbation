To solve this problem, we can make use of the properties of a rhombus. The area of a rhombus can be given by the formula:

\[ \text{Area} = s^2 \cdot \sin(\theta) \]

where \( s \) is the length of a side of the rhombus, and \( \theta \) is the angle between two adjacent sides.

In this problem, each side of the rhombus measures \( 2 \) cm, and the angle between two adjacent sides is \( 60^\circ \). First, we need to convert the angle to radians because the `sin` function in Python's `math` module uses radians. However, for this specific problem, we can directly use the sine of 60 degrees since it is a common trigonometric value.

We know that:

\[ \sin(60^\circ) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \]

So, substituting \( s = 2 \) and \( \theta = 60^\circ \) into the formula, we get:

\[ \text{Area} = 2^2 \cdot \sin(60^\circ) = 4 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 2 \sqrt{3} \]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python.
```python
import math

# Given values
side_length = 2
angle_degrees = 60

# Convert angle to radians because math.sin expects the angle in radians
angle_radians = math.radians(angle_degrees)

# Calculate the area of the rhombus
area = side_length ** 2 * math.sin(angle_radians)
print(area)
```

\[ 2 \sqrt{3} \approx 2 \cdot 1.7320508075688772 \approx 3.4641016151377544 \]

Thus, the area of the rhombus is:

\(\boxed{2 \sqrt{3}}\)
