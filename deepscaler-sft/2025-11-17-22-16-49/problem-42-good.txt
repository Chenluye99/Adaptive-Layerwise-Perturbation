To solve this problem, we need to calculate the probability that the top card drawn from a standard deck of 52 cards is red, and the second card drawn is black.

1. **Determine the Number of Red and Black Cards:**
    - There are 26 red cards (13 hearts and 13 diamonds).
    - There are 26 black cards (13 spades and 13 clubs).

2. **Calculate the Probability Step-by-Step:**
    - The probability that the top card is red is \(\frac{26}{52} = \frac{1}{2}\).
    - After drawing one red card, there are 51 cards left in the deck, with 26 of them being black.
    - So, the probability that the second card is black, given that the first card was red, is \(\frac{26}{51}\).
    - The combined probability that the top card is red and the second card is black is the product of these probabilities:
      \[
      P(\text{Top card is red and second card is black}) = \frac{26}{52} \cdot \frac{26}{51} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{26}{51} = \frac{26}{102} = \frac{13}{51}
      \]

Let's confirm this step-by-step calculation using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
from fractions import Fraction

# Number of red and black cards in a deck of 52 cards
num_red_cards = 26
num_black_cards = 26
total_cards = 52

# Probability that the top card is red
prob_top_card_red = Fraction(num_red_cards, total_cards)

# After drawing one red card, there are 51 cards left, with 26 of them being black
remaining_cards = total_cards - 1
num_black_cards_remaining = num_black_cards

# Probability that the second card is black, given that the first card was red
prob_second_card_black_given_first_was_red = Fraction(num_black_cards_remaining, remaining_cards)

# Combined probability that the top card is red and the second card is black
prob_top_red_second_black = prob_top_card_red * prob_second_card_black_given_first_was_red

print(prob_top_red_second_black)
```
Code execution result: 13/51

Thus, the probability that the top card is red and the second card is black is \(\boxed{\frac{13}{51}}\). The calculation is verified to be accurate through both reasoning and Python code execution.
