First, let's start by calculating the volume of the cylinder and the volume of the two cones.

1. **Volume of the Cylinder:**

The formula for the volume of a cylinder is given by:
\[ V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi r^2 h \]
where \( r \) is the radius and \( h \) is the height of the cylinder. Given that the radius of the cylinder is 12 cm and the height of the cylinder is 24 cm, we can substitute these values into the formula:
\[ V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi (12)^2 (24) = \pi \cdot 144 \cdot 24 = 3456 \pi \, \text{cm}^3 \]

2. **Volume of Each Cone:**

The formula for the volume of a cone is given by:
\[ V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h \]
where \( r \) is the radius and \( h \) is the height of the cone. Given that the radius of each cone is 12 cm and the height of each cone is 12 cm, we can substitute these values into the formula for one cone:
\[ V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3} \pi (12)^2 (12) = \frac{1}{3} \pi \cdot 144 \cdot 12 = \frac{1}{3} \pi \cdot 1728 = 576 \pi \, \text{cm}^3 \]
Since there are two cones, the total volume of the two cones is:
\[ V_{\text{2 cones}} = 2 \cdot 576 \pi = 1152 \pi \, \text{cm}^3 \]

3. **Volume of the Cylinder Not Occupied by the Cones:**

The volume of the cylinder not occupied by the cones is the volume of the cylinder minus the total volume of the two cones:
\[ V_{\text{unoccupied}} = V_{\text{cylinder}} - V_{\text{2 cones}} = 3456 \pi - 1152 \pi = 2304 \pi \, \text{cm}^3 \]

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure the accuracy of the result.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given dimensions
radius = 12
height_cylinder = 24

volume_cylinder = sp.pi * radius**2 * height_cylinder

# Calculate the volume of one cone and then of two cones
volume_cone = (1/3) * sp.pi * radius**2 * height_cone
volume_two_cones = 2 * volume_cone

# Calculate the volume of the cylinder not occupied by the cones
volume_unoccupied = volume_cylinder - volume_two_cones
print(volume_unoccupied)
```
