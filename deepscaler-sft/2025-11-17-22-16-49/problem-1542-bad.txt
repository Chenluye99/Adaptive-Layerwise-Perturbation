First, let's outline the problem and solve it step-by-step.

1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - For the first three days of January, the probability that it snows on any given day is \(\frac{1}{3}\).
    - For the next four days of January, the probability that it snows on any given day is \(\frac{1}{4}\).
    - We need to find the probability that it snows at least once during the first week (i.e., the first 7 days) of January.

2. **Mathematical Formulation:**
    - It's often easier to find the probability of the complementary event (i.e., the probability that it does not snow at all during the first week) and then subtract it from 1.
    - The probability that it does not snow on any of the first three days is \(\left(1 - \frac{1}{3}\right)^3 = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^3\).
    - The probability that it does not snow on any of the next four days is \(\left(1 - \frac{1}{4}\right)^4 = \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^4\).
    - The probability that it does not snow at all during the first week is the product of these two probabilities: \(\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^3 \cdot \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^4\).
    - The probability that it snows at least once during the first week is \(1 - \left(\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^3 \cdot \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^4\right)\).

3. **Implementation in Python:**
Let's use Python to compute this step-by-step.

```python
import sympy as sp

# Calculate the probability that it does not snow at all during the first week
prob_no_snow_first_3_days = (2 / 3) ** 3
prob_no_snow_next_4_days = (3 / 4) ** 4
prob_no_snow_whole_week = prob_no_snow_first_3_days * prob_no_snow_next_4_days

# Calculate the probability that it snows at least once during the first week
prob_snow_at_least_once = 1 - prob_no_snow_whole_week

print(prob_snow_at_least_once)
```
