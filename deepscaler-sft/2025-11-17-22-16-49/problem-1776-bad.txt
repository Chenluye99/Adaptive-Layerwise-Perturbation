To solve this problem, we need to determine how many integers from 1 to 100 leave a remainder of 1 when divided by 5, and then find what percentage that number is of the total integers from 1 to 100.

1. **Identify the Numbers that Leave a Remainder of 1 When Divided by 5:**
    - A number that leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 5 can be expressed as \( n = 5k + 1 \) for some integer \( k \).
    - We need to find all such numbers from 1 to 100.

2. **Find the Range for \( k \):**
    - The smallest number in the range 1 to 100 that satisfies this condition is 1 (when \( k = 0 \), i.e., \( 5 \cdot 0 + 1 = 1 \)).
    - The largest number in the range 1 to 100 that satisfies this condition is 96 (when \( k = 19 \), i.e., \( 5 \cdot 19 + 1 = 96 \)).
    - So, \( k \) ranges from 0 to 19, giving us 20 such numbers.

3. **Calculate the Percentage:**
    - There are 100 integers from 1 to 100.
    - The number of integers that leave a remainder of 1 when divided by 5 is 20.
    - The percentage is given by \(\frac{20}{100} \times 100 = 20\%\).

Let's implement this step-by-step reasoning in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Step 1: Identify integers from 1 to 100 that leave a remainder of 1 when divided by 5
numbers = [num for num in range(1, 101) if num % 5 == 1]

# Step 2: Calculate the percentage
total_count = 100
count_of_interest = len(numbers)
percentage = (count_of_interest / total_count) * 100

print(percentage)
```
Code execution result: 20.0

Thus, the percentage of the integers from 1 to 100 that leave a remainder of 1 when divided by 5 is \(\boxed{20}\). This confirms our earlier reasoning and calculations.
