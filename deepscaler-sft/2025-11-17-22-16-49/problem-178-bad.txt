To solve this problem, we can use the law of total probability and Bayes' theorem.

1. **Find the Probability that a Randomly Selected Person Has Influenza:**

   Let's denote the populations of regions \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\) as \(3k\), \(5k\), and \(2k\) respectively. The total population of the three regions combined is \(3k + 5k + 2k = 10k\).

   - The percentage of people with influenza in region \(A\) is 6%, so the number of people with influenza in region \(A\) is \(0.06 \cdot 3k\).
   - The percentage of people with influenza in region \(B\) is 5%, so the number of people with influenza in region \(B\) is \(0.05 \cdot 5k\).
   - The percentage of people with influenza in region \(C\) is 4%, so the number of people with influenza in region \(C\) is \(0.04 \cdot 2k\).

   The total number of people with influenza in the three regions combined is:
   \[
   0.06 \cdot 3k + 0.05 \cdot 5k + 0.04 \cdot 2k
   \]

   Therefore, the probability that a randomly selected person from the three regions has influenza is the total number of people with influenza divided by the total population:
   \[
   P(\text{Influenza}) = \frac{0.06 \cdot 3k + 0.05 \cdot 5k + 0.04 \cdot 2k}{10k}
   \]

2. **Find the Probability that a Person with Influenza is from Region \(A\):**

   Using Bayes' theorem, we want to find \(P(\text{Region } A | \text{Influenza})\), which is the probability that a person is from region \(A\) given that they have influenza. By Bayes' theorem:
   \[
   P(\text{Region } A | \text{Influenza}) = \frac{P(\text{Influenza} | \text{Region } A) \cdot P(\text{Region } A)}{P(\text{Influenza})}
   \]

   - \(P(\text{Influenza} | \text{Region } A) = 0.06\)
   - \(P(\text{Region } A) = \frac{3k}{10k} = \frac{3}{10}\)

   We have already calculated \(P(\text{Influenza})\) in the previous step.

Let's implement this in Python to get the accurate values.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define variables
k = sp.symbols('k', positive=True)

# Population of regions A, B, C
pop_A = 3 * k
pop_B = 5 * k
pop_C = 2 * k

# Number of people with influenza in each region
flu_A = 0.06 * pop_A
flu_B = 0.05 * pop_B
flu_C = 0.04 * pop_C

# Total number of people with influenza
total_flu = flu_A + flu_B + flu_C

# Total population
total_population = pop_A + pop_B + pop_C

# Probability that a randomly selected person has influenza
P_influenza = total_flu / total_population

# Probability that a person with influenza is from region A
P_region_A_given_flu = (0.06 * pop_A / total_population) / P_influenza

print(P_influenza)
print(P_region_A_given_flu)
```
