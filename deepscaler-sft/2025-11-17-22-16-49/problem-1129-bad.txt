    - By properties of logarithms, \(\log_2(r_1) + \log_2(r_2) + \log_2(r_3) = \log_2(r_1 \cdot r_2 \cdot r_3) = 4\), which means \(r_1 \cdot r_2 \cdot r_3 = 2^4 = 16\).

2. **Using Vieta's Formulas:**
    - For the polynomial \(9x^3 + 5ax^2 + 4bx + a = 0\), Vieta's formulas give us:
      - \(r_1 + r_2 + r_3 = -\frac{5a}{9}\),
      - \(r_1 r_2 + r_2 r_3 + r_3 r_1 = \frac{4b}{9}\),
      - \(r_1 r_2 r_3 = -\frac{a}{9}\).

    - We know from above that \(r_1 \cdot r_2 \cdot r_3 = 16\).
    - So, we can substitute this into the third Vieta formula: \(-\frac{a}{9} = 16 \implies a = -144\).
