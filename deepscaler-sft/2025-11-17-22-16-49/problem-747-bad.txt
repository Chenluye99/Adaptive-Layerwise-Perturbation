To solve this problem, let's start by calculating the time it takes for both Malcolm and Joshua to finish a 10-mile race.

1. **Calculate Malcolm's finishing time:**
   - Malcolm runs at a speed of 6 minutes per mile.
   - For a 10-mile race, the time taken by Malcolm is \( 10 \text{ miles} \times 6 \text{ minutes/mile} = 60 \text{ minutes} \).

2. **Calculate Joshua's finishing time:**
   - Joshua runs at a speed of 8 minutes per mile.
   - For a 10-mile race, the time taken by Joshua is \( 10 \text{ miles} \times 8 \text{ minutes/mile} = 80 \text{ minutes} \).

3. **Find the difference in their finishing times:**
   - The difference in their finishing times is \( 80 \text{ minutes} - 60 \text{ minutes} = 20 \text{ minutes} \).

So, Joshua will cross the finish line 20 minutes after Malcolm.

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Defining the speeds of Malcolm and Joshua in minutes per mile
malcolm_speed = 6  # Malcolm's speed in minutes per mile
joshua_speed = 8   # Joshua's speed in minutes per mile

# Defining the race distance in miles
race_distance = 10  # Race distance in miles

# Calculating the time taken for Malcolm and Joshua to finish the 10-mile race
malcolm_time = race_distance * malcolm_speed
joshua_time = race_distance * joshua_speed

# Calculating the difference in their finishing times
time_difference = joshua_time - malcolm_time
print(time_difference)
```
