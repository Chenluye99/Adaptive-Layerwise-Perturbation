First, let's start by understanding the properties of the ellipse. The endpoints of the major axis are \((-11, 4)\) and \((9, 4)\). This means that the major axis is horizontal and its center is the midpoint of these two points. 

The midpoint (and thus the center of the ellipse) is:
\[
\left( \frac{-11 + 9}{2}, \frac{4 + 4}{2} \right) = (-1, 4)
\]

The length of the semi-major axis \(a\) is half the distance between \((-11, 4)\) and \((9, 4)\):
\[
a = \frac{9 - (-11)}{2} = \frac{20}{2} = 10
\]

So, the equation of the ellipse in standard form, centered at \((-1, 4)\), is:
\[
\frac{(x + 1)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y - 4)^2}{b^2} = 1
\]
where \(a = 10\) and \(b\) is the length of the semi-minor axis, which we need to determine. We know the ellipse passes through the point \((7, 7)\), so we can substitute \(x = 7\) and \(y = 7\) into the equation to find \(b\):
\[
\frac{(7 + 1)^2}{10^2} + \frac{(7 - 4)^2}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{8^2}{10^2} + \frac{3^2}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{64}{100} + \frac{9}{b^2} = 1 \implies \frac{16}{25} + \frac{9}{b^2} = 1
\]
\[
\frac{9}{b^2} = 1 - \frac{16}{25} = \frac{25}{25} - \frac{16}{25} = \frac{9}{25}
\]
\[
\frac{9}{b^2} = \frac{9}{25} \implies b^2 = 25 \implies b = 5
\]

Now that we have both \(a\) and \(b\), we can find the area of the ellipse. The area \(A\) of an ellipse is given by the formula:
\[
A = \pi \cdot a \cdot b
\]
Substituting \(a = 10\) and \(b = 5\):
\[
A = \pi \cdot 10 \cdot 5 = 50\pi
\]

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given values
a = 10
b = 5

# Calculate the area of the ellipse
area = sp.pi * a * b
print(area)
```
Code execution result: 50*pi

Thus, the area of the ellipse is \(\boxed{50\pi}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
