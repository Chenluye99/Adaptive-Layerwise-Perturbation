To solve this problem, we need to find the minimum possible slope of a line that passes through the point \((4, 365)\) and has its y-intercept on or between 1 and 9.

The equation of a line in slope-intercept form is given by:
\[ y = mx + b \]
where \( m \) is the slope and \( b \) is the y-intercept.

Since the line passes through the point \((4, 365)\), we can substitute \( x = 4 \) and \( y = 365 \) into the line equation to get:
\[ 365 = 4m + b \]
Rearrange this equation to express \( b \) in terms of \( m \):
\[ b = 365 - 4m \]

We are given that the y-intercept \( b \) is on or between 1 and 9, so:
\[ 1 \leq b \leq 9 \]
Substitute the expression for \( b \) into this inequality:
\[ 1 \leq 365 - 4m \leq 9 \]

We will solve this compound inequality step-by-step.

1. First, solve the left part of the inequality:
\[ 1 \leq 365 - 4m \]
\[ 1 - 365 \leq -4m \]
\[ -364 \leq -4m \]
\[ 364 \geq 4m \]
\[ 91 \geq m \]
\[ m \leq 91 \]

2. Next, solve the right part of the inequality:
\[ 365 - 4m \leq 9 \]
\[ 365 - 9 \leq 4m \]
\[ 356 \leq 4m \]
\[ 89 \leq m \]
\[ m \geq 89 \]

Combining these two results, we get:
\[ 89 \leq m \leq 91 \]

This means the slope \( m \) must be between 89 and 91, inclusive. To find the minimum possible slope, we choose the smallest value in this interval, which is 89.

```python
import sympy as sp
```
