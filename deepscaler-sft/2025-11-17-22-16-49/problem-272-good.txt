Let's denote the three consecutive even integers as \( n-2 \), \( n \), and \( n+2 \), where \( n \) is an even number. The product of these three consecutive even integers is:

\[ P = (n-2) \cdot n \cdot (n+2) \]

We can iterate through possible values of \( n \) and check these conditions. We will start by checking if the product of the three consecutive even integers is a 6-digit number, and then we will check if the product starts and ends with the digit 2. Let's implement this step-by-step using Python.

# Initialize a variable to store the result

    product = (n - 2) * n * (n + 2)

print(result)
