\[
\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}
\]

where \(s\) is the semi-perimeter of the triangle:

\[
s = \frac{a + b + c}{2}
\]

- \(a = 28\),

- \(c = 16\).

\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)} = \sqrt{37(37 - 28)(37 - 30)(37 - 16)} = \sqrt{37 \cdot 9 \cdot 7 \cdot 21}
