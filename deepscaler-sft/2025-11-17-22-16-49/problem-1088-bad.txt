First, let's start with the given conditions for the cube of side length \( s \).

1. **Surface Area of the Cube:**
   The surface area \( A \) of a cube with side length \( s \) is given by:
   \[
   A = 6s^2
   \]

2. **Volume of the Cube:**
   The volume \( V \) of a cube with side length \( s \) is given by:
   \[
   V = s^3
   \]

3. **Given Condition:**
   The problem states that the number of square units in the surface area of the cube equals \(\frac{1}{6}\) of the number of cubic units in the volume of the cube. Mathematically, this can be written as:
   \[
   6s^2 = \frac{1}{6} s^3
   \]

Let's solve this equation for \( s \):

\[
6s^2 = \frac{s^3}{6}
\]

Multiply both sides by 6 to clear the fraction:

\[
36s^2 = s^3
\]

Rearrange the equation to:

\[
s^3 - 36s^2 = 0
\]

Factor out \( s^2 \):

\[
s^2 (s - 36) = 0
\]

This gives us two solutions:

\[
s^2 = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad s - 36 = 0
\]

Since \( s = 0 \) is not a feasible solution for a physical cube (as side lengths must be positive), we get:

\[
s = 36
\]

So, the side length of the cube is \( s = 36 \).

Next, we need to find the side length of a square for which the number of square units in the area of the square is equal to the number of cubic units in the volume of the cube.

4. **Volume of the Cube with \( s = 36 \):**
   Substitute \( s = 36 \) into the volume formula:

   \[
   V = s^3 = 36^3
   \]

5. **Area of the Square:**
   Let the side length of the square be \( a \). The area of the square is given by:

   \[
   a^2 = V = 36^3
   \]

   So,

   \[
   a = \sqrt{36^3}
   \]

Let's calculate this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given side length of the cube
s = 36

# Calculate the volume of the cube
volume_of_cube = s ** 3

# Calculate the side length 'a' of the square such that the area of the square is equal to the volume of the cube
a = sp.sqrt(volume_of_cube)
print(a)
```
