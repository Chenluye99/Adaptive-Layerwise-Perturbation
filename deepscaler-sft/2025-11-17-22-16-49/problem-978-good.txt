To solve for the vector \(\mathbf{v}\) in the equation
\[
\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 & -1 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 4 & 0 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 27 \\ -14 \end{pmatrix},
\]
we need to find the inverse of the matrix \(\mathbf{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 & -1 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 4 & 0 & -2 \end{pmatrix}\) and then multiply it by the vector \(\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 27 \\ -14 \end{pmatrix}\). The solution is given by \(\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{A}^{-1} \mathbf{b}\).

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python using `sympy`.

```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the matrix A and the vector b
A = sp.Matrix([[2, 3, -1], [0, 4, 5], [4, 0, -2]])
b = sp.Matrix([2, 27, -14])

# Find the inverse of matrix A
A_inv = A.inv()

# Compute the solution by multiplying A_inv by b
v = A_inv * b

print(v)
```
Code execution result: Matrix([[-2], [3], [3]])

So, the solution is the vector \(\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 3 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\).
