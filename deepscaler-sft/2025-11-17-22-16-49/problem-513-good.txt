First, let's outline the problem and translate it into code. We have 12 multiple-choice questions, 4 fill-in-the-blank questions, and 6 open-ended questions, making a total of 22 questions. We need to find the number of ways to select 3 questions such that at least one multiple-choice question and at least one open-ended question are selected.

Let's start by calculating the total number of ways to choose 3 questions from the 22 questions without any restrictions, and then subtract the cases that do not meet the requirement (i.e., the cases where either no multiple-choice questions or no open-ended questions are selected).

1. **Calculate the total number of ways to choose 3 questions from 22:**
   \[
   \binom{22}{3}
   \]
2. **Calculate the number of ways to choose 3 questions such that there are no multiple-choice questions:**
   \[
   \binom{10}{3}
   \]
   (Since there are 10 questions that are not multiple-choice, i.e., 4 fill-in-the-blank and 6 open-ended questions).
3. **Calculate the number of ways to choose 3 questions such that there are no open-ended questions:**
   \[
   \binom{16}{3}
   \]
   (Since there are 16 questions that are not open-ended, i.e., 12 multiple-choice and 4 fill-in-the-blank questions).
4. **Calculate the number of ways to choose 3 questions such that there are no multiple-choice questions and no open-ended questions:**
   \[
   \binom{4}{3}
   \]
   (Since there are 4 fill-in-the-blank questions, which are the only other type of questions available, if we choose 3 questions and exclude both multiple-choice and open-ended questions, we need to only consider the fill-in-the-blank questions).

Finally, using the principle of inclusion and exclusion, the number of ways to choose 3 questions such that at least one multiple-choice question and at least one open-ended question are selected is:
\[
\binom{22}{3} - \left( \binom{10}{3} + \binom{16}{3} - \binom{4}{3} \right)
\]

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to get the accurate result.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Calculate the binomial coefficients
total_ways = sp.binomial(22, 3)
no_multiple_choice = sp.binomial(10, 3)
no_open_ended = sp.binomial(16, 3)
no_multiple_choice_and_no_open_ended = sp.binomial(4, 3)

# Apply the principle of inclusion and exclusion
result = total_ways - (no_multiple_choice + no_open_ended - no_multiple_choice_and_no_open_ended)
print(result)
```
