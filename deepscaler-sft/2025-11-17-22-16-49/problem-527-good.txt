First, let's start by understanding the problem and solve it step-by-step.

1. **Initial Setup:**
    - We are given that the average age of 8 people is 35 years.
    - The formula for the average age is:
      \[
      \text{Average Age} = \frac{\text{Total Age of All People}}{\text{Number of People}}
      \]
    - So, for 8 people, we can write:
      \[
      35 = \frac{\text{Total Age of All People}}{8}
      \]
    - Rearranging this equation, we get:
      \[
      \text{Total Age of All People} = 35 \times 8 = 280
      \]

2. **Person Leaves the Room:**
    - A 22-year-old person leaves the room.
    - The total age of the 7 remaining people will be:
      \[
      \text{Total Age of Remaining People} = 280 - 22 = 258
      \]

3. **Calculate the New Average Age:**
    - Now, we need to find the average age of the 7 remaining people:
      \[
      \text{New Average Age} = \frac{\text{Total Age of Remaining People}}{\text{Number of Remaining People}} = \frac{258}{7}
      \]

Let's implement this in Python to get the precise answer.
```python
# Given data
initial_average_age = 35
num_people_initial = 8
age_person_leaves = 22

# Calculate the total age of all 8 people
total_age_initial = initial_average_age * num_people_initial

# Calculate the total age of the 7 remaining people after the 22-year-old person leaves
total_age_remaining = total_age_initial - age_person_leaves
num_people_remaining = num_people_initial - 1

# Calculate the new average age of the 7 remaining people
new_average_age = total_age_remaining / num_people_remaining
print(new_average_age)
```
