To solve this problem, we need to evaluate the infinite series \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty \left(\tan^{-1}\sqrt{n} - \tan^{-1}\sqrt{n+1}\right)\).

1. **Understand the Series:**
    - Each term in the series is of the form \(\tan^{-1} \sqrt{n} - \tan^{-1} \sqrt{n+1}\).
    - When we sum these terms, we get a telescoping series, where most terms cancel out.

2. **Telescoping Series:**
    - Write out the first few terms of the series to see the pattern:
      \[
      \left(\tan^{-1} \sqrt{1} - \tan^{-1} \sqrt{2}\right) + \left(\tan^{-1} \sqrt{2} - \tan^{-1} \sqrt{3}\right) + \left(\tan^{-1} \sqrt{3} - \tan^{-1} \sqrt{4}\right) + \cdots
      \]
    - Notice that \(\tan^{-1} \sqrt{2}\) cancels with \(- \tan^{-1} \sqrt{2}\), \(\tan^{-1} \sqrt{3}\) cancels with \(- \tan^{-1} \sqrt{3}\), and so on.
    - Therefore, the series simplifies to:
      \[
      \tan^{-1} \sqrt{1} - \lim_{n \to \infty} \tan^{-1} \sqrt{n+1}
      \]

3. **Evaluate the Limits:**
    - \(\tan^{-1} \sqrt{1} = \tan^{-1} 1 = \frac{\pi}{4}\).
    - As \(n \to \infty\), \(\sqrt{n+1} \to \infty\), and \(\tan^{-1} \sqrt{n+1} \to \frac{\pi}{2}\).

    Therefore, the series becomes:
    \[
    \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{2\pi}{4} = -\frac{\pi}{4}
    \]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python and checking if the partial sums converge to this value. We'll compute the partial sum of the first \(N\) terms for increasing values of \(N\) until we can see that it stabilizes around \(-\frac{\pi}{4}\).
