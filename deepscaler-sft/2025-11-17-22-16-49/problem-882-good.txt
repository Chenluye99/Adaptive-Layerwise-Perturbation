We are given the task of finding the sum of all the real coefficients of the expansion of \((1 + ix)^{2009}\), and then determining \(\log_{2}(S)\).

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We need to find the sum of all the real coefficients in the expansion of \((1 + ix)^{2009}\).
    - After obtaining this sum, we need to compute \(\log_{2}(S)\).

2. **Mathematical Background:**
    - The expansion of \((1 + ix)^{2009}\) can be done using the binomial theorem:
      \[
      (1 + ix)^{2009} = \sum_{k=0}^{2009} \binom{2009}{k} (1)^{2009 - k} (ix)^k = \sum_{k=0}^{2009} \binom{2009}{k} (ix)^k
      \]
    - The coefficient of \(x^k\) in this expansion is \(\binom{2009}{k} i^k\).
    - To get the real coefficients, we need the terms where \(i^k\) is a real number. \(i^k\) is real if and only if \(k\) is an even number (since \(i^0 = 1\), \(i^1 = i\), \(i^2 = -1\), \(i^3 = -i\), and \(i^4 = 1\), and the pattern repeats every 4 terms).
    - So, we need to sum the coefficients where \(k\) is even.

3. **Find the Sum of Real Coefficients:**
    - We can substitute \(x = 1\) and \(x = -1\) in \((1 + ix)^{2009}\) to extract the real and imaginary parts, but a more efficient method is to evaluate \((1 + i)^{2009}\) and separate the real part.
    - Let's evaluate \((1 + i)\):
      \[
      1 + i = \sqrt{2} \left(\cos\frac{\pi}{4} + i \sin\frac{\pi}{4}\right)
      \]
    - Raising to the 2009th power:
      \[
      (1 + i)^{2009} = \left( \sqrt{2} \right)^{2009} \left( \cos \frac{2009 \pi}{4} + i \sin \frac{2009 \pi}{4} \right)
      \]
    - Simplify \(\frac{2009 \pi}{4}\):
      \[
      \frac{2009 \pi}{4} = 502 \pi + \frac{\pi}{4}
      \]
      Since \(\cos (502 \pi + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \cos \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\) and \(\sin (502 \pi + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\), we get:
      \[
      (1 + i)^{2009} = \left( \sqrt{2} \right)^{2009} \left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + i \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right) = 2^{1004.5} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} (1 + i) = 2^{1004} \cdot (1 + i)
      \]
      - The real part of \((1 + i)^{2009}\) is \(2^{1004} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot \sqrt{2} = 2^{1004} \).
    - Therefore, the sum of all the real coefficients of \((1 + ix)^{2009}\) is the real part of \((1 + i)^{2009}\), which is \(2^{1004}\).

4. **Find \(\log_{2}(S)\):**
    - We have \( S = 2^{1004} \).
    - Now, \(\log_{2}(S) = \log_{2}(2^{1004}) = 1004 \).
