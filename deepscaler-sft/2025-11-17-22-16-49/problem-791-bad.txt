      \[
      x^2 - 7x + 7 = -3
      \]

      \[
      x^2 - 7x + 7 + 3 = 0 \implies x^2 - 7x + 10 = 0

    - We can use the quadratic formula \(x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\), where \(a = 1\), \(b = -7\), and \(c = 10\), but it's often more straightforward to factorize this quadratic equation if possible.

      \[
      x^2 - 7x + 10 = (x - 2)(x - 5) = 0
      \]
    - Setting each factor to zero gives:
      \[
      x - 2 = 0 \implies x = 2
      \]
      \[
      x - 5 = 0 \implies x = 5
      \]
    - So, the solutions to the equation are \(x = 2\) and \(x = 5\). These are the x-coordinates of the intersection points.

3. **Find the Sum of the x-coordinates:**

      \[
      2 + 5 = 7
