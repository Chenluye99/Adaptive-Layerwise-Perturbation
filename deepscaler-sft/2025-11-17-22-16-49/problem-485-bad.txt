To solve this problem, let's begin by summarizing the key pieces of information given:

1. The height of the cylinder is \( h = 2 \).
2. The circumferences of the two bases of the cylinder lie on the surface of the same sphere with a diameter of \( 2\sqrt{6} \), which means the radius of the sphere is \( \sqrt{6} \).

When the circumferences of the bases of the cylinder lie on the surface of the sphere, the distance from the center of the sphere to the center of either base of the cylinder is the same as the distance from the center of the sphere to the center of the.circle formed by the intersection of the sphere and the plane of either base of the cylinder.

Let's denote:
- The radius of the base of the cylinder as \( r \).
- The center of the sphere as \( O \).
- The center of either base of the cylinder as \( O_1 \).

The distance from the center of the sphere \( O \) to the center of either base of the cylinder (which is also the midpoint of the height of the cylinder) is \( \sqrt{(\sqrt{6})^2 - r^2} = \sqrt{6 - r^2} \). This is because the distance from the center of the sphere to the center of either base is the leg of a right triangle where the hypotenuse is the radius of the sphere, and the other leg is the radius of the base of the cylinder.

Since the height of the cylinder is 2, the distance from the center of either base to the top or bottom base along the height is half of the height of the cylinder, i.e., \( 1 \). Therefore, we have a right triangle formed where the hypotenuse is the radius of the sphere (which is \(\sqrt{6}\)), one leg is \( r \) (the radius of the base of the cylinder), and the other leg is the distance from the center of the sphere to the center of either base of the cylinder, which is \(\sqrt{6 - r^2}\).

By the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle formed by the radius of the sphere, the radius of the base of the cylinder, and the distance from the center of the sphere to the center of either base of the cylinder, we get:
\[ (\sqrt{6 - r^2})^2 = 1^2 + r^2 \]
\[ 6 - r^2 = 1 + r^2 \]
\[ 6 - 1 = r^2 + r^2 \]
\[ 5 = 2r^2 \]
\[ r^2 = \frac{5}{2} \]
\[ r = \sqrt{\frac{5}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2} \]

The surface area \( S \) of a cylinder is given by the formula:
\[ S = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h \]
Substituting \( r = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2} \) and \( h = 2 \):
\[ S = 2\pi \left( \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2} \right)^2 + 2\pi \left( \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2} \right) (2) \]
\[ S = 2\pi \left( \frac{10}{4} \right) + 2\pi \left( \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2} \right) (2) \]
\[ S = 2\pi \left( \frac{5}{2} \right) + 2\pi \left( \frac{2 \sqrt{10}}{2} \right) \]
\[ S = 2\pi \cdot \frac{5}{2} + 2\pi \cdot \sqrt{10} \]
\[ S = 5\pi + 2\pi \sqrt{10} \]
