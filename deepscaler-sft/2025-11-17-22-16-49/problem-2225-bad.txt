First, let's determine how many 3-inch cubes can fit inside a rectangular box with interior dimensions 6 inches by 5 inches by 10 inches.

1. **Determine the number of cubes that fit along each dimension:**
    - Along the 6-inch dimension, $\frac{6}{3} = 2$ cubes can fit.
    - Along the 5-inch dimension, $\frac{5}{3} \approx 1$ cube can fit (we take the integer part because we can only fit whole cubes).
    - Along the 10-inch dimension, $\frac{10}{3} \approx 3$ cubes can fit (again, we take the integer part).

2. **Calculate the total number of 3-inch cubes that can fit inside the box:**
    - The total number of cubes is $2 \times 1 \times 3 = 6$.

3. **Calculate the volume of the rectangular box:**
    - The volume of the box is $6 \times 5 \times 10 = 300$ cubic inches.

4. **Calculate the volume of one 3-inch cube:**
    - The volume of one cube is $3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27$ cubic inches.

5. **Calculate the total volume taken up by the 6 cubes:**
    - The total volume taken up by the 6 cubes is $6 \times 27 = 162$ cubic inches.

6. **Calculate the percentage of the volume of the box taken up by the cubes:**
    - The percentage is given by $\left( \frac{\text{Volume taken up by the cubes}}{\text{Volume of the box}} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{162}{300} \right) \times 100$.

Now, let's implement this in Python to get the precise answer.
```python
# Given dimensions of the box
length_box, width_box, height_box = 6, 5, 10

# Given dimensions of the cube
cube_side = 3

# Calculate how many cubes fit along each dimension of the box
num_cubes_along_length = length_box // cube_side
num_cubes_along_width = width_box // cube_side
num_cubes_along_height = height_box // cube_side

# Calculate the total number of cubes that can fit inside the box
total_num_cubes = num_cubes_along_length * num_cubes_along_width * num_cubes_along_height

# Calculate the volume of the box
volume_box = length_box * width_box * height_box

# Calculate the volume of one cube
volume_cube = cube_side * cube_side * cube_side

# Calculate the total volume taken up by the cubes
total_volume_cubes = total_num_cubes * volume_cube

# Calculate the percentage of the volume of the box taken up by the cubes
percentage_volume_taken_up = (total_volume_cubes / volume_box) * 100
print(percentage_volume_taken_up)
```
Code execution result: 54.0

Thus, the percentage of the volume of the box that is taken up by the cubes is \(\boxed{54}\)%.
