To solve the problem, we need to simulate rolling two six-sided dice and then determine the probability that the sum of the numbers on the two dice is less than 11.

1. **Step-by-Step Plan:**
    - We know that a six-sided die has faces showing numbers from 1 to 6.
    - When two six-sided dice are rolled, the possible outcomes for the sum of the numbers on the two dice range from 2 (1 + 1) to 12 (6 + 6).
    - We need to count the number of outcomes where the sum of the numbers is less than 11.
    - There are a total of \(6 \times 6 = 36\) possible outcomes when two dice are rolled.
    - We will iterate through all 36 outcomes and count how many of them result in a sum that is less than 11.
    - The probability is then given by the ratio of the number of successful outcomes to the total number of outcomes.

2. **Implementation in Python:**
    - We can use Python to perform this counting process accurately.
    - We will use a loop to iterate over all possible outcomes of rolling two six-sided dice and then check the condition that the sum is less than 11.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Initialize counter for successful outcomes and total outcomes
successful_outcomes = 0
total_outcomes = 0

# Iterate through all possible outcomes of rolling two six-sided dice
for die1 in range(1, 7):
    for die2 in range(1, 7):
        total_outcomes += 1
        if (die1 + die2) < 11:
            successful_outcomes += 1

# Calculate the probability
probability = successful_outcomes / total_outcomes
print(probability)
```
