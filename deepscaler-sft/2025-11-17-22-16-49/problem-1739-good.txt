To find the coordinates of \(A\), we need to satisfy the distances \(AB = 5\) and \(AC = 7\). So, if \(A = (x, y)\), then:
\[
x^2 + y^2 = 25 \quad \text{(1)}
\]
\[
(x - 8)^2 + y^2 = 49 \quad \text{(2)}
\]

We can solve these equations simultaneously to get the coordinates of \(A\). Let's subtract equation (1) from equation (2):

\[
(x - 8)^2 + y^2 - (x^2 + y^2) = 49 - 25
\]
\[
(x - 8)^2 - x^2 = 24
\]
\[
x^2 - 16x + 64 - x^2 = 24
\]
\[
-16x + 64 = 24
\]
\[
-16x = -40
\]
\[
x = \frac{40}{16} = \frac{5}{2}
\]

Substitute \( x = \frac{5}{2} \) back into equation (1):

\[
\left( \frac{5}{2} \right)^2 + y^2 = 25
\]
\[
\frac{25}{4} + y^2 = 25
\]
\[
y^2 = 25 - \frac{25}{4}
\]
\[
y^2 = \frac{100}{4} - \frac{25}{4}

\[
y^2 = \frac{75}{4}
\]
\[
y = \pm \frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2}

We can choose either value for \( y \), but for simplicity, we'll take the positive value (the choice will not affect the final result as the orthocenter calculations are symmetrical):

\[
y = \frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2}
\]

So, the coordinates of \( A \) are \(\left( \frac{5}{2}, \frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2} \right)\).

### Steps to Find the Orthocenter \( H \):

1. **Find the slopes of the sides of the triangle:**
   - Slope of \( BC \) is \( \frac{0 - 0}{8 - 0} = 0 \), so the line \( BC \) is horizontal.
   - Slope of \( AC \) is \( \frac{\frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2} - 0}{\frac{5}{2} - 8} = \frac{\frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2}}{-\frac{11}{2}} = -\frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{11} \).
   - Slope of \( AB \) is \( \frac{\frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2} - 0}{\frac{5}{2} - 0} = \frac{\frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2}}{\frac{5}{2}} = \sqrt{3} \).

2. **Find the slopes of the altitudes:**
   - The altitude from \( A \) to \( BC \) is a vertical line through \( A \), so its equation is \( x = \frac{5}{2} \).
   - The slope of the altitude from \( B \) to \( AC \) is the negative reciprocal of \(-\frac{5 \sqrt{3}}{11}\), so it is \( \frac{11}{5 \sqrt{3}} = \frac{11 \sqrt{3}}{15} \). The equation of this altitude, passing through \( B (0, 0) \), is \( y = \frac{11 \sqrt{3}}{15} x \).
   - The slope of the altitude from \( C \) to \( AB \) is the negative reciprocal of \(\sqrt{3}\), so it is \(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \). The equation of this altitude, passing through \( C (8, 0) \), is \( y = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} (x - 8) \).

3. **Find the intersection of the altitudes to get the orthocenter \( H \):**
   - Set the equations of the altitudes equal to each other to find the intersection point.
   \[
   \frac{11 \sqrt{3}}{15} x = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} (x - 8)
   \]
   \[
   \frac{11 \sqrt{3}}{15} x = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} x + \frac{8 \sqrt{3}}{3}
   \]
   Divide both sides by \(\sqrt{3}\):
   \[
   \frac{11}{15} x = -\frac{1}{3} x + \frac{8}{3}
   \]
   Get rid of the fractions by multiplying through by 15:
   \[
   11 x = -5 x + 40
   \]
   \[
   16 x = 40
   \]
   \[
   x = \frac{40}{16} = \frac{5}{2}
   \]
   Substitute \( x = \frac{5}{2} \) back into \( y = \frac{11 \sqrt{3}}{15} x \):
   \[
   y = \frac{11 \sqrt{3}}{15} \cdot \frac{5}{2} = \frac{11 \sqrt{3} \cdot 5}{15 \cdot 2} = \frac{55 \sqrt{3}}{30} = \frac{11 \sqrt{3}}{6}
   \]
So, the coordinates of \( H \) are \(\left( \frac{5}{2}, \frac{11 \sqrt{3}}{6} \right) \).
