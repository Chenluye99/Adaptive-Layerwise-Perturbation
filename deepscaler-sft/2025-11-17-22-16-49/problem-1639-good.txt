First, let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Determine Total Weeks in a Lifetime:**
    - We know that 1 year is 52 weeks.
    - And the average life-span is 75 years.
    - Therefore, the total number of weeks in a lifetime is \( 75 \times 52 \).

2. **Calculate Pounds of Candy Consumed Per Week:**
    - The average American consumes 1483 pounds of candy in a lifetime.
    - To find out how many pounds of candy the average American consumes per week, we divide 1483 pounds by the total number of weeks in a lifetime.

3. **Express the Answer Rounded to the Nearest Hundredth:**
    - After performing the division, we need to round the result to the nearest hundredth.

Let's perform these steps using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Step 1: Calculate the total number of weeks in a lifetime
years_in_life_span = 75
weeks_per_year = 52
total_weeks_in_lifetime = years_in_life_span * weeks_per_year

# Step 2: Calculate pounds of candy consumed per week
pounds_of_candy_in_lifetime = 1483
pounds_of_candy_per_week = pounds_of_candy_in_lifetime / total_weeks_in_lifetime

# Step 3: Round the result to the nearest hundredth
pounds_of_candy_per_week_rounded = round(pounds_of_candy_per_week, 2)

print(pounds_of_candy_per_week_rounded)
```
Code execution result: 0.38

Thus, the average American consumes \(\boxed{0.38}\) pounds of candy per week. This is the final answer, expressed as a decimal to the nearest hundredth.
