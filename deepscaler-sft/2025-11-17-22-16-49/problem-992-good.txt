\[a = 3, \quad b = \frac{10}{3}, \quad c = \frac{1}{3}.\]

The discriminant \(\Delta\) of a quadratic equation \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) is given by the formula:

\[\Delta = b^2 - 4ac.\]

Substituting the values of \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\) into the formula, we get:

\[
\Delta = \left(\frac{10}{3}\right)^2 - 4 \cdot 3 \cdot \frac{1}{3}.
\]
