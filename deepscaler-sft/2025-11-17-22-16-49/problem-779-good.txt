First, let's start by solving the equation \(\frac{4}{3} (r + s + t) = 12\).

1. **Isolate the Sum \(r + s + t\):**
   \[
   \frac{4}{3} (r + s + t) = 12
   \]
   To get rid of the fraction, multiply both sides by 3:
   \[
   4 (r + s + t) = 36
   \]
   Then, divide both sides by 4:
   \[
   r + s + t = 9
   \]

2. **Find the Average of \(r, s, t\):**
   The average of \(r, s, t\) is given by:
   \[
   \text{Average} = \frac{r + s + t}{3}
   \]
   Substituting \(r + s + t = 9\) into the equation, we get:
   \[
   \text{Average} = \frac{9}{3} = 3
   \]

So, the average of \(r, s, t\) is \(3\). Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python.
