To solve the problem, we need to consider all possible outcomes of rolling the two dice and then determine the probability that the sum of the numbers on the top faces of the two dice is 3, 7, or 8.

1. **Define the Dice Faces:**
    - Die 1: [1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 5]
    - Die 2: [2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7]

2. **Enumerate All Possible Outcomes:**
    - We need to get all possible sums of the numbers from the two dice.
    - For each face of Die 1, we pair it with each face of Die 2 and record the sum.

3. **Count Favorable Outcomes:**
    - Check how many of these sums are equal to 3, 7, or 8.

4. **Calculate the Probability:**
    - The probability is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of outcomes.
    - There are 6 faces on Die 1 and 6 faces on Die 2, so there are \(6 \times 6 = 36\) possible outcomes in total.

Let's implement this step-by-step plan in Python to get the accurate result.
```python
# Define the faces of the two dice
die1_faces = [1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 5]
die2_faces = [2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7]

# Initialize a counter for favorable outcomes
favorable_outcomes = 0

# Get all possible sums and count how many of these sums are 3, 7, or 8
for face1 in die1_faces:
    for face2 in die2_faces:
        if (face1 + face2 == 3) or (face1 + face2 == 7) or (face1 + face2 == 8):
            favorable_outcomes += 1

# Total number of outcomes is 6 * 6 = 36
total_outcomes = len(die1_faces) * len(die2_faces)

# Calculate the probability
probability = favorable_outcomes / total_outcomes
print(probability)
```
