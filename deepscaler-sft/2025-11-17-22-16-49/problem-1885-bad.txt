First, let's understand the conversions involved in the money system:

1. \(4 \text{ farthings} = 1 \text{ pence}\)
2. \(12 \text{ pence} = 1 \text{ shilling}\)
3. \(20 \text{ shillings} = 1 \text{ pound}\)

We are asked to find out how many total farthings are equivalent to 1 pound and 5 pence.

1. **Convert 1 pound to farthings**:
    - First, we know that \(1 \text{ pound} = 20 \text{ shillings}\).
    - Each shilling is equivalent to 12 pence, so \(20 \text{ shillings} = 20 \times 12 = 240 \text{ pence}\).
    - Next, since \(4 \text{ farthings} = 1 \text{ pence}\), it follows that \(1 \text{ pence} = 4 \text{ farthings}\). Therefore, \(240 \text{ pence} = 240 \times 4 = 960 \text{ farthings}\).

2. **Convert 5 pence to farthings**:
    - Since \(4 \text{ farthings} = 1 \text{ pence}\), it means \(1 \text{ pence} = 4 \text{ farthings}\). Therefore, \(5 \text{ pence} = 5 \times 4 = 20 \text{ farthings}\).

3. **Combine 1 pound and 5 pence into farthings**:
    - To get the total farthings that are equivalent to 1 pound and 5 pence, we add the farthings obtained from 1 pound and the farthings obtained from 5 pence.
    - So, \(960 \text{ farthings} + 20 \text{ farthings} = 980 \text{ farthings}\).

Now, let's confirm this by coding it in Python to make sure our answer is accurate.
```python
# Define the conversions
farthings_per_pence = 4
pence_per_shilling = 12
shillings_per_pound = 20

# Calculate the farthings equivalent for 1 pound and 5 pence

# First, calculate how many farthings are equivalent to 1 pound
farthings_in_1_pound = shillings_per_pound * pence_per_shilling * farthings_per_pence

# Then, calculate how many farthings are equivalent to 5 pence
farthings_in_5_pence = 5 * farthings_per_pence

# Now, get the total farthings for 1 pound and 5 pence
total_farthings_for_1_pound_and_5_pence = farthings_in_1_pound + farthings_in_5_pence
print(total_farthings_for_1_pound_and_5_pence)
```
Code execution result: 980

Thus, the total number of farthings that are equivalent to 1 pound and 5 pence is \(\boxed{980}\). This confirms our calculations are accurate.
