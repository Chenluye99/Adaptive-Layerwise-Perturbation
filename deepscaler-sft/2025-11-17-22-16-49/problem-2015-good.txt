1. **Define the Variables:**
    - Let the length of the side parallel to the house be \( y \).
    - Let the length of each of the other two sides be \( x \).

2. **Formulate the Perimeter Constraint:**
    - Since the fencing covers three sides of the backyard (two sides of length \( x \) and one side of length \( y \)), the total length of the fencing used is \( 2x + y = 360 \).

3. **Express \( y \) in terms of \( x \):**
    - From the equation \( 2x + y = 360 \), we can solve for \( y \):
      \[
      y = 360 - 2x
      \]

4. **Formulate the Area Function:**
    - The area \( A \) of the rectangular backyard is given by:
      \[
      A = x \cdot y
      \]
    - Substituting the expression for \( y \) into the area formula, we get:
      \[
      A = x \cdot (360 - 2x) = 360x - 2x^2
      \]

5. **Find the Maximum Area:**
    - To find the maximum area, we take the derivative of the area function with respect to \( x \) and set it to zero:
      \[
      \frac{dA}{dx} = 360 - 4x
      \]
      Setting the derivative equal to zero gives:
      \[
      360 - 4x = 0 \implies 4x = 360 \implies x = 90
      \]

      \[
      \frac{d^2A}{dx^2} = -4
      \]
      Since the second derivative is negative, the function has a maximum at \( x = 90 \).

      \[
      y = 360 - 2 \cdot 90 = 360 - 180 = 180
      \]
