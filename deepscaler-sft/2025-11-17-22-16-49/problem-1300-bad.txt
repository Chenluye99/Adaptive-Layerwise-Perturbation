Next, we seek to find $\cos \theta$ where $\theta$ is the angle between $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}$.

The cosine of the angle between two vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ is given by the dot product formula:
\[
\cos \theta = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\| \|\mathbf{v}\|}
\]

In our case, $\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}$. Thus,
\[
\cos \theta = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c})}{\|\mathbf{a}\| \|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}\|}
\]

First, let's calculate the dot product $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c})$:
\[
\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c}
\]
Given that $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0$ and $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c} = 0$, this simplifies to:
\[
\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a} = \|\mathbf{a}\|^2 = k^2
\]

Next, we need to calculate the magnitude of $\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}$:
\[
\|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}\| = \sqrt{(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) \cdot (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c})}
\]
Expanding the dot product:
\[
(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) \cdot (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c} \cdot \mathbf{c} + 2 (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c} + \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c})

\[

So,
