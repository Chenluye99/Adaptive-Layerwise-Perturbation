To solve for the greatest common factor (GCF) of 518 and 294, we can use the Euclidean algorithm. This algorithm is efficient and involves a series of division steps in which the divisor in each step becomes the dividend in the next step, and the remainder becomes the new divisor. The process continues until the remainder is zero. The divisor at that step will be the GCF.

The steps for the Euclidean algorithm can be outlined as follows:

1. Divide 518 by 294 and find the remainder.
2. Replace 518 with 294 and 294 with the remainder from the previous step.
3. Repeat the process until the remainder is 0. The last non-zero remainder is the GCF.

Let's implement this in Python to get the accurate result.
```python
def gcd(a, b):
    while b:
        a, b = b, a % b
    return a

# Find the GCD of 518 and 294
gcd_result = gcd(518, 294)
print(gcd_result)
```
Code execution result: 14

Thus, the greatest common factor of 518 and 294 is \(\boxed{14}\). This is the final answer.
