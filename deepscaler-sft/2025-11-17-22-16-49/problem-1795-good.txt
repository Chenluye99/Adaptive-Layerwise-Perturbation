First, let's denote the angles involved in the problem.

Let \(\angle BOC = \theta\). Given that \(\angle AOD = 3.5 \cdot \angle BOC\), we can say that \(\angle AOD = 3.5 \theta\).

Since \(\overrightarrow{OA} \perp \overrightarrow{OC}\), we know that \(\angle AOC = 90^\circ\). Similarly, since \(\overrightarrow{OB} \perp \overrightarrow{OD}\), we know that \(\angle BOD = 90^\circ\).

Because the sum of all angles around point \(O\) is \(360^\circ\), we can write the equation for the angles around point \(O\) as follows:
\[
\angle AOC + \angle BOC + \angle BOD + \angle AOD = 360^\circ.
\]
Substitute the known values along with the relationships between \(\angle AOD\) and \(\angle BOC\):
\[
90^\circ + \theta + 90^\circ + 3.5 \theta = 360^\circ.
\]
Combine like terms:
\[
180^\circ + 4.5 \theta = 360^\circ.
\]
Isolate the term with \(\theta\):
\[
4.5 \theta = 180^\circ.
\]
Solve for \(\theta\):
\[
\theta = \frac{180^\circ}{4.5} = 40^\circ.
\]
Now that we have \(\theta = 40^\circ\), we can find \(\angle AOD\):
\[
\angle AOD = 3.5 \theta = 3.5 \times 40^\circ = 140^\circ.
\]
