To solve the problem of simplifying \(\frac{3 + 4i}{1 + 2i}\), we need to perform complex number division. To divide by a complex number, we multiply the numerator and the denominator by the complex conjugate of the denominator.

1. The complex conjugate of \(1 + 2i\) is \(1 - 2i\).
2. Multiply both the numerator and the denominator by this conjugate:
\[
\frac{3 + 4i}{1 + 2i} \cdot \frac{1 - 2i}{1 - 2i} = \frac{(3 + 4i)(1 - 2i)}{(1 + 2i)(1 - 2i)}
\]

3. First, compute the denominator:
\[
(1 + 2i)(1 - 2i) = 1^2 - (2i)^2 = 1 - (-4) = 1 + 4 = 5
\]

4. Next, compute the numerator:
\[
(3 + 4i)(1 - 2i) = 3 \cdot 1 + 3 \cdot (-2i) + 4i \cdot 1 + 4i \cdot (-2i) = 3 - 6i + 4i - 8i^2
\]
Since \(i^2 = -1\), we have:
\[
- 8i^2 = - 8(-1) = 8
\]
Thus:
\[
3 - 6i + 4i + 8 = 11 - 2i
\]

5. So, the expression becomes:
\[
\frac{11 - 2i}{5} = \frac{11}{5} - \frac{2}{5}i
\]

The final answer is:
\[
\frac{11}{5} - \frac{2}{5}i
\]
