To solve this problem, we need to calculate the lengths of all three sides of the triangle with the given vertices, and then determine which of these lengths is the longest. The vertices of the triangle are given as \((11, 1)\), \((2, 3)\), and \((3, 7)\).

We can use the distance formula to find the lengths of the sides of the triangle. The distance formula between two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is given by:
\[ \text{Distance} = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} \]

Let's denote the vertices as follows:
- \(A = (11, 1)\)
- \(B = (2, 3)\)
- \(C = (3, 7)\)

We will calculate the lengths of the sides \(AB\), \(BC\), and \(CA\) using the distance formula.

1. Calculate the length of side \(AB\):
\[ AB = \sqrt{(2 - 11)^2 + (3 - 1)^2} = \sqrt{(-9)^2 + (2)^2} = \sqrt{81 + 4} = \sqrt{85} \]

2. Calculate the length of side \(BC\):
\[ BC = \sqrt{(3 - 2)^2 + (7 - 3)^2} = \sqrt{(1)^2 + (4)^2} = \sqrt{1 + 16} = \sqrt{17} \]

3. Calculate the length of side \(CA\):
\[ CA = \sqrt{(3 - 11)^2 + (7 - 1)^2} = \sqrt{(-8)^2 + (6)^2} = \sqrt{64 + 36} = \sqrt{100} = 10 \]

Now, we compare the lengths of these sides to find the longest side:
- \(AB = \sqrt{85}\)
- \(BC = \sqrt{17}\)
- \(CA = 10\)

To determine which of these is the longest, we can compare the values directly. We can also square the lengths to avoid dealing with square roots if necessary, but in this case, it is clear that 10 is the largest value when compared to \(\sqrt{85}\) and \(\sqrt{17}\), because both 85 and 17 are much smaller than 100.

So, the longest side of the triangle is \(CA\) with a length of 10 units. Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python and getting the precise lengths and the maximum value.
```python
import math
```
