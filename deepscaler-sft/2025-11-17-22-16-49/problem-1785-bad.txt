First, let's start by recalling some vector identities and properties. Using the vector triple product identity, we have:

\[
\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c}) \mathbf{b} - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}) \mathbf{c}
\]

According to the problem, we are given that:

\[
\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = \frac{\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}}{\sqrt{2}}
\]

By equating the two expressions for \(\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})\), we get:

\[
(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c}) \mathbf{b} - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}) \mathbf{c} = \frac{\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}}{\sqrt{2}}
\]

Since \(\mathbf{b}\) and \(\mathbf{c}\) are linearly independent, we can equate the coefficients of \(\mathbf{b}\) and \(\mathbf{c}\) from both sides of the equation:

\[
(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c}) \mathbf{b} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \mathbf{b}
\]
\[
-(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}) \mathbf{c} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \mathbf{c}
\]

From the above, we get:

\[
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\]
\[
-\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \implies \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\]

Now, \(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \cos \theta\), where \(\theta\) is the angle between \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\). Therefore, we have:

\[
\cos \theta = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\]

The angle \(\theta\) that satisfies this equation is:

\[
\theta = 135^\circ
\]

import numpy as np
