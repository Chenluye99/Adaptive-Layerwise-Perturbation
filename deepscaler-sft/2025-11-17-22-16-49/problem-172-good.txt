First, let's understand the problem. We have 2017 points on a circle, and each point is distinct. We need to find the maximum number of chords that can intersect with a line passing through \(A_1\) but not through any of the points \(A_2, \ldots, A_{2017}\).

2. **Intersection Condition:**
    - A chord will intersect with a line passing through \(A_1\) if and only if one of its endpoints is on either side of the line.
    - To maximize the number of chords that intersect with this line, we should consider the arrangement that places as many chords as possible so that their endpoints are on opposite sides of the line.

3. **Maximizing Intersection:**
