To solve this problem, let's define the problem more formally.

1. **Problem Description:**
    - We have two mathematicians, each with a lifespan of 100 years.
    - Each mathematician is equally likely to be born at any point during a 500-year period.
    - We need to find the probability that the two mathematicians' lifespans overlap, i.e., they were contemporaries for any length of time.

2. **Formulation:**
    - Let's denote the birth years of the two mathematicians as \(B_1\) and \(B_2\) respectively, where both \(B_1\) and \(B_2\) are uniformly distributed over the interval [0, 500].
    - The first mathematician will be alive from year \(B_1\) to \(B_1 + 100\).
    - The second mathematician will be alive from year \(B_2\) to \(B_2 + 100\).
    - For the two mathematicians to have been contemporaries, there needs to be some overlap in the years they were both alive, i.e., the intervals \([B_1, B_1 + 100]\) and \([B_2, B_2 + 100]\) must intersect.

3. **Mathematical Condition for Overlap:**
    - The intervals overlap if and only if the start of one interval is before the end of the other interval. Mathematically, this means:
      \[
      B_1 < B_2 + 100 \quad \text{and} \quad B_2 < B_1 + 100
      \]
    - Rearrange these inequalities, we get:
      \[
      B_1 - B_2 < 100 \quad \text{and} \quad B_2 - B_1 < 100
      \]
    - This can also be written as:
      \[
      -100 < B_1 - B_2 < 100 \quad \text{or} \quad |B_1 - B_2| < 100
      \]

4. **Integral Solution:**
    - To find the probability, we need to integrate over the region where this condition holds true over the 500 by 500 square defined by the birth years of the two mathematicians.
    - The probability is given by the integral of the condition \(|B_1 - B_2| < 100\) over the square \([0, 500] \times [0, 500]\), divided by the total area of the square, which is 500 * 500 = 250000.

Let's implement this in Python to get the accurate result.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
B1, B2 = sp.symbols('B1 B2')

# Define the constraints for the integrand based on our condition |B1 - B2| < 100
condition = (B1 - B2 < 100) & (B2 - B1 < 100)

# Set up the double integral over [0, 500] x [0, 500]
integral = sp.integrate(sp.Piecewise((1, condition), (0, True)), (B1, 0, 500), (B2, 0, 500))

# Calculate the probability as the ratio of the integral to the area of the square [0, 500] x [0, 500]
probability = integral / 500**2
print(probability)
```
Code execution result: 9/25

Thus, the probability that the two mathematicians were contemporaries for any length of time is \(\boxed{\frac{9}{25}}\). This is the final answer.
