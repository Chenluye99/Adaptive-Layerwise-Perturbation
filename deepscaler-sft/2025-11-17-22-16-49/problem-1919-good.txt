1. **Determine the circumference of the ceiling fan:**
    - The diameter of the ceiling fan is 6 feet.
    - The circumference \( C \) of a circle is given by the formula:
      \[
      C = \pi \times \text{diameter}
      \]
    - Substituting the given diameter:
      \[
      C = \pi \times 6 = 6\pi \text{ feet}
      \]

2. **Find out how many revolutions the fan makes to travel \( 19,404 \pi \) feet:**
    - If the fan makes 20 revolutions per minute, then in one revolution, the distance traveled by a point on the outer edge of the fan is the circumference, which is \( 6\pi \) feet.
    - To find the total number of revolutions to travel \( 19,404 \pi \) feet, we use:
      \[
      \text{Number of revolutions} = \frac{19,404 \pi}{6 \pi} = \frac{19,404}{6}
      \]
    - Perform the division:
      \[
      \frac{19,404}{6} = 3234
      \]
      So, the fan makes 3234 revolutions to travel \( 19,404 \pi \) feet.

3. **Determine the time taken for these 3234 revolutions:**
    - The fan rotates at a rate of 20 revolutions per minute.
    - To find the time taken for 3234 revolutions, we use:
      \[
      \text{Time (in minutes)} = \frac{\text{Number of revolutions}}{\text{Revolutions per minute}} = \frac{3234}{20}
      \]
    - Perform the division:
      \[
      \frac{3234}{20} = 161.7
      \]

4. **Round the result to the nearest whole number:**
    - The time obtained is 161.7 minutes. Rounding this to the nearest whole number gives 162.

Thus, the housefly had been on the fan for 162 minutes during the time it took to travel \( 19,404 \pi \) feet. The final answer is:

\(\boxed{162}\)
