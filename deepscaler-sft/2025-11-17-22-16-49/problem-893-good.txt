To solve this problem, we need to use the law of total probability. We want to find the probability that Sheila will attend the picnic, which we can denote as \( P(\text{Attend}) \).

We know the following probabilities:
- The probability that it rains is \( P(\text{Rain}) = 0.40 \).
- The probability that it is sunny is \( P(\text{Sunny}) = 1 - P(\text{Rain}) = 1 - 0.40 = 0.60 \).
- If it rains, the probability that Sheila will attend the picnic is \( P(\text{Attend} \mid \text{Rain}) = 0.20 \).
- If it is sunny, the probability that Sheila will attend the picnic is \( P(\text{Attend} \mid \text{Sunny}) = 0.80 \).

By the law of total probability, we have:
\[
P(\text{Attend}) = P(\text{Attend} \mid \text{Rain}) \cdot P(\text{Rain}) + P(\text{Attend} \mid \text{Sunny}) \cdot P(\text{Sunny}).
\]

Substituting the values we know into this equation, we get:
\[
P(\text{Attend}) = 0.20 \cdot 0.40 + 0.80 \cdot 0.60.
\]

Now, let's compute this step-by-step.
```python
# Given probabilities
P_Rain = 0.40
P_Sunny = 1 - P_Rain
P_Attend_given_Rain = 0.20
P_Attend_given_Sunny = 0.80

# Applying the law of total probability
P_Attend = P_Attend_given_Rain * P_Rain + P_Attend_given_Sunny * P_Sunny
P_Attend_percent = P_Attend * 100  # Convert to percentage

print(P_Attend_percent)
```
