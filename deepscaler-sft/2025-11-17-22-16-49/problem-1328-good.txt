To solve the inequality \(\sqrt{x^2 - x - 6} < 2x - 3\), let's break it down step-by-step:

1. **Determine the Domain of the Inequality:**

      \[
      x^2 - x - 6 \geq 0.

      \[
      x \in (-\infty, -2] \cup [3, \infty).
      \]

      \[
      2x - 3 > 0 \implies x > \frac{3}{2}.

      \[
      x \in [3, \infty).

      \[
      x^2 - x - 6 < 4x^2 - 12x + 9.
      \]
    - Rearrange the inequality to bring all terms to one side:
      \[
