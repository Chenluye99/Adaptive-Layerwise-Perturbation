First, let's start by determining the relevant radii and areas.

1. **Identify Radii:**
    - The radius of the larger circle is given as 6.
    - The smaller circles touch the larger circle at their circumferences and also touch each other at the center of the larger circle. This means that the diameter of each smaller circle is equal to the radius of the larger circle because the smaller circles touch at the center of the larger circle and their centers are at the endpoints of the diameter of the larger circle. Therefore, the radius of each smaller circle is half the radius of the larger circle, which is \(6 / 2 = 3\).

2. **Calculate Areas:**
    - **Area of the Larger Circle:**
      The formula for the area of a circle is \(\pi r^2\). For the larger circle with radius 6, the area is:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\text{large}} = \pi \cdot 6^2 = 36\pi
      \]
    - **Area of Each Smaller Circle:**
      For each smaller circle with radius 3, the area is:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\text{small}} = \pi \cdot 3^2 = 9\pi
      \]
      Since there are two smaller circles, their combined area is:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\text{2 small}} = 2 \cdot 9\pi = 18\pi
      \]
    - **Area of the Shaded Region:**
      The shaded region is the area of the larger circle minus the combined area of the two smaller circles:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\text{shaded}} = \text{Area}_{\text{large}} - \text{Area}_{\text{2 small}} = 36\pi - 18\pi = 18\pi
      \]

```python
import sympy as sp

# Given radius of the larger circle
R_large = 6

# Calculate radius of the smaller circles
R_small = R_large / 2

# Calculate areas
area_large_circle = sp.pi * R_large**2

area_two_small_circles = 2 * area_small_circle

# Calculate the area of the shaded region
area_shaded_region = area_large_circle - area_two_small_circles
print(area_shaded_region)
```
