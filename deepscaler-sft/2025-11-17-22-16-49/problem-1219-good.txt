1. **Identify the sides of triangle \(CAB\)**:

      \[
      2k = 10 \implies k = 5

      \[
      3k = 3 \cdot 5 = 15, \quad 4k = 4 \cdot 5 = 20

2. **Utilize the Angle Bisector Theorem**:
    - The Angle Bisector Theorem states that the angle bisector of an angle in a triangle divides the opposite side into two segments that are proportional to the adjacent sides. For segment \(BD\) which is the angle bisector of \(\angle ABC\) and divides \(AC\) into \(AD\) and \(DC\), the ratio of \(AD\) to \(DC\) is the same as the ratio of the other two sides of the triangle, i.e., \(AB\) to \(BC\). 
    - In this case, the sides adjacent to \(\angle ABC\) are \(AB = 15\) and \(BC = 20\). Therefore, the ratio in which \(BD\) divides \(AC\) is \(15:20 = 3:4\).

      \[
      AD = 3x \quad \text{and} \quad DC = 4x
      \]
      Such that
      \[
      AD + DC = AC \implies 3x + 4x = 10 \implies 7x = 10 \implies x = \frac{10}{7}

      \[
