To solve the problem of finding \(\tan 150^\circ\), we can use the known trigonometric identities and values. The angle \(150^\circ\) is in the second quadrant where the tangent function is negative. The reference angle for \(150^\circ\) is \(180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ\), and we know from trigonometric identities that:

\[
\tan 150^\circ = \tan (180^\circ - 30^\circ) = -\tan 30^\circ
\]

We also know that:

\[
\tan 30^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\tan 150^\circ = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}
\]

```python
import math

# Get the tangent of 150 degrees
tan_150 = math.tan(math.radians(150))

print(tan_150)
```
