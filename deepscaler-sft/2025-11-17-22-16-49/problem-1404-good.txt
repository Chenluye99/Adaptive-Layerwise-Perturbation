To solve this problem, we need to find the point of intersection of the lines given by the equations \(x + 2y = 7t + 3\) and \(x - y = 2t - 2\) for any real number \(t\). Then, we need to determine if all these intersection points lie on a line and, if so, find the slope of this line.

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Find the Intersection Point:**
    - We have the system of equations:
      \[
      \begin{cases}
      x + 2y = 7t + 3, \\
      x - y = 2t - 2.
      \end{cases}
      \]
    - To find the intersection point, we can solve this system of equations for \(x\) and \(y\) in terms of \(t\).

2. **Solve the System of Equations:**
    - We can use substitution or elimination to solve for \(x\) and \(y\).
    - Subtract the second equation from the first to eliminate \(x\):
      \[
      (x + 2y) - (x - y) = (7t + 3) - (2t - 2) \implies 3y = 5t + 5 \implies y = \frac{5t + 5}{3}.
      \]
    - Substitute \(y = \frac{5t + 5}{3}\) back into the second equation to solve for \(x\):
      \[
      x - \frac{5t + 5}{3} = 2t - 2 \implies x = 2t - 2 + \frac{5t + 5}{3}.
      \]
      To combine the terms on the right-hand side, get a common denominator:
      \[
      x = \frac{3(2t - 2) + (5t + 5)}{3} = \frac{6t - 6 + 5t + 5}{3} = \frac{11t - 1}{3}.
      \]
    - So, the intersection point is \(\left( \frac{11t - 1}{3}, \frac{5t + 5}{3} \right)\).

3. **Check If All Intersection Points Lie on a Line:**
    - To determine if all points \(\left( \frac{11t - 1}{3}, \frac{5t + 5}{3} \right)\) lie on a line, we can consider two different values of \(t\) and check if the points formed by varying \(t\) define a line.
    - Let's choose \(t_1\) and \(t_2\) and get the corresponding points \(\left( \frac{11t_1 - 1}{3}, \frac{5t_1 + 5}{3} \right)\) and \(\left( \frac{11t_2 - 1}{3}, \frac{5t_2 + 5}{3} \right)\).
    - The slope between these points should be constant regardless of the choice of \(t_1\) and \(t_2\).

4. **Find the Slope:**
    - The slope \(m\) between two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is given by:
      \[
      m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}.
      \]
    - Substituting \(\left( \frac{11t_1 - 1}{3}, \frac{5t_1 + 5}{3} \right)\) and \(\left( \frac{11t_2 - 1}{3}, \frac{5t_2 + 5}{3} \right)\) into the slope formula, we get:
      \[
      m = \frac{\frac{5t_2 + 5}{3} - \frac{5t_1 + 5}{3}}{\frac{11t_2 - 1}{3} - \frac{11t_1 - 1}{3}} = \frac{\frac{5(t_2 - t_1)}{3}}{\frac{11(t_2 - t_1)}{3}} = \frac{5(t_2 - t_1)}{11(t_2 - t_1)} = \frac{5}{11}.
      \]
    - As \(t_1\) and \(t_2\) are arbitrary, the slope is constant and equals \(\frac{5}{11}\).
