First, let's start by determining the side lengths of the equilateral triangle and the square based on their given perimeters.

1. **Perimeter of the Equilateral Triangle:**
   - An equilateral triangle has 3 sides of equal length.
   - If the perimeter of the equilateral triangle is 48 inches, then each side of the equilateral triangle, denoted as \( s_{\text{triangle}} \), is given by:
     \[
     3 \cdot s_{\text{triangle}} = 48 \implies s_{\text{triangle}} = \frac{48}{3} = 16 \text{ inches}
     \]

2. **Perimeter of the Square:**
   - A square has 4 sides of equal length.
   - If the perimeter of the square is 48 inches, then each side of the square, denoted as \( s_{\text{square}} \), is given by:
     \[
     4 \cdot s_{\text{square}} = 48 \implies s_{\text{square}} = \frac{48}{4} = 12 \text{ inches}
     \]

3. **Finding the Ratio:**
   - The ratio of the length of the side of the triangle to the length of the side of the square is:
     \[
     \frac{s_{\text{triangle}}}{s_{\text{square}}} = \frac{16}{12} = \frac{4}{3}
     \]
