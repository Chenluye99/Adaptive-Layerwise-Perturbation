### Part (Ⅰ): Determine the Smallest Positive Period of \( f(x) \)

    - The function involves trigonometric functions \(\cos x\) and \(\sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)\).
    - Both \(\cos x\) and \(\sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)\) are periodic functions with a period of \(2\pi\).

2. **Simplify the Function (if Possible):**
    - Use the angle addition formula for sine: \(\sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right) = \sin x \cos \frac{\pi}{6} + \cos x \sin \frac{\pi}{6} = \sin x \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \cos x \cdot \frac{1}{2}\).

      \]
    - Use the double-angle identities: \(\cos x \sin x = \frac{1}{2} \sin 2x\) and \(\cos^2 x = \frac{1 + \cos 2x}{2}\):
      \[

      \[
      f(x) = \sqrt{3} \sin 2x + \cos 2x = 2 \left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \sin 2x + \frac{1}{2} \cos 2x \right) = 2 \sin \left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right).
      \]
3. **Determine the Period:**

Therefore, the period of \( \sin \left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right) \) is \(\frac{2\pi}{2} = \pi\).
    - Hence, the smallest positive period of \( f(x) \) is \(\pi\).
