First, let's start by setting up the equation for the distance between the points \((2a, a - 4)\) and \((4, -1)\) and use the fact that the distance is given as \(2\sqrt{10}\).

The distance formula between two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is given by:
\[
\text{Distance} = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}
\]
Plugging in the coordinates \((2a, a - 4)\) and \((4, -1)\), we get:
\[
\text{Distance} = \sqrt{(4 - 2a)^2 + (-1 - (a - 4))^2} = \sqrt{(4 - 2a)^2 + (-1 - a + 4)^2} = \sqrt{(4 - 2a)^2 + (3 - a)^2}
\]
We are given that this distance is \(2\sqrt{10}\), so we can set up the equation:
\[
\sqrt{(4 - 2a)^2 + (3 - a)^2} = 2\sqrt{10}
\]
Square both sides to eliminate the square root:
\[
(4 - 2a)^2 + (3 - a)^2 = (2\sqrt{10})^2
\]
\[
(4 - 2a)^2 + (3 - a)^2 = 4 \cdot 10
\]
\[
(4 - 2a)^2 + (3 - a)^2 = 40
\]
Now, let's expand and simplify the left-hand side:
\[
(4 - 2a)^2 = 16 - 16a + 4a^2
\]
\[
(3 - a)^2 = 9 - 6a + a^2
\]
So we have:
\[
16 - 16a + 4a^2 + 9 - 6a + a^2 = 40
\]
Combine like terms:
\[
5a^2 - 22a + 25 = 40
\]
Move 40 to the left side of the equation:
\[
5a^2 - 22a + 25 - 40 = 0
\]
\[
5a^2 - 22a - 15 = 0
\]
We now have a standard quadratic equation \(5a^2 - 22a - 15 = 0\). To find the product of all possible values for \(a\), we use the property of quadratic equations that states the product of the roots of \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) is given by \(\frac{c}{a}\). For our equation \(5a^2 - 22a - 15 = 0\), we have \(a = 5\), \(b = -22\), and \(c = -15\). So, the product of the roots is:
\[
\frac{c}{a} = \frac{-15}{5} = -3
\]
Thus, the product of all possible values for \(a\) is \(-3\). To ensure accuracy, let's solve this quadratic equation using Python to get the roots and verify the product.

import sympy as sp
