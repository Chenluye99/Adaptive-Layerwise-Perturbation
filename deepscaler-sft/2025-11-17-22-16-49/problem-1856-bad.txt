First, let's start by setting up the problem.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have a right triangle \(ABC\) with legs of lengths 6 cm and 8 cm, and a right angle at \(A\).
    - The hypotenuse of triangle \(ABC\) can be computed using the Pythagorean theorem: \[
      \text{Hypotenuse} = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10 \, \text{cm}
    \]
    - We need to find the side length of a square such that one of its sides is on the hypotenuse, and its other two vertices are on the legs of the triangle.

2. **Set Up the Equation:**
    - Let the side length of the square be \( s \).
    - When the square is positioned as described, the top-right vertex of the square (let's call it \( D \)) will be on \( BC \), and the bottom-left and bottom-right vertices of the square will be on \( AB \) and \( AC \) respectively.
    - If we place the triangle in a coordinate system, we can set \( A = (0, 0) \), \( B = (8, 0) \), and \( C = (0, 6) \). The hypotenuse of the triangle is the line segment connecting \( B \) and \( C \).

3. **Geometric Relationship:**
    - When a square with side length \( s \) is placed as described, the top-right corner of the square will be at coordinates \((s, s)\) because the square's sides are horizontal and vertical.
    - This top-right corner point \((s, s)\) lies on the line \( BC \). The equation of the hypotenuse \( BC \) can be derived from its endpoints \( B = (8, 0) \) and \( C = (0, 6) \).

   The slope of \( BC \) is: \[
   \frac{6 - 0}{0 - 8} = -\frac{3}{4}
   \]
   So, the equation of the line \( BC \) in slope-intercept form is: \[
   y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 6
   \]
   Substitute \( (s, s) \) into this equation: \[
   s = -\frac{3}{4}s + 6
   \]

4. **Solve for \( s \):**
   Let's solve this equation step-by-step:

   \[
   s = -\frac{3}{4}s + 6
   \]
   \[
   s + \frac{3}{4}s = 6
   \]
   \[
   \frac{4}{4}s + \frac{3}{4}s = 6
   \]
   \[
   \frac{7}{4}s = 6
   \]
   \[
   s = 6 \cdot \frac{4}{7} = \frac{24}{7}
   \]

So, the side length of the square is \(\frac{24}{7}\). Let's confirm this by coding it to ensure accuracy.
```python
from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variables
s = symbols('s')

# Equation based on the geometric relationship
# s = - (3/4) * s + 6
equation = Eq(s, - (3/4) * s + 6)

# Solve for s
solution = solve(equation, s)[0]
print(solution)
```
