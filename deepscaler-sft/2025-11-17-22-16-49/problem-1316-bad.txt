To solve the problem of finding the sum of the distinct prime factors of 315, we can follow these steps:

1. **Find the Prime Factors of 315:**
    - Determine the prime factors of 315. We can do this by dividing 315 by the smallest prime number (2) and continue dividing by prime numbers until we can no longer divide evenly.
   
2. **Get the Distinct Prime Factors:**
    - From the list of prime factors, get only the distinct (unique) prime factors.

3. **Sum the Distinct Prime Factors:**
    - Calculate the sum of these distinct prime factors.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy

# Find the prime factors of 315
n = 315
prime_factors = list(sympy.primefactors(n))

# Get the distinct prime factors (though list(sympy.primefactors(n)) inherently provides distinct prime factors)
distinct_prime_factors = set(prime_factors)

# Calculate the sum of the distinct prime factors
sum_of_distinct_prime_factors = sum(distinct_prime_factors)
print(sum_of_distinct_prime_factors)
```
Code execution result: 15

Thus, the sum of the distinct prime factors of 315 is \(\boxed{15}\). This is our final answer.
