    - The height of the entire silo is 27 meters. Let the height of the cylinder be \( h_c \). The height of the cone, \( h_{cn} \), is given to be half the height of the cylinder, so \( h_{cn} = \frac{h_c}{2} \).
    - The total height of the silo is the sum of the height of the cylinder and the height of the cone, so:
      \[
      h_c + h_{cn} = 27 \implies h_c + \frac{h_c}{2} = 27 \implies \frac{3 h_c}{2} = 27 \implies 3 h_c = 54 \implies h_c = 18
      \]
      Therefore, the height of the cone is:
      \[
      h_{cn} = \frac{h_c}{2} = \frac{18}{2} = 9
      \]

    - **Volume of the Cylinder:**

      \[
      V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi r^2 h_c

      \[
      V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi (5)^2 (18) = \pi \cdot 25 \cdot 18 = 450 \pi
      \]

    - **Volume of the Cone:**

      \[
      V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h_{cn}

    - **Total Volume of the Silo:**

      V_{\text{silo}} = V_{\text{cylinder}} + V_{\text{cone}} = 450 \pi + 75 \pi = 525 \pi
