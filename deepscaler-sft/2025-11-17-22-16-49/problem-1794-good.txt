To solve this problem, let's use the properties of ellipses and the relationship given in the problem statement.

1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - The radius of the right circular cylinder is 1. When a plane intersects the cylinder, it forms an ellipse with semi-major and semi-minor axes.
    - Let the length of the minor axis of the ellipse be \(2b\). Then, the length of the major axis of the ellipse is \(2a\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are the semi-major and semi-minor axes of the ellipse, respectively.
    - According to the problem, the major axis is 50% longer than the minor axis. This means:
      \[
      2a = 1.5 \times 2b \implies a = 1.5b
      \]
    - The semi-minor axis \(b\) of the ellipse is the same as the radius of the cylinder, so \(b = 1\). Therefore:
      \[
      a = 1.5 \times 1 = 1.5
      \]
    - The length of the major axis is \(2a = 2 \times 1.5 = 3\).

2. **Formalize and Implement:**
    - We can confirm this by writing a small Python script to validate our solution, but the mathematical reasoning above should be sufficient. However, let's implement it to ensure there are no mistakes.

Let's implement this in Python to confirm our solution.
```python
# Given that the radius of the cylinder is 1, this means that the semi-minor axis (b) of the ellipse is 1.
b = 1

# The relationship given in the problem is that the major axis (2a) is 50% longer than the minor axis (2b).
# So, we have 2a = 1.5 * 2b which simplifies to a = 1.5 * b.
a = 1.5 * b

# The length of the major axis is 2a.
major_axis_length = 2 * a
print(major_axis_length)
```
Code execution result: 3.0

Thus, the length of the major axis is \(\boxed{3}\). This confirms that our solution is accurate.
