To solve the problem of simplifying the radical $\sqrt{2940}$, we can follow these steps:

1. **Find the Prime Factorization of 2940:**
   To find the square root in its simplest radical form, it's helpful to determine the prime factorization of 2940.
   
   Let's start by dividing 2940 by the smallest prime numbers until we get 1.

   \[
   2940 \div 2 = 1470 \\
   1470 \div 2 = 735 \\
   735 \div 3 = 245 \\
   245 \div 5 = 49 \\
   49 \div 7 = 7 \\
   7 \div 7 = 1
   \]

   So, the prime factorization of 2940 is:
   \[
   2940 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7^2
   \]

2. **Simplify the Square Root:**
   Using the prime factorization, we can simplify the square root by taking out pairs of prime factors from under the square root.

   \[
   \sqrt{2940} = \sqrt{2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7^2} = \sqrt{2^2} \cdot \sqrt{7^2} \cdot \sqrt{3 \cdot 5} = 2 \cdot 7 \cdot \sqrt{15} = 14 \sqrt{15}
   \]

Thus, the simplified form of $\sqrt{2940}$ is $14 \sqrt{15}$.
