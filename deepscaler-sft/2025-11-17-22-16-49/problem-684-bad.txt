    - First, compute the total number of ways to choose 5 out of 12 CDs.
    - Then, compute the number of ways to choose 5 CDs such that at least one of the categories is missing.
    - Subtract the number of ways where at least one category is missing from the total number of ways to get the number of ways where all three categories are represented.
    - The probability is the ratio of the number of ways where all three categories are represented to the total number of ways to choose 5 CDs out of 12.

3. **Formula Setup:**
    - The total number of ways to choose 5 CDs out of 12 is given by the combination formula \( \binom{12}{5} \).
    - We use the principle of inclusion-exclusion to find the number of ways that at least one of the categories is missing.
    - Denote:
      - \( A \): The event that no rap CDs are chosen.
      - \( B \): The event that no country CDs are chosen.
      - \( C \): The event that no heavy metal CDs are chosen.
    - We need to find:
      \[
      |A^c \cap B^c \cap C^c|
      \]
      Where \( A^c, B^c, C^c \) are the complements of \( A, B, C \) respectively, i.e., the events where at least one CD from each of the three categories is chosen.
