First, let's understand what we have. We know that a full circle represents 100% of the surveyed people, and a full circle has 360 degrees. The central angle of the "Pop" sector is given as \(251^\circ\).

To find out how many of the 472 people chose "Pop", we can use the proportion of the central angle of the "Pop" sector to the full circle:

\[
\text{Number of people who chose "Pop"} = \left( \frac{251}{360} \right) \times 472
\]

We can solve this equation using Python to get an accurate result.
```python
# Given data
total_people = 472
angle_pop = 251
full_circle_degrees = 360

# Calculate the number of people who chose "Pop"
num_people_who_chose_pop = (angle_pop / full_circle_degrees) * total_people
num_people_who_chose_pop_rounded = round(num_people_who_chose_pop)

print(num_people_who_chose_pop_rounded)
```
Code execution result: 329

Thus, the number of people who chose "Pop" is \(\boxed{329}\). This is our final answer.
