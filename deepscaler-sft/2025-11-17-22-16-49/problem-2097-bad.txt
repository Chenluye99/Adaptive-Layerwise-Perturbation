\[ r_1 + r_2 = s \]

\[ r_1^2 + r_2^2 = (r_1 + r_2)^2 - 2r_1 r_2 = s^2 - 2p \]

\[ s = s^2 - 2p \]
\[ s^2 - s - 2p = 0 \]

This is a quadratic equation in terms of \( s \) and \( p \). We can solve this equation for \( p \):

\[ p = \frac{s^2 - s}{2} \]

\[ r_1^3 + r_2^3 = (r_1 + r_2)(r_1^2 - r_1 r_2 + r_2^2) = s \cdot (s^2 - 3pr) \]

Setting this equal to \( s \):

\[ s = s \cdot (s^2 - 3pr) \]

If \( s \neq 0 \), we can divide both sides by \( s \):

\[ 1 = s^2 - 3pr \]

Substitute \( p = \frac{s^2 - s}{2} \) into the above equation:

\[ 1 = s^2 - \frac{3s (s^2 - s)}{2} \]
