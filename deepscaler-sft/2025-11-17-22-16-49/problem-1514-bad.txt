First, let's break down the problem. We need to determine in how many ways parents can assign 3 different service providers to Laura and her 2 younger brothers such that no two of the three children get the same service provider.

1. **Identify the Problem Constraints:**
    - There are 20 different service providers.
    - There are 3 children (Laura and her 2 younger brothers).
    - Each child must get a service provider that no other child gets, meaning the choices are distinct for each child.

2. **Formulate the Solution Mathematically:**
    - We need to choose 3 distinct service providers from 20 and assign them to the 3 children. The number of ways to choose 3 distinct service providers from 20 is given by permutations because the order in which we assign the service providers to the children matters.
    - The number of ways to choose 3 distinct service providers from 20 is \( P(20, 3) \), which is calculated as \( \frac{20!}{(20-3)!} = 20 \times 19 \times 18 \).

Let's proceed with the Python code to compute this.
```python
import math

# Number of ways to assign 3 distinct service providers to 3 children from 20 service providers
num_service_providers = 20
num_children = 3

# Calculate permutations P(20, 3)
num_ways = math.perm(num_service_providers, num_children)
print(num_ways)
```
Code execution result: 6840

Thus, the number of ways the parents can grant the children their phones such that no two children get the same service provider is \(\boxed{6840}\). This result is obtained by computing the permutations of choosing 3 distinct service providers from 20 and assigning them to the 3 children.
