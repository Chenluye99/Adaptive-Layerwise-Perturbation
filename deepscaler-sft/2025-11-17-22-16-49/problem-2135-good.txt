Let's start by setting up the problem and solve it step-by-step.

1. **Define the Problem:**
    - We have three known lucky numbers: 57, 13, and 72.
    - Let the fourth lucky number be \( x \), which is a two-digit positive integer, so \( 10 \leq x \leq 99 \).
    - The sum of the digits of 57 is \( 5 + 7 = 12 \).
    - The sum of the digits of 13 is \( 1 + 3 = 4 \).
    - The sum of the digits of 72 is \( 7 + 2 = 9 \).
    - Let the sum of the digits of \( x \) be \( S(x) \).
    - The sum of all four numbers is \( 57 + 13 + 72 + x = 142 + x \).
    - The sum of the digits of all four numbers is \( 12 + 4 + 9 + S(x) = 25 + S(x) \).
    - According to the problem, the sum of the digits of all four numbers equals \(\frac{1}{5}\) of the sum of all four numbers:
      \[
      25 + S(x) = \frac{1}{5} (142 + x)
      \]
    - We can multiply both sides of the equation by 5 to clear the fraction:
      \[
      5 (25 + S(x)) = 142 + x \implies 125 + 5 S(x) = 142 + x \implies 5 S(x) - x = 17
      \]
    - Rearrange this equation to:
      \[
      5 S(x) - x = 17 \implies x = 5 S(x) - 17
      \]
    - Now, we need to check all possible values of \( x \) (from 10 to 99) to find the smallest possibility that satisfies this equation and ensures that \( S(x) \) is the sum of the digits of \( x \).

2. **Implement in Python:**
    - We will iterate over all possible values of \( x \) from 10 to 99, compute \( S(x) \), and check if the equation \( x = 5 S(x) - 17 \) holds.

Let's write the Python code to find the solution.
```python
# Initialize a variable to store the smallest valid x found
smallest_x = None

# Iterate through all two-digit numbers to check the condition
for x in range(10, 100):
    sum_of_digits_x = sum(int(digit) for digit in str(x))  # Calculate the sum of the digits of x
    if 5 * sum_of_digits_x - x == 17:  # Check if the equation holds
        smallest_x = x
        break  # We want the smallest possibility, so we can break once we find the first valid solution

print(smallest_x)
```
