By the AM-GM Inequality, we have:
\[
x^3 + 2y^3 + 4z^3 \geq 3 \sqrt[3]{x^3 \cdot 2y^3 \cdot 4z^3} = 3 \sqrt[3]{8 \cdot x^3 y^3 z^3} = 3 \sqrt[3]{8} \sqrt[3]{x^3 y^3 z^3} = 3 \cdot 2 \cdot xyz = 6xyz.
\]
Thus, we have:
\[
\frac{x^3 + 2y^3 + 4z^3}{xyz} \geq \frac{6xyz}{xyz} = 6.
\]
