We begin by understanding the problem and drawing it out logically.

1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - There are 24 square posts.
    - These posts are arranged to enclose a square field, meaning there are 4 sides of the square field.
    - Each post is 4 inches wide.
    - Adjacent posts have 5 feet between them.

2. **Setting Up the Solution:**
    - First, convert all units to be consistent. We will use feet because the distances between posts are given in feet, and the width of each post is given in inches. 
    - Note that 4 inches is equal to \(\frac{4}{12} = \frac{1}{3}\) feet.
    - To determine the side length of the square, consider how the posts and the gaps between them contribute to the overall perimeter. Each side of the square is formed by several posts and the spaces between them.

3. **Determining the Number of Posts Per Side:**
    - Since it's a square field and there are 24 posts placed around it, let's denote the number of posts on each side of the square as \( n \). 
    - Each corner post would be shared by two sides, so if there are \( n \) posts per side, the total count of posts would be \( 4 \times n - 4 \) (we subtract 4 because the 4 corner posts would have been counted twice).
    - Therefore, we have:
      \[
      4n - 4 = 24 \implies 4n = 28 \implies n = 7
      \]
    - So, there are 7 posts per side of the square.

4. **Calculating the Length of Each Side of the Square:**
    - Each side of the square contains 7 posts. With each post being 4 inches (which is \(\frac{1}{3}\) feet), there are \( 7 - 1 = 6 \) gaps of 5 feet each between these posts.
    - The total length contributed by the gaps between the posts along one side is \( 6 \times 5 = 30 \) feet.
    - The total length contributed by the posts along one side is \( 7 \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{7}{3} \) feet.
    - Thus, the total length of one side of the square field is:
      \[
      30 + \frac{7}{3} = \frac{90}{3} + \frac{7}{3} = \frac{97}{3} \text{ feet}
      \]

5. **Calculating the Outer Perimeter:**
    - The perimeter of a square is four times the length of one of its sides. Thus, the outer perimeter of the fence is:
      \[
      4 \times \frac{97}{3} = \frac{388}{3} \text{ feet}
      \]

We can express \(\frac{388}{3}\) as a mixed number to get the final answer.

Let's perform this calculation using Python to ensure accuracy.
