To solve for \( XY \) in the given right triangle, we need to use the properties of the triangle and trigonometric relationships. Let's analyze the given problem step-by-step.

1. **Identify the Triangle Properties:**

    - The angle at \( Y \) is \( 45^\circ \).
    - The length of \( RZ \) is \( 12 \sqrt{2} \).

2. **Determine the Properties Based on the Angle:**
    - Since the angle at \( Y \) is \( 45^\circ \), and it is a right triangle, the triangle must be a 45-45-90 triangle. In a 45-45-90 triangle, the legs are of equal length, and the hypotenuse is \(\sqrt{2}\) times the length of each leg.

3. **Set Up Relationships:**
    - In a 45-45-90 triangle, if one of the legs is \( a \), then the other leg is also \( a \), and the hypotenuse is \( a \sqrt{2} \).
    - Here, \( RZ \) is the hypotenuse of this right triangle and it is given as \( 12 \sqrt{2} \).
    - Let the length of each of the legs \( XZ \) and \( XY \) be \( a \). Then, the hypotenuse would be \( a \sqrt{2} \).

4. **Formulate the Equation:**
    - Since the hypotenuse is given as \( 12 \sqrt{2} \):
      \[
      a \sqrt{2} = 12 \sqrt{2}
      \]
    - By cancelling \(\sqrt{2}\) from both sides, we get:
      \[
      a = 12
      \]
    - Therefore, both legs of the triangle, \( XZ \) and \( XY \), are of length 12.

Let's write this in Python to confirm our result:

```python
import sympy as sp

# We assign the length of XY as 'a' and set up the equation based on the properties of a 45-45-90 triangle

# We know that for a 45-45-90 triangle, the hypotenuse is a * sqrt(2)

# Equation representing the relationship: a * sqrt(2) = 12 * sqrt(2)
```
