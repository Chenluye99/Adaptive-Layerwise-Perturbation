To solve this problem, we will use some properties of the triangle and formulas related to the inscribed circle (incircle).

1. **Properties and Formulas:**
    - **Semiperimeter (s) of the Triangle:** The semiperimeter \( s \) of a triangle with sides \( a \), \( b \), and \( c \) is given by
      \[
      s = \frac{a + b + c}{2}
      \]
    - **Area of the Triangle using Heron's Formula:** The area \( \Delta \) of a triangle with sides \( a \), \( b \), and \( c \) can be found using Heron's formula:
      \[
      \Delta = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}
      \]
    - **Radius of the Inscribed Circle (Inradius) (r):** The radius of the incircle of a triangle is given by
      \[
      r = \frac{\Delta}{s}
      \]

2. **Identify the Triangle's Sides:**
    - For triangle \( ABC \) with \( AB = AC = 7 \) and \( BC = 6 \), we identify the sides as:
      - \( a = 7 \)
      - \( b = 7 \)
      - \( c = 6 \)

3. **Calculate the Semiperimeter:**
    - Substituting the values into the formula for the semiperimeter:
      \[
      s = \frac{7 + 7 + 6}{2} = \frac{20}{2} = 10
      \]

4. **Calculate the Area Using Heron's Formula:**
    - Substitute \( s = 10 \), \( a = 7 \), \( b = 7 \), and \( c = 6 \) into Heron's formula:
      \[
      \Delta = \sqrt{10 (10 - 7) (10 - 7) (10 - 6)} = \sqrt{10 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 \cdot 4} = \sqrt{360} = 6 \sqrt{10}
      \]

5. **Calculate the Radius of the Inscribed Circle:**
    - Substitute \( \Delta = 6 \sqrt{10} \) and \( s = 10 \) into the formula for the inradius:
      \[
      r = \frac{\Delta}{s} = \frac{6 \sqrt{10}}{10} = \frac{3 \sqrt{10}}{5}
      \]

Let's confirm this by implementing the steps in Python.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given side lengths
a = 7
b = 7
c = 6

# Calculate the semiperimeter
s = (a + b + c) / 2

# Calculate the area using Heron's formula
area = sp.sqrt(s * (s - a) * (s - b) * (s - c))

# Calculate the radius of the inscribed circle
r = area / s
print(r)
```
Code execution result: 1.89736659610103
