To solve the problem, we need to evaluate \(\lfloor 17.2 \rfloor + \lfloor -17.2 \rfloor\).

1. The floor function, denoted by \(\lfloor x \rfloor\), returns the greatest integer less than or equal to \(x\).
2. \(\lfloor 17.2 \rfloor = 17\) because 17 is the greatest integer that is less than or equal to 17.2.
3. \(\lfloor -17.2 \rfloor = -18\) because -18 is the greatest integer that is less than or equal to -17.2.

So, we need to compute:
\[
\lfloor 17.2 \rfloor + \lfloor -17.2 \rfloor = 17 + (-18) = -1
\]

```python
import math
```
