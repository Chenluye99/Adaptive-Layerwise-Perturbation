To solve this problem, let's use some properties of complex numbers and computational verification.

1. **Properties of Pure Imaginary Numbers:**
   - If \(\frac{z_2}{z_1}\) is pure imaginary, then \(\frac{z_2}{z_1} = bi\) for some real number \(b\), i.e., \(z_2 = bi \cdot z_1\).
   - This means that \(z_2 = bi \cdot z_1\) for some real \(b \neq 0\).

2. **Expression to Evaluate:**
   - We need to evaluate \(\left| \frac{2z_1 + 7z_2}{2z_1 - 7z_2} \right|\).

3. **Substitute \(z_2 = bi \cdot z_1\):**
   - Substitute \(z_2 = bi \cdot z_1\) into the expression \(\frac{2z_1 + 7z_2}{2z_1 - 7z_2}\):
     \[
     \frac{2z_1 + 7(z_1 \cdot bi)}{2z_1 - 7(z_1 \cdot bi)} = \frac{2z_1 + 7bi \cdot z_1}{2z_1 - 7bi \cdot z_1} = \frac{z_1 (2 + 7bi)}{z_1 (2 - 7bi)} = \frac{2 + 7bi}{2 - 7bi}.
     \]
   - The \(z_1\) terms cancel out because \(z_1 \neq 0\) (otherwise, the problem constraints would be trivially satisfied).

4. **Find the Magnitude:**
   - To find the magnitude of \(\frac{2 + 7bi}{2 - 7bi}\), we can use the property that the magnitude of a quotient of two complex numbers is the quotient of their magnitudes:
     \[
     \left| \frac{2 + 7bi}{2 - 7bi} \right| = \frac{|2 + 7bi|}{|2 - 7bi|}.
     \]
   - The magnitude of a complex number \(a + ci\) is given by \(\sqrt{a^2 + c^2}\). Thus,
     \[
     |2 + 7bi| = \sqrt{2^2 + (7b)^2} = \sqrt{4 + 49b^2},
     \]
     \[
     |2 - 7bi| = \sqrt{2^2 + (-7b)^2} = \sqrt{4 + 49b^2}.
     \]
   - Therefore,
     \[
     \left| \frac{2 + 7bi}{2 - 7bi} \right| = \frac{\sqrt{4 + 49b^2}}{\sqrt{4 + 49b^2}} = 1.
     \]

Hence, the magnitude is 1. We can confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure our solution is accurate.
