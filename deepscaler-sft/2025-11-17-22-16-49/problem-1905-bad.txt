1. **Equation of the Line:**
   The equation of the line is \( y = \frac{5}{2} x + 4 \). Any point on this line can be written as \((x, \frac{5}{2} x + 4)\). So, a general vector on this line can be written as:
   \[
   \mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ \frac{5}{2} x + 4 \end{pmatrix}
   \]
   for any value of \( x \).

2. **Vector Projection:**
   The projection of a vector \(\mathbf{v}\) onto another vector \(\mathbf{w}\) is given by:
   \[
   \text{proj}_{\mathbf{w}} \mathbf{v} = \frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w}}{\mathbf{w} \cdot \mathbf{w}} \mathbf{w}
   \]
   For the projection to always result in the same vector \(\mathbf{p}\) for any \(\mathbf{v}\) on the line, it implies that \(\mathbf{p}\) must be in the direction of \(\mathbf{w}\) and that the projection formula simplifies such that \(\mathbf{p}\) is independent of \(x\).

3. **Finding \(\mathbf{p}\):**

   - For \( x = 2 \), the vector is \(\mathbf{v_2} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ \frac{5}{2} \cdot 2 + 4 = 9 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 9 \end{pmatrix}\).
