Let's start by defining the angle in question as \(\theta\).

1. **Definitions:**
    - The supplement of an angle \(\theta\) is \(180^\circ - \theta\).
    - The complement of an angle \(\theta\) is \(90^\circ - \theta\).

2. **Formulating the Equation:**
    - According to the problem, the supplement of the angle is six times its complement. This gives us the equation:
      \[
      180^\circ - \theta = 6 \cdot (90^\circ - \theta)
      \]

3. **Solving the Equation:**
    - Let's solve this equation step-by-step.
      \[
      180 - \theta = 6 \cdot (90 - \theta)
      \]
      \[
      180 - \theta = 540 - 6\theta
      \]
      \[
      180 - \theta + 6\theta = 540
      \]
      \[
      180 + 5\theta = 540
      \]
      \[
      5\theta = 540 - 180
      \]
      \[
      5\theta = 360
      \]
      \[
      \theta = \frac{360}{5}
      \]
      \[
      \theta = 72
      \]

Let's verify this solution by coding it in Python to ensure its accuracy.
```python
from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variable
theta = symbols('theta')

# Create the equation based on the problem statement
equation = Eq(180 - theta, 6 * (90 - theta))

# Solve the equation
solution = solve(equation, theta)[0]
print(solution)
```
