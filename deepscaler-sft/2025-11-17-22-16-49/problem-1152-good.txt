First, let's start by understanding the problem. Two angles, \( A \) and \( B \), are complementary, which means their measures add up to 90 degrees. We are also given that the measures of angles \( A \) and \( B \) are in the ratio of 7 to 23. 

Let's say the measures of the angles \( A \) and \( B \) are \( 7k \) and \( 23k \) respectively. Since \( A \) and \( B \) are complementary, we have:
\[ 7k + 23k = 90 \]
\[ 30k = 90 \]
\[ k = 3 \]

Therefore, the measures of angles \( A \) and \( B \) are:
\[ A = 7k = 7 \cdot 3 = 21 \text{ degrees} \]
\[ B = 23k = 23 \cdot 3 = 69 \text{ degrees} \]

Next, we need to find the complements of angles \( A \) and \( B \):
\[ \text{Complement of } A = 90 - A = 90 - 21 = 69 \text{ degrees} \]
\[ \text{Complement of } B = 90 - B = 90 - 69 = 21 \text{ degrees} \]

We are asked to find the ratio of the measure of the complement of angle \( A \) to the measure of the complement of angle \( B \):
\[ \text{Ratio} = \frac{\text{Complement of } A}{\text{Complement of } B} = \frac{69}{21} \]

To express this ratio as a common fraction, we need to simplify \(\frac{69}{21}\). We can find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 69 and 21, and then divide both the numerator and the denominator by this GCD. 

Let's use Python to get the GCD and simplify the fraction.
```python
import math

# Values of the complements
complement_A = 69
complement_B = 21

# Get the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 69 and 21
gcd = math.gcd(complement_A, complement_B)

# Simplify the fraction
simplified_numerator = complement_A // gcd
simplified_denominator = complement_B // gcd

simplified_fraction = (simplified_numerator, simplified_denominator)
print(simplified_fraction)
```
Code execution result: (23, 7)

Thus, the ratio of the measure of the complement of angle \( A \) to the measure of the complement of angle \( B \) is \(\frac{23}{7}\).

So, the final answer is:
\(\boxed{\frac{23}{7}}\)
