To solve the problem of finding the number of ways to choose a 2-person committee from a group of 5 people, we need to calculate the combination of 5 people taken 2 at a time, which is represented mathematically as \( \binom{5}{2} \).

The formula for combinations is given by:

\[
\binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}
\]

Plugging in \( n = 5 \) and \( r = 2 \):

\[
\binom{5}{2} = \frac{5!}{2!(5-2)!} = \frac{5!}{2! \cdot 3!} = \frac{5 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1}{2 \times 1 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1} = \frac{5 \times 4}{2 \times 1} = \frac{20}{2} = 10
\]

We can confirm this result using Python code. Let's implement it.
```python
import math

# Calculate the combination of 5 choose 2
n = 5
r = 2
num_ways = math.comb(n, r)
print(num_ways)
```
Code execution result: 10

Thus, the number of ways to choose a 2-person committee from a group of 5 people is \(\boxed{10}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
