1. **Geometric Reasoning:**
    - When the ball is removed, it leaves a spherical cap in the ice.
    - The spherical cap has a base diameter of 24 cm, so its base radius is 12 cm.
    - The depth of the spherical cap is 8 cm.
    - For a spherical cap, the relation between the radius of the sphere \(R\), the radius of the base of the cap \(a\), and the depth \(h\) of the cap is given by the formula:
      \[
      R = \frac{h^2 + a^2}{2h}
      \]
    - Substituting \(a = 12\) and \(h = 8\) into the formula, we get:
      \[
      R = \frac{8^2 + 12^2}{2 \cdot 8} = \frac{64 + 144}{16} = \frac{208}{16} = 13
      \]

2. **Verification Using Python:**
    - We can verify this result by implementing it in Python and ensuring there are no errors in the computation.

```python
# Define the variables for the base radius of the cap and the depth of the cap
```
