To evaluate the determinant of the given matrix
\[
\begin{vmatrix} 
1 & x & y \\ 
1 & x + y & y \\ 
1 & x & x + y 
\end{vmatrix},
\]
we can use the formula for the determinant of a 3x3 matrix. For a general 3x3 matrix
\[
\begin{vmatrix}
a & b & c \\ 
d & e & f \\ 
g & h & i 
\end{vmatrix},
\]
the determinant is given by:
\[
a(ei - fh) - b(di - fg) + c(dh - eg).
\]

For our specific matrix, we substitute \(a = 1\), \(b = x\), \(c = y\), \(d = 1\), \(e = x + y\), \(f = y\), \(g = 1\), \(h = x\), and \(i = x + y\). So, the determinant is:
\[
\begin{vmatrix} 
1 & x & y \\ 
1 & x + y & y \\ 
1 & x & x + y 
\end{vmatrix} = 1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} x + y & y \\ x & x + y \end{vmatrix} - x \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 1 & y \\ 1 & x + y \end{vmatrix} + y \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 1 & x + y \\ 1 & x \end{vmatrix}.
\]

We then need to compute each of the 2x2 determinants:
\[
\begin{vmatrix} x + y & y \\ x & x + y \end{vmatrix} = (x + y)(x + y) - (y)(x) = (x + y)^2 - xy,
\]
\[
\begin{vmatrix} 1 & y \\ 1 & x + y \end{vmatrix} = (1)(x + y) - (y)(1) = x + y - y = x,
\]
\[
\begin{vmatrix} 1 & x + y \\ 1 & x \end{vmatrix} = (1)(x) - (x + y)(1) = x - (x + y) = x - x - y = -y.
\]

Substitute these back into the expression for the determinant of the 3x3 matrix, we get:
\[
1 \cdot ((x + y)^2 - xy) - x \cdot x + y \cdot (-y) = (x + y)^2 - xy - x^2 - y^2.
\]

Now, let's simplify \((x + y)^2 - xy - x^2 - y^2\):
\[
(x + y)^2 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2,
\]
so,
\[
(x + y)^2 - xy - x^2 - y^2 = (x^2 + 2xy + y^2) - xy - x^2 - y^2 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2 - xy - x^2 - y^2 = xy.
\]

Thus, the determinant of the matrix is \(xy\). Let's verify this by implementing it in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
x, y = sp.symbols('x y')

# Define the matrix
matrix = sp.Matrix([
    [1, x, y],
    [1, x + y, y],
    [1, x, x + y]
])

# Compute the determinant
determinant = matrix.det()
print(determinant)
```
Code execution result: x*y

Thus, the determinant of the matrix \(\begin{vmatrix} 1 & x & y \\ 1 & x + y & y \\ 1 & x & x + y \end{vmatrix}\) is \(\boxed{xy}\). This confirms our analytical solution is accurate.
