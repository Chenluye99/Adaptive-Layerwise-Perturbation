First, let's understand the geometric problem and solve it step-by-step.

1. **Problem Understanding**:
    - We have two concentric circles (circles with a common center).
    - Chord AB of the larger circle is 80 units long and it is tangent to the smaller circle.
    - We need to find the area of the shaded region, which is the area of the larger circle minus the area of the smaller circle.

2. **Geometric Properties and Calculations**:
    - The chord AB is tangent to the smaller circle, which means a radius of the smaller circle is perpendicular to the chord AB at the midpoint of AB.
    - Let's say the center of the circles is \(O\) and the radius of the smaller circle is \(r_1\) and the radius of the larger circle is \(r_2\).
    - The midpoint of AB is the point of tangency, and since AB has a length of 80 units, the distance from the center \(O\) to the midpoint of AB is a radius of the smaller circle (because it is perpendicular and tangent to AB at the midpoint).
    - If we consider a right triangle formed by half of the chord AB, the radius of the larger circle, and the radius of the smaller circle, the half-length of the chord is 40 (since 80/2 = 40).
    - We have a right triangle with legs of lengths 40 (half the length of chord AB) and \(r_1\) (radius of the smaller circle), and the hypotenuse is the radius of the larger circle \(r_2\). By the Pythagorean theorem:
      \[
      r_2^2 = 40^2 + r_1^2
      \]

4. **Compute Areas of Circles**:
    - Once we have the correct radii, we can compute the areas of both the larger and smaller circles.
    - The area of a circle is given by \( \text{Area} = \pi r^2 \).
    - The area of the larger circle is \( \pi r_2^2 \).
    - The area of the smaller circle is \( \pi r_1^2 \).
    - The area of the shaded region is the difference between the area of the larger circle and the area of the smaller circle: \( \pi r_2^2 - \pi r_1^2 = \pi (r_2^2 - r_1^2) \).

```python
import sympy as sp

# Let's define the variables
r1, r2 = sp.symbols('r1 r2', positive=True)

# The equation based on our earlier derived relationship
eq = sp.Eq(r2**2, 40**2 + r1**2)

# We can arbitrarily choose a value for \( r_1 \) and solve for \( r_2 \), then we get specifics values for radii.
solution = sp.solve(eq, r2)

# We choose the positive solution for \( r_2 \)
radius_smaller_circle = solution[1]  # We choose the positive root
radius_larger_circle = sp.sqrt(40**2 + radius_smaller_circle**2)

# Compute the areas of the circles
area_larger_circle = sp.pi * radius_larger_circle**2
area_smaller_circle = sp.pi * radius_smaller_circle**2

# The area of the shaded region is the difference between the larger and smaller circle's areas
area_shaded_region = area_larger_circle - area_smaller_circle

print(area_shaded_region)
```
