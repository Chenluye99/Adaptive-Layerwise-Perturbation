To find the projection of vector $\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$ onto vector $\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$, we use the formula for the projection of $\mathbf{a}$ onto $\mathbf{b}$, which is given by:

\[
\text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}} \mathbf{a} = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{b}} \mathbf{b}
\]

First, we need to compute the dot product $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}$:

\[
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 1 \cdot 8 + (-2) \cdot 1 = 8 - 2 = 6
\]

Next, we compute the dot product $\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{b}$:

\[
\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 8 \cdot 8 + 1 \cdot 1 = 64 + 1 = 65
\]

Now, we substitute these values into the formula for the projection:

\[
\text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}} \mathbf{a} = \frac{6}{65} \mathbf{b} = \frac{6}{65} \begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{6}{65} \cdot 8 \\ \frac{6}{65} \cdot 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{48}{65} \\ \frac{6}{65} \end{pmatrix}
\]

So, the projection of $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$ onto $\begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is $\begin{pmatrix} \frac{48}{65} \\ \frac{6}{65} \end{pmatrix}$.
