To solve the problem, we need to find the monthly rent per square meter that is required to recoup the total investment over 50 years with an annual interest rate of 5%.

Here are the steps to solve the problem:

1. **Calculate the Total Investment:**
   The total investment is the product of the cost per square meter and the number of square meters. For this problem, we just need the cost per square meter, which is 250 yuan per square meter (assuming the number of square meters is 1 for simplicity, as it will cancel out in the calculations).

2. **Understand the Problem:**
   The investment should be recouped over a period of 50 years with an annual interest rate of 5%. To find the required monthly rent, we need to find the monthly payment that will pay off the principal investment of 250 yuan per square meter, given the interest.

3. **Set Up the Equation:**
   To recoup the investment, the present value of a series of monthly rent payments over 50 years (which is 600 months) must be equal to the initial investment. The present value of an annuity formula is given by:
   \[
   PV = \frac{R \cdot (1 - (1 + i)^{-n})}{i}
   \]
   where:
   - \( PV \) is the present value of the investment (250 yuan per square meter)
   - \( R \) is the monthly rent per square meter (what we need to find)
   - \( i \) is the monthly interest rate (annual interest rate of 5% is equivalent to a monthly interest rate of \(\frac{5\%}{12}\))
   - \( n \) is the total number of months (50 years is 600 months)

   Rearrange this formula to solve for \( R \):
   \[
   R = \frac{PV \cdot i}{1 - (1 + i)^{-n}}
   \]

   Substituting in the values, we have:
   - \( PV = 250 \)
   - \( i = \frac{0.05}{12} = \frac{1}{240} \)
   - \( n = 50 \times 12 = 600 \)

4. **Implement this in Python:**
   Now let's implement this step-by-step calculation in Python.
```python
# Defining the variables
PV = 250  # Total investment per square meter
annual_interest_rate = 0.05
monthly_interest_rate = annual_interest_rate / 12
n = 50 * 12  # Total number of months in 50 years

# Calculating the monthly rent per square meter
R = (PV * monthly_interest_rate) / (1 - (1 + monthly_interest_rate) ** (-n))
print(R)
```
