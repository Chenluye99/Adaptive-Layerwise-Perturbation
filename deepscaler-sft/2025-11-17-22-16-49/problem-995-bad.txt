1. **Place the Octagon in a Coordinate System:**
   - A regular octagon with side length 2 can be placed in a coordinate system such that its center is at the origin (0, 0).
   - The vertices of the octagon can be determined using the properties of a regular octagon, which is a polygon with 8 equal sides and 8 equal angles. Each internal angle of a regular octagon is \( \frac{3\pi}{4} \) or 135°.
   - For simplicity, we can use the fact that a regular octagon can be constructed from an inscribed circle and a circumscribed circle. However, for precise calculations, it is useful to place it such that each side is of length 2.

2. **Find the Coordinates of the Vertices:**
   - If we place the octagon in such a way that one of its vertices is at (2, 0), then the other vertices can be calculated using rotations by \( \frac{\pi}{4} \) (45°) around the center of the octagon (which can be derived by rotating points appropriately).
   - The vertices of the octagon can be placed at appropriate coordinates considering the side length is 2.

(x, y) = (2 \cdot \cos(\theta), 2 \cdot \sin(\theta))

3. **Find the Area of Triangle \( \triangle ADG \):**
   - The coordinates of \(A, D, G\) are:
     - \(A = (2, 0)\)
     - \(D = (-\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2})\)
     - \(G = (0, -2)\)
   - The formula to find the area of a triangle with vertices \((x_1, y_1)\), \((x_2, y_2)\), and \((x_3, y_3)\) is:
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2) \right|
     \]
   - Substituting in the coordinates of \(A, D, G\):
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| 2 \left( \sqrt{2} - (-2) \right) + (-\sqrt{2}) \left( -2 - 0 \right) + 0 \left( 0 - \sqrt{2} \right) \right|
     \]
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| 2 \left( \sqrt{2} + 2 \right) + (-\sqrt{2}) \left( -2 \right) + 0 \right|
     \]
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| 2 \sqrt{2} + 4 + 2 \sqrt{2} \right|
     \]
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| 4 \sqrt{2} + 4 \right|
     \]
     \[
     \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (4 \sqrt{2} + 4)
     \]
     \[
     \text{Area} = 2 \sqrt{2} + 2
     \]

```python
import sympy as sp

# Coordinates of the vertices
A = (2, 0)
D = (-sp.sqrt(2), sp.sqrt(2))
G = (0, -2)

# Function to calculate the area of triangle given its vertices
def triangle_area(x1, y1, x2, y2, x3, y3):
    return sp.Rational(1, 2) * abs(x1*(y2 - y3) + x2*(y3 - y1) + x3*(y1 - y2))

# Calculate the area of triangle ADG
area_ADG = triangle_area(A[0], A[1], D[0], D[1], G[0], G[1])
print(area_ADG)
```
