To solve the given infinite product problem:

\[
\prod_{k=0}^\infty \left( 1 + \frac{1}{14^{2^k}} \right),
\]

we can use numerical methods because finding an exact symbolic solution may be very complex or even impractical. We'll compute this by evaluating the product up to a sufficiently large number of terms such that the contribution of additional terms becomes negligible.

Let's write a Python function to compute this product up to a reasonable number of terms, say 20, to ensure accuracy, as higher terms contribute very insignificantly to the product.

We will iterate from \( k = 0 \) to a sufficiently large number and compute the product of the terms \( \left( 1 + \frac{1}{14^{2^k}} \right) \).
```python
import numpy as np

# Initialize the product
product = 1.0

# Compute the product up to a reasonable number of terms, say 20
for k in range(20):
    term = 1 + 1 / (14 ** (2 ** k))
    product *= term

print(product)
```
