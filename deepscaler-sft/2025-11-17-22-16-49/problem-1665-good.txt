First, let's determine the total number of votes cast in the referendum:
\[ \text{Total votes} = 36 + 24 = 60 \]

To create a pie chart, each vote corresponds to an angle at the center of the circle. Since a full circle is 360 degrees, each vote corresponds to \(\frac{360}{60} = 6\) degrees.

To find out the angle corresponding to "YES" votes and "NO" votes, we can use the following formulas:
1. Angle corresponding to "YES" votes = \(\frac{24}{60} \times 360 = 144\) degrees.
2. Angle corresponding to "NO" votes = \(\frac{36}{60} \times 360 = 216\) degrees.

The lesser angle between the "YES" votes and "NO" votes in the pie chart will be the smaller of these two angles. Let's confirm this by writing the Python code.
```python
# Number of "YES" and "NO" votes
num_yes_votes = 24
num_no_votes = 36

# Total number of votes
total_votes = num_yes_votes + num_no_votes

# Calculate the angle for YES and NO votes in the pie chart
angle_yes = (num_yes_votes / total_votes) * 360
angle_no = (num_no_votes / total_votes) * 360

# Find the lesser measure of angles A and B in the pie chart
lesser_angle = min(angle_no, angle_yes)
print(lesser_angle)
```
Code execution result: 144.0

Thus, the lesser measure in degrees of angle $ACB$ is \(\boxed{144}\). This is the angle corresponding to the "YES" votes, as it is the smaller of the two angles calculated for "YES" and "NO" votes in the pie chart.
