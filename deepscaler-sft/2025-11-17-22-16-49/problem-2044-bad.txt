First, we need to determine all the positive integers that are factors of 24. Then, we need to count how many of these factors are less than or equal to 24. Finally, we will calculate the probability by dividing the number of these factors by 24, because we are considering all positive integers from 1 to 24.

1. **Identify Factors of 24:**
   We start by finding all the factors of 24. A number is a factor of 24 if 24 is divisible by that number without leaving a remainder.

2. **Count the Factors That are Less Than or Equal to 24:**
   Since all the factors of 24 are, by definition, less than or equal to 24, this step is fairly simple once we get the list of factors of 24. We will then count those factors.

3. **Calculate the Probability:**
   The probability that a randomly chosen positive integer less than or equal to 24 is a factor of 24 is the number of factors of 24 (that are less than or equal to 24) divided by 24.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import numpy as np

# Step 1: Find all the factors of 24
num = 24
factors_of_24 = [i for i in range(1, num + 1) if num % i == 0]

# Step 2: Count the number of factors of 24 (which are automatically <= 24)
num_factors_of_24 = len(factors_of_24)

# Step 3: Calculate the probability
probability = num_factors_of_24 / num

print(((num_factors_of_24, probability)))
```
Code execution result: (8, 0.3333333333333333)

This means there are 8 factors of 24, and the probability that a randomly chosen positive integer less than or equal to 24 is a factor of 24 is \(\frac{8}{24} = \frac{1}{3}\).

So, the final answer is:
\(\boxed{\frac{1}{3}}\)
