First, let's solve the problem step by step.

1. **Determine the Rate of the Faucets:**
    - Three faucets fill a 100-gallon tub in 6 minutes.
    - Therefore, the combined rate of three faucets is \(\frac{100 \text{ gallons}}{6 \text{ minutes}} = \frac{100}{6} \text{ gallons per minute} = \frac{50}{3} \text{ gallons per minute}\).
    - The rate for one faucet would then be \(\frac{50}{3 \cdot 3} = \frac{50}{9} \text{ gallons per minute}\).

2. **Find the Rate of Six Faucets:**
    - If one faucet has a rate of \(\frac{50}{9} \text{ gallons per minute}\), then six faucets would have a combined rate of \(6 \cdot \frac{50}{9} = \frac{300}{9} = \frac{100}{3} \text{ gallons per minute}\).

3. **Determine the Time to Fill a 25-Gallon Tub with Six Faucets:**
    - We need to find how long it will take for six faucets to fill a 25-gallon tub. If the rate of six faucets is \(\frac{100}{3} \text{ gallons per minute}\), then the time \( t \) required to fill a 25-gallon tub is given by:
      \[
      t = \frac{25 \text{ gallons}}{\frac{100}{3} \text{ gallons per minute}} = 25 \cdot \frac{3}{100} = \frac{75}{100} = \frac{3}{4} \text{ minutes}
      \]
    - Since the question asks for the time in seconds, we convert \(\frac{3}{4} \text{ minutes}\) to seconds. There are 60 seconds in a minute, so:
      \[
      \frac{3}{4} \text{ minutes} \times 60 \text{ seconds/minute} = \frac{3 \times 60}{4} = \frac{180}{4} = 45 \text{ seconds}
      \]

Let's confirm this by coding it in Python.
```python
# Given data
gallons_tub_1 = 100
time_for_three_faucets = 6  # in minutes
num_faucets_initial = 3

# Calculate the rate of one faucet
combined_rate_three_faucets = gallons_tub_1 / time_for_three_faucets  # in gallons per minute
rate_one_faucet = combined_rate_three_faucets / num_faucets_initial

# Calculate the combined rate for six faucets
num_faucets_final = 6
combined_rate_six_faucets = rate_one_faucet * num_faucets_final  # in gallons per minute

# Given data for the second part of the problem
gallons_tub_2 = 25

# Calculate the time required for six faucets to fill a 25-gallon tub in minutes
time_required_min = gallons_tub_2 / combined_rate_six_faucets

# Convert the time to seconds
time_required_sec = time_required_min * 60
print(time_required_sec)
```
Code execution result: 45.0

Thus, the time it takes for six faucets to fill a 25-gallon tub is \(\boxed{45}\) seconds. This confirms our reasoning and calculations are accurate.
