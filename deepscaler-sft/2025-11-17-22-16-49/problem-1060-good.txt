To find the equation of the directrix of the parabola given by the equation \(x = -\frac{1}{6} y^2\), let's follow these steps:

1. **Understand the Equation:**
    - The standard form of a parabola that opens left or right is \( (y - k)^2 = 4p (x - h) \).
    - We need to manipulate the given equation \( x = -\frac{1}{6} y^2 \) to match this standard form.

2. **Convert the Equation:**
    - Rewrite \( x = -\frac{1}{6} y^2 \) in a more recognizable form. Multiply both sides by -6 to get:
      \[
      -6 x = y^2 \implies y^2 = -6 x
      \]
    - Now, the equation \( y^2 = -6 x \) is in the form \( y^2 = 4p x \), where \( 4p = -6 \). Hence, \( p = -\frac{3}{2} \).

3. **Find the Directrix:**
    - For a parabola of the form \( y^2 = 4p x \), the directrix is given by \( x = -p \).
    - Substituting \( p = -\frac{3}{2} \), the equation of the directrix is:
      \[
      x = - \left( - \frac{3}{2} \right) = \frac{3}{2}
      \]
