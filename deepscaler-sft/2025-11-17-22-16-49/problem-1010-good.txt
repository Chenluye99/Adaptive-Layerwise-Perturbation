To solve this problem, we need to determine the height of the right pyramid with a square base and edge lengths of 4 inches, and then calculate its volume.

1. **Understand the Geometry of the Pyramid:**
    - The base of the pyramid is a square with side length 4 inches.
    - Each of the edges connecting the base to the apex of the pyramid is 4 inches long.

2. **Find the Height of the Pyramid:**
    - Consider a right triangle formed by:
      - The height of the pyramid, \( h \),
      - The distance from the center of the base to a midpoint of one of the base edges (which is half the length of the diagonal of the square base), and
      - The slant height of the pyramid's triangular face (which is 4 inches, as it is one of the edges of the pyramid).
    - First, calculate the length of the diagonal of the square base. Since the side length of the square base is 4, the length of the diagonal is \( 4\sqrt{2} \). The distance from the center of the square to the midpoint of one of its sides is half of this diagonal's half, i.e., \( \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{2} = 2\sqrt{2} \).
    - Now, we form a right triangle where:
      - One leg is the height of the pyramid, \( h \),
      - The other leg is \( 2\sqrt{2} \),
      - The hypotenuse is 4 (the edge length of the pyramid).
    - Using the Pythagorean theorem, we get:
      \[
      4^2 = h^2 + (2\sqrt{2})^2 \implies 16 = h^2 + 8 \implies h^2 = 8 \implies h = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}
      \]

3. **Calculate the Volume of the Pyramid:**
    - The volume \( V \) of a pyramid is given by the formula:
      \[
      V = \frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}
      \]
    - The base area of our pyramid is the area of the square with side length 4, i.e., \( 4 \times 4 = 16 \).
    - The height of the pyramid, as found, is \( 2\sqrt{2} \).
    - Plugging these into the volume formula gives:
      \[
      V = \frac{1}{3} \times 16 \times 2\sqrt{2} = \frac{32 \sqrt{2}}{3}
      \]
    - To get the numerical value and ensure accuracy, we'll compute this using Python.

Let's proceed with the Python code to get the final, precise answer.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Calculate the height of the pyramid
h = 2 * sp.sqrt(2)

# Calculate the volume of the pyramid
base_area = 4 * 4
volume = (1/3) * base_area * h

# Evaluate the volume numerically to the nearest hundredth
volume_evaluated = round(float(volume), 2)
print(volume_evaluated)
```
