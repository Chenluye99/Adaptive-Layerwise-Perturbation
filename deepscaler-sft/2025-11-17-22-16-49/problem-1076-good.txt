First, let's solve the problem step-by-step:

1. **Find numbers that are multiples of both 3 and 5:**
    - A number that is a multiple of both 3 and 5 is a multiple of their least common multiple (LCM). The LCM of 3 and 5 is 15. So, we need to find multiples of 15 between 1 and 200.

2. **Exclude numbers that are multiples of 4 or 7:**
    - We then need to check that these multiples of 15 are not multiples of 4 or 7.

3. **Iterate and count the numbers that meet the criteria:**
    - We iterate through the multiples of 15 from 1 to 200 and count only those numbers that are not multiples of 4 or 7.

Let's implement this step-by-step process in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Initialize a counter for the numbers that meet the criteria
count = 0

# Iterate through the numbers between 1 and 200
for num in range(1, 201):
    # Check if the number is a multiple of 15
    if num % 15 == 0:
        # Check if the number is not a multiple of 4 or 7
        if num % 4 != 0 and num % 7 != 0:
            count += 1

print(count)
```
Code execution result: 9

Thus, the number of integers between 1 and 200 that are multiples of both 3 and 5 but not of either 4 or 7 is \(\boxed{9}\). This is our final answer.
