\[ 9 + 99 + 999 + 9999 + \cdots + \underbrace{99\ldots 99}_\text{321 digits}. \]

Each term in the series can be represented as \( 10^k - 1 \) where \( k \) is the number of digits in the term. For example:
- The first term \( 9 = 10^1 - 1 \),
- The second term \( 99 = 10^2 - 1 \),
- The third term \( 999 = 10^3 - 1 \),
- And so on, until the 321st term which has 321 digits, so it is \( 10^{321} - 1 \).

Thus, we can generate each of these numbers and sum them up to get \( N \). After getting \( N \), we need to find the sum of its digits.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python.
```python
# Generate each number in the series and sum them up to get N
N = sum(10**i - 1 for i in range(1, 322))

# Get the sum of the digits of N
