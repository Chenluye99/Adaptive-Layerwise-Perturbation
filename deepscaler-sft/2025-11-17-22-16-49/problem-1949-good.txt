To solve the problem, we need to determine the number of ways to choose a 5-person committee consisting of 3 Republicans and 2 Democrats from a Senate with 55 Republicans and 45 Democrats.

This is a combinatorial problem where we use binomial coefficients (combinations). The number of ways to choose 3 Republicans out of 55 is given by \(\binom{55}{3}\), and the number of ways to choose 2 Democrats out of 45 is given by \(\binom{45}{2}\). The total number of ways to choose the committee is the product of these two combinations:

\[
\binom{55}{3} \times \binom{45}{2}
\]

The formula for combinations is given by:

\[
\binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}
\]

We can use Python to compute this accurately. Let's calculate it step-by-step.
```python
import math

# Calculate the combinations
comb_55_3 = math.comb(55, 3)
comb_45_2 = math.comb(45, 2)

# Calculate the total number of ways to form the committee
total_ways = comb_55_3 * comb_45_2
print(total_ways)
```
Code execution result: 25972650

Thus, the number of ways to choose a 5-person committee consisting of 3 Republicans and 2 Democrats is \(\boxed{25972650}\). This is our final answer.
