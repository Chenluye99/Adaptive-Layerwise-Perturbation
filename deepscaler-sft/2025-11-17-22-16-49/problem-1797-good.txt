To solve the problem, let's follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the area of the floor:**
    - The floor dimensions are 9 feet by 12 feet.
    - First, convert these dimensions to inches because the tile dimensions are given in inches.
    - 1 foot = 12 inches, so the floor dimensions in inches are:
      - Length = 9 feet * 12 inches/foot = 108 inches
      - Width = 12 feet * 12 inches/foot = 144 inches
    - The area of the floor in square inches is:
      \[
      \text{Area of the floor} = 108 \, \text{inches} \times 144 \, \text{inches}
      \]

2. **Calculate the area of one tile:**
    - Each tile is 4 inches by 6 inches, so the area of one tile is:
      \[
      \text{Area of one tile} = 4 \, \text{inches} \times 6 \, \text{inches} = 24 \, \text{square inches}
      \]

3. **Determine the number of tiles needed:**
    - The number of tiles required to cover the floor is the area of the floor divided by the area of one tile:
      \[
      \text{Number of tiles} = \frac{\text{Area of the floor}}{\text{Area of one tile}}
      \]

Let's compute this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Step 1: Calculate the area of the floor in square inches
length_in_feet, width_in_feet = 9, 12
length_in_inches = length_in_feet * 12
width_in_inches = width_in_feet * 12
area_of_floor = length_in_inches * width_in_inches

# Step 2: Calculate the area of one tile in square inches
tile_length, tile_width = 4, 6
area_of_one_tile = tile_length * tile_width

# Step 3: Calculate the number of tiles needed
num_tiles_needed = area_of_floor / area_of_one_tile
print(num_tiles_needed)
```
Code execution result: 648.0

Thus, the number of 4-inch-by-6-inch tiles needed to cover the 9-foot-by-12-foot floor is \(\boxed{648}\). This is our final answer.
