First, let's start by factoring the expression \(46x^3 - 115x^7\).

1. **Identify the Common Factors:**
    - The coefficients 46 and 115 have a common factor that we need to determine.
    - The variable part of the terms are \(x^3\) and \(x^7\), and the common variable factor is \(x^3\) because \(x^3\) is the smallest power of \(x\) present in both terms.

2. **Find the GCD of the Coefficients:**
    - We need to find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 46 and 115.
    - We can use the Euclidean algorithm to find the GCD:
        - \(115 \div 46 = 2\) with a remainder of 23.
        - \(46 \div 23 = 2\) with a remainder of 0.
    - Therefore, the GCD of 46 and 115 is 23.

3. **Factor Out the GCD and the Common Variable Factor:**
    - The GCD of the coefficients 46 and 115 is 23, and the smallest power of \(x\) common to both terms is \(x^3\).
    - Therefore, we can factor out \(23x^3\) from the expression:
      \[
      46x^3 - 115x^7 = 23x^3(2 - 5x^4)
      \]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure that our solution is accurate.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the expression

# Factor the expression
factored_expr = sp.factor(expr)

```
