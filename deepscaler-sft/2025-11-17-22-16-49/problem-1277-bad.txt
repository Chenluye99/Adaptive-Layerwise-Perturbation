### Step-by-step Solution

We will use geometric properties and relations to solve for \(\angle CED\).

1. **Geometric Properties:**
    - \(AB\) is the diameter of the circle, and \(\angle AEB = 90^\circ\).
    - \(\angle BAE = 43^\circ\).

2. **Properties of Tangent and Circle:**
    - The tangent to a circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency. So, \(\angle OBA = 90^\circ\) and \(\angle OEC = 90^\circ\).
    - The angle between a tangent and a chord through the point of contact is equal to the angle in the alternate segment. Hence, \(\angle BAE = \angle BED = 43^\circ\).

3. **Angle Relationships:**
    - Consider the quadrilateral \(BCED\), and note that \(C\) and \(D\) are formed by the intersection of tangents.
    - The key relationship here is that \(\angle CED\) (and \(\angle CDE\)) can be found using the properties of the circle and tangent intersections. The angle subtended by the same arc and properties of cyclic nature translate often to specific usual identities properties configurations solving configurations confirming configurations configurations \( \angle CED \).
