First, let's start by simplifying the given equation:

\[
\frac{y^2 - 9y + 8}{y - 1} + \frac{3y^2 + 16y - 12}{3y - 2} = -3
\]

### Step 1: Factorize the Numerators

1. **Factorize \( y^2 - 9y + 8 \):**
   \[
   y^2 - 9y + 8 = (y - 1)(y - 8)
   \]

   So, we have:
   \[
   \frac{(y - 1)(y - 8)}{y - 1} = y - 8 \quad \text{(for } y \neq 1 \text{)}
   \]

2. **Factorize \( 3y^2 + 16y - 12 \):**
   To factorize \( 3y^2 + 16y - 12 \), we look for two numbers that multiply to \( 3 \cdot (-12) = -36 \) and add to 16. These numbers are 18 and -2.
   \[
   3y^2 + 16y - 12 = 3y^2 + 18y - 2y - 12 = 3y(y + 6) - 2(y + 6) = (3y - 2)(y + 6)
   \]

   So, we have:
   \[
   \frac{(3y - 2)(y + 6)}{3y - 2} = y + 6 \quad \text{(for } y \neq \frac{2}{3} \text{)}
   \]

### Step 2: Substitute the Simplified Expressions Back Into the Equation

Substitute the simplified expressions back into the original equation:

\[
(y - 8) + (y + 6) = -3
\]

Combine like terms:

\[
y - 8 + y + 6 = -3 \implies 2y - 2 = -3
\]

Solve for \( y \):

\[
2y - 2 = -3 \implies 2y = -1 \implies y = -\frac{1}{2}
\]
