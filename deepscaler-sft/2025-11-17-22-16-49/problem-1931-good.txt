To solve the problem, we need to find the length of the space diagonal of a rectangular prism with dimensions 10 inches by 20 inches by 10 inches. Points \( A \) and \( B \) are located at opposite corners of the prism, meaning the diagonal we need to find goes from one corner of the prism to the opposite corner.

The formula for the length of the space diagonal \( d \) of a rectangular prism with side lengths \( a \), \( b \), and \( c \) is given by:

\[ d = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2} \]

In this case, the side lengths of the rectangular prism are 10, 20, and 10. Substituting these values into the formula, we get:

\[ d = \sqrt{10^2 + 20^2 + 10^2} \]
\[ d = \sqrt{100 + 400 + 100} \]
\[ d = \sqrt{600} \]
\[ d = \sqrt{100 \cdot 6} \]
\[ d = 10 \sqrt{6} \]

Let's confirm this with Python to ensure the accuracy of the result.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the dimensions of the rectangular prism
a, b, c = 10, 20, 10

# Calculate the length of the space diagonal
d = sp.sqrt(a**2 + b**2 + c**2)
print(d)
```
Code execution result: 10*sqrt(6)

Thus, the length of the diagonal connecting points \( A \) and \( B \) is \(\boxed{10 \sqrt{6}}\). This confirms that our solution is accurate.
