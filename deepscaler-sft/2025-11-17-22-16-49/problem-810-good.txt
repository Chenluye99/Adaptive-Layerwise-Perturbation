First, let's understand the problem:

1. We have a 6-by-6-by-6 cube made up of smaller unit cubes.
2. The entire outer surface of this 6-by-6-by-6 cube is painted.
3. We need to find the fraction of the unit cubes that have at least two painted faces.

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Determine the Total Number of Unit Cubes:**
   A 6-by-6-by-6 cube contains \(6^3 = 216\) unit cubes.

2. **Identify the Unit Cubes with at Least Two Painted Faces:**
   - **Unit cubes with three painted faces:** These are the corner cubes of the 6-by-6-by-6 cube. A 6-by-6-by-6 cube has 8 corners, so there are 8 unit cubes with three painted faces.
   - **Unit cubes with exactly two painted faces:** These are the unit cubes on the edges of the cube, excluding the corners. A 6-by-6-by-6 cube has 12 edges, and each edge is 6 units long, but we exclude the 2 corner unit cubes from each edge, so each edge has \(6 - 2 = 4\) unit cubes with exactly two painted faces. Therefore, there are \(12 \times 4 = 48\) unit cubes with exactly two painted faces.
   - **Combine the Two Types:**
     Unit cubes with at least two painted faces are those with either three or two painted faces. So, there are \(8 + 48 = 56\) such unit cubes.

3. **Find the Fraction:**
   The fraction of the unit cubes that have at least two painted faces is given by \(\frac{56}{216}\). We can simplify this fraction by finding the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 56 and 216, which is 8. Thus, \(\frac{56 \div 8}{216 \div 8} = \frac{7}{27}\).

Let's confirm this step-by-step process by coding it to ensure the correctness.
```python
import math

# Total number of unit cubes in a 6-by-6-by-6 cube
total_unit_cubes = 6 * 6 * 6

# Finding the unit cubes that have at least two painted faces

# 1. Count the unit cubes with three painted faces (corner cubes)
num_cubes_three_faces = 8  # There are 8 corners in a cube

# 2. Count the unit cubes with exactly two painted faces (edge cubes, excluding corners)
num_edges = 12  # A cube has 12 edges
length_of_edge = 6  # Each edge is 6 units long, but we exclude the 2 corner cubes from each edge, hence 6 - 2 = 4
num_cubes_two_faces = num_edges * (length_of_edge - 2)

# 3. Count the unit cubes with at least two painted faces
num_cubes_at_least_two_faces = num_cubes_three_faces + num_cubes_two_faces

# 4. Find the fraction and simplify it
numerator = num_cubes_at_least_two_faces
denominator = total_unit_cubes

gcd = math.gcd(numerator, denominator)

simplified_fraction_numerator = numerator // gcd
simplified_fraction_denominator = denominator // gcd

print(((simplified_fraction_numerator, simplified_fraction_denominator)))
```
Code execution result: (7, 27)

Thus, the fraction of the unit cubes that have at least two painted faces is \(\boxed{\frac{7}{27}}\). This confirms our reasoning and solution is accurate.
