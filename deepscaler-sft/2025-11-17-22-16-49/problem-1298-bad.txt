To solve this problem, we need to determine the range of possible values for \( n \) such that the three sticks of lengths 5 cm, 9 cm, and \( n \) cm can form a triangle. For three lengths to be able to form a triangle, they must satisfy the triangle inequality theorem. The triangle inequality theorem states that for any three lengths to form a triangle, the sum of the lengths of any two sides must be greater than the length of the remaining side. So, for the sides 5, 9, and \( n \):

1. \( 5 + 9 > n \)
2. \( 5 + n > 9 \)
3. \( 9 + n > 5 \)

Let's simplify each of these inequalities:

1. \( 5 + 9 > n \implies 14 > n \implies n < 14 \)
2. \( 5 + n > 9 \implies n > 4 \)
3. \( 9 + n > 5 \implies n > -4 \) (This inequality is always true for positive lengths, so we can ignore it.)

Combining the first two inequalities, we get:
\[ 4 < n < 14 \]

This means that \( n \) can take all whole number values from 5 to 13, inclusive. We need to find the sum of all possible whole number values of \( n \) in this range.

We can generate the list of these values and then compute their sum using Python.
```python
# Determine the range of possible values for n
possible_values = [n for n in range(5, 14)]

# Calculate the sum of all possible values of n
sum_of_possible_values = sum(possible_values)
print(sum_of_possible_values)
```
