First, let's define the variables and set up the equations based on the problem statement.

1. Let's say Bertha has \( x \) daughters who each have 6 daughters.
2. Consequently, the remaining \( 6 - x \) daughters have no daughters.
3. The total number of Bertha's daughters and granddaughters is 30. Bertha has 6 daughters, and each of the \( x \) daughters has 6 granddaughters, so the number of granddaughters is \( 6x \). Therefore, the equation for the total number of daughters and granddaughters is:
   \[
   6 + 6x = 30
   \]
4. We can solve this equation for \( x \):
   \[
   6x = 24 \implies x = 4
   \]
   So, 4 of Bertha's daughters each have 6 daughters, and the remaining \( 6 - 4 = 2 \) daughters have no daughters.
5. The number of granddaughters is \( 6 \times 4 = 24 \). So, we have 24 granddaughters and 2 daughters who have no daughters. In total, we want to find how many of Bertha's daughters and granddaughters have no daughters. This includes:
   - The 2 daughters who have no daughters.
   - All 24 granddaughters, because granddaughters are not allowed to have daughters according to the problem statement.

Therefore, the total number of daughters and granddaughters who have no daughters is \( 2 + 24 = 26 \).

Let's confirm this by coding the solution in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# We solve for x as described and then compute the result programmatically to ensure accuracy

# Number of Bertha's daughters
num_daughters_bertha = 6

# Equation to solve: 6 + 6 * x = 30
# Solve for x
x = (30 - num_daughters_bertha) // 6

# Number of Bertha's daughters who each have 6 daughters (granddaughters)
num_daughters_with_granddaughters = x

# Number of Bertha's daughters who have no daughters
num_daughters_without_granddaughters = num_daughters_bertha - x

# Number of granddaughters
num_granddaughters = num_daughters_with_granddaughters * 6

# Total count of daughters and granddaughters who have no daughters
result = num_daughters_without_granddaughters + num_granddaughters
print(result)
```
Code execution result: 26

Thus, the number of Bertha's daughters and granddaughters who have no daughters is \(\boxed{26}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
