To solve this problem, let's start by understanding how drawing lines on a 4 by 5 piece of paper can create basic rectangles. First, we need to consider how lines drawn on the paper intersect to form smaller rectangles.

1. **Initial Setup:**
    - A 4 by 5 piece of paper has dimensions 4 units by 5 units.
    - If we draw horizontal and vertical lines parallel to the edges of the paper, we can form multiple smaller rectangles on the piece of paper.
    - Suppose we draw \( h \) horizontal lines including the boundaries of the paper (so we actually draw \( h-1 \) additional lines inside the boundaries), and we draw \( v \) vertical lines including the boundaries of the paper (so we actually draw \( v-1 \) additional lines inside the boundaries). Then, the total length of all the lines drawn will be \( 4 \cdot (v-1) + 5 \cdot (h-1) \), because each of the \( h-1 \) horizontal lines is 5 units long (equal to the width of the paper), and each of the \( v-1 \) vertical lines is 4 units long (equal to the height of the paper).

2. **Formulating the Constraint:**
    - The condition provided states that the total length of all lines drawn is exactly 2007 units. Therefore, we have:
      \[
      4 \cdot (v-1) + 5 \cdot (h-1) = 2007
      \]

3. **Formulating Basic Rectangles:**
    - A basic rectangle is formed by intersections of the drawn lines, and it should satisfy the following conditions:
      - All four sides of the rectangle are segments of drawn line segments.
      - No segments of drawn lines lie inside the rectangle.
    - To form a basic rectangle, we need at least 2 horizontal and 2 vertical lines (so that we have multiple segments along the height and width for forming a smaller rectangle), so \( h \geq 3 \) and \( v \geq 3 \).
    - Each way of choosing 2 horizontal lines out of \( h \) and 2 vertical lines out of \( v \) will form a basic rectangle. The number of such choices is given by \( \binom{h}{2} \cdot \binom{v}{2} \).

4. **Maximizing the Number of Basic Rectangles:**
    - We want to maximize the product \( \binom{h}{2} \cdot \binom{v}{2} \) subject to the constraint \( 4 \cdot (v-1) + 5 \cdot (h-1) = 2007 \).

max_basic_rectangles = 0
