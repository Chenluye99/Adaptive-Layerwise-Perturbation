To solve this problem, let's start by understanding the problem statement and setting up the equations.

1. **Problem Understanding:**
    - A domino consists of two congruent squares placed side by side.
    - The perimeter of the resulting shape (the domino) is given as 60 units.
    - We need to find the area of the domino.

2. **Setting Up the Equations:**
    - Let's assume each of the two congruent squares has a side length of \( s \).
    - When these two squares are placed side by side, the resulting domino will have a length of \( 2s \) and a width of \( s \).
    - The perimeter of this domino can be calculated as the sum of all its sides. Therefore, the perimeter is given by:
      \[
      2 \cdot (2s + s) = 2 \cdot (3s) = 6s
      \]
    - We know that the perimeter of the domino is 60 units, so we can set up the following equation:
      \[
      6s = 60
      \]
    - Solving for \( s \):
      \[
      s = \frac{60}{6} = 10
      \]

3. **Finding the Area of the Domino:**
    - The area of one square is \( s^2 \), and since there are two squares in the domino, the total area of the domino is:
      \[
      2 \cdot s^2 = 2 \cdot (10)^2 = 2 \cdot 100 = 200
      \]

Let's confirm this step-by-step solution by implementing it in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# We can solve this step by step programmatically as well. First, let's define the perimeter equation and solve for s, then compute the area of the domino.

# As derived, the equation is 6s = 60.

s = 60 / 6

# Now, compute the area of the domino which consists of two squares, each with side length s.
area_of_dominos = 2 * (s ** 2)
print(area_of_dominos)
```
