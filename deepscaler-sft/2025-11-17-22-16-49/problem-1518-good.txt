To solve this problem, we need to perform polynomial multiplication and then identify the coefficient of the \(x^3\) term in the resulting polynomial.

1. **Define the Polynomials:**
   - Let \( P(x) = 24x^4 + 6x^3 + 4x^2 - 7x - 5 \).
   - Let \( Q(x) = 6x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 4 \).

2. **Multiply the Polynomials:**
   - We need to multiply \( P(x) \) by \( Q(x) \).

3. **Get the Resultant Polynomial:**
   - After multiplying, we get a new polynomial, and we need to identify the coefficient of the \(x^3\) term in this new polynomial.

Let's implement this step-by-step process using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variable
x = sp.symbols('x')

# Define the polynomials
P = 24*x**4 + 6*x**3 + 4*x**2 - 7*x - 5
Q = 6*x**3 + 3*x**2 + 3*x + 4

# Multiply the polynomials
product = sp.expand(P * Q)

# Get the coefficient of the x^3 term
coeff_x3 = product.coeff(x, 3)
print(coeff_x3)
```
Code execution result: -15

Thus, the coefficient of \( x^3 \) in the product of the polynomials \( 24x^4 + 6x^3 + 4x^2 - 7x - 5 \) and \( 6x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 4 \) is \(\boxed{-15}\). This is our final answer.
