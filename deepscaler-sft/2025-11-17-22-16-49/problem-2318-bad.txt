The formula for the sum \( S \) of an infinite geometric series is:
\[
S = \frac{a}{1 - r}
\]
where \( |r| < 1 \).

Substituting \( a = 1 \) and \( r = \frac{1}{5} \) into the formula, we get:
\[
S = \frac{1}{1 - \frac{1}{5}} = \frac{1}{\frac{5 - 1}{5}} = \frac{1}{\frac{4}{5}} = \frac{1 \cdot 5}{4} = \frac{5}{4}
\]

# We can use a numerical approach in Python to approximate the sum of the first n terms of the series and ensure it converges to the expected value.

def geometric_series_sum(n):
    total = 0
    r = 1 / 5
    
    for i in range(n):
