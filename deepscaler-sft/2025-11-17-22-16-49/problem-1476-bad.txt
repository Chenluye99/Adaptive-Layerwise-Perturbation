1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have a total of 20 members (10 boys and 10 girls).
    - We need to form a 4-person committee from these 20 members.
    - We need to find the probability that the committee has at least 1 boy and at least 1 girl.

2. **Formulate the Solution:**
    - First, compute the total number of ways to choose a 4-person committee from 20 members. This is given by the combination formula \( \binom{20}{4} \).
    - Next, compute the number of ways to choose a 4-person committee that does not meet the condition of having at least 1 boy and 1 girl. This includes two scenarios: (1) choosing a 4-person committee consisting of only boys (which is impossible since there are only 10 boys), and (2) choosing a 4-person committee consisting of only girls.
    - Calculate the number of ways to choose a 4-person committee consisting of only girls, which is given by \( \binom{10}{4} \).
    - The number of ways to form a 4-person committee that has at least 1 boy and at least 1 girl is the total number of ways to choose a 4-person committee minus the number of ways to choose a 4-person committee consisting only of girls.
    - Finally, compute the probability by dividing the number of favorable outcomes by the total number of outcomes.

3. **Implement the Solution in Python:**
    - Use the `math` library to compute combinations.
    - Implement the calculations step-by-step to get the answer.

Let's go ahead and implement this step-by-step in Python.
```python
import math

# Calculate the total number of ways to choose a 4-person committee from 20 members
total_ways = math.comb(20, 4)

# Calculate the number of ways to choose a 4-person committee consisting of only girls
ways_only_girls = math.comb(10, 4)

# Calculate the number of ways to form a 4-person committee that has at least 1 boy and 1 girl
ways_at_least_1_boy_1_girl = total_ways - ways_only_girls

# Calculate the probability
probability = ways_at_least_1_boy_1_girl / total_ways
print(probability)
```
