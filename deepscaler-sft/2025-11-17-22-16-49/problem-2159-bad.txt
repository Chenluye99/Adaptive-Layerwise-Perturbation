To solve the problem, we need to perform the multiplication of the two complex numbers \((2 - 2i)(5 + 5i)\).

1. Use the distributive property (also known as the FOIL method for binomials) to expand the product:
\[
(2 - 2i)(5 + 5i) = 2 \cdot 5 + 2 \cdot 5i - 2i \cdot 5 - 2i \cdot 5i
\]
2. Perform each multiplication:
\[
= 10 + 10i - 10i - 10i^2
\]
3. Recall that \(i^2 = -1\), so substitute \(-1\) for \(i^2\):
\[
= 10 + 10i - 10i - 10(-1)
\]
4. Simplify the expression:
\[
= 10 + 10i - 10i + 10
\]
\[
= 10 + 10
\]
\[
= 20
\]
