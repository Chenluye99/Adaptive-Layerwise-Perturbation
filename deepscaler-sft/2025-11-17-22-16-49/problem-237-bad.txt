To solve this problem, we need to determine the probability that students A and B are standing together when three students (A, B, and C) are lined up in a row.

1. **Enumerate All Possible Outcomes:**
   When three students (A, B, and C) are lined up, there are 3! (3 factorial) = 3 × 2 × 1 = 6 possible permutations.

2. **Identify the Favorable Outcomes:**
   We need to find the permutations where A and B are standing together. We can consider A and B as a "block" or "super-student" where they can swap places within this block. This block can be in three possible positions in the lineup with C, and within the block, A and B can switch places, giving 2 ways for each block position.

   The "blocks" we can consider are (AB, C), (BA, C), (A, BC), (B, AC), but those last two don't place A and B together as a unit. So, the valid ways are the permutations where A and B are grouped as a block alongside C, giving us:
   - AB, C
   - BA, C
   - C, AB
   - C, BA

   Let's enumerate all 6 permutations of [A, B, C] and check which of these have A and B together:
   - ABC
   - ACB
   - BAC
   - BCA
   - CAB
   - CBA

   Now, let's check which of these have A and B together:
   - ABC: No (A and B are not together)
   - ACB: Yes (A and B are together as AB)
   - BAC: Yes (A and B are together as BA)
   - BCA: No (A and B are not together)
   - CAB: No (A and B are not together)
   - CBA: Yes (A and B are together as AB)

   So, the permutations where A and B are together are ACB, BAC, and CBA. Therefore, there are 3 favorable outcomes out of 6 possible outcomes.

3. **Calculate the Probability:**
   The probability is the ratio of the number of favorable outcomes to the total number of outcomes. So, the probability that A and B stand together is \(\frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\).

Let's confirm this by writing Python code to enumerate all permutations and count the favorable outcomes.
```python
import itertools

# List of students
students = ['A', 'B', 'C']

# Generate all permutations of the list
permutations = list(itertools.permutations(students))

# Count total permutations
total_permutations = len(permutations)

# Count permutations where 'A' and 'B' are together
favorable_count = 0

for perm in permutations:
    # Check if 'A' and 'B' are adjacent (standing together)
    if (perm.index('A') - perm.index('B') == 1) or (perm.index('A') - perm.index('B') == -1):
        favorable_count += 1

# Calculate the probability
probability = favorable_count / total_permutations
print(probability)
```
