To solve the problem, let's start by considering the geometric properties of the regular hexagons and the circle.

1. **Properties of a Regular Hexagon:**
    - A regular hexagon can be divided into 6 equilateral triangles.
    - The side length of these equilateral triangles is equal to the radius of the circle if the hexagon is inscribed in the circle.
    - If the hexagon is circumscribed about the circle, the circle is the incircle of the hexagon, so the radius of the circle is the apothem of the hexagon.

2. **Inscribed Hexagon:**
    - If the radius of the circle is \(r\), then for the inscribed hexagon, each side of the hexagon is also \(r\).
    - The area of a regular hexagon with side length \(a\) is given by \(\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} a^2\).
    - So, for the inscribed hexagon with side length \(r\), the area \(A_{\text{inscribed}}\) is:
      \[
      A_{\text{inscribed}} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} r^2
      \]

3. **Circumscribed Hexagon:**
    - For the circumscribed hexagon, the circle is inscribed in the hexagon, and the relationship between the radius of the circle and the side length of the hexagon changes.
    - Let the side length of the circumscribed hexagon be \(s\). The radius of the circle, which is also the inradius of the circumscribed hexagon, is related to the side length \(s\) of the circumscribed hexagon. For a regular hexagon, the inradius \(r_{\text{in}}\) (which is the radius of the circle in this problem) is given by:
      \[
      r_{\text{in}} = \frac{s \sqrt{3}}{2}
      \]
      But since \( r_{\text{in}} = r \), we have:
      \[
      r = \frac{s \sqrt{3}}{2} \implies s = \frac{2r}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{2r \sqrt{3}}{3}
      \]
    - Now, substitute \( s = \frac{2r \sqrt{3}}{3} \) into the area formula:
      \[
      A_{\text{circumscribed}} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} \left( \frac{2r \sqrt{3}}{3} \right)^2 = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot \frac{4r^2 \cdot 3}{9} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot \frac{4r^2}{3} = \frac{3\sqrt{3} \cdot 4r^2}{6} = 2 \sqrt{3} r^2
      \]

4. **Find the Ratio:**
    - Now, we want to find the ratio of the area of the larger (circumscribed) hexagon to the area of the smaller (inscribed) hexagon:
      \[
      \text{Ratio} = \frac{A_{\text{circumscribed}}}{A_{\text{inscribed}}} = \frac{2 \sqrt{3} r^2}{\frac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2} r^2} = \frac{2 \sqrt{3}}{\frac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2}} = \frac{2 \sqrt{3} \cdot 2}{3 \sqrt{3}} = \frac{4}{3}
      \]

Let's confirm this result with Python to ensure accuracy.

```python
# Define the variables

# Calculate the areas

area_inscribed = (3 * sp.sqrt(3) / 2) * side_length_inscribed**2

# Calculate the ratio of the areas
ratio = area_circumscribed / area_inscribed
print(ratio)
```
