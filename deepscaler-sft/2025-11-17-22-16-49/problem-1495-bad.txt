To solve this problem, we will use the Shoelace formula (also known as the Surveyor's formula) to find the area of the quadrilateral \(XYZW\). The formula for the area of a polygon given its vertices \((x_0, y_0), (x_1, y_1), \ldots, (x_{n-1}, y_{n-1})\) is:

\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} (x_i y_{i+1} - y_i x_{i+1}) \right|
\]
