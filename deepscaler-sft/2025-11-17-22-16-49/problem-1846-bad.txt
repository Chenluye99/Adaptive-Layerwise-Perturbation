To solve this problem, we need to perform the following steps:

1. Identify the four smallest prime numbers.
2. Identify the four smallest composite numbers greater than 0.
3. Compute the product of these four smallest prime numbers.
4. Compute the product of these four smallest composite numbers.
5. Find the positive difference between these two products.

First, let's list out the four smallest prime numbers and the four smallest composite numbers greater than 0:

- The four smallest prime numbers are: 2, 3, 5, 7.
- The four smallest composite numbers greater than 0 are: 4, 6, 8, 9.

Next, we compute the product of the four smallest prime numbers:

\[ 2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 7 \]

Then, we compute the product of the four smallest composite numbers:

\[ 4 \times 6 \times 8 \times 9 \]

Finally, we find the positive difference between these two products.

Let's write the Python code to get the accurate result.
```python
import sympy

# List the four smallest prime numbers
smallest_primes = list(sympy.primerange(1, 10))[:4]

# List the four smallest composite numbers greater than 0
composites = [i for i in range(2, 100) if not sympy.isprime(i)][0:4]

# Calculate the product of the four smallest prime numbers
product_of_primes = 1
for prime in smallest_primes:
    product_of_primes *= prime

# Calculate the product of the four smallest composite numbers
product_of_composites = 1
for composite in composites:
    product_of_composites *= composite

# Calculate the positive difference between the two products
positive_difference = abs(product_of_primes - product_of_composites)

print(((product_of_primes, product_of_composites, positive_difference)))
```
