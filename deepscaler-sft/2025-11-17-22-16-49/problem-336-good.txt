To solve this problem, we need to find the surface area of the space between the two cylinders.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - The inner cylinder has a radius of 3 inches and a height of 8 inches.
    - The outer cylinder has a radius of 3 + 1 = 4 inches and a height of 8 inches.

2. **Formulate the Surface Area Calculation:**
    - The surface area of a cylinder includes the lateral surface area and the area of the two circular bases. However, since the space between the two cylinders is considered vertically, we only need to consider the lateral surface areas (the areas of the sides of the cylinders) for this problem, as the bases of the inner and outer cylinders do not contribute to the surface area of the space between them.

3. **Calculate the Lateral Surface Areas:**
    - The lateral surface area of a cylinder is given by the formula \(2 \pi r h\), where \(r\) is the radius and \(h\) is the height.
    - For the inner cylinder, the lateral surface area is \(2 \pi \cdot 3 \cdot 8 = 48 \pi\).
    - For the outer cylinder, the lateral surface area is \(2 \pi \cdot 4 \cdot 8 = 64 \pi\).

4. **Find the Surface Area of the Space Between the Cylinders:**
    - The surface area of the space between the two cylinders is the lateral surface area of the outer cylinder minus the lateral surface area of the inner cylinder.
    - So, the surface area of the space between the two cylinders is \(64 \pi - 48 \pi = 16 \pi\).

Let's confirm this with Python code to ensure the accuracy of the result.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the radii and height
r_inner = 3
r_outer = 4
height = 8

# Calculate the lateral surface areas
lateral_surface_area_inner = 2 * sp.pi * r_inner * height
lateral_surface_area_outer = 2 * sp.pi * r_outer * height

# Calculate the surface area of the space between the two cylinders
surface_area_between_cylinders = lateral_surface_area_outer - lateral_surface_area_inner
print(surface_area_between_cylinders)
```
Code execution result: 16*pi

Thus, the total surface area of the space between the two cylinders is \(\boxed{16\pi}\) square inches. This confirms our solution is accurate.
