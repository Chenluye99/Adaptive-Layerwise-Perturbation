To solve the problem, we'll use the formula for the area of a trapezoid. The formula for the area \(A\) of a trapezoid is given by:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times (\text{Base}_1 + \text{Base}_2) \times \text{Height} \]

In this problem, we are given:
- One base of the trapezoid is equal to its height, so one base is \( x \).
- The other base of the trapezoid is twice as long as the height, so the other base is \( 2x \).
- The height of the trapezoid is \( x \).

Substituting these values into the formula, we get:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times (x + 2x) \times x = \frac{1}{2} \times 3x \times x = \frac{1}{2} \times 3x^2 = \frac{3x^2}{2} \]

So the expression for the area of the trapezoid is \(\frac{3x^2}{2}\). Let's confirm this with Python code.
