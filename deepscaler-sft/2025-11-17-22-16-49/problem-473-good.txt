To solve the problem, let's start by understanding the setup.

1. **Setup of the Problem:**
    - We have a semicircle in which a \(1 \times 3\) rectangle is inscribed with the longer side (3) on the diameter of the semicircle.
    - Let the radius of the semicircle be \( r \). Then, the diameter of the semicircle is \( 2r \).
    - Since the longer side of the rectangle (which is 3) is on the diameter, the diameter of the semicircle is at least 3. So, we have:
      \[
      2r \geq 3 \implies r \geq \frac{3}{2}
      \]
    - The shorter side of the rectangle (which is 1) extends vertically from the diameter and touches the semicircle. The top corners of the rectangle will touch the semicircle, and the distance from the center of the semicircle to these top corners will be \( r \).

2. **Geometric Constraints:**
    - Consider the coordinates. Place the semicircle centered at the origin with its diameter along the x-axis from \(-r\) to \(r\). The equation of the upper half of the circle (the semicircle) is \( y = \sqrt{r^2 - x^2} \).
    - The top corners of the rectangle are at \(( \pm 1.5, 1 )\), because the center of the semicircle is the midpoint of the diameter, so it is at \( x = 0 \), and the height of the rectangle is 1, so the top corners of the rectangle have \( y = 1 \).

3. **Substitute the Coordinates into the Circle Equation:**
    - Substitute \( x = 1.5 \) and \( y = 1 \) into the equation of the semicircle \( y = \sqrt{r^2 - x^2} \):
      \[
      1 = \sqrt{r^2 - 1.5^2} \implies 1 = \sqrt{r^2 - 2.25}
      \]
    - Square both sides of the equation:
      \[
      1 = r^2 - 2.25 \implies r^2 = 3.25 = \frac{13}{4}
      \]
    - So, the radius \( r \) is:
      \[
      r = \sqrt{\frac{13}{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{13}}{2}
      \]

4. **Find the Area of the Semicircle:**
    - The area of a full circle with radius \( r \) is \( \pi r^2 \), so the area of the semicircle is half of that:
      \[
      \text{Area of the semicircle} = \frac{1}{2} \pi r^2 = \frac{1}{2} \pi \left( \frac{\sqrt{13}}{2} \right)^2 = \frac{1}{2} \pi \cdot \frac{13}{4} = \frac{13 \pi}{8}
      \]
