The sum of these roots (which are \( A \) and \( B \)) can be found using Vieta's formulas, which state that for a quadratic equation of the form \( ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \), the sum of the roots is given by \( -\frac{b}{a} \).

For the quadratic equation \( 2x^2 - 6x + 4 = 0 \), we have \( a = 2 \), \( b = -6 \), and \( c = 4 \). Therefore, by Vieta's formulas, the sum of the roots \( A \) and \( B \) is given by:
\[
A + B = -\frac{-6}{2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3
\]
