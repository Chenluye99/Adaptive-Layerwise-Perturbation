To solve this problem, we need to determine the number of ways to choose 3 men out of 30 and 4 women out of 40, and then multiply these two results together. This is a combination problem where the order does not matter.

1. First, we use the combination formula to find the number of ways to choose 3 men out of 30. The combination formula is given by:
   \[
   \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}
   \]
   where \( n \) is the total number of items to choose from, and \( k \) is the number of items to choose.

   For the men, this is:
   \[
   \binom{30}{3} = \frac{30!}{3!(30-3)!} = \frac{30!}{3! \cdot 27!}
   \]

2. Similarly, we find the number of ways to choose 4 women out of 40. Using the combination formula, this is:
   \[
   \binom{40}{4} = \frac{40!}{4!(40-4)!} = \frac{40!}{4! \cdot 36!}
   \]

3. The total number of ways to form the 7-person steering committee with 3 men and 4 women is the product of these two combinations:
   \[
   \text{Total ways} = \binom{30}{3} \times \binom{40}{4}
   \]

Let's calculate this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Calculate combinations
comb_30_choose_3 = math.comb(30, 3)
comb_40_choose_4 = math.comb(40, 4)

# Calculate the total number of ways
total_ways = comb_30_choose_3 * comb_40_choose_4
print(total_ways)
```
