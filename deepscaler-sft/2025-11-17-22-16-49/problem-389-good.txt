To solve the problem, we need to use the Law of Cosines and the formula for the area of a triangle.

### Step 1: Use the Law of Cosines to Find Side \( b \)
The Law of Cosines states that:
\[ b^2 = a^2 + c^2 - 2ac \cos(B) \]

Given:
\[ a = 3 \sqrt{3}, \quad c = 2, \quad B = 150^\circ \]

First, we need to compute \(\cos(150^\circ)\):
\[ \cos(150^\circ) = \cos(180^\circ - 30^\circ) = -\cos(30^\circ) = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \]

Substitute the given values and \(\cos(150^\circ)\) into the Law of Cosines formula:
\[ b^2 = (3 \sqrt{3})^2 + 2^2 - 2 \cdot (3 \sqrt{3}) \cdot 2 \cdot \left( - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right) \]
\[ b^2 = 27 + 4 + 2 \cdot 3 \sqrt{3} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \]
\[ b^2 = 27 + 4 + 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{3}{2} \]
\[ b^2 = 27 + 4 + 18 \]
\[ b^2 = 49 \]
\[ b = \sqrt{49} = 7 \]

### Step 2: Use the Formula for the Area of a Triangle
The formula for the area of a triangle when two sides and the included angle are known is:
\[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} ac \sin(B) \]

Given:
\[ a = 3 \sqrt{3}, \quad c = 2, \quad B = 150^\circ \]

First, we need to compute \(\sin(150^\circ)\):
\[ \sin(150^\circ) = \sin(180^\circ - 30^\circ) = \sin(30^\circ) = \frac{1}{2} \]

Substitute the given values and \(\sin(150^\circ)\) into the area formula:
\[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \sqrt{3} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \]
\[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \sqrt{3} \cdot 1 = \frac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2} \]
