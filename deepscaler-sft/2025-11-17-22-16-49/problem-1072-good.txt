Let's start by introducing some notation and recalling properties of geometric sequences.

1. **Geometric Sequence Properties:**
    - A geometric sequence has the form \(a, ar, ar^2, ar^3, \ldots\), where \(a\) is the first term and \(r\) is the common ratio.
    - The sum of the first \(n\) terms of a geometric sequence is given by:
      \[
      S_n = a \frac{1 - r^n}{1 - r} \quad \text{(for } r \neq 1\text{)}
      \]
      where \(S_n\) is the sum of the first \(n\) terms.

2. **Given Information:**
    - The sum of the first 2011 terms is 200:
      \[
      S_{2011} = a \frac{1 - r^{2011}}{1 - r} = 200
      \]
    - The sum of the first 4022 terms is 380:
      \[
      S_{4022} = a \frac{1 - r^{4022}}{1 - r} = 380
      \]

3. **Formulate Equations:**
    - Let's denote:
      \[
      k = \frac{a}{1 - r}
      \]
      Then, we can rewrite the given equations as:
      \[
      k (1 - r^{2011}) = 200
      \]
      \[
      k (1 - r^{4022}) = 380
      \]

4. **Form a Ratio:**
    - Dividing the second equation by the first equation, we get:
      \[
      \frac{1 - r^{4022}}{1 - r^{2011}} = \frac{380}{200} = \frac{19}{10}
      \]
      So:
      \[
      1 - r^{4022} = \frac{19}{10} (1 - r^{2011})
      \]

5. **Solve for \(r\):**
    - Let \(x = r^{2011}\). Then the equation becomes:
      \[
      1 - x^2 = \frac{19}{10} (1 - x)

      \[
      1 - x^2 = \frac{19}{10} - \frac{19}{10} x

      \[
      1 - x^2 = 1.9 - 1.9x

      - x^2 = 0.9 - 1.9x

      \[
