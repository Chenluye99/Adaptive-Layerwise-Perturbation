First, let's start by recalling that the inverse of a 2x2 matrix \(\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}\) is given by:
\[
\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad - bc} \begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix}
\]
We are given that:
\[
\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{a} & \frac{1}{b} \\ \frac{1}{c} & \frac{1}{d} \end{pmatrix}
\]
Substituting the expression for the inverse, we get:
\[
\frac{1}{ad - bc} \begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{a} & \frac{1}{b} \\ \frac{1}{c} & \frac{1}{d} \end{pmatrix}
\]
This gives us the following system of equations by comparing the corresponding elements of the matrices:
1. \(\frac{d}{ad - bc} = \frac{1}{a}\)
2. \(\frac{-b}{ad - bc} = \frac{1}{b}\)
3. \(\frac{-c}{ad - bc} = \frac{1}{c}\)
4. \(\frac{a}{ad - bc} = \frac{1}{d}\)

Let's solve these equations step-by-step.

### Step 1: Set Up Equations
From the above, we get:
1. \(\frac{d}{ad - bc} = \frac{1}{a} \implies d \cdot a = ad - bc \implies ad = ad - bc \implies bc = 0\)
2. \(\frac{-b}{ad - bc} = \frac{1}{b} \implies -b \cdot b = ad - bc \implies -b^2 = ad - bc\)
3. \(\frac{-c}{ad - bc} = \frac{1}{c} \implies -c \cdot c = ad - bc \implies -c^2 = ad - bc \implies ad - bc = -c^2\)
4. \(\frac{a}{ad - bc} = \frac{1}{d} \implies a \cdot d = ad - bc \implies ad = ad - bc \implies bc = 0\)

From the first and fourth equations, we get \(bc = 0\). This means either \(b = 0\) or \(c = 0\).

\[
