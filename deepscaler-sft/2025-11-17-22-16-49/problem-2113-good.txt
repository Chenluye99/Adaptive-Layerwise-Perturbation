First, let's identify the prime numbers within the range from 1 to 20. The prime numbers in this range are: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19. So, there are 8 prime numbers out of the 20 numbers.

We want to find the probability that both numbers chosen at random from the integers 1 through 20 are prime. 

1. **Calculate the total number of ways to choose 2 different integers from 20:**
   The number of ways to choose 2 different integers from 20 can be calculated using the combination formula \( \binom{20}{2} \):
   \[
   \binom{20}{2} = \frac{20!}{2!(20-2)!} = \frac{20 \times 19}{2 \times 1} = 190
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of ways to choose 2 prime numbers from the 8 prime numbers:**
   Similarly, the number of ways to choose 2 different prime numbers from the 8 prime numbers can be calculated using the combination formula \( \binom{8}{2} \):
   \[
   \binom{8}{2} = \frac{8!}{2!(8-2)!} = \frac{8 \times 7}{2 \times 1} = 28
   \]

3. **Calculate the probability:**
   The probability that both chosen numbers are prime is the number of ways to choose 2 prime numbers divided by the total number of ways to choose 2 different integers from 20:
   \[
   P(\text{both numbers are prime}) = \frac{28}{190} = \frac{14}{95}
   \]

Let's confirm this by coding it out to ensure the result is accurate.
```python
import math

# Step 1: Get the list of primes between 1 and 20
prime_numbers = [num for num in range(1, 21) if all(num % i != 0 for i in range(2, int(math.sqrt(num)) + 1)) and num > 1]

# Total number of ways to choose 2 different integers from 20
total_ways = math.comb(20, 2)

# Number of ways to choose 2 prime numbers from the prime numbers list
prime_count = len(prime_numbers)
ways_to_choose_2_primes = math.comb(prime_count, 2)

# Probability that both chosen numbers are prime
probability = ways_to_choose_2_primes / total_ways

print(((probability, ways_to_choose_2_primes, total_ways, prime_numbers)))
```
