First, let's start by understanding the problem requirements:

1. We have 8 distinguishable rings.
2. We want to select 5 rings out of 8 and arrange them on 4 fingers (let's call them finger 1, 2, 3, and 4). Each of the 5 rings can go on any of the 4 fingers, and the order of rings on each finger matters.

To solve this, we can follow these steps:

1. **Choosing 5 Rings Out of 8:**
   The number of ways to choose 5 rings out of 8 is given by the binomial coefficient:
   \[
   \binom{8}{5} = \frac{8!}{5!(8-5)!} = \frac{8!}{5!3!}
   \]

2. **Arranging 5 Rings:**
   For each choice of 5 rings, there are \(5!\) ways to arrange these 5 rings in a sequence.

3. **Distributing 5 Rings to 4 Fingers:**
   For each arrangement of the 5 rings, we need to distribute these 5 rings among the 4 fingers. Each of the 5 rings can be placed on any of the 4 fingers, so for each of the 5 positions, we have 4 choices of fingers, leading to \(4^5\) ways of placing these 5 rings on the 4 fingers.

Combining these steps, the total number of ways, \(n\), to choose and arrange 5 rings on 4 fingers is:
\[
n = \binom{8}{5} \cdot 5! \cdot 4^5
\]

Let's calculate this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Step 1: Calculate the binomial coefficient (8 choose 5)
binom_8_5 = math.comb(8, 5)

# Step 2: Calculate 5!
factorial_5 = math.factorial(5)

# Step 3: Calculate 4^5
power_4_5 = 4 ** 5

# Combine all parts to get n
```
