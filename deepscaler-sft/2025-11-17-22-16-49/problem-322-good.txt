To solve this problem, we need to find the expected value of \(N\) given that a randomly drawn slip from the hat is numbered 2. 

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Define the Problem:**
    - The probability of picking a number \( n \) initially is \( 2^{-n} \).
    - After picking \( N \), there are \( N \) slips of paper, each numbered from 1 to \( N \).
    - We know that one of these slips is drawn and it turns out to be numbered 2.
    - We need to find the conditional expectation \( E[N \mid X = 2] \), where \( X \) is the number drawn from the hat, and it is 2.

2. **Set Up the Probabilities:**
    - First, calculate the probability of drawing the number 2 from the hat for a given \( N \):
        - If \( N = 2 \), then there are 2 slips with numbers 1 and 2, so the probability of drawing a 2 is \( \frac{1}{2} \).
        - If \( N = 3 \), there are 3 slips with numbers 1, 2, and 3, so the probability of drawing a 2 is \( \frac{1}{3} \).
        - In general, if \( N \) is chosen such that \( N = k \), then there is exactly one slip numbered 2 out of the \( k \) slips, so the probability of drawing a 2 is \( \frac{1}{k} \).

3. **Joint Probability Calculation:**
    - The probability of choosing \( N = k \) and then drawing a 2 is \( 2^{-k} \cdot \frac{1}{k} \).
    - The total probability of drawing a 2 (over all possible \( N \)) is:
      \[
      P(X = 2) = \sum_{k=2}^{\infty} 2^{-k} \cdot \frac{1}{k}
      \]

4. **Conditional Probability:**
    - By Bayes' theorem, the conditional probability \( P(N = k \mid X = 2) \) is:
      \[
      P(N = k \mid X = 2) = \frac{P(X = 2 \mid N = k) \cdot P(N = k)}{\sum_{j=2}^{\infty} P(X = 2 \mid N = j) \cdot P(N = j)}
      \]
      Substituting in our expressions, this becomes:
      \[
      P(N = k \mid X = 2) = \frac{2^{-k} \cdot \frac{1}{k}}{\sum_{j=2}^{\infty} 2^{-j} \cdot \frac{1}{j}}
      \]

5. **Expected Value Calculation:**
    - The expected value of \( N \) given that a 2 is drawn is:
      \[
      E[N \mid X = 2] = \sum_{k=2}^{\infty} k \cdot P(N = k \mid X = 2)
      \]
      Substituting our expression for \( P(N = k \mid X = 2) \):
      \[
      E[N \mid X = 2] = \sum_{k=2}^{\infty} k \cdot \frac{2^{-k} \cdot \frac{1}{k}}{\sum_{j=2}^{\infty} 2^{-j} \cdot \frac{1}{j}} = \frac{\sum_{k=2}^{\infty} 2^{-k}}{\sum_{j=2}^{\infty} 2^{-j} \cdot \frac{1}{j}}
      \]

      \[
      \sum_{j=2}^{\infty} 2^{-j} \cdot \frac{1}{j} = \sum_{j=2}^{\infty} \frac{2^{-j}}{j}
      \]
    - Next, calculate the numerator:
      \[
      \sum_{k=2}^{\infty} 2^{-k} = \sum_{k=2}^{\infty} 2^{-k}
