First, let's start by understanding and setting up the equations based on the given problem.

We are given the following conditions:
\[
\frac{XZ}{XY} = \frac{ZY}{XY} = \frac{1}{2}
\]
This implies that \( Z \) is the midpoint of the segment \( XY \). If the coordinates of \( X \) are \((x_1, y_1)\) and the coordinates of \( Y \) are \((1, 7)\), and the coordinates of \( Z \) are \((-1, -7)\), then the midpoint formula can be used. The midpoint formula for a segment with endpoints \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is given by:
\[
\left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right)
\]
Setting the midpoint of \( X \) and \( Y \) equal to the coordinates of \( Z \):
\[
\left( \frac{x_1 + 1}{2}, \frac{y_1 + 7}{2} \right) = (-1, -7)
\]
This gives us a system of equations to solve for \( x_1 \) and \( y_1 \):
\[
\frac{x_1 + 1}{2} = -1 \quad \text{(1)}
\]
\[
\frac{y_1 + 7}{2} = -7 \quad \text{(2)}
\]

Let's solve these equations step-by-step.

1. Solve Equation (1):
\[
\frac{x_1 + 1}{2} = -1
\]
Multiply both sides by 2:
\[
x_1 + 1 = -2
\]
Subtract 1 from both sides:
\[
x_1 = -3
\]

2. Solve Equation (2):
\[
\frac{y_1 + 7}{2} = -7
\]
Multiply both sides by 2:
\[
y_1 + 7 = -14
\]
Subtract 7 from both sides:
\[
y_1 = -21
\]

Thus, the coordinates of \( X \) are \((-3, -21)\). The sum of the coordinates of \( X \) is:
\[
-3 + (-21) = -24
