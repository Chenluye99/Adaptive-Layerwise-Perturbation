Each of them arrives at a random time between 1:00 and 2:00 (i.e., within a one-hour window, which we can consider from time 0 to 60 minutes past 1:00), and each stays for 15 minutes.

Let's denote:
- The time Allen arrives as \( a \) (where \( 0 \leq a < 60 \)).
- The time Bethany arrives as \( b \) (where \( 0 \leq b < 60 \)).

Allen will be at the party from \( a \) to \( a + 15 \), and Bethany will be at the party from \( b \) to \( b + 15 \). For Allen and Bethany to see each other, their intervals must overlap. That means there needs to be some time during which both are present.

\[ \max(a, b) < \min(a + 15, b + 15) \]

1. \( a < b + 15 \)
