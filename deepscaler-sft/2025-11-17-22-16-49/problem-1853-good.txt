First, let's start by identifying the coordinates of the points:

- Point \( A \) is at \((-6, 6)\)
- Point \( B \) is at \((9, 6)\)
- Point \( C \) is at \((9, -2)\)

When these points are connected, they form a polygon. To find the perimeter of this polygon, we need to calculate the lengths of the sides \( AB \), \( BC \), and \( CA \), and then sum these lengths.

1. Calculate the length of \( AB \):
The distance formula between two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is given by:
\[
\text{Distance} = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}
\]
For points \( A(-6, 6) \) and \( B(9, 6) \):
\[
AB = \sqrt{(9 - (-6))^2 + (6 - 6)^2} = \sqrt{(9 + 6)^2 + 0^2} = \sqrt{15^2} = 15
\]

2. Calculate the length of \( BC \):
For points \( B(9, 6) \) and \( C(9, -2) \):
\[
BC = \sqrt{(9 - 9)^2 + (-2 - 6)^2} = \sqrt{0^2 + (-8)^2} = \sqrt{64} = 8
\]

3. Calculate the length of \( CA \):
For points \( C(9, -2) \) and \( A(-6, 6) \):
\[
CA = \sqrt{(9 - (-6))^2 + (-2 - 6)^2} = \sqrt{(9 + 6)^2 + (-2 - 6)^2} = \sqrt{15^2 + (-8)^2} = \sqrt{225 + 64} = \sqrt{289} = 17
\]

The perimeter of the polygon is the sum of the lengths of its sides:
\[
\text{Perimeter} = AB + BC + CA = 15 + 8 + 17 = 40
\]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure the accuracy of the result.
