When a polynomial is divided by another polynomial, the degree of the remainder must be less than the degree of the divisor. In this case, the divisor is \(-3x^5 + 10x - 11\), which is a polynomial of degree 5.

Therefore, the possible degrees of the remainder are 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4 (since the degree of the remainder must be less than 5).

Let's confirm this with a small piece of code to ensure the logic is correct and output the possible degrees of the remainder.
```python
# The degree of the divisor polynomial -3x^5 + 10x - 11 is 5.
degree_of_divisor = 5

# The possible degrees of the remainder are 0, 1, 2, 3, 4.
possible_degrees_of_remainder = list(range(degree_of_divisor))

print(possible_degrees_of_remainder)
```
