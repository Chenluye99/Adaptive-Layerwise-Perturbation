First, let's simplify the given expression \(\frac{4k + 8}{4}\).

We can split the expression as follows:
\[
\frac{4k + 8}{4} = \frac{4k}{4} + \frac{8}{4} = k + 2
\]
Here, the expression \( k + 2 \) is in the form \( ak + b \) where \( a = 1 \) and \( b = 2 \).

We need to find \(\frac{a}{b}\). Substituting the values of \( a \) and \( b \):
\[
\frac{a}{b} = \frac{1}{2}
\]

We will confirm this by coding a small Python script to ensure our solution is accurate.
```python
# Values of a and b as derived from the expression
```
