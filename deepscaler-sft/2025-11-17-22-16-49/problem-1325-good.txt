First, we need to find the semi-perimeter ($s$) of triangle $ABC$ with sides $a = BC = 14$, $b = AC = 12$, and $c = AB = 22$:

\[ s = \frac{a + b + c}{2} = \frac{14 + 12 + 22}{2} = 24 \]

The formula to find the area ($K$) of a triangle using Heron's formula is:

\[ K = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)} \]

Substituting the values of $a, b, c,$ and $s$ into the formula, we get:

\[ K = \sqrt{24 (24 - 14) (24 - 12) (24 - 22)} = \sqrt{24 \cdot 10 \cdot 12 \cdot 2} = \sqrt{5760} = \sqrt{576 \cdot 10} = 24 \sqrt{10} \]

The radius ($r$) of the inscribed circle (incircle) of a triangle is given by the formula:

\[ r = \frac{K}{s} \]

Substituting the values of $K$ and $s$ into this formula, we get:

\[ r = \frac{24 \sqrt{10}}{24} = \sqrt{10} \]
