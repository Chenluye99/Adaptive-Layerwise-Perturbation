\[ OH^2 = 9R^2 - (a^2 + b^2 + c^2) \]

- \( R = 7 \)
- \( a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = 29 \)

\[ OH^2 = 9 \cdot 7^2 - 29 \]

First, let's compute \( 9 \cdot 7^2 \):

\[ 9 \cdot 7^2 = 9 \cdot 49 = 441 \]

Now, subtract 29 from 441:

\[ OH^2 = 441 - 29 = 412 \]

\[ OH^2 = 412 \]
