To solve the problem, we need to determine the area enclosed by the region defined by the equation \(x^2 + y^2 + 10x + 24y = 0\). This equation represents a circle, and we need to find its center and radius.

First, we will rewrite the equation in the standard form of a circle equation, \((x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2\), by completing the square.

The given equation is:

\[x^2 + y^2 + 10x + 24y = 0\]

1. **Complete the Square:**
    - For the \(x\) terms: \(x^2 + 10x\), take the coefficient of \(x\), which is 10, divide it by 2 to get 5, and then square it to get 25. Add and subtract 25:
      \[
      x^2 + 10x = (x + 5)^2 - 25
      \]
    - For the \(y\) terms: \(y^2 + 24y\), take the coefficient of \(y\), which is 24, divide it by 2 to get 12, and then square it to get 144. Add and subtract 144:
      \[
      y^2 + 24y = (y + 12)^2 - 144
      \]

2. **Substitute Back:**
    - Substitute these completed squares back into the original equation:
      \[
      (x + 5)^2 - 25 + (y + 12)^2 - 144 = 0
      \]
    - Simplify:
      \[
      (x + 5)^2 + (y + 12)^2 - 169 = 0 \implies (x + 5)^2 + (y + 12)^2 = 169
      \]

This is now in the standard form of a circle equation \((x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2\), where the center of the circle is \((-5, -12)\) and the radius \(r\) is \(\sqrt{169} = 13\).

3. **Find the Area of the Circle:**
    - The area \(A\) of a circle is given by the formula \(A = \pi r^2\).
    - Substituting the radius \(r = 13\) into the formula, we get:
      \[
      A = \pi (13)^2 = 169 \pi
      \]

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the equation
x, y = sp.symbols('x y')
equation = x**2 + y**2 + 10*x + 24*y

# Complete the square and get the standard form
completed_square = sp.expand((x + 5)**2 + (y + 12)**2 - 169)

# Confirm that the completed square form is equivalent to the original equation
assert equation == completed_square, "The equation does not match after completing the square."

# Extract the radius from the equation (x + 5)^2 + (y + 12)^2 = 169
radius = 13

# Calculate the area of the circle
area = sp.pi * radius**2
print(area)
```
Code execution result: 169*pi

Thus, the area enclosed by the region defined by the equation \(x^2 + y^2 + 10x + 24y = 0\) is \(\boxed{169\pi}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
