To ensure the points are chosen such that they form a triangle, we must exclude any sets of three points that are collinear.

    - The number of ways to choose 3 points from the 5 collinear points on $\overline{AB}$ is \( \binom{5}{3} \).
    - The number of ways to choose 3 points from the 6 collinear points on $\overline{BC}$ is \( \binom{6}{3} \).
    - The number of ways to choose 3 points from the 7 collinear points on $\overline{CA}$ is \( \binom{7}{3} \).

4. **Calculate the Final Number of Triangles with Positive Area:**
    - Subtract the number of ways to choose 3 collinear points from the total number of ways to choose 3 points from 15.

```python
import sympy as sp

# Calculate total ways to choose 3 points from 15
total_ways = sp.binomial(15, 3)

# Calculate ways to choose 3 points from each of the collinear sets
collinear_AB = sp.binomial(5, 3)

collinear_CA = sp.binomial(7, 3)

# Calculate the number of ways to form triangles with positive area
ways_with_positive_area = total_ways - (collinear_AB + collinear_BC + collinear_CA)
```
