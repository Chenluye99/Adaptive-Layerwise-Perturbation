To solve this problem, we will follow these steps:

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have a standard deck of 52 cards.
    - We need to find the probability that the first card drawn is a 6 and the second card drawn is a Queen.
    
2. **Identify the Cards:**
    - There are 4 sixes in a deck (one from each suit: hearts, diamonds, clubs, spades).
    - There are 4 queens in a deck (one from each suit: hearts, diamonds, clubs, spades).

3. **Calculate the Probability Step-by-Step:**
    - The probability that the first card drawn is a 6 is \(\frac{4}{52}\).
    - After drawing a 6, there are 51 cards left in the deck. The probability that the second card drawn is a Queen is \(\frac{4}{51}\).
    - The combined probability of both events happening in sequence (first card is a 6 and second card is a Queen) is the product of these probabilities:
      \[
      P(\text{First card is 6 and Second card is Queen}) = \frac{4}{52} \times \frac{4}{51}
      \]

Let's implement this in Python to get the precise result.
```python
# Calculating the probability
prob_first_card_is_6 = 4 / 52
prob_second_card_is_queen_given_first_was_6 = 4 / 51

# Combined probability
combined_probability = prob_first_card_is_6 * prob_second_card_is_queen_given_first_was_6
print(combined_probability)
```
