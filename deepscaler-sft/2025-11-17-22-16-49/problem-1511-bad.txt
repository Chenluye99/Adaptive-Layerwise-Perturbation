First, we need to rewrite the equation of the circle in standard form. The given equation is:

\[ x^2 + 2y - 9 = -y^2 + 18x + 9. \]

Let's move all terms to one side of the equation to start forming the standard equation of a circle:

\[ x^2 + 2y - 9 + y^2 - 18x - 9 = 0, \]

which simplifies to:

\[ x^2 - 18x + y^2 + 2y - 18 = 0. \]

To convert this into the standard form \((x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2\), we complete the square for the \(x\) and \(y\) terms.

1. For the \(x\) terms: \(x^2 - 18x\),
   - Take the coefficient of \(x\), which is \(-18\), divide it by 2 to get \(-9\), and then square it to get \(81\).
   - Add and subtract 81: \(x^2 - 18x + 81 - 81\).

2. For the \(y\) terms: \(y^2 + 2y\),
   - Take the coefficient of \(y\), which is \(2\), divide it by 2 to get \(1\), and then square it to get \(1\).
   - Add and subtract 1: \(y^2 + 2y + 1 - 1\).

So, rewriting the equation by adding 81 and 1 to both sides (to keep the equation balanced) and then combining constants on the right, we get:

\[ (x^2 - 18x + 81) + (y^2 + 2y + 1) = 18 + 1 + 18, \]

which simplifies to:

\[ (x - 9)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 37. \]

This is now in the standard form \((x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2\), where \((a, b)\) is the center and \(r\) is the radius. From the equation \((x - 9)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 37\), we identify:
- The center \((a, b) = (9, -1)\),
- The radius \(r = \sqrt{37}\).

We need to find the value of \(a + b + r\):

\[ a = 9, \quad b = -1, \quad r = \sqrt{37}. \]

So,

\[ a + b + r = 9 + (-1) + \sqrt{37} = 8 + \sqrt{37}. \]
