1. **Understanding the Problem:**
    - We are given that the area of the rhombus is 108 square units.
    - The ratio of the lengths of its diagonals is 3:2.
    - We need to find the length of the longest diagonal.

2. **Mathematical Formulation:**
    - Let the lengths of the diagonals be \(3x\) and \(2x\) (since their ratio is 3:2).
    - The area \(A\) of a rhombus with diagonals of lengths \(d_1\) and \(d_2\) is given by the formula:
      \[
      A = \frac{1}{2} \times d_1 \times d_2
      \]
      Substituting \(d_1 = 3x\) and \(d_2 = 2x\), we get:
      \[
      108 = \frac{1}{2} \times (3x) \times (2x)
      \]
    - Simplifying the right-hand side:
      \[
      108 = \frac{1}{2} \times 6x^2 = 3x^2
      \]
    - Solving for \(x^2\):
      \[
      3x^2 = 108 \implies x^2 = \frac{108}{3} = 36 \implies x = \sqrt{36} = 6
      \]
    - The lengths of the diagonals are then \(3x = 3 \cdot 6 = 18\) and \(2x = 2 \cdot 6 = 12\).
    - The longest diagonal is 18.

3. **Implementation in Python:**
    - We can use the above steps to confirm our result with Python code.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variable
x = sp.symbols('x', positive=True)

# Create the equation based on the area formula of the rhombus
equation = 3 * x**2 - 108

# Solve the equation for x
solution = sp.solve(equation, x)[0]

# Calculate the lengths of the diagonals
d1 = 3 * solution
d2 = 2 * solution

# Determine the longest diagonal
longest_diagonal = max(d1, d2)
print(longest_diagonal)
```
Code execution result: 18

Thus, the length of the longest diagonal is \(\boxed{18}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
