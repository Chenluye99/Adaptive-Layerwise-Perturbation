First, we substitute the point \((6, 10)\) into both equations.

\[ 10 = m \cdot 6 + 4. \]
This simplifies to:
\[ 10 = 6m + 4. \]
Subtract 4 from both sides:
\[ 6 = 6m. \]
Divide both sides by 6:
\[ m = 1. \]

\[ 10 = 3 \cdot 6 + b. \]
This simplifies to:
\[ 10 = 18 + b. \]
Subtract 18 from both sides:
\[ -8 = b. \]
So, we have:
\[ b = -8. \]

\[ b + m = -8 + 1 = -7. \]
