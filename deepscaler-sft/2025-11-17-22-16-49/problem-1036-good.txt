To solve the problem, we need to find the orthocenter of the triangle defined by the points \( A = (1, 2, 3) \), \( B = (5, 3, 1) \), and \( C = (3, 4, 5) \). The orthocenter of a triangle is the point where the three altitudes of the triangle intersect.

Here is a step-by-step breakdown of how to solve this problem:

1. **Find the slopes and equations of the altitudes of the triangle:**
    - An altitude of a triangle is a line passing through a vertex and perpendicular to the opposite side.
    - To find the altitude from a vertex, we first need to find the slope of the opposite side and then determine the slope of the perpendicular line.

2. **Find the equations of two altitudes:**
    - **Altitude from \( A \) to \( BC \):**
        - First, find the slope of \( BC \). The direction vector of \( BC \) is \( C - B = (3 - 5, 4 - 3, 5 - 1) = (-2, 1, 4) \).
        - The slope of \( BC \) is such that a perpendicular line will have a slope that is the negative reciprocal in the appropriate sense for 3D space. The direction vector of the altitude from \( A \) to \( BC \) will be perpendicular to \( BC \). We can use the cross product to get a vector perpendicular to \( BC \), but here, we will take a different approach by finding the Foot of the perpendicular from \( A \) to \( BC \).
    - **Altitude from \( B \) to \( AC \):**
        - First, find the slope of \( AC \). The direction vector of \( AC \) is \( C - A = (3 - 1, 4 - 2, 5 - 3) = (2, 2, 2) \).
        - Similarly, the direction vector of the altitude from \( B \) to \( AC \) will be perpendicular to \( AC \).

3. **Find the intersection of the altitudes:**
    - Once we have the equations of two altitudes, we can find their intersection, which is the orthocenter.

Let's implement this step-by-step process in Python to get the precise answer.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the points
A = sp.Point3D(1, 2, 3)
B = sp.Point3D(5, 3, 1)
C = sp.Point3D(3, 4, 5)

# Get the sides of the triangle
AB = sp.Line3D(A, B)
BC = sp.Line3D(B, C)
CA = sp.Line3D(C, A)

# Find the altitudes
altitude_A = sp.Line3D(A, BC.projection(A))
altitude_B = sp.Line3D(B, CA.projection(B))

# Find the intersection of the altitudes (orthocenter)
orthocenter = altitude_A.intersection(altitude_B)[0]
print(orthocenter)
```

Thus, the orthocenter of the triangle \(ABC\) is \(\left( \frac{5}{2}, 3, \frac{7}{2} \right)\).
