\[ a^3 b^5 = 1 \]

We will approach this problem by iterating through possible values for \(a\) and \(b\) and check if both conditions are satisfied.

1. **Expressing the Equations:**
    - From the first equation, \( a^3 b^5 = 1 \), we can express \( b^5 \) as:
      \[
      b^5 = \frac{1}{a^3}
      \]
    - From the second equation, \( a^7 b^2 = 1 \), we can express \( b^2 \) as:
      \[
      b^2 = \frac{1}{a^7}
      \]

2. **Relating the Expressions:**

    - This gives us two equations:
      \[
      a^6 b^{10} = 1

      \[
      a^{35} b^{10} = 1
      \]
    - By dividing these two equations, we get:
      \[

import sympy as sp
