To solve this problem, let's denote the revenue and expenses of the insurance company in 2005 as \( R \) and \( E \) respectively. Then, for 2006, the revenue and expenses will be modified as follows:

- The revenue in 2006, denoted as \( R_{2006} \), will be \( R_{2006} = R \cdot 1.25 \).
- The expenses in 2006, denoted as \( E_{2006} \), will be \( E_{2006} = E \cdot 1.15 \).
- The profit in 2006, denoted as \( \text{Profit}_{2006} \), will be \( \text{Profit}_{2006} = R_{2006} - E_{2006} = R \cdot 1.25 - E \cdot 1.15 \).
- The profit in 2005, denoted as \( \text{Profit}_{2005} \), will be \( \text{Profit}_{2005} = R - E \).
- We are also given that the profit in 2006 increased by 40% compared to the profit in 2005, so we have the equation: \[ \text{Profit}_{2006} = \text{Profit}_{2005} \cdot 1.40. \]

Substituting the expressions for \(\text{Profit}_{2006}\) and \(\text{Profit}_{2005}\), we get: \[ R \cdot 1.25 - E \cdot 1.15 = (R - E) \cdot 1.40. \]

Let's solve this equation step-by-step:

1. Expand and rearrange the equation:
\[ 1.25R - 1.15E = 1.40R - 1.40E, \]
\[ 1.40E - 1.15E = 1.40R - 1.25R, \]
\[ 0.25E = 0.15R, \]
\[ \frac{E}{R} = \frac{0.15}{0.25} = \frac{15}{25} = \frac{3}{5} = 0.6. \]

So, \( E = 0.6R \). This means that expenses are 60% of the revenues in 2006.

Let's confirm this by coding it to ensure the accuracy of the result.
```python
from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variables
R, E = symbols('R E')

# Define the equation based on the given problem
eq = Eq(1.25 * R - 1.15 * E, 1.40 * (R - E))

# Solve the equation for E/R
solution = solve(eq, E)[0]
percentage_of_revenue_that_expenses_are = (solution / R) * 100
print(percentage_of_revenue_that_expenses_are)
```
