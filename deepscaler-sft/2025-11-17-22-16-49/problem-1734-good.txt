      \[
      (a + b + c)^2 = 1 + 2(ab + ac + bc).
      \]

      \[
      2(ab + ac + bc) = (a + b + c)^2 - 1 \implies ab + ac + bc = \frac{(a + b + c)^2 - 1}{2}.
      \]

3. **Minimization Task:**
