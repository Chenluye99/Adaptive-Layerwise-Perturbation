First, let's analyze and solve the problem step-by-step.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We have an equilateral triangle with a side length of 12 cm.
    - One of the sides of the equilateral triangle is the diameter of a circle. Therefore, the diameter of the circle is 12 cm, and the radius is \( r = \frac{12}{2} = 6 \) cm.
    - The circle intersects the equilateral triangle, creating two smaller segments whose areas need to be calculated and summed up.

2. **Calculate the Area of the Circle:**
    - The area of the whole circle is given by the formula \( \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = \pi r^2 = \pi (6)^2 = 36\pi \).

3. **Find the Area of the Segments:**
    - The angle subtended by each side of the equilateral triangle at the center of the circle is 120° (since each angle of an equilateral triangle is 60°, and 2 * 60° = 120°).
    - The area of a sector of the circle with a central angle of 120° is given by:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\text{sector}} = \frac{120}{360} \cdot 36\pi = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 36\pi = 12\pi
      \]
    - To get the area of each of the small segments, we need to subtract the area of the corresponding triangle from the area of the sector. Each of these small segments consists of a 120° sector minus an equilateral triangle with side length 6 (since each triangle is formed by the center of the circle and two points on the circle's edge defining a side of 12 cm of the original equilateral triangle).

4. **Find the Area of Equilateral Triangle Segments:**
    - First, let's consider the equilateral triangle with a side length of 6 cm. The area of an equilateral triangle is given by:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\text{triangle}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} s^2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} (6)^2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 36 = 9\sqrt{3}
      \]
    - Each of the smaller segments is then the 120° sector minus the equilateral triangle with side length 6:
      \[
      \text{Area}_{\text{segment}} = 12\pi - 9\sqrt{3}
      \]

5. **Find the Area of the Two Small Segments:**
    - Since there are two such segments, the total shaded area is:
      \[
      \text{Total Area} = 2 \cdot (12\pi - 9\sqrt{3}) = 24\pi - 18\sqrt{3}
      \]
    - Thus, we have \( a = 24 \), \( b = 18 \), and \( c = 3 \).

6. **Find the Sum \( a + b + c \):**
    - So, \( a + b + c = 24 + 18 + 3 = 45 \).

import sympy as sp

# Given values
side_length = 12
radius = side_length / 2

# Calculate the area of the circle
area_circle = sp.pi * radius**2

# Calculate the area of the 120-degree sector of the circle
sector_angle = 120
sector_area = (sector_angle / 360) * area_circle

# Calculate the area of the equilateral triangle with side length 6 (half of the original 12 cm side)
side_length_small_triangle = radius
area_small_triangle = (sp.sqrt(3) / 4) * side_length_small_triangle**2

# Calculate the area of one of the smaller segments
segment_area = sector_area - area_small_triangle

# Calculate the total area of the two smaller shaded regions
total_shaded_area = 2 * segment_area

# Extract the coefficients a, b, and c from the expression a * pi - b * sqrt(c)
expression = total_shaded_area
coefficients = sp.collect(expression, [sp.pi, sp.sqrt(3)], evaluate=False)

# Get a, b, and c from the expression
a = coefficients[sp.pi]
b = -coefficients[sp.sqrt(3)]
c = 3  # From the term sqrt(3), so c = 3

# Compute a + b + c
result = a + b + c
print(result)
