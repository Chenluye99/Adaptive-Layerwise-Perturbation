1. First, let's determine the number of women and men on the cruise ship.
   - We know that half of the 2000 adults were women, so the number of women is:
     \[
     \text{Number of women} = \frac{2000}{2} = 1000
     \]
   - Consequently, the number of men is also:
     \[
     \text{Number of men} = \frac{2000}{2} = 1000
     \]

2. Next, we calculate how many women and men were wearing sunglasses.
   - We know that 20% of the women were wearing sunglasses:
     \[
     \text{Number of women wearing sunglasses} = 0.20 \times 1000 = 200
     \]
   - Similarly, 9% of the men were wearing sunglasses:
     \[
     \text{Number of men wearing sunglasses} = 0.09 \times 1000 = 90
     \]

3. Finally, to find the total number of men and women wearing sunglasses, we sum the above results:
   \[
   \text{Total number of people wearing sunglasses} = 200 + 90 = 290
   \]

Let's confirm this with Python code to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given data
total_adults = 2000

# Calculate number of women and men
num_women = total_adults // 2
num_men = total_adults // 2

# Calculate the number of women and men wearing sunglasses
women_wearing_sunglasses = 0.20 * num_women
men_wearing_sunglasses = 0.09 * num_men

# Calculate the total number of people wearing sunglasses
total_wearing_sunglasses = women_wearing_sunglasses + men_wearing_sunglasses
print(total_wearing_sunglasses)
```
