To solve this problem, we need to determine the average number of people moving to Florida each hour over a period of three days. Here are the steps:

1. **Determine the Total Number of Hours in Three Days:**
    - There are 24 hours in a day.
    - Therefore, in three days, there are \(3 \times 24 = 72\) hours.

2. **Calculate the Average Number of People Moving Each Hour:**
    - We know that 2000 people will move to Florida over the course of these three days.
    - To find the average number of people moving each hour, we divide 2000 by 72.

3. **Express the Answer to the Nearest Whole Number:**
    - After calculating the average, we need to round the result to the nearest whole number.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Total number of people moving to Florida in three days
total_people = 2000

# Total number of hours in three days
total_hours = 3 * 24

# Calculate the average number of people moving to Florida each hour
average_people_per_hour = total_people / total_hours

# Round the result to the nearest whole number
average_people_per_hour_rounded = round(average_people_per_hour)

print(average_people_per_hour_rounded)
```
Code execution result: 28

Thus, the average number of people who will move to Florida each hour, expressed to the nearest whole number, is \(\boxed{28}\). This is the final answer.
