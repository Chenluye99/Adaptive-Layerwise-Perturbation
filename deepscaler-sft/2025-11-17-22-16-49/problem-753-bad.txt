To solve the problem, we need to find the angle between the vectors \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\) given that \(\|\mathbf{a}\| = \|\mathbf{b}\| = \|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\|\).

1. **Start with the Given Condition:**
    - We know that \(\|\mathbf{a}\| = \|\mathbf{b}\| = k\) for some constant \(k\).
    - Therefore, \(\|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\| = k\).

2. **Use the Formula for the Norm of a Sum of Vectors:**
    - The norm of \(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\) is given by:
      \[
      \|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\|^2 = (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) \cdot (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) = \|\mathbf{a}\|^2 + \|\mathbf{b}\|^2 + 2 \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}
      \]
    - Substituting \(\|\mathbf{a}\| = \|\mathbf{b}\| = k\), we get:
      \[
      \|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\|^2 = k^2 + k^2 + 2 \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 2k^2 + 2 \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}
      \]
    - Since \(\|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\| = k\), it follows that:
      \[
      \|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\|^2 = k^2
      \]
    - Therefore, we have:
      \[
      2k^2 + 2 \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = k^2
      \]
    - Simplifying this equation, we get:
      \[
      2 \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = k^2 - 2k^2 = -k^2 \implies 2 \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = -k^2 \implies \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = -\frac{k^2}{2}
      \]

3. **Use the Dot Product Definition:**
    - The dot product \(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\| \|\mathbf{b}\| \cos \theta = k \cdot k \cdot \cos \theta = k^2 \cos \theta\).
    - Substituting this into the equation \(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = -\frac{k^2}{2}\), we have:
      \[
      k^2 \cos \theta = -\frac{k^2}{2}
      \]
    - Dividing both sides by \(k^2\) (since \(k \neq 0\)), we get:
      \[
      \cos \theta = -\frac{1}{2}
      \]

4. **Find the Angle:**
    - The angle \(\theta\) that satisfies \(\cos \theta = -\frac{1}{2}\) is \(\theta = 120^\circ\).

```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the angle symbolically
theta = sp.symbols('theta', real=True, positive=True)

# Equation based on cos(theta) = -1/2
equation = sp.Eq(sp.cos(theta), -1/2)

# Solve the equation for theta in degrees
solution = sp.solve(equation, theta)

# Convert the solution to degrees
angle_in_degrees = [sp.deg(sol.evalf()) for sol in solution if sol.evalf() > 0 and sol.evalf() < sp.pi]

print(angle_in_degrees)
```
