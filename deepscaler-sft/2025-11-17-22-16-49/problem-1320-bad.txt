To solve the problem, we need to determine the focus of the parabola given by the equation \( y = 4x^2 - 3 \).

1. **Understand the Equation:**
    - The standard form of a parabola that opens upwards or downwards is given by \( y = a(x - h)^2 + k \), where \((h, k)\) is the vertex of the parabola.
    - The given equation is \( y = 4x^2 - 3 \). This can be considered as \( y = 4(x - 0)^2 - 3 \), so the vertex of this parabola is \((0, -3)\).

2. **Identify Key Parameters:**
    - For a parabola of the form \( y = a(x - h)^2 + k \), the coefficient \( a \) determines the shape and direction of the parabola. Here, \( a = 4 \).
    - The focus of a parabola \( y = a(x - h)^2 + k \) is located at \((h, k + \frac{1}{4a})\).

3. **Find the Focus:**
    - For our specific parabola \( y = 4x^2 - 3 \), we have \( a = 4 \), \( h = 0 \), and \( k = -3 \).
    - The focus is at \((h, k + \frac{1}{4a}) = (0, -3 + \frac{1}{4 \cdot 4}) = (0, -3 + \frac{1}{16}) = (0, -3 + \frac{1}{16}) = (0, -3 + 0.0625) = (0, -2.9375) \).

Let's confirm this by implementing it in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
# Given the equation y = 4x^2 - 3, we have a = 4, h = 0, and k = -3.
a = 4
h = 0
k = -3

# Calculate the focus of the parabola
focus_y = k + 1 / (4 * a)
focus = (h, focus_y)
print(focus)
```
Code execution result: (0, -2.9375)

Thus, the focus of the parabola \( y = 4x^2 - 3 \) is \(\boxed{(-2.9375)}\). This corresponds to the point \((0, -2.9375)\) in coordinate form.
