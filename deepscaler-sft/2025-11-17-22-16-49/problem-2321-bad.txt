   The roots of the quadratic equation \(x^2 - x \sqrt{5} + 1 = 0\) can be found using the quadratic formula:
   \[
   x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
   \]

   \[
   x = \frac{\sqrt{5} \pm \sqrt{(\sqrt{5})^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot 1}}{2 \cdot 1} = \frac{\sqrt{5} \pm \sqrt{5 - 4}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{5} \pm 1}{2}
   \]
   So, the roots are:
   \[
   r = \frac{\sqrt{5} + 1}{2}, \quad s = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}

2. **Calculate \(r^8 + s^8\):**
   To find \(r^8 + s^8\), we can make use of properties of symmetric polynomials and recurrence relations. We start by using the relations derived from the original quadratic equation.

   By Vieta's formulas, we know:
   \[
   r + s = \sqrt{5} \quad \text{and} \quad rs = 1
   \]

   - First, calculate \(r^2 + s^2\):
     \[
     r^2 + s^2 = (r + s)^2 - 2rs = (\sqrt{5})^2 - 2 \cdot 1 = 5 - 2 = 3

     \[
