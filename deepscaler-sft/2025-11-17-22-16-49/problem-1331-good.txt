1. **Define the Relationship Between GCD and LCM:**
    - For any two integers \(a\) and \(b\), the product of their greatest common divisor (GCD) and least common multiple (LCM) is equal to the product of the numbers themselves:
      \[
      \text{GCD}(a, b) \times \text{LCM}(a, b) = a \times b
      \]
    - We know from the problem that:
      \[
      \text{GCD}(a, b) \times \text{LCM}(a, b) = 180
      \]
      Therefore, we have:
      \[
      a \times b = 180
      \]

    - Substituting these into the equation \(a \times b = 180\), we get:
      \[
      (dx) \times (dy) = 180 \implies d^2 \cdot (x \cdot y) = 180
      \]
    - This means that \(d^2\) must be a divisor of 180, and \(\text{GCD}(x, y) = 1\).
