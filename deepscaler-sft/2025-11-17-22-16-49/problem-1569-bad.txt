To solve the problem of finding the equation of the directrix of the parabola given by the equation \( y = -2x^2 + 4x - 8 \), let's follow these steps:

1. **Convert the Equation to Standard Form:**
   The standard form of a parabola that opens upwards or downwards is \((x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k)\), where \((h, k)\) is the vertex, and \(p\) is the distance from the vertex to the focus (or the directrix).

2. **Find the Vertex:**
   To convert the given equation to standard form, we need to complete the square.

   Given equation:
   \[
   y = -2x^2 + 4x - 8
   \]

   Factor out the coefficient of \(x^2\) from the first two terms:
   \[
   y = -2(x^2 - 2x) - 8
   \]

   Complete the square for the expression inside the parentheses:
   \[
   x^2 - 2x \quad \text{can be written as} \quad (x - 1)^2 - 1
   \]
   So we get:
   \[
   y = -2((x - 1)^2 - 1) - 8 = -2(x - 1)^2 + 2 - 8 = -2(x - 1)^2 - 6
   \]

   Thus, the equation in vertex form is:
   \[
   y = -2(x - 1)^2 - 6
   \]
   Here, the vertex \((h, k)\) of the parabola is \((1, -6)\).

3. **Find the Focus and Directrix:**
   The standard form \((x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k)\) for a parabola that opens upwards or downwards implies that \(4p = -\frac{1}{-2} = \frac{1}{2}\), so \(4p = \frac{1}{2}\) and thus \( p = \frac{1}{8} \).

   For a parabola of the form \((x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k)\), the directrix is given by the equation \( y = k - p \). Here, \( k = -6 \) and \( p = \frac{1}{8} \), so the equation of the directrix is:
   \[
   y = -6 - \frac{1}{8} = -\frac{48}{8} - \frac{1}{8} = -\frac{49}{8}
   \]

Let's verify this with Python to ensure our solution is accurate.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
x, y = sp.symbols('x y')

# Define the equation of the parabola
parabola_eq = -2*x**2 + 4*x - 8

# Find the vertex of the parabola by completing the square or using sympy's method
vertex = sp.solve(sp.diff(parabola_eq, x), x)[0]
y_vertex = parabola_eq.subs(x, vertex)

# Get the coefficient of the x^2 term to find 4p
coeff_x2 = -2
four_p = 1 / (4 * coeff_x2)

# Get p
p = four_p / 4

# Get the equation of the directrix
k = y_vertex
directrix = k - p

print(directrix)
```
