To solve this problem, we can use the concept of relative speed and distance.

1. **Setup the Problem:**
    - The younger brother starts walking at a speed of 4 km/h.
    - Two hours after the younger brother starts, the older brother starts riding a bicycle at a speed of 20 km/h.
    - When the older brother starts, the younger brother has already been walking for 2 hours, so the younger brother has a head start of \(4 \, \text{km/h} \times 2 \, \text{hours} = 8 \, \text{km}\).

2. **Formulate the Equations:**
    - Let's say it takes \( t \) hours for the older brother to catch up with the younger brother after the older brother starts.
    - In \( t \) hours, the younger brother will have walked an additional distance of \( 4 \, \text{km/h} \times t \, \text{hours} = 4t \, \text{km} \).
    - So, the total distance the younger brother has traveled when the older brother catches up is \( 8 \, \text{km} + 4t \, \text{km} \).
    - In the same \( t \) hours, the older brother will have traveled a distance of \( 20 \, \text{km/h} \times t \, \text{hours} = 20t \, \text{km} \).
    - When the older brother catches up with the younger brother, they will have traveled the same distance, so we can set up the following equation:
      \[
      8 + 4t = 20t
      \]

3. **Solve the Equation:**
    - Rearrange the equation to solve for \( t \):
      \[
      8 = 20t - 4t \implies 8 = 16t \implies t = \frac{8}{16} = \frac{1}{2} \, \text{hours}
      \]

Let's implement this reasoning in Python to confirm our solution.
```python
from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variable
t = symbols('t')

# Set up the equation based on the distances traveled by the younger and older brother
# 8 + 4 * t = 20 * t
equation = Eq(8 + 4 * t, 20 * t)

# Solve for t
time_to_catch_up = solve(equation, t)[0]
print(time_to_catch_up)
```
Code execution result: 1/2

So, it will take the older brother \(\frac{1}{2}\) hours to catch up with the younger brother after the older brother starts.

To get the total time the older brother has been traveling (including the time the younger brother was already walking for 2 hours), we need to add those 2 hours to the time it took him to catch up. Hence, the total time taken for the older brother to catch up with the younger brother is:

\[ 2 \, \text{hours} + \frac{1}{2} \, \text{hours} = 2.5 \, \text{hours} \]

So, the final answer is:

\(\boxed{2.5}\)
