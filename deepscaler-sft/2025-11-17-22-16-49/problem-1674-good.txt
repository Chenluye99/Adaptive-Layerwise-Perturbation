To solve the problem, we need to find the standard form of the circle's equation by completing the square for the given equation \(2x^2 + 2y^2 + 10x - 6y - 18 = 0\).

1. **Rewrite the Equation:**
   First, we can divide the entire equation by 2 to simplify it:
   \[
   x^2 + y^2 + 5x - 3y - 9 = 0
   \]

2. **Complete the Square:**
   - For the \(x\) terms: \(x^2 + 5x\)
     - Take the coefficient of \(x\), which is 5, divide it by 2, and square it: \(\left(\frac{5}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{25}{4}\).
     - Add and subtract \(\frac{25}{4}\) inside the equation.
   - For the \(y\) terms: \(y^2 - 3y\)
     - Take the coefficient of \(y\), which is -3, divide it by 2, and square it: \(\left(\frac{-3}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{4}\).
     - Add and subtract \(\frac{9}{4}\) inside the equation.

   So, the equation becomes:
   \[
   \left(x^2 + 5x + \frac{25}{4}\right) - \frac{25}{4} + \left(y^2 - 3y + \frac{9}{4}\right) - \frac{9}{4} - 9 = 0
   \]
   Simplify it:
   \[
   \left(x + \frac{5}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{25}{4} + \left(y - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{9}{4} - 9 = 0
   \]
   Combine the constants:
   \[
   \left(x + \frac{5}{2}\right)^2 + \left(y - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{25}{4} - \frac{9}{4} - 9 = 0
   \]
   \[
   \left(x + \frac{5}{2}\right)^2 + \left(y - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{34}{4} - 9 = 0
   \]
   \[
   \left(x + \frac{5}{2}\right)^2 + \left(y - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{34}{4} - \frac{36}{4} = 0
   \]
   \[
   \left(x + \frac{5}{2}\right)^2 + \left(y - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{70}{4} = 0
   \]
   \[
   \left(x + \frac{5}{2}\right)^2 + \left(y - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{70}{4} = \frac{35}{2}
   \]

   This is now in the standard form of a circle's equation \((x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2\), where \((h, k)\) is the center and \(r\) is the radius. So, the radius \(r\) of the circle is \(\sqrt{\frac{35}{2}}\).

3. **Find the Area of the Circle:**
   The area \(A\) of a circle is given by the formula \(A = \pi r^2\). Substituting the radius, we get:
   \[
   A = \pi \left( \frac{35}{2} \right) = \frac{35 \pi}{2}
   \]
