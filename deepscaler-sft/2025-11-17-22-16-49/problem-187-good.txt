Let's solve the problem step-by-step.

1. **Understand the Problem:**
    - We are given two non-zero vectors \(\overrightarrow{a}\) and \(\overrightarrow{b}\) such that \(|\overrightarrow{a}| = 2|\overrightarrow{b}|\).
    - We also know that \((\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}) \bot \overrightarrow{b}\), which means the dot product of \((\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b})\) and \(\overrightarrow{b}\) is zero: \((\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}) \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 0\).

2. **Formulate the Equation:**
    - Use the dot product properties: \((\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}) \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 0\).
    - This simplifies to: \(\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = |\overrightarrow{b}|^2\).

3. **Express \(\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}\) in Terms of Magnitudes and Angle:**
    - The dot product \(\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = |\overrightarrow{a}| |\overrightarrow{b}| \cos \theta\), where \(\theta\) is the angle between \(\overrightarrow{a}\) and \(\overrightarrow{b}\).
    - We also know that \(|\overrightarrow{a}| = 2 |\overrightarrow{b}|\), so substitute this into the dot product equation:
      \[
      |\overrightarrow{a}| |\overrightarrow{b}| \cos \theta = |\overrightarrow{b}|^2 \implies (2 |\overrightarrow{b}|) |\overrightarrow{b}| \cos \theta = |\overrightarrow{b}|^2 \implies 2 |\overrightarrow{b}|^2 \cos \theta = |\overrightarrow{b}|^2.
      \]
    - Dividing both sides by \(|\overrightarrow{b}|^2\) (since \(|\overrightarrow{b}| \neq 0\)):
      \[
      2 \cos \theta = 1 \implies \cos \theta = \frac{1}{2}.
      \]
    - The angle \(\theta\) that satisfies \(\cos \theta = \frac{1}{2}\) is \(\theta = \frac{\pi}{3}\).

Let's confirm this by coding it in Python using sympy.

```python
import sympy as sp

# Define variables
theta = sp.symbols('theta')

# Equation based on the above derivation
eq = sp.Eq(2 * sp.cos(theta), 1)

# Solve for theta
solution = sp.solve(eq, theta)

# Filter the solution to get the angle between 0 and pi
angle = [sol.evalf() for sol in solution if 0 <= sol <= sp.pi][0]
print(angle)
```
