First, let's set up the problem. We need to find the value of \( k \) such that a line with slope \( k \) intersects the parabola \( y^2 = 4x \) at points \( A \) and \( B \), and is tangent to the circle \((x - 5)^2 + y^2 = 9\) at point \( M \), where \( M \) is the midpoint of segment \( AB \).

1. **Equation of the Line:**
   The equation of a line with slope \( k \) can be written as \( y = kx + c \). Substituting this into the equation of the parabola \( y^2 = 4x \), we get:
   \[
   (kx + c)^2 = 4x \implies k^2 x^2 + 2ckx + c^2 = 4x \implies k^2 x^2 + (2ck - 4)x + c^2 = 0.
   \]
   This is a quadratic equation in \( x \), and its roots are the x-coordinates of points \( A \) and \( B \). Let the roots be \( x_1 \) and \( x_2 \). By Vieta's formulas, we have:
   \[
   x_1 + x_2 = \frac{4 - 2ck}{k^2}.
   \]
   The corresponding y-coordinates are \( y_1 = kx_1 + c \) and \( y_2 = kx_2 + c \). The midpoint \( M \) of segment \( AB \) is given by:
   \[
   M \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right) = M \left( \frac{2 - ck}{k^2}, \frac{k(x_1 + x_2) + 2c}{2} \right) = M \left( \frac{2 - ck}{k^2}, \frac{k \cdot \frac{4 - 2ck}{k^2} + 2c}{2} \right).
   \]
   Simplifying, we get:
   \[
   M \left( \frac{2 - ck}{k^2}, \frac{\frac{4k - 2ck^2 + 2ck^2}{k^2}}{2} \right) = M \left( \frac{2 - ck}{k^2}, \frac{4k}{2k^2} \right) = M \left( \frac{2 - ck}{k^2}, \frac{2}{k} \right).
   \]

2. **Tangent Condition to the Circle:**
   The circle is given by \((x - 5)^2 + y^2 = 9\). The condition for the line \( y = kx + c \) to be tangent to the circle is that the perpendicular distance from the center of the circle \((5, 0)\) to the line is equal to the radius of the circle, which is 3. The perpendicular distance from a point \((x_0, y_0)\) to a line \( Ax + By + C = 0 \) is given by:
   \[
   \text{Distance} = \frac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}.
   \]
   For the line \( y = kx + c \) or equivalently \( kx - y + c = 0 \), and the point \((5, 0)\), the distance is:
   \[
   \frac{|5k + c|}{\sqrt{k^2 + 1}} = 3.
   \]
   Squaring both sides, we get:
   \[
   (5k + c)^2 = 9(k^2 + 1).
   \]
   Expanding and simplifying, we get:
   \[
   25k^2 + 10kc + c^2 = 9k^2 + 9 \implies 16k^2 + 10kc + c^2 - 9 = 0.
   \]
