Let's start by analyzing the given triangle \(PQR\):

- \( \angle QPR = 30^\circ \)
- \( PR = 9 \sqrt{3} \)
- \( \angle PQR = 90^\circ \)

We are asked to find the length of \( PQ \).

Given that \( \angle QPR = 30^\circ \) and \( \angle PQR = 90^\circ \), \( \angle PRQ = 60^\circ \). This makes triangle \( PQR \) a right triangle with \( \angle QPR = 30^\circ \), so it is a 30-60-90 triangle.

In a 30-60-90 triangle, the sides are in the ratio \( 1 : \sqrt{3} : 2 \). Here, \( PR \) is the side opposite the 30-degree angle, and \( PQ \) is the side opposite the 60-degree angle.

Let's denote the sides of the triangle as follows:
- The side opposite the 30-degree angle (which is \( PR \)) is \( 9 \sqrt{3} \).
- The side opposite the 60-degree angle (which is \( PQ \)) is \( PQ \).

In a 30-60-90 triangle, the side opposite the 60-degree angle is \(\sqrt{3}\) times the side opposite the 30-degree angle. So, we can set up the following equation:

\[ PQ = 9 \sqrt{3} \cdot \sqrt{3} = 9 \cdot 3 = 27 \]

Let's confirm this by coding it to ensure the result is accurate.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Given values
PR = 9 * sp.sqrt(3)

# In a 30-60-90 triangle, the side opposite the 60 degree angle is sqrt(3) times the side opposite the 30 degree angle.
PQ = PR * sp.sqrt(3)

print(PQ)
```
Code execution result: 27

Thus, the length of \( PQ \) is \(\boxed{27}\). This confirms that our reasoning and solution are accurate.
