First, we need to find \( N \) by computing \( 34 \cdot 34 \cdot 63 \cdot 270 \). Once we have \( N \), we can find all its divisors, both odd and even, and then compute their respective sums.

Let's start by calculating \( N \):

\[ N = 34 \cdot 34 \cdot 63 \cdot 270 \]

Afterward, we will find all divisors of \( N \) and then separate those divisors into odd and even categories. Finally, we will calculate the sums of these odd and even divisors and find the ratio of these sums.

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python.
```python
import numpy as np

# Step 1: Calculate N
N = 34 * 34 * 63 * 270

# Step 2: Find all divisors of N
divisors = [i for i in range(1, N + 1) if N % i == 0]

# Step 3: Separate odd and even divisors and calculate their sums
odd_divisors = [d for d in divisors if d % 2 != 0]
even_divisors = [d for d in divisors if d % 2 == 0]

sum_odd_divisors = sum(odd_divisors)
sum_even_divisors = sum(even_divisors)

# Step 4: Calculate the ratio of the sum of the odd divisors to the sum of the even divisors
ratio = sum_odd_divisors / sum_even_divisors
print(ratio)
```
Code execution result: 0.07142857142857142

Thus, the ratio of the sum of the odd divisors of \( N \) to the sum of the even divisors of \( N \) is \(\boxed{0.07142857142857142}\). This can also be expressed as \(\frac{1}{14}\) if you prefer a fractional form.
