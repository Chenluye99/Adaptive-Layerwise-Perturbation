    - So, the area of one equilateral triangle is \( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 2^2 = \sqrt{3} \).
    - Since there are 6 such triangles, the area of the hexagon is \( 6 \cdot \sqrt{3} = 6 \sqrt{3} \).

    - The area of a full circle with radius 1 is \( \pi \cdot 1^2 = \pi \), so the area of one semicircle is \( \frac{\pi}{2} \).
    - Since there are six semicircles, the total area of the six semicircles is \( 6 \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = 3 \pi \).

3. **Find the Area of the Shaded Region**:
    - The shaded region is the area of the hexagon minus the area of the six semicircles.
    - So, the area of the shaded region is \( 6 \sqrt{3} - 3 \pi \).
