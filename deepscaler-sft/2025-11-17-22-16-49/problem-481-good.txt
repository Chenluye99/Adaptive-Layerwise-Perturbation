To solve this problem, let's start by setting up the equations for the distance each person has traveled.

1. **Set Up the Equations:**
    - The younger brother starts walking 2 hours before his older brother and walks at a speed of 4 kilometers per hour.
    - The older brother starts riding his bicycle at a speed of 20 kilometers per hour after his younger brother has already been walking for 2 hours.
    - We need to find out how long it takes for the older brother to catch up with the younger brother. Let's call this time \( t \) (in hours) after the older brother starts riding.

2. **Distance Calculation:**
    - By the time the older brother starts riding, the younger brother has already walked for 2 hours. So, the younger brother has walked a distance of \( 4 \text{ km/h} \times 2 \text{ h} = 8 \text{ km} \).
    - After the older brother starts riding, the younger brother continues to walk at 4 kilometers per hour, and the older brother rides at 20 kilometers per hour. The distance covered by the younger brother after the older brother starts riding is \( 4t \) (since his speed is 4 km/h and he walks for \( t \) hours). Adding the initial 8 km, the total distance walked by the younger brother when the older brother catches up is \( 8 + 4t \).
    - The distance covered by the older brother in \( t \) hours is \( 20t \).

3. **Set Up the Equation to Find When They Meet:**
    - The older brother catches up to the younger brother when both have covered the same distance, so we set their distances equal to each other:
      \[
      8 + 4t = 20t
      \]

4. **Solve for \( t \):**
    - Rearrange the equation to solve for \( t \):
      \[
      8 = 20t - 4t \implies 8 = 16t \implies t = \frac{8}{16} = \frac{1}{2}
      \]

Thus, it will take the older brother \( \frac{1}{2} \) hour to catch up with the younger brother. Let's confirm this by coding the solution in Python.
```python
from sympy import symbols, Eq, solve

# Define the variable
t = symbols('t', positive=True)

# Set up the equation based on the problem statement
equation = Eq(8 + 4 * t, 20 * t)

# Solve the equation
solution = solve(equation, t)[0]
print(solution)
```
Code execution result: 1/2

Thus, the solution obtained from both reasoning and code execution is that it will take the older brother \(\frac{1}{2}\) hour to catch up with the younger brother. 

So, the final answer is \(\boxed{\frac{1}{2}}\). This means it takes 30 minutes for the older brother to catch up with the younger brother.
