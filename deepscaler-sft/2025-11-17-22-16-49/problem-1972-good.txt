To solve this problem, we need to find the length of the handrail of a spiral staircase.

1. **Understanding the Problem:**
    - The staircase turns \(270^\circ\) (or \(\frac{3}{4} \times 360^\circ = 270^\circ\)).
    - The staircase rises 10 feet vertically.
    - The radius of the staircase is 3 feet.

2. **Visualizing the Problem:**
    - When we "unroll" the spiral staircase, we get a right-angled triangle where:
      - One side of the triangle is the vertical rise of the staircase, which is 10 feet.
      - The other side of the triangle is the distance traveled along the circumference of a circle if unwound for \(270^\circ\).

3. **Calculating the Length Traveled Along the Circumference:**
    - The full circumference of a circle with radius 3 feet is \(2 \pi \times 3 = 6 \pi\).
    - For \(270^\circ\), which is \(\frac{3}{4}\) of a full circle, the length traveled along the circumference is \(\frac{3}{4} \times 6 \pi = \frac{3}{4} \times 6 \pi = \frac{18 \pi}{4} = \frac{9 \pi}{2}\).

4. **Using the Pythagorean Theorem:**
    - The length of the handrail is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle with legs of lengths 10 feet and \(\frac{9 \pi}{2}\) feet.
    - We use the Pythagorean theorem: \( L = \sqrt{\left(10\right)^2 + \left(\frac{9 \pi}{2}\right)^2} \).

Let's compute this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Given values
vertical_rise = 10
radius = 3
angle_degrees = 270

# Calculate the length traveled along the circumference for 270 degrees
angle_radians = math.radians(angle_degrees)
circumference = 2 * math.pi * radius
length_traveled_along_circumference = (angle_degrees / 360) * circumference

# Use Pythagorean theorem to get the length of the handrail
handrail_length = math.sqrt(vertical_rise**2 + length_traveled_along_circumference**2)

print(handrail_length)
```
