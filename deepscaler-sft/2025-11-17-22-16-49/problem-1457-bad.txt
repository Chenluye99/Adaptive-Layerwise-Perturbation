First, let's extract the data from the stem-and-leaf plot and then compute the mean height of the players.

The stem-and-leaf plot can be translated into a list of heights as follows:

- \( 4|9 \) represents 49 inches.
- \( 5|2 \; 3 \; 5 \; 8 \; 8 \; 9 \) represents 52, 53, 55, 58, 58, 59 inches.
- \( 6|0 \; 1 \; 1 \; 2 \; 6 \; 8 \; 9 \; 9 \) represents 60, 61, 61, 62, 66, 68, 69, 69 inches.

So, the list of heights is: \([49, 52, 53, 55, 58, 58, 59, 60, 61, 61, 62, 66, 68, 69, 69]\).

To find the mean height, we need to compute the average of these heights. The mean is given by the formula:
\[
\text{Mean} = \frac{\sum \text{heights}}{\text{number of heights}}
\]

Let's use Python to calculate this accurately.
```python
# List of heights based on the stem-and-leaf plot
heights = [49, 52, 53, 55, 58, 58, 59, 60, 61, 61, 62, 66, 68, 69, 69]

# Calculate the mean height
mean_height = sum(heights) / len(heights)
print(mean_height)
```
Code execution result: 60.0

Thus, the mean height of the players on the team is \(\boxed{60}\) inches. This is our final answer.
