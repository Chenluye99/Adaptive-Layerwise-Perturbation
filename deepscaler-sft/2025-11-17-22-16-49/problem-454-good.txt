1. **Generate all five-digit numbers.**
   Five-digit numbers range from 10000 to 99999.

2. **Count the numbers that are not divisible by 5.**
   A number is not divisible by 5 if its last digit is not 0 or 5.

3. **Count the numbers whose first and second digits from the left are not a 5.**
   We need to check the first and second digits of each number and ensure that neither of them is 5.

4. **Compare the two counts to determine which of the two sets is larger.**

Let's implement this step-by-step in Python.
```python
# Step 1: Generate all five-digit numbers
five_digit_numbers = list(range(10000, 100000))

# Step 2: Count the numbers that are not divisible by 5
count_not_divisible_by_5 = sum(1 for num in five_digit_numbers if num % 5 != 0)

# Step 3: Count the numbers whose first and second digits are not 5
count_first_and_second_digits_not_5 = sum(1 for num in five_digit_numbers if str(num)[0] != '5' and str(num)[1] != '5')

# Step 4: Compare the two counts
print(((count_not_divisible_by_5, count_first_and_second_digits_not_5, count_not_divisible_by_5 > count_first_and_second_digits_not_5)))
```
