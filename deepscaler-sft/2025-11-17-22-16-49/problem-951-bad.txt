To solve this problem, we need to find the areas of circles \(A\) and \(B\) and then compute the ratio of their areas.

1. **Calculate the Radii of the Circles:**
    - Circle \(A\) has a diameter of 12 inches, so its radius is \( \frac{12}{2} = 6 \) inches.
    - Circle \(B\) has a diameter of 22 inches, so its radius is \( \frac{22}{2} = 11 \) inches.

2. **Calculate the Areas of the Circles:**
    - The area of a circle is given by the formula \( \text{Area} = \pi \cdot \text{radius}^2 \).
    - Therefore, the area of circle \(A\) is \( \pi \cdot 6^2 = 36\pi \).
    - The area of circle \(B\) is \( \pi \cdot 11^2 = 121\pi \).

3. **Find the Ratio of the Areas:**
    - The ratio of the area of circle \(A\) to the area of circle \(B\) is given by:
      \[
      \frac{\text{Area of circle } A}{\text{Area of circle } B} = \frac{36\pi}{121\pi} = \frac{36}{121}
      \]
    - The \(\pi\) terms cancel out, leaving us with the ratio \(\frac{36}{121}\).

We can confirm this by using Python to ensure our result is accurate.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the radii of the circles
radius_A = 6
radius_B = 11

# Calculate the areas of the circles
area_A = sp.pi * radius_A ** 2
area_B = sp.pi * radius_B ** 2

# Calculate the ratio of the areas
ratio = area_A / area_B
print(ratio)
```
Code execution result: 36/121

Thus, the ratio of the area of circle \(A\) to the area of circle \(B\) is \(\boxed{\frac{36}{121}}\). This confirms our solution is accurate.
