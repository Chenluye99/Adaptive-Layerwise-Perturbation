First, let's define the steps to solve this problem systematically.

1. **Define the Suggested Retail Price (SRP):**
   Let's assume the suggested retail price (SRP) of the book is \( P \).

2. **Determine the Marked Price:**
   The marked price of the book is 30% less than the suggested retail price. So, the marked price can be calculated as:
   \[
   \text{Marked Price} = P \times (1 - 0.30) = 0.70 \times P
   \]

3. **Determine the Price Alice Paid:**
   Alice purchased the book for half the marked price during the Fiftieth Anniversary sale. So, the price Alice paid is:
   \[
   \text{Price Alice Paid} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Marked Price} = \frac{1}{2} \times 0.70 \times P = 0.35 \times P
   \]

4. **Find the Percentage of the Suggested Retail Price Alice Paid:**
   To find out what percentage of the suggested retail price Alice paid, we compute:
   \[
   \text{Percentage of SRP} = \left( \frac{\text{Price Alice Paid}}{P} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{0.35 \times P}{P} \right) \times 100 = 0.35 \times 100 = 35\%
   \]

We'll verify this step-by-step process using Python to ensure our result is accurate.
```python
# Let's define the suggested retail price as P
P = 1  # We can assume P to be 1 (100%) for simplicity, as it will cancel out in the percentage calculation

# Calculate the marked price which is 30% less than the suggested retail price
marked_price = P * (1 - 0.30)

# Calculate the price Alice paid, which is half of the marked price
price_alice_paid = 0.5 * marked_price

# Calculate what percentage of the suggested retail price Alice paid
percentage_of_srp = (price_alice_paid / P) * 100
print(percentage_of_srp)
```
Code execution result: 35.0

Thus, Alice paid \(\boxed{35}\) percent of the suggested retail price. This confirms our solution is accurate.
