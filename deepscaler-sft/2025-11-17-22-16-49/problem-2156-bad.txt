To solve the problem of factoring the expression \( x^8 - 256 \), we can follow these steps:

1. **Recognize the Structure of the Expression:**
    - Notice that 256 is \( 2^8 \), so the expression can be written as \( x^8 - 2^8 \).
    - This is a difference of squares, and we can use the difference of squares formula \( a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b) \) repeatedly to factor the expression.

2. **First Application of Difference of Squares:**
    - Rewrite \( x^8 - 2^8 \) as \( (x^4)^2 - (2^4)^2 \), which is \( (x^4 - 16)(x^4 + 16) \).
    - Notice that \( 16 = 2^4 \), so we can further factor \( x^4 - 16 \) using the difference of squares again, because \( x^4 - 16 = (x^2)^2 - 4^2 = (x^2 - 4)(x^2 + 4) \).
    - We can factor \( x^2 - 4 \) again using the difference of squares, because \( x^2 - 4 = (x)^2 - (2)^2 = (x - 2)(x + 2) \).
    - Now, \( x^4 + 16 \) cannot be factored further over the real numbers because it does not have real roots and is irreducible over the reals in its current form. However, it can be factored over the complex numbers, but for most practical purposes, we will stick to factoring over the reals unless otherwise specified.

3. **Second Application of Difference of Squares:**
    - Next, consider \( x^2 - 4 \), which we already factored as \( (x - 2)(x + 2) \).
    - So, we have \( x^4 - 16 = (x^2 - 4)(x^2 + 4) = (x - 2)(x + 2)(x^2 + 4) \).
    - Therefore, the full factorization of \( x^8 - 256 \) over the reals is:

      x^8 - 256 = (x - 2)(x + 2)(x^2 + 4)(x^4 + 16)
