First, let's understand the problem and solve it step-by-step.

1. **Identify the Sides of the First Triangle:**
    - We are given two sides of an isosceles triangle as 10 inches and 20 inches.
    - In an isosceles triangle, two sides are equal. So, the possible configurations for the sides of the triangle are:
        - 10, 10, 20
        - 20, 20, 10
    - Clearly, a triangle with sides 10, 10, 20 is invalid because it does not satisfy the triangle inequality theorem (10 + 10 is not greater than 20). Therefore, the only valid configuration is 20, 20, 10, or 20, 20, 10 (same thing, just different order).

2. **Identify the Similar Triangle:**
    - We are given that the shortest side of a similar triangle is 50 inches. Since the shortest side of the original triangle is 10 inches, the scaling factor between the two similar triangles is \(\frac{50}{10} = 5\).
    - This scaling factor of 5 means that each side of the larger triangle is 5 times the corresponding side of the smaller triangle.

3. **Determine the Sides of the Larger Triangle:**
    - If the original triangle has sides 20, 20, 10, then the larger triangle will have sides that are 5 times the lengths of these sides:
        - 20 * 5 = 100
        - 20 * 5 = 100
        - 10 * 5 = 50
    - So, the sides of the larger triangle are 100, 100, and 50.

4. **Calculate the Perimeter of the Larger Triangle:**
    - The perimeter of a triangle is the sum of its sides. For the larger triangle, the perimeter will be \(100 + 100 + 50 = 250\).
