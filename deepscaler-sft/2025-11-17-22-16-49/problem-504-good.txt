### Step-by-Step Solution

1. **Setup the Coordinates:**
    - Place point \( B \) at the origin, so \( B = (0, 0) \).
    - Place point \( A \) at \( ( \sqrt{3}, 0 ) \).
    - Place point \( D \) at \( ( x_D, y_D ) \) such that \( AD = 1 \), so \((x_D - \sqrt{3})^2 + y_D^2 = 1 \).
    - Place point \( C \) at \( ( x_C, y_C ) \) such that \( CB = 1 \), so \(x_C^2 + y_C^2 = 1 \), and \( DC = 1 \), so \((x_C - x_D)^2 + (y_C - y_D)^2 = 1 \).

2. **Calculate the Areas \( S \) and \( T \):**
    - The area of triangle \( \triangle ABD \) with vertices at \( A(\sqrt{3}, 0) \), \( B(0, 0) \), and \( D(x_D, y_D) \) is given by:
      \[
      S = \frac{1}{2} \left| \sqrt{3} \cdot 0 + 0 \cdot y_D + x_D \cdot 0 - (0 \cdot 0 + 0 \cdot x_D + \sqrt{3} \cdot y_D) \right| = \frac{1}{2} \left| -\sqrt{3} y_D \right| = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} |y_D|
      \]
    - The area of triangle \( \triangle BCD \) with vertices at \( B(0, 0) \), \( C(x_C, y_C) \), and \( D(x_D, y_D) \) is given by:
      \[
      T = \frac{1}{2} \left| 0 \cdot y_C + x_C \cdot y_D + x_D \cdot 0 - (0 \cdot x_C + 0 \cdot y_D + x_C \cdot y_D) \right| = \frac{1}{2} \left| x_C y_D - x_D y_C \right|
      \]

3. **Set Up the Constraints and Optimization:**
    - We need to maximize \( S^2 + T^2 \) under the constraints given by the distances and the unit lengths of \( AD, DC, \) and \( CB \).

We will use SymPy to solve this optimization problem.
