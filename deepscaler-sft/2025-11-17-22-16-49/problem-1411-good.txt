First, let's understand the problem. We have 9 representatives (3 from England, 4 from Germany, and 2 from France) and we need to arrange them around a circular table such that all representatives from the same country sit together. To solve this, we can consider each group of representatives from the same country as a single "super person". So, we will consider there are 3 groups (one for England, one for Germany, and one for France) and arrange these 3 groups around a circular table.

1. **Arrange 3 Groups Around a Circular Table:**
    - When arranging \( n \) objects in a circle, we fix one position and arrange the others relative to this fixed position. Hence, the number of ways to arrange 3 groups around a circular table is \((3 - 1)!\), which is \( 2! = 2 \) ways. We fix one group, and then arrange the other 2 groups around this fixed group, which gives 2 ways to arrange 3 items in a circle.

2. **Arrange Representatives Within Each Country:**
    - Within the group of 3 English representatives, there are \( 3! \) ways to arrange them.
    - Within the group of 4 German representatives, there are \( 4! \) ways to arrange them.
    - Within the group of 2 French representatives, there are \( 2! \) ways to arrange them.

Combining both parts, the total number of ways is the product of the ways to arrange the groups around the table and the ways to arrange the representatives within their respective groups.

So, the formula is:
\[ \text{Total ways} = 2! \times 3! \times 4! \times 2! \]

Let's compute this step-by-step using Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import math

# Calculate the factorial values
factorial_2 = math.factorial(2)
factorial_3 = math.factorial(3)
factorial_4 = math.factorial(4)
factorial_2_2 = math.factorial(2)

# Calculate the total number of ways based on our formula
total_ways = factorial_2 * factorial_3 * factorial_4 * factorial_2_2
print(total_ways)
```
Code execution result: 576

Thus, the number of ways all nine representatives can sit around a circular table such that representatives of the same country sit together is \(\boxed{576}\). This is the final answer.
