First, let's establish the given conditions in mathematical form:

1. The magnitude of $\overrightarrow{a}$ is $|\overrightarrow{a}| = \sqrt{2}$.
2. The magnitude of $\overrightarrow{b}$ is $|\overrightarrow{b}| = 2$.
3. The vectors $(\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b})$ and $\overrightarrow{a}$ are perpendicular, so their dot product is zero: $(\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}) \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = 0$.

Let's expand the dot product condition:

\[
(\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}) \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = 0
\]

This means:

\[
\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = \overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a}
\]

We know that $\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = |\overrightarrow{a}|^2 = (\sqrt{2})^2 = 2$. So, we get:

\[
2 = \overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a}
\]

The dot product $\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a}$ can also be written using the magnitudes of the vectors $\overrightarrow{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{b}$, and the cosine of the angle $\theta$ between them as follows:

\[
\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = |\overrightarrow{b}| |\overrightarrow{a}| \cos \theta = 2 \cdot \sqrt{2} \cdot \cos \theta = 2 \sqrt{2} \cos \theta
\]

So, we set this equal to 2:

\[
2 = 2 \sqrt{2} \cos \theta
\]

Dividing both sides by $2 \sqrt{2}$, we get:

\[
\cos \theta = \frac{2}{2 \sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\]

The angle $\theta$ that satisfies $\cos \theta = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ is $\theta = \frac{\pi}{4}$.

Let's verify this solution using Python to ensure its accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variables
theta = sp.symbols('theta', real=True, positive=True)

# Equation based on the derived cosine value
eq = sp.Eq(sp.cos(theta), sp.sqrt(2) / 2)

# Solve the equation for theta
solution = sp.solve(eq, theta)

print(solution)
```
