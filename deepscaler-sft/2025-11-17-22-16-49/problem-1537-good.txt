To solve the problem, let's start with what we know about the pyramid:

1. The pyramid has an equilateral triangular base.
2. The area of one lateral face of the pyramid is 75 square meters.
3. The slant height of the pyramid is 30 meters.

A lateral face of the pyramid is a triangle that has its base as one side of the equilateral triangular base of the pyramid and its height as the slant height of the pyramid.

The formula for the area of a triangle is given by:
\[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} \]

For one of the lateral faces of the pyramid, if the length of the side of the equilateral triangular base is \( s \), then the base of the lateral face triangle is \( s \) and its height (slant height of the pyramid) is 30 meters. The area of this lateral face is given as 75 square meters. So, we can set up the equation:
\[ 75 = \frac{1}{2} \times s \times 30 \]

We can solve this equation for \( s \):
\[ 75 = 15s \]
\[ s = \frac{75}{15} \]
\[ s = 5 \]

So, the length of the side of the base of the pyramid is 5 meters. Let's confirm this by coding the solution in Python to ensure the accuracy of our result.
```python
# Given values
area_of_lateral_face = 75
slant_height = 30

# Calculate the side length s of the equilateral triangular base
s = (2 * area_of_lateral_face) / slant_height
print(s)
```
Code execution result: 5.0

Thus, the length of the side of the base of the pyramid is \(\boxed{5}\) meters. This confirms that our solution is accurate.
