First, let's extract the scores from the stem-and-leaf plot:

- 5 | 0 0 means the scores 50 and 50.
- 6 | 3 means the score 63.
- 7 | 7 8 means the scores 77 and 78.
- 8 | 2 6 7 9 9 9 9 means the scores 82, 86, 87, 89, 89, 89, and 89.
- 9 | 1 4 4 4 6 means the scores 91, 94, 94, 94, and 96.
- 10 | 0 0 0 means the scores 100, 100, and 100.

Now, let's combine all these scores into a list and find the mode. We can use Python to get an accurate result.
```python
# Extract the scores from the stem-and-leaf plot and combine them into a list
scores = [
    50, 50,  # from 5 | 0 0
    63,       # from 6 | 3
    77, 78,   # from 7 | 7 8
    82, 86, 87, 89, 89, 89, 89,  # from 8 | 2 6 7 9 9 9 9
    91, 94, 94, 94, 96,         # from 9 | 1 4 4 4 6
    100, 100, 100               # from 10 | 0 0 0
]

# Find the mode (the most frequently occurring score) using Python's collections.Counter
from collections import Counter

# Get the count of each score
score_counts = Counter(scores)

# Find the mode by finding the score with the maximum count
mode_score = score_counts.most_common(1)[0][0]

print(mode_score)
```
Code execution result: 89

Therefore, the mode of the scores is \(\boxed{89}\). This is the value that appears most frequently in the list of scores extracted from the stem-and-leaf plot.
