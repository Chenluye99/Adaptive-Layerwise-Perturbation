First, let's start with understanding the right triangle described in the problem.

1. **Properties of a 45°-45°-90° Triangle:**
    - A right triangle with a 45° angle is an isosceles right triangle, meaning both of its other angles are 45°, and its legs are of equal length.
    - In a 45°-45°-90° triangle, if the length of each leg is \( a \), then the hypotenuse is given by \( a \sqrt{2} \).

2. **Given Information:**
    - The hypotenuse of the triangle is \( 6 \sqrt{2} \) inches.
    - One of the angles is 45°, confirming that this is a 45°-45°-90° triangle.

3. **Finding the Length of the Legs:**
    - Using the property of 45°-45°-90° triangles, we can set up the equation for the hypotenuse: 
      \[
      a \sqrt{2} = 6 \sqrt{2}
      \]
    - By cancelling \(\sqrt{2}\) from both sides, we get:
      \[
      a = 6
      \]
    - Therefore, both legs of the triangle are 6 inches each.

4. **Calculating the Area of the Triangle:**
    - The area \( A \) of a right triangle is given by:
      \[
      A = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}
      \]
    - Here, both the base and the height are 6 inches, so:
      \[
      A = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 6 = \frac{1}{2} \times 36 = 18
      \]
