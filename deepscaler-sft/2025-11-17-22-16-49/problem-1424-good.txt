First, let's start by understanding the problem:

1. **Problem Statement Breakdown**:
    - The fan rotates at 80 revolutions per minute.
    - A point halfway between the center of the fan and the outer edge of the fan travels 97968 inches in 15 minutes.
    - We need to find out how far a point on the outer edge of the fan travels in 30 minutes.

2. **Formulate the Solution Steps**:
    - First, determine the radius of the fan and the speed of the point halfway between the center and the outer edge.
    - Use the information that a point halfway between the center and the outer edge of the fan travels 97968 inches in 15 minutes to find the radius of the fan.
    - Once the radius is known, determine the speed of a point on the outer edge of the fan, which would be traveling at twice the radius from the center.
    - Finally, use the angular speed of the fan to determine how far a point on the outer edge of the fan travels in 30 minutes.

3. **Steps in Detail**:
    - The fan makes 80 revolutions per minute.
    - Therefore, in 15 minutes, the fan makes \(80 \times 15 = 1200\) revolutions.
    - A point halfway between the center and the outer edge of the fan traces a circle whose radius is half of the radius of the outer edge of the fan. If the radius of the outer edge of the fan is \(R\), then the radius of the circle traced by the point halfway between the center and the outer edge is \(\frac{R}{2}\).
    - The circumference of the circle traced by the point halfway between the center and the outer edge is \(2 \pi \cdot \frac{R}{2} = \pi R\).
    - Since this point completes 1200 revolutions in 15 minutes, the distance it travels in 15 minutes is \(1200 \cdot \pi R = 97968\) inches.
    - From this, we can solve for \(R\):
      \[
      1200 \cdot \pi R = 97968 \implies \pi R = \frac{97968}{1200} \implies \pi R = 81.64 \implies R = \frac{81.64}{\pi}
      \]
    - Now, we find the circumference of the circle traced by a point on the outer edge of the fan. The radius of this circle is \(R\), so its circumference is \(2 \pi R\).
    - The fan makes 80 revolutions per minute, so in 30 minutes, the fan makes \(80 \times 30 = 2400\) revolutions. Therefore, a point on the outer edge of the fan travels a distance of \(2400 \cdot 2 \pi R\).
    - Substitute \( R = \frac{81.64}{\pi} \) into the equation to get the final answer.

Let's proceed with implementing this step-by-step in Python to ensure accuracy.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the equation based on the given information
R = sp.symbols('R')
eq = 1200 * sp.pi * R - 97968

# Solve for R
radius_solution = sp.solve(eq, R)[0]

# Calculate the distance traveled by a point on the outer edge of the fan in 30 minutes
revolutions_per_minute = 80
minutes = 30
circumference_outer_edge = 2 * sp.pi * radius_solution
distance_traveled_outer_edge = revolutions_per_minute * minutes * circumference_outer_edge

print(distance_traveled_outer_edge.evalf())
```
