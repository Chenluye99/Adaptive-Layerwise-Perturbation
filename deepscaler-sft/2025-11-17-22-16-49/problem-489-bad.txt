   \[

   \]

\[
a + b = a + 2a = 3a
\]

\[
b + c = 2a + 6a = 8a
\]

So, the ratio \(\frac{a + b}{b + c}\) becomes:

\[
\frac{a + b}{b + c} = \frac{3a}{8a} = \frac{3}{8}
