To solve this problem, let's start by setting up the equations based on the problem statement.

1. **Problem Statement Translation:**
    - The product of two inches and the circumference of a circle is equal to the circle's area.
    - Mathematically, the circumference \(C\) of a circle with radius \(r\) is given by:
      \[
      C = 2 \pi r
      \]
    - The area \(A\) of a circle with radius \(r\) is given by:
      \[
      A = \pi r^2
      \]
    - According to the problem, the product of 2 inches and the circumference of the circle is equal to the circle's area:
      \[
      2 \cdot (2 \pi r) = \pi r^2
      \]

2. **Formulate the Equation:**
    - Simplify the left side of the equation:
      \[
      4 \pi r = \pi r^2
      \]
    - To solve for \( r \), we can divide both sides of the equation by \(\pi\):
      \[
      4 r = r^2
      \]
    - Rearrange the equation to bring all terms to one side:
      \[
      r^2 - 4 r = 0
      \]
    - Factor out \( r \):
      \[
      r (r - 4) = 0
      \]
    - This gives us two solutions:
      \[
      r = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad r = 4
      \]
    - Since the radius \( r \) of a circle cannot be 0, we get:
      \[
      r = 4
      \]

3. **Verification:**
    - We can verify this solution by substituting \( r = 4 \) back into the original equation to ensure it holds true.

Let's confirm this solution by coding it in Python.
```python
import sympy as sp

# Define the variable
r = sp.symbols('r', positive=True)

# Set up the equation based on the problem statement
equation = 2 * (2 * sp.pi * r) - sp.pi * r**2

# Solve the equation
solution = sp.solve(equation, r)
print(solution)
```
