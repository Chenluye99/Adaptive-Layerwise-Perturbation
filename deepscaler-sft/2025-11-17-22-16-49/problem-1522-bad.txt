To solve this problem, we need to determine the probability that Melinda will form a two-digit number between 10 and 20, inclusive, by rolling two standard six-sided dice.

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Generate All Possible Outcomes:**
   - When rolling two six-sided dice, each die can show a number from 1 to 6.
   - Therefore, there are \(6 \times 6 = 36\) possible outcomes when rolling two dice.
   - For each outcome, we can form two possible two-digit numbers by placing the numbers in different orders (e.g., rolling a 6 and a 3 can be 36 or 63).

2. **Form Two-Digit Numbers:**
   - Given the two numbers \(a\) and \(b\) (where \(a, b \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}\)), the possible two-digit numbers are \(10a + b\) and \(10b + a\).

3. **Check for Desired Outcomes:**
   - We need to check which of these two-digit numbers fall between 10 and 20, inclusive.
   - The numbers between 10 and 20 are 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19.

4. **Count Successful Outcomes:**
   - For each pair of rolls, check if either of the two-digit numbers formed is in the range from 10 to 20.
   - Count the number of successful outcomes where at least one of the numbers (formed from the two dice rolls) is between 10 and 20.

5. **Calculate the Probability:**
   - The probability is the ratio of the number of successful outcomes to the total number of outcomes (which is 36).

Let's implement this step-by-step plan in Python to get the accurate result.
```python
import itertools

# Initialize a counter for successful outcomes
successful_outcomes = 0

# Generate all possible outcomes when rolling two six-sided dice
dice_rolls = list(itertools.product(range(1, 7), repeat=2))

# Check for each outcome if any of the possible two-digit numbers formed is between 10 and 20 inclusive
for roll in dice_rolls:
    num1, num2 = roll
    num1, num2 = min(num1, num2), max(num1, num2)
    num1_result = 10 * num1 + num2
    num2_result = 10 * num2 + num1
    
    if 10 <= num1_result <= 20 or 10 <= num2_result <= 20:
        successful_outcomes += 1

# Calculate the probability
total_outcomes = len(dice_rolls)
probability = successful_outcomes / total_outcomes

print(probability)
```
