To solve the problem, we need to find the present value (initial investment) that will grow to $500,000 in 10 years with an annual interest rate of 5%.

The formula for the future value of an investment with annual compounding interest is given by:

\[ FV = PV \cdot (1 + r)^n \]

where:
- \( FV \) is the future value of the investment,
- \( PV \) is the present value (initial investment) of the investment,
- \( r \) is the annual interest rate,
- \( n \) is the number of years.

In this problem:
- \( FV = 500,000 \),
- \( r = 0.05 \),
- \( n = 10 \).

We are solving for \( PV \). Rearrange the formula to solve for \( PV \):

\[ PV = \frac{FV}{(1 + r)^n} \]

Now, substitute in the values:

\[ PV = \frac{500,000}{(1 + 0.05)^{10}} = \frac{500,000}{1.05^{10}} \]

We'll use Python to compute this accurately.
```python
# Given values
FV = 500000
r = 0.05
n = 10

# Calculate present value (PV)
PV = FV / (1 + r) ** n
PV_rounded = round(PV, 2)
print(PV_rounded)
```
Code execution result: 306956.63

Thus, the initial investment required to have \$500,000 in 10 years at an annually compounded interest rate of 5% is \(\boxed{306956.63}\). This is the final answer.
